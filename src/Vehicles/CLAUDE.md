# Vehicles (`src/Vehicles/`)

Machines left in the world, damage, wrecks and their network sync.

Index only: one line per note in `docs/notes/vehicles/<name>.md`. Read a note only when the task
touches its topic; search with `grep -ril <word> docs/notes/vehicles`.

## Architecture

- `garage-tuning` — Garage tuning (#56): `CarTuning` 15 slots in a long, tyre models, `GarageUi` (T, `GarageNear` hook, `--tuning`), hinged car doors (G), their sync, `--tuningcheck`, `--garagecheck`
- `vehicles-vs-equipment` — Vehicles vs equipment: (`src/Vehicles/`). `Rideable.IsVehicle` (bike, helicopter, plane) splits machines that are...
- `garage-buildings` — Garages (#56, #139): GKLAS 1242 -> `BuildingKind.Garage` (.bldg v2), sign and eave stripe flags in UV2.y, a portal interior (no hollow bay), roll-up `DoorLeaf` on the facade, doors open for a vehicle heading at them, generated-world garages, `--garagecheck drive/watch` (`--traffic 0`)
- passengers (#158): `PassengerService` (World/Passengers) seats players in vehicles others drive and moves a vehicle between hosts; see the player note `passengers`
- trucks and buses park as whole trains (trailer, angles, bus flags in `VehicleState`), a lone trailer is `RideKind.Trailer` (`ParkedTrailer`): see the player note `trucks-buses`
- `door-reach` — Getting in by the door (#261): `VehicleReach` aims at one door (view ray, then the door you stand at, 1.35 m), E opens a shut door then gets in, G works it, door-less machines by hull/entry point, outline on the door, `TryGetIn` for probes
- `dormant-vehicles` — Dormant vehicles (#499, the layer #496 phase 3 consumes): placement a pure function of the tile (`DormantSlots`, pinned), only on the tiles round an anchor (#552), drawn as the woken model merged once per look (`DormantLooks`) and instanced, a static body each with shared shapes, harbour boats as a third provider that respawns (#554), a building site's machines as the fourth (#616, `tools/sitemachinenetcheck.sh`), a seamless wake: trains posed on the ground section by section, woken settled where the copy stood, `--wakecheck` (#560), woken once by the aim ray through `VehicleManager.Place` under a deterministic name, nothing about the fleet replicated
- `industrial-yards` — Industrial yards (#516, #496 phase 3): `DormantSlots.ForSite` is the second dormant provider — rows across a site's own front, the mix by trade (a depot's artics and trailers, a forecourt's stock rows), a coupled artic as ONE slot; `SiteYards` is the Godot half; a lorry is drawn as the lorry it wakes as
- `admin-only-spawning` — Only an admin conjures vehicles online: the server counts claims and refuses other parks unless `MayPark`...
- `perf-parked-vehicles` — Nothing per frame for a parked vehicle: dressed once at rest, cached sections, one reused ground ray query (#221)
- `vehicles-in-holds` — Vehicles carried in a carrier's hold (#418): a carrier fills `VehicleDeck.CargoBays` (ramp/tailgate = door parts), fit from hull bounds (`CargoFit`), carried like a deck walker (`FootPlayer.Hold`), handbrake ties down, parked in a hold = `VehicleState.Carrier` posed from the carrier on every peer, `--holdcheck`, `tools/holdnetcheck.sh`
- `forklift` — A drivable forklift (#583; every one: hall (#630), apron, admin): RideKind 193 shaped on the airstairs, a two-stage mast moved rather than rebuilt (`ForkliftMast`), the mast on the free shift paddles (keyboard, pad, VR cab lever), a 1.1 m turning circle, the hull not out at the tines, `--forkliftcheck`; the handbrake holds (it is not the reverse pedal), the seat a chair's high hip
- `hall-cars` — The cars in an underground car park's bays are vehicles asleep (#558 PR 3): `HallCarRule` (pure: owner `<building>_c`, the hash that picks the ordinary car, `NoseYaw`), `HallCars.SlotOf`, one `ParkedCars` node per interior (a MultiMesh per look, a `DormantBody` per bay, carved out of the merged mesh), server wake for a peer inside, the sleeper hides when the vehicle node exists, the bay refills `RespawnSeconds` after the car is driven off, `--flatcheck`, `tools/hallcarnetcheck.sh`
- `pallets` — Pallets a forklift lifts (#583 phase 2): a hall's loose floor pallets carved out of the merged mesh as `PalletNode`s (no plan change), the load byte (goods roll + square deck), ids, the four-way fork rule (along or across the runners; no button: raised through `Seat` lifts, under `SetDown` sets down), carried as `Forklift.Carrying` in the pose, `PalletService` at World/Pallets (server, session-only, Taken + Loose in LV95), a moved pallet is no loot container, `--palletcheck`, `tools/palletnetcheck.sh`; phase 3: apron stacks by trade (`SitePallets`, `SiteFront`: the yard is centred on the door, not the facade) drawn with the dormant fleet, a forklift slot in a warehouse's and a works' yard, yard takes checked against the tile's own files; #615: any machine with tines lifts them (`IForks`: the telehandler, the fork loader `RideKind.WheelLoaderForks`; the server's check by kind, `PalletService.CarryingOf`; `--forkcheck`, a telehandler round in `tools/palletnetcheck.sh`); a building site's pallets of materials too (`<building>:c<slot>`, `SitePlans.PalletAt`, `tools/sitepalletnetcheck.sh`)
- `excavator` — The tracked excavator (#611, epic #605): RideKind 194, the yaw is the cab's and it travels along its tracks through `Slip = −Slew`, turns on the spot, a drive / dig toggle (C / pad B) with the arm on both sticks ISO pattern (WASD + arrows, VR cab joysticks as `Kind.Hold` levers), joints that stop where let go, the arm in the pose and packed in the flags, protocol 20, `--excavatorcheck`, `tools/excavatornetcheck.sh`; the mini (#614, RideKind 196) is the same class over `ExcavatorSpec`, with a dozer blade (Shift/Ctrl, RB/LB) carried in the pose's bucket float and in 3 bits of the flags, protocol 22, `--excavatorcheck mini`
- `mini-dumper` — The tracked mini dumper (#614): RideKind 200, tracks turning it on the spot, a front skip on the rig's tipping node that tips on the tipper's action (N / VR console button), stopped; one bit in the pose and the parked flags, protocol 30, `--dumpercheck`, a round in `tools/excavatornetcheck.sh`
- `compact-roller` — The compact tandem roller (#614): RideKind 197, frame steering shared with the loader (`FrameSteering`), a hydrostatic drive, drums vibrating on the work toggle (C / pad B / a VR console button) with a 55 Hz hum (`SiteSfx.RollerDrum`) and a ride-camera tremble (`Rideable.CameraShake`, scaled by the setting, never in VR), protocol 23, `--rollercheck`, a roller round in `tools/excavatornetcheck.sh`
- `telehandler` — The telehandler (#614): RideKind 198, a boom from the back of the chassis with a self-levelling fork carriage (lift / extend / tilt in work mode on the arrows, right stick and the mast paddles), front / four-wheel / crab steering on the roof switch (a bicycle with both axles steered, circles measured = computed), mode and tilt packed into the pose's floats, protocol 24, `--telehandlercheck`, a round in `tools/excavatornetcheck.sh`
- `wheel-loader` — The articulated wheel loader (#612): RideKind 195, yaw is the rear frame's, frame-steering kinematics (4.5 m circle measured = computed), work mode on the excavator's toggle but still driving, lift and tilt on the right stick / arrows / VR levers, three angles in the pose and flags, protocol 21, `--loadercheck`, a loader round in `tools/excavatornetcheck.sh`
- `airstairs` — Mobile airstairs (#417): RideKind 126, platform raised to a door's sill found from the aircraft deck's `DoorStep` boxes (any walkable `Flyer`), docks when let go by a door, parked height follows the sill, shoved clear by a taxiing aircraft, `AirstairsDock.PlaceAt` for stands (#422), cab with a heavy cockpit and walkers carried (#445), `--stairscheck`, `tools/stairsnetcheck.sh`
- `ground-query` — the ground under a point for a vehicle (driven truck sections, parked `VehicleBody`) is `World.GroundQuery.Under(self, ray, exclude, p, terrain, pastPlayers)`; never another copy (#221)
