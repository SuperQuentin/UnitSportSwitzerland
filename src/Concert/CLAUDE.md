# Concerts (`src/Concert/`)

Random artist events in the stadiums (#885): the event generator, stages, the DMX rig, the light desk.

Index only: one line per note in `docs/notes/concert/<name>.md`. Read a note only when the task
touches its topic; search with `grep -ril <word> docs/notes/concert`.

- `concerts` — Concerts (#885): a seeded event a day per stadium (artist, genre, layout end/side/thrust/round, screens, rig), `StagePlan`, real DMX layouts patched into universes, `LightDesk` writes / `ConcertRig` reads, made-up songs on the shared clock until the music drives it, `--concert N|off`, `--concertcheck` ; show control: Art-Net/sACN/OSC into the operator's game (`ShowInput`), `/concert host|input|patch`, DMX relayed by `ConcertService` (channel 4), `tools/concertnetcheck.sh` ; music (a CD setlist from the PA drives the desk), effects (flames, CO2, confetti, gerbs, airbursts, fog on DMX), the crowd (instanced, one shader), the light desk screen at FOH, `--concertdeskcheck` ; cameras: rigs (rails, wire cam, jib, drone...), IMAG, program, NDI out, a crew running them (`/concert camera take`), beams stopped by walls, `--concertcamcheck` ; phrases (sync, stagger, mirror, cycle...), rhythm pump, stepper motors, the PA bouncing on the kick, `/concert debug` beat window, performance (IMAG range, pose cache)
- `cd-vocal` (audio) — the lead artist lip-syncs the CD's vocal at the listener's delay, melody runs lift and tint the heads, long notes put the spots on the singer, `/concert cue voice|music`
- `mvr` — MVR/GDTF (#941): `/concert export` (rig + GDTFs + stage glTF to `user://concert/mvr/`), `/concert import <file> [stage|stadium]|off` on a hosted set (checked, replicated `RigSet`, protocol 42), the desk's MVR row, frames and axes, mapping anyone's GDTF onto our attributes and kinds, `MvrTests`, pymvr-checked
- `mvr-scene-objects` — MVR scene objects (#958): Truss/Support/SceneObject/VideoScreen/Projector/FocusPoint, Symdefs, `MvrGeometry` (3DS + glTF, pure .NET), the venue skipped (names, >28 m), `RigData` meshes/objects and their caps, MultiMesh drawing
- `mvr-fixture-details` — MVR fixture details (#959): Focus as pan/tilt offsets, DMXInvertPan/Tilt, multi-break patching, VideoScreen as a screen fixture, zoom CustomCommands, 1.6 export (IDs, uuids, Class/Position, FocusPoints), original GDTFs kept on re-export by the importing machine
- `mvr-xchange` — MVR-xchange station (#960): `/concert xchange on|off|list|pull|commit|join|ws`, TCP packets and JSON messages, our own mDNS on 5353, WebSocket client, the desk's Xchange row, interop with BlenderDMX
- `gdtf-bodies` — GDTF fixture bodies (#961): `GdtfBody` (geometry tree to base/yoke/head, 3DS/glTF models or primitives, 600 triangles a part), `RigData` bodies and their caps, pan turns the yoke, tilt the head, the beam from the lens; showcase "GDTF bodies"
- `mvr-collision` — collision on imported MVR objects (#974): `MvrCollision` judges each piece (none, hull for low walkable pieces so stairs are ramps, own triangles, oriented box), caps, beams stop on them (`BeamReach`), crowd footprints, client-only like the stage; `--concertwalkcheck`
- `mvr-textures` — per-face colours and textures on imported 3D (#973): 3DS materials and maps, glTF baseColor factor/texture, `MvrImage` (pure .NET PNG, shrunk to 64 px), `RigData.Textures` and its budget, one surface per texture, `prop.gdshaderinc` albedo_texture
- `mvr-video` — video on imported screens (#975): size from the screen's geometry (1 cm proud), Ogg Theora File sources cached by hash in `user://concert/mvr/video/` (never sent; `/concert videos <mvr>`), NDI receive (`ConcertNdi.Receiver`), CITP/CaptureDevice/projectors noted
- `crew-and-zones` — the FOH crew (sound, light, video) and camera operators (`ConcertCrew`, animated on the beat), crew zones in `StagePlan.Zones` (FOH, delay towers, pit, camera footprints from `CameraRig.Footprint`, the far camera tower) the crowd never fills, crew barriers round them (#985)
- `wristbands` — LED wristbands in the crowd (#986): IR emitter heads, IR floods and a transmitter on DMX (kinds, channels, how many per layout), the crowd shader's per-band cone test with a 0.5 s hold and sweep trail, the desk's looks (sweeps, stand chases, all on at the drop, ballad twinkle), what turns bands on
- `stadium-sound` — Stadium sound (#984): the PA late by distance (343 m/s, towers timed to the mains, pitch pull not seek), line-array throw, air, coverage, muffled outside the bowl; the Concert bus slap and reverb by venue; the reacting crowd (Crowd bus); `--concertsoundprobe`
