# World systems (`src/World/`)

Day/night, traffic, tree collision and player races.

Index only: one line per note in `docs/notes/world/<name>.md`. Read a note only when the task
touches its topic; search with `grep -ril <word> docs/notes/world`.

## Architecture

- `fun-world` — The fun world (#1396), on the Earth too (#1648: `FunWorld.Place`, the floor through the frame and growing with the height, no globe): `Terrain/Fixture/FunCourse` (venues, race track, kart loop, runway, snow slope + T-bar, arena, PvP block, stunt park, truck yard, lake, festival field) handed to the game through `Venues.Override`, `StadiumCatalog.UseWorld`, `FixtureChunkSource.Fleet`, piste tiles; `World/Fun`: `FunWorld` rules (dusk clock, Älplerfest all year, PvP only in its block, `FunCrates`), `FunBackdrop` (synthwave sky, grid floor, wireframe mountains, palms, neon), `FunRings` air rings
- `day-night` — Day/night: (`World/DayNight`): four global shader uniforms declared in `project.godot` `[shader_globals]` —...; the sky draws the sun's disc only, `--skyshot` (#1798); indoors: sky-coloured glass, room-lit cameras, open doors light the street at night (#134); `/time` set/add/speed (#202); online one server-owned `WorldClock` on `ServerNow`, game logic reads `WorldClock.CurrentHour` (#452); the real sun and moon from the date and the camera's place, `--sun fixed` (#1430); `--terminatorcheck` against NOAA from orbit (#1845)
- `local-time` — `/time` where the player stands (#1801): named hours off the real sun at the place (solar noon, real rise/set, day/night offsets, polar messages), the zone word (`here`/`swiss`/`utc+8`, default the player's: Swiss at home, UTC + lon/15 away), Switzerland (250 km of Bern) unchanged, the server reads the admin's body; `--localtimecheck [home]`, `tools/localtimenetcheck.sh`
- `earth-curvature` — #187: the world flat on the CPU, round where drawn: `CURVED` in every world shader (`shaders/common/curvature.gdshaderinc`, round the view's own camera), `Core/Curvature` (k = 0.13, 246 m at 60 km), `world_curvature` global, `--curvature 0`; the server's sight lines add the bulge; what is not dropped; horizon cull margin
- `night-sky` — Stars (Yale BSC), planets and their axes (JPL elements, IAU), Jupiter's moons and Titan, Saturn's rings, satellites (SGP4, CelesTrak once a day): `StarField`, `SkyBodies`, `Satellites`, `--skycheck` (#1444); from space read at the camera itself, the moon's halo over its disc (#1845)
- `sun` — The sun as a body (#1479): `SunDisc` at the ephemeris sun (core, corona by air, starburst; `sun_look.gdshaderinc` shared with the space sun), the sky never draws the light under the real sun, light shafts (`SunShafts`, "Sun rays" setting), stars hidden in its glare, `--skyshot sun` / `--skyshot-slats`
- `object-containers` — Server object containers (#689): a vehicle, item or radio nobody has been near sleeps in `user://containers/server/E_N.json` and wakes when a player returns or after a restart; stable oid + generation, write order keeps it exactly once through crashes (model-checked), awake slots kept, a woken car back in its bay becomes scenery again, `tools/containernetcheck.sh`
- `locate` — `/locate <thing> [+ selector] [in <town>]` (#1277): the server's lists + a building index built at boot (cached), spires, garage doors on the real footprint; `@E,N` in chat gets a pin opening Teleport/Track/Map/Copy; the index fills round players first and partial answers say so; the "/" picker and the quick menu's Chat wedge for pad and VR
- `traffic-generators` — Traffic generators and destinations (#971): `TrafficEndpoint`, shares, spawn queue, server list, the test world's arms, debug menu, path drawing
- `traffic-trains` — Traffic and trains: (`World/Traffic`, `World/LaneGraph`): local and cosmetic per client (not replicated), but solid...
- `lane-query` — `--lanequery` (#1760): traffic's car lane graph off the road tiles without a world: the long drive's route and where one-way cuts it (each wrong-way edge named), edges/islands/poles/segment records near a place, CSV dump; rebuilt-tile checks on a scratch copy
- `traffic-trams` — Trams (#1057): tram graph from TLM single tracks oriented by their partner, RoadGen's tram paths through the cores, line/curve speeds, following, tram bar groups, stops with dwell, cars giving way, `--trafficcheck --mover tram`
- `traffic-on-leaves` — #1636 G2 C6: under `--frame earth` the graphs come from the leaves round the view (`Traffic.Leaves`, `EarthLeaves.RoadsOf`); `TilePlace` places every builder's records (LV95 unchanged, leaves by anchor) and keys ends by Earth place (`EarthKey`); `OnAnchor` leaf tiles, rebuild at 800 m, signal arms turn on re-anchors
- `traffic-modes` — Traffic modes' shared base (#1060): walk, ride, tram graphs built per mode (`Traffic.Walkers`/`Riders`/`Trams` seams), pooled `SlowMover`s, the blocky walker/rider/tram models (#1147, `TrafficModeMeshBuilder`), trams as train-like `Vehicle`s, the crossers `Coming` and the cars' following read, densities and `--walkers`/`--bikes`/`--trams`, `--trafficcheck --mover`
- `traffic-walkers` — Walkers (#1033): walk graph from sidewalks, paths, `CPAR` crossings (two-stage islands) and zebras read off the paint, unmarked crossings over side roads (on a gap), ties that never cross a carriageway, verge walkways at rural lights; green-only crossing, zebra claims the cars stop for (`ZebraWait`), `--trafficcheck --mover walker` counts
- `traffic-riders` — Riders (#1056): the ride graph (lanes, paths, car lanes, ways, riders' and cars' junction paths, contraflow lanes), lines from transition strips, bike groups and their stop lines, right on red, `--trafficcheck --mover rider [--dense]`, what is not done
- `traffic-and-races` — Traffic around a race (#85, #159): drivers react only to racers they can see, give way at junctions, no spawns in front of fast racers; racers sense traffic joining and across bridged junctions, pass standing traffic, retire when wrecked; before/after numbers
- `trees-solid` — Trees are solid: (`World/TreeColliders`, issue #14): no per-tile tree collision — 100k+ trees a forest tile. A pool...
- `races` — Races between players in anything (`World/RaceManager`, `RaceCourse`, `RaceGates`, `Player/GatePilot`): concurrent races, NPC entrants, road routes and air gates,...
- `npc-arrivals` — Race NPCs drive in to the grid: (`World/NpcArrival`, #51) out of sight on the race road, from behind or from ahead with a three-point turn, handbrake turn or donut; `--arrivalcheck`...
- `npc-handoff` — Race NPCs and races belong to nobody (#50): simulator = authority, 600 m zone, handoff on leave/crash/distance, host migration
- `africa-twin-egg` — A random Africa Twin in a parking bay at Riddes (#55): `TileEntered`, server-placed, server authority at rest, `--eggcheck`
- `perf-traffic-tick` — Traffic per tick: road sampled only near an obstacle, gap check on the X-sorted `_byX` window, cars drawn to 600 m, shared cached meshes (#221)
- `landings` — Piers, landings and jetties (#377): `landings.json` from TLM stops, ferry ends, `Hafensteg` lines and `Steg` roads (`--landings`), `World.Landings` (the API for #379: berths with keel, heading, depth), piers drawn by `PierMeshBuilder` in the roads mesh with the Prop role and collided as road cells, `AssetKind.Landings`, the steamer alongside Nyon's pier, boat drivers onto jetties, `--steamercheck pier|nyon`; #383: the gangway plank tilting to a head at the Steg's height (`GangwayFit`), marina boats along the jetties as dormant slots (`DormantSlots.ForMarina`, #554; `Jetty.BoatBerths`; on earth leaves `ForLeafMarina`, #1735, and the decks `LeafPiers`, #1736, bridge decks collided both ways)
- `airports` — Aircraft parked at GVA and ZRH stands (#422): `airports.json` from OSM stands on TLM paved area (`--airports`, `AirportPlanner`), `World/AirportStands` places A320s with stairs docked, AN-124, freighter (server, refill after 300 s), runway profiles (no smoothing needed), fixture `airport`, `--airportcheck`, `tools/airportnetcheck.sh`
- `water-field` — Water (#299): `WaterField` API (TryLevelAt, IsUnderwater, Height/Normal/Velocity on the server clock), `WaveSpectrum` the one source of the wave constants (pushed as shader globals), sea state (`/seastate`, `--sea-state`, replicated), floating-origin safe, underwater look, `/water`, checks
- `perf-racenpc-server-physics` — `RaceNpc` has no physics step on the dedicated server (a data proxy, never the simulator) (#221)
- `perf-race-tick` — `RaceManager.ServerTick` per frame: no LINQ/`GetNodeOrNull`; node lookups on the 1 s review pass (#221)
- `perf-player-snapshot` (player) — `Traffic` obstacles come from the tick's `PlayerSnapshot` into reused lists, never a group scan + `ToList` per tick (#221)
- `ski-snow` — Ski slopes' snow (#887): ski day 06:00-06:00 and the reset, deterministic `SnowWeather`, `SnowModel` (corduroy, loose, ice, slush, moguls), wear = crowd + real skiers (server, replicated), `/weather` and `--weather`, `PisteLayer` + `piste_snow` shader, trees cleared, `Surfaces` snow/ice, skis friction, `SlopeHud`, roads under slopes snowed over, `SlopeMarkers` poles, `SlopeMap` ski minimap; on earth leaves through their stand-ins (#1735: `piste_offset`, the geoid shift, own-leaf poles, `--pistecheck`; #1737: ski map and ski races, fixture line runs)
- `ski-lifts` — Ski lifts (#912, #915): OSM types, speeds, seats and names (`ropeways.json`), cable cars shuttle, twins merged, seated riders placed from the clock on every peer, stations, `liftnetcheck`; ropeway pieces joined into lines (`RopewayJoin`), carriers looping on the shared clock (`RopewayMotion`, no traffic), `RopewayLayer` MultiMesh (pinned at the origin, `CustomAabb`), board with E / pad / VR grip at a bottom station on foot or skis, `Carrier` up, `ReleaseRiding` on skis at the top, drag lifts let go, lift camera, `--ride skis --lift`
- `air-lift` — Thermals by sun and slope, ridge lift by the wind of the day, updraft volumes (campfires, `Volumes.Add`) in pure `AirLift`, `SunPath` out of `DayNight`, `AirLiftField` feeds `FlightEnv.Air` to the own canopy/wingsuit/pigeon, off in a match and with `--lift off`, `--flycheck thermal|campfire|pigeon-thermal` (#1225)
- `body-ground` — The ground on another body (#1850): `BodyHeights` (pyramid + craters), `BodyGround`'s 512 m square round the camera eased into the globe (a square hole in its tiles), `EarthWorld.OtherBody` (`IBodyGround`) for every ground query off the Earth, `BodyGravity`, Earth-only systems guarded; `--mooncheck --bodies`
- `saturn-rings` — Saturn's rings (#1854): `ring_slab.gdshader` (each pixel's ray through a 400 m slab, stepped optical depth from the map's alpha: face-on, edge-on band, haze inside), ice chunks the same on every peer (`RingField` hashed cells, `RingChunks` 1.5 km round the camera, solid within 500 m, `RingRocks`), `CameraGlobal.CloseContent`, `--ringcheck`
- `moon-sites` — The Moon's historic and invented sites (#1939, plan `docs/plans/moon-surface.md`): `MoonSites` table (Apollo, Luna, Chang'e, S-IVB impacts, wrecks, flags) at LOLA's selenographic coordinates, `SurfaceStamps` in `BodyHeights` (calm, level, strip, crater), `MoonSurface` building sites within 3 km and placing them from doubles (a plain `Node`: a `Node3D` holder snapped to 32 m), `MoonSiteMeshes` in the viewer, footpaths and tracks, `--moonsites`, `--moondance`
- `moon-colonies` — Moon colonies (#1940): `MoonColonies` table (Artemis Base by `--moonsurvey`), `Level` plateau stamps, `ColonyPlan` (hub, spines of cans, hangar, pads, domes placed only where `Clear`), `ColonyKit` modules in the viewer (hub, can, stack, moondust dome, glass biodome, hangar, tubes, alleys, pads, road; lit windows on the glass surface), `ColonyBuilder`, `--moonsites artemis[:close]`
- `moon-interiors` — Moon colonies walk-in (#1941): hollow modules (double-sided shells, doorways at `FloorY` 1.55 m, tubes 2.8 m across), furniture by room (`ColonyInteriors`, viewer "Moon rooms"), airlocks at the spine ends (`ColonyAirlocks`: two sliding doors never both open, a 3 s pump, E / pad Y / VR grip through `TryInteract`), the air (`Pressurised`), the suit and `Headwear.SpaceHelmet` (`MoonSuit`, set by `OccasionHats`), `--airlockcheck`
- `body-air` — A body's air as data (#1853): `Core/BodyAir` (the shell's fine and coarse parts, absorption, sun power, drag density), the Earth's = the shader's old constants (tested), Mars' butterscotch day and blue sunset, the shell round any body with air from the ground up, that body's own sun, `SpaceOrbit` drag by body
