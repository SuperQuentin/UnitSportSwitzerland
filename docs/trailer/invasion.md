# "Invasion, Swiss Style": film script (#866)

The third film of the UFO plan (`docs/plans/ufo-feature-films.md`, piece F): a 4:22 tour of the
whole game with a story. A fleet of UFOs invades Switzerland and beams up whatever it fancies; the
Swiss answer with everything they have, Swiss style, and nobody really gets hurt. It ends with the
aliens dancing at the Christmas party on the Gornergrat. It plays without sound: the dialogue is in
comic speech bubbles (#862), short and in English, with subtitle tracks in French, German, Italian
and Rumantsch (`invasion.subtitles.json`).

- **Shots**: `src/Trailer/InvasionScript.cs`, `FILM=invasion tools/trailer.sh stills|preview|render`.
- **Look**: every shot is in **Cartoon**. There is no PS1 or Realistic shot; the styles feature is
  shown by the Cartoon look itself. Every shot is on **real terrain** (the built tiles).
- **Status**: stage 1 is done (the story, the places, the non-UFO shots framed). Stage 2 merges the
  UFO (#861), the bubbles (#862) and the cows (#863), then stages their beats and renders.
