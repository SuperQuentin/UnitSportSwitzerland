# "I Got a Stick": a night at the Drognens barracks (#717)

A ~30 s music clip, filmed in the game by the trailer director (`src/Trailer/DrognensScript.cs`,
`FILM=drognens tools/trailer.sh render`).

## The song

"I Got a Stick Feat James Gavins" by Kevin MacLeod, James Gavins (voice) and Bryan Teoh
(incompetech.com), licensed CC BY 4.0. It is funk at 121 BPM (1.98 s a bar), first downbeat at
0.322 s. The music lasts 14 bars and fades out by 28.5 s; the film runs a 15th bar, to 30.07 s,
for the end card. Phrases are 4 bars long, and the horns brighten at bar 9.

## The place

The Place d'armes de Drognens, Siviriez/Romont FR, opened in 1972. The army's logistics schools
train there: the supply school E rav 45 and the traffic and transport school E CT 47. The barracks
stand at about E 2'558'330, N 1'169'420:
- two long blocks;
- the garages and the multi-purpose hall;
- a sports pitch;
- a ring road round the north block.

Recruits there learn to drive and keep the army's vehicles, so the yards are full of them:
- the Mowag Duro (the troop transporter, #714);
- the Mercedes G 300 (the Puch's successor);
- Iveco lorries.

## Easter eggs

- **The room**: number **61-405**, from the army's photo.
- **The kit**: the helmets on the wardrobes and the packs on the bed ends.
- **The poker table**: a Gruyère, Biberli and the Radio on it.
- **The vehicles**: the Duro rows, the Puch's successor, the "M" plates.
- **The garage**: garage 4.
- **The chat**: "lights out at 22:00" at 22:47.
- **The ending**: push-ups replaced by a salute, as every recruit wishes.
