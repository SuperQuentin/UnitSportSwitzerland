# Server regions: a frame and a physics space per cluster of bodies (#1456)

G2a of global tiling (#1443); design and audit in `docs/plans/server-regions.md`.

- **What**: the dedicated server runs its physics in several local frames, one per cluster of bodies
  (`Net/Regions`). A bot 300 km from the server's origin walks at the same float precision as one beside
  it; with the global tiling (G2) no flat frame has to serve players 500 km apart.
- **The main region** is the server's world as it always was: the manifest origin, the root world,
  `World/Terrain`. A body within `RegionPlanner.HomeM` (20 km) of it, or linked to one that is, stays
  there, so the net checks near the default spawn run exactly as before. On the real map the
  manifest origin is LV95 2541000/1135500, so Riddes and Geneva (~45 km) play in regions of their own.
- **Clusters** (`RegionPlanner`, pure, `RegionPlannerTests`): bodies within `LinkM` 2 km always share a
  region, part past `SplitM` 6 km; a region whose members reach 8 km from its anchor and whose centre
  drifted 4 km re-anchors (a new region at the centre, every member handed over). Decided every 0.5 s
  (`ServerRegions.Decide`). An empty region is freed after 10 s.
- **A region** (`ServerRegion`): a `WorldOrigin`, a `SubViewport` with an explicit `World3D` (not
  `OwnWorld3D`: its `World3D` then reads null) holding its own `ChunkManager` (collision round its bots,
  coarse grids round its proxies, the same tile cache and generated fill) and `TreeColliders`.
- **A body in a region** (`FootPlayer.Region`, server only): its node stays at `World/Players/<name>`
  (RPC paths must match the clients'); `PhysicsServer3D.BodySetSpace` moves its RID, again on every
  `NodeAdded` (entering a tree resets it) and `NotificationEnabled`; its parts with collision (a truck's
  sections) follow through `NodeAdded`. `Origin`, `Terrain`, `Space` resolve through the region.
  - **Never `GetWorld3D().DirectSpaceState` in body code**: `FootPlayer.Space`. On the server the
    viewport's world is the main region's whatever region the body is in.
  - Statics bound to the main terrain step aside while a far body steps and thinks:
    `WaterField.In(body.RegionTerrain)`.
- **Spawning**: `PlayerReplication.CreateNpc` asks `ServerRegions.For(at)`: a bot spawned far away starts in
  its own frame, never through the main frame's coarse floats. A player's proxy starts in main and is
  handed over at the next decision (exact, from `NetGlobal`).
- **A handover** (`FootPlayer.EnterRegion`): a proxy at once; a bot once the target has collision under it
  (a ghost anchor asks for it first), or once the body has none either (in the air), or after 15 s. The
  shift moves what the body keeps in world space (`OriginShifter.Move`, the handlers), then the position
  is set exact from the Earth position and the kinematic body teleported. `ServerRegions.Put(body, at)`
  moves a body somewhere else now, region and all (a bot called to a Schwingen ring).
- **Shadows**: the main `ChunkManager` keeps a plain anchor for every body in another region, in the
  main frame. The data the server serves and reads there (heights for the ground lambdas, the steamer,
  the airport stands, the dormant fleets, the generated fill reaching out) loads as before.
- **Mirrors** (`RegionMirrors`): every solid thing the server holds in its main frame (parked vehicles,
  Versteckis decor, BR structures and crates) gets a static copy in each far region within 300 m of
  its bots, placed from its Earth position (a vehicle's exact `Global`) and following the original.
  Areas are not copied. `--nomirrors` turns them off.
- **Measuring across frames** (the audit's ~70 sites):
  - a service in the main frame reads a body as `origin.ToWorld(body.Global)` (`PlayerPositions`, the
    birds' observers, doors, pistes, chat's `WorldOf`), or measures in `GlobalPos` (crate reach, revives,
    watched bots, BR zone, pings);
  - a body reads another as `body.WhereIs(other)` (a player or a vehicle), a main-frame point as
    `body.FromFrame(frame, p)` and back with `ToFrame` (crates, stops, the plane, saucers, cars);
  - a bot's lists of bodies are its region's (`BotWorld.Bodies`, `PlayerHits.Targets`); its shots go out
    from its frame (`ItemEvents.ServerSend/ServerHit(..., frame)`).
- **Checks**: `tools/regioncheck.sh` (net tier for the region code): at the spawn and 300 km east, a
  walker into a blocker and a second into a parked car; the tracks with both sites match each site
  alone to 0.000 cm. `BEFORE=1` adds `--noregions` (the far site 3.7 cm off) and `--nomirrors` (the far
  walker goes 22.7 m through the car). `--regionat E,N --regionfar E,N --regionwander S` for real terrain
  (`--chunks D:/...`): Geneva and Riddes (the D: set spans 93 km), every bot strolled 60-90 m among trunks
  and houses, on the ground 100 % of the time.
- **Run Godot by hand with a scratch `user://`** (`APPDATA=...`): the server files parked cars in its
  object containers, and the check's cars stayed in the real save once.
- **Not yet**: a region's frame is an LV95 offset; G2 gives it #1430's anchor frame (`OriginFrame.Axes`,
  #1460), and a handover's `Frame.Since` then carries the rotation. Interiors are still not built on
  the server (`bots/server-collision`).
