# Online between bodies (#1852, C3b of `docs/plans/planets.md`)

A server hosts the Moon (any landable body) next to the Earth: players step in there, see each other, and the server runs
bots there on its own ground. Protocol 97 (on top of D2's 96).

- **The body on the wire**: `FootPlayer.NetBody` and `NetPlace.NetBody` (a `BodyId`, the Earth 0) are sent before the
  three doubles, so `NetZ`'s setter reads the place whole with its body (`GlobalPos.FromWire(x, y, z, body)`).
  `GlobalPos.Write` adds `<key>_b` off the Earth (spawn data, state dictionaries); a bot's spawn array carries it at
  index 10 (`PlayerReplication.BotData`). A remote whose body changes is snapped once (`RemoteInterpolator.Push`).
- **Still Earth-only**: RPCs that take `double x, double y, double z` (items, builds, combat, birds, adventure…). An
  action on the Moon that sends a place lands in the Earth's frame on the server. To audit before those features go
  to the Moon.
- **Distances across bodies are infinite** (`GlobalPos.DistanceTo`, `HorizontalDistanceTo`): "is it near" on a server
  with players on two bodies is "no", so the many proximity checks (bots watched, entity interest, …) need no change.
  Arithmetic across bodies (`OffsetTo`, `Moved`, `-`) still throws: convert with `In(body, moment)`.
- **Interest** (`InterestService`): two players on different bodies are never in each other's interest. On one other
  body the viewer works in its own east-up-south there (`OffsetTo`), the body's sphere the only occluder
  (`Net/BodySight`: the polar radius, or the lower of the two places' distance from the centre, less 2 km), the height
  above ground from `ServerBodies.HeightUnder`. No interior surface point off the Earth.
- **Server regions per body** (`net/server-regions`): `RegionPlanner` never links two bodies, home is only on the
  main anchor's body, a cluster's centre goes on its own body's ellipsoid (`BodyMean`), and a region is claimed only by
  bodies on its body. `ServerRegions.Create` makes an ENU frame on that body and asks `MakeBodyGround`
  (`World/Bodies/ServerBodies.Ground`): a `ServerBodyGround`, collision squares round the region's solid anchors (bots,
  handover ghosts), each the clients' near square (`BodyGround.NearGrid`: 512 m of 2 m cells from
  `BodyGround.HeightsOf`, the same heights), built on a worker, dropped when no anchor stands on it; heights answered
  anywhere. The region's space gets the body's gravity; `FootPlayer.Gravity` follows the region's body on the server
  (the anchor's on clients). No shadows nor mirrors of the main frame off the Earth. A body on a body the server makes
  no ground for (a giant, the Sun's space, or `MakeBodyGround` null on a flat server) is parked in main, as in Space.
- **Bots on the Moon** (the colonists, #1945): `BotDirector.Add(at, …)` with a place on the Moon (`GlobalPos.On(BodyId.Moon, …)`) spawns the bot straight into the server's Moon region (`ServerRegions.For`), on its ground, at its gravity; `/bot add` does it at an admin standing there. The client draws it like any copy.
- **The server's data**: `--bodies` as the clients'; without it the bots stand on the generated relief on the datum
  while the clients draw no ground there. Server and clients need the same data.
- **`/goto` online** (an admin's): the server checks the line (`BodyGoto.TryParse`) and sends `SentToBody` to the
  sender, whose client moves its fly camera; stepping in publishes the body on that body.
- **The far-marker list** (`Net/FarMarkers` at `World/FarMarkers`): once a second the server sends each peer every human
  player out of its interest (peer, body, three doubles, ride), an empty list once after a non-empty one. Bots are not
  listed. `FarMarkers.Current` / `Of(peer)` / `Client.NameOf(peer)` (the chat's tag names) on the client.
- **The telescope** (`items/telescope`): each marker in the round picture gets a label beside its place (name, body,
  distance in m/km/AU), placed in doubles through the frame tree at the sky moment (`TelescopeView.MarkerSight`: a
  place on the Moon converted to the Earth's frame). The target menu lists the markers after the bodies;
  `SelectPlayer(peer)` locks on one and the lock follows it; the info panel says who, which body, how far. Desktop only
  (VR's eyepiece is a panel: no labels yet). `TelescopeView.Labels` lists what is labelled, for the checks.
- **Check**: `tools/moonnetcheck.sh` (net tier, ~6 min, `BODIES=` the planets data): B's telescope on the Earth, A to
  the Moon by `/goto` and T; B's marker for A within 0.1 m of A's own place, the lock holding A's label in the middle,
  on the Moon's disc; B beside A; each sees the other walk (place within 1 m) and jump; `/bot add 2` there: the server's
  Moon region and ground, both clients see the bots stand. Unit: `OnlineBodiesTests`.
- **Not yet**: the shuttle's HUD target cue lists no players; vehicles in a Moon region on the server fall at
  `Rideable.Gravity` (the anchor's, the Earth's on the server); bots' brains sense with the Earth's systems.
