# Positions on the wire are LV95, never world space

- **Every peer has its own origin** and it moves (#185, `core/floating-origin`), so a world-space
  `Vector3` means nothing to the receiver. Positions cross the network as LV95 + altitude doubles
  (`Core/GlobalPos`); velocities, directions and rotations stay `Vector3` (`OriginShift.Direction`
  turns them, the identity until #187). Breaking this rule shows as things kilometres off, or a
  remote that is right only until one side shifts: test with `--originstress` on the clients.
- **One position**: three doubles. RPCs take `double e, double n, double alt` (`CombatManager.Shot`,
  `ItemEvents.Relay`, `BirdNet`); dictionaries use `GlobalPos.Write(d, key)` / `GlobalPos.Read`
  (`VehicleState`, `RadioState`, `DropState` hold a `GlobalPos`).
- **A list of points**: one anchor (`OriginFrame.Anchor(e, n)`, near the points) plus float offsets;
  the receiver maps them with `origin.Since(anchor)`. Race roads and gates (`RaceManager.Setup`,
  `NpcArrive`: the race's frame) and bird snapshots (each packet starts with an anchor near the peer).
- **Synchronized bodies**: `FootPlayer` replicates `NetE`/`NetN`/`NetAlt` (`NetGlobal`) in place of a
  position; vehicles, radios and dropped items carry a `Net/NetPlace` child whose three doubles
  replace `.:position` in their synchronizer (`NetPlace.Properties`, added before the synchronizer:
  it applies the spawn state). The authority publishes; every other peer is moved when `NetAlt`
  arrives. Spawn data (`PlayerReplication.NpcData`, the state dictionaries) is LV95 too.
- **The server relays LV95 unchanged** and measures with it (`FootPlayer.Global`, `VehicleBody.Global`:
  the last published value, exact however far the player is from the server's own origin). Its own
  world floats are only for metre-scale checks between nearby bodies (door reach, despawn ranges).
- A new networked feature: put `GlobalPos` in its payload from the start; a new synchronized body
  gets a `NetPlace`. Any change to what goes on the wire bumps `Handshake.Protocol`.
