# Positions on the wire are ECEF, directions local ENU, headings true north

Since protocol 93 (#1503, G2 A2 of `docs/plans/global-tiling-g2.md`). Before it: LV95 and world axes.

- **Every peer has its own origin and its own axes** (#185, `core/floating-origin`): a world `Vector3` or a world
  yaw means nothing to the receiver. A client's frame can be an anchor turned against the grid (`--originyaw`,
  Space), and the flat world's axes are the LV95 grid's, which turn from true north by the meridian convergence
  (0 at Bern, 0.95° at Geneva). Breaking this rule shows as things kilometres off, or a degree turned, or a
  remote that is right only until one side shifts: test with `--originstress 20 --originyaw 2` on the clients.
- **One position**: three ECEF doubles (`GlobalPos.ToWire` / `FromWire`, ~1 µs each way). RPCs take
  `double x, double y, double z`; dictionaries use `GlobalPos.Write(d, key)` (`_x/_y/_z`) / `GlobalPos.Read`.
  A ground point with no height (a teleport, a spawn point, a pin) goes at altitude 0.
- **A direction or velocity**: in the local east-up-south at the object (x east, y up, z south):
  `origin.EnuOf(at, v)` / `WorldOfEnu(at, enu)`. **A heading**: from true north at the object, radians in
  `Rotation.Y`'s sense: `origin.TrueYaw(at, yaw)` / `WorldYaw(at, t)`. **A rotation**: `EnuTurn` / `WorldTurn`.
  `EnuAt(at)` (an `EnuAxes`) does all of them at once (~3 µs); `EnuNear` keeps it for what is published every frame.
- **Records and LV95 maths** keep directions and headings in `GlobalPos`'s offset axes (the grid's, which a flat
  frame's axes are): `g.EnuFromOffset` / `OffsetFromEnu`, `g.TrueYaw` / `OffsetYaw` convert at the edge without a
  frame; `frame.WorldYawOf(g, y)`, `WorldOfOffset`, `WorldTurnOf` and their inverses turn them into a world, free in
  a flat frame. The server's frames are always flat, so there a world yaw is a record's. A state that is saved too
  (`VehicleState`, `DropState`, `RadioState` in the object containers) saves its wire form, `ToDict`; `FromDict` also
  reads a container record saved before A2b (the keys tell). Saves: `core/saves-in-earth-positions`.
- **A list of points**: an ECEF origin near them and float offsets in the ENU there
  (`OriginFrame.AnchorAt(origin)`, the same axes on every peer): the sender maps with `frame.ToAnchor(origin)`, the
  receiver with `origin.SinceAnchor(ecef)`; between two flat frames the pair is their exact translation. Race roads
  and gates (`RaceManager.Setup`, `NpcArrive`), bird snapshots (per bird the yaw turned by the shift's `Yaw`), trial
  ghosts (`GhostTrack` `GSH2`; a `GSH1` the server stored is rewritten as `GSH2` when read, never sent).
- **Synchronized bodies**: `FootPlayer` replicates `NetX/NetY/NetZ` (`NetGlobal`, read whole when `NetZ` arrives),
  `NetVel` (ENU) and `NetYaw` (true north). Read `GlobalYaw` / `GlobalVelocity` for LV95 maths and records,
  `WorldYaw` / `WorldVelocity` for this peer's world; `SetNetState` publishes from a record. Copies interpolate in
  GlobalPos's axes (`RemoteInterpolator`) and turn the heading into their frame on the way out. Vehicles, radios and
  dropped items carry a `Net/NetPlace` child: `NetPlace.Properties` (ECEF) then `NetPlace.Turn` (the body's rotation
  in ENU) and, for a vehicle, `NetPlace.Velocity`, in place of `.:position`, `.:rotation`, `.:velocity`; a flyer's or
  a boat's attitude goes as `VehicleBody.NetTilt`. The authority publishes; every other peer is moved and turned when
  they arrive. Spawn data (`PlayerReplication.NpcData`, the state dictionaries) is ECEF and true north too.
- **The server relays what it received unchanged** where it does not need it, and measures with `GlobalPos`
  (`FootPlayer.Global`, `VehicleBody.Global`: the last published value, exact however far the player is from the
  server's own origin). Its own world floats are only for metre-scale checks between nearby bodies, and a body's
  floats are in its server region's frame (`net/server-regions`): across bodies, read `origin.ToWorld(body.Global)`
  or `body.WhereIs(other)`, never two `GlobalPosition`s.
- What is not a position stays as it is: tile ids (`TileId` E/N kilometres, until G2 D), tile-local poses (a
  crane's slew), zone offsets in metres from an area centre.
- A new networked feature: ECEF, ENU and true north in its payload from the start; a new synchronized body gets a
  `NetPlace`. Any change to what goes on the wire bumps `Handshake.Protocol`. Unit tests: `WireFrameTests`.
