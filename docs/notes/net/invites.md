# Invites: one system for "someone asks you to do something together" (#840)

- **Where**: `src/Net/Invites/`. `InviteBook` and `PartyBook` are plain C# (unit-tested,
  `InviteBookTests`); `InviteService` is the node at `World/Invites` and `Parties` at `World/Parties`,
  on the server and every client (RPCs route by path). Both are made in `ServerWorld` right after the
  chat (before races, fights and the Battle Royale, which register on them) and in
  `ClientWorld.StartNetworking`; `Swarm` builds the client copies too.
- **An invite**: kind, sender (`From`, 0 = the console), recipient (`To`, a peer or `Invite.Everyone`),
  title and detail lines, `Key` (the kind's handle: a race id, a lobby's seed), expiry (clamped
  10..300 s). One live invite per (kind, sender, recipient, key): sending again replaces it. 8 open per
  sender. An invite to everyone is answered per peer (`Answered`); a direct one is gone once answered.
- **A kind** (`InviteKind`, registered on the server): `Id`, `Label`, `Seconds`, `Accept(peer, invite)`
  (runs on the server, returns the accepter's reply, does the thing), optional `Declined`, `Lapsed`;
  `Mutual` (sending one back answers it: two fight challenges crossing); `TellAccepted` (the generic
  "X accepts your invite" line to the sender, off when the kind says something itself).
- **Server API**: `Send(kind, from, to, title, detail, out sent, key, seconds)` (returns the sender's
  reply), `Dismiss(kind, key, peer)` (that peer joined by other means: `/br join` while invited),
  `Withdraw(kind, key)` (the lobby closed, the race started), `WithdrawInvolving(kind, peer)` (busy now),
  `Find`/`FindAll`, `Answer(peer, invite, accept)`, `Command` (`/invites /accept /decline`), `SendTo`
  (a joining peer gets the invites to everyone), `PeerLeft`. Only peers past the handshake are asked
  (`ChatManager.OnlinePeers`).
- **Client**: `InviteService.Client` keeps `Incoming` and `Sent` (`InviteView`: id, kind, peer and name,
  title, detail, `Until` on `GameClock`), raises `Changed` and `Arrived`; `Accept(id)`, `Decline(id)`,
  `Withdraw(id)` go to the server by RPC. `From(peer, kind)` is what the fight prompt reads.
  `Resolved(kind, name, outcome)` (accepted, declined, lapsed) tells the sender of a direct invite how it ended
  (protocol 55, #1253): the phone's Contacts result line.
- **Bots** are never offered one (a negative id in `RpcId` means "every peer but that one"); they join
  activities through each activity's own server method.

## The kinds

| Kind | Sent by | To | Accept does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `br` | a lobby opening (`BrManager.Open`, key = seed) | everyone | `BrManager.Join` |
| `br` | `/br invite <player>` (an entrant or an admin) | one player | `BrManager.Join` |
| `tp` | `/tpa <player>` (anyone, 20 s cooldown) | that player | the sender goes to the accepter (`ForceTeleport`) |
| `tphere` | `/tphere <player>` | that player | the accepter goes to the sender |
| `party` | `/party invite <player>` (the leader, or anyone with no party) | that player | `PartyBook.Join` |
| `fight` | E at a player, `/fight <player>` (mutual) | that player | `FightManager.Begin` after the range and on-foot checks |
| `aura` | `/aura <player>`, Activities (mutual, #907) | that player | `AuraManager.Begin` after the range, on-foot and not-in-a-fight checks |
| `race` | `/race duel <player>`, once the course is built (key = race id) | that player | `RaceManager.Join`; a decline ends the duel |
| `race` | `/race start`, once open | everyone | `RaceManager.Join` |

- Teleport requests are refused, on sending and again on accepting, while either side is in a running
  Battle Royale or a race (`ChatManager.TeleportRefusal`). Admin `/tp` and `/bring` are unchanged.
- **Parties**: a leader and up to 8 for the session; the leader leaving hands over, one member left
  disbands. Members get `Parties.State` (peers and names). A Battle Royale entrant takes the party's
  `TeamName` (`party<id>`) as their `/br team` name when joining; `/br team` still overrides it.

## Answering, on each device

- Toasts (`Ui/InviteToasts`, right edge, a third down, 3 at most, a timer bar, `InviteToasts.Chime`).
- Keyboard: a tap of **F2** accepts the newest, **Shift+F2** declines it (`Ui/QuickMenu`, `ui/quick-menu`).
- Pad: the Start menu's first entry is `Accept: <title>` (Start, A); the quick menu's Invites wedge.
- VR: the wrist menu's Invites entry; Menu tap gives the Start menu as on a pad.
- Everywhere: the Activities screen's Invites tab (`ui/activities`), `/accept [player|kind|#id]`,
  `/decline`, `/invites`.
