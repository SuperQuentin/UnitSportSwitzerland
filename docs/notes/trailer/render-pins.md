# Render pins: what makes a render the same twice (#924)

Phase 1 of `docs/plans/scene-format.md` (option D: films stay C#; takes later). Every input of a
trailer-director render is either pinned or recorded, and `tools/trailer-drift.py` measures what
still drifts. The measurement and the recommendation for takes: the plan's "Measurement" section.

## The render profile (`tools/trailer.sh`)

- **A fresh `user://` every run**: `test_output/trailer/<film>/profile` (macOS `HOME=`, Linux
  `XDG_DATA_HOME=`, Windows `APPDATA=`, as `tools/lib/twoclient.sh`): none of the player's
  settings, placed objects, structures, chunk cache, `occasions.json` or world clock. Only the
  compiled shader caches (`shader_cache`, `vulkan`) are carried from run to run
  (`profile_caches`): they change no pixel.
- **The settings are the defaults plus `RENDER`** (default `--rings 6 --detail high --horizon 150
  --fog off --vsync off --render-scale 1 --aa fxaa`, what the player's file had when the films were
  first made; `--render-scale` and `--aa` were added for it). The film's own `/style`, `/seastate`,
  traffic and occasions per shot on top, as before.
- **`PINS=0`**: a run as before #924 (the player's `user://`, the real date, no seeds pinned:
  `--trailer-unpinned`). A baseline only, never for a film to keep.
- `GODOT_ARGS` passes engine arguments (`--screen 1`).

## The date (`Core.Calendar`)

The one reader of today's date for the occasions, the farm month, the birds, fishing and shops
(the clock-discipline guard's `calendar:` entry). `--date YYYY-MM-DD` pins it; a trailer run always
runs on its film's date, `TrailerDirector.FilmDate` (mid-July 2026 for every film so far: no
occasion runs then), so a shot with no `Shot.Occasion` gets that day's, never the render day's
(an October render put Halloween on the showcase). A film set on another day adds its line there.
Church bells and a carol on the hour still read the wall clock: they are audio, not in the picture.

## Seeds (`Core.RunSeeds`)

The director pins a scope per shot, `<film>/<shot>` as it starts staging, then `<film>/<shot>/go` as
its actors are let go. Every stream is seeded from (scope, its own name), FNV-1a (never
`string.GetHashCode`, which .NET randomises per process). `RunSeeds.Reseeded` tells the systems that
live across shots to start again, so a shot never depends on the shots filmed before it nor on how
long its ground took to stream in:

| What | How |
|---|---|
| Birds (`BirdLife`) | at the go every bird goes, the stream is the shot's, a dozen spawn at once |
| Traffic | at the go every car and train goes (the lane graphs stay), the shot's stream, the roads filled at once |
| Godot's own generator (`GD.Randf`: the occasions' creatures, droppings) | `GD.Seed` at each pin |
| Particles | every `Gpu/CpuParticles3D` entering the tree while pinned: `UseFixedSeed`, seeded from its parent's and its own name |
| Staged herds (`Herds.Stage`) | the shot's seed, not the run's staging count; their walk rewound to the go (`Herds.Rewind`) |
| Tractor beam | the hold's bob from zero at each pin (it was a run-long clock) |
| A crash's stay-or-go coin | from the scope and the body's name |
| Vehicle ids (`VehicleState.NewOid`) | the scope's own sequence |
| GPX cinema seed | FNV of the track name (was `GetHashCode`) |
| A new camera track's seed (studio) | the scope's when pinned |

Normal play is unchanged: nothing is pinned unless a film or a probe calls `RunSeeds.Begin`.

## Timing

- **The go on a frame**: at 30 fps there are two physics steps a frame, and placing ran on both, so
  whichever found the actors ready shifted them one step against the camera (a saucer 36 cm, the
  Tremola car 28 cm). Pinned, the director places once a frame.
- **`GameClock.Fixed` on macOS** was always false (the hosted CLR sees no argv), so every
  `--fixed-fps` run kept `ClockSync` on the wall clock (a staged herd walked 2.04 s of its clock for
  3.03 s of film). It reads the argv from the kernel now (`general/fast-checks`).
- `--shot` settles on the tiles too (`core/commands`).

## What a render records

- **Terrain fingerprint**: each manifest tile's content hash (`tools/commands`, `--manifest-hash`);
  a shot's fingerprint is `TileFingerprint.OfSet` over the tiles `FilmCoverage.Tiles` gives for its
  cameras and actors, the run's over all its shots. A tile with no hash counts as such
  (`unhashed`): a fixture or a manifest from before #924.
- **`version.txt`** (render): `godot binary`, `profile`, and the director's `[trailer] pin:` lines:
  `commit` (`+dirty` with changes under `src`/`tools`, from `--trailer-commit`), `godot`, `gpu`
  (adapter, vendor, renderer, API), `os`, `terrain`, `terrain shared`, `date`, `occasions`,
  `styles`, `song` (sha256), `settings`, `user`, `seeds`. Stills print the same into `stills.log`.
- **`shotNN.pins.json`** beside each shot's MP4 (or stills, or track dump): the same per shot, with
  its own terrain fingerprint, the occasions running as it rolls, hour, sea, frames, size and the
  full settings.

## Measuring drift

```
TRACKS=/tmp/a FILM=fondue tools/trailer.sh stills 3,4,22
TRACKS=/tmp/b FILM=fondue tools/trailer.sh stills 3,4,22
tools/trailer-drift.py /tmp/a /tmp/b [--markdown] [--shots 22] [--objects actor,cow]
```

`--trailer-tracks DIR` writes `shotNN.tracks.csv`: from the go, every frame (pre-roll negative), the
camera, each actor (pose kind or ragdoll/swim/fly, ride, beam), its ragdoll's pelvis, every prop and
craft, the vehicles within 600 m, each staged cow and sighting saucer, LV95 to the millimetre. The
script gives per object the first frame that differs, the largest and mean position difference, the
largest yaw difference and a verdict: identical, sub-cm, small (< 5 cm), visible, diverged (> 1 m).

## What still drifts

- **Ground vehicles under a controller** (autopilot, follow): a few centimetres to over a metre,
  from a fraction of a second after the go. Their contacts with the terrain's and the roads'
  collision differ from run to run: the tiles stream in on threads, so the collision bodies reach
  Jolt in another order and through another history of origin shifts. Building one tile at a time
  (`--builds 1` in `RENDER`) does not remove it. Measured on one Mac (the plan's "Measurement"):
  the Tremola autopilot 8-20 cm, the six-car pack 13 cm-2 m, the field machines identical, 13 cm or
  1.6 m. Scripted flight, walkers, standing actors, craft, beams, herds and a crash's ragdoll are
  identical. Phase 2's takes are for those vehicles.
- Not pinned, not in the picture: impact and swim sounds (`Random.Shared`, a static stream).
- Recorded, not pinned: the Godot build, the GPU and driver, the song file (downloaded from
  incompetech: a different file shows as another checksum).
- The fight animation's phase and a dance on the beat read the wall clock (presentation in the
  clock-discipline table): a pose, not a position.
