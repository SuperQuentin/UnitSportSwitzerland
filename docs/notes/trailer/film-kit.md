## Craft (UFO props, `CraftProp`)

`new Craft(spot, heading)`: #861's saucer rig standing on its legs (H 0) or hovering (H above the
ground), its front (where the ramp goes down) to the compass heading. `Switches` over time (default:
lamps lit, beam off, ramp shut, legs down under 7 m). `Craft.Crashed(spot, heading)`: legs up, belly
0.5 m into the ground, nose down 12°, rolled 8°, smoking, lamps flickering (or set `Nose`, `Bank`,
`Sink`, `Smoke` yourself). `Mothership = true`: the 121 m mothership, its lamps turning, its beam drawn
only (the beam's 45 m cone reaches no ground from up there). A saucer craft's beam is a real one
(`TractorBeams.Scripted`): what is under it rises, as under an actor's.

## Gotchas

- **Swim deep enough**: water under ~1.5 m and the swimmer stands and wades (the game ends a swim on
  the floor). Give it 2 s of pre-roll to take its stroke; a camera on it (`Pt.On`) at 2.2 m stays over the water.
- **Smoke takes its time**: particles start when the prop is made; 3 s of pre-roll for a crash's column.
- A beam lifts at 3.2 m/s and holds things 4.5 m under the hatch: a saucer hovering 16 m up takes
  ~4 s to bring a cow up. Switch the beam on accordingly.
- The craft's ramp is drawn without collision: only an actor with `DownRamp` gets a solid one.
- An `ItemCue` with no actor at that index is skipped with a warning.
- The map screen needs the country map's data (it shows what is downloaded): fine on this machine.
