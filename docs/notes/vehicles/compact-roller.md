# The compact tandem roller (#614, part of the building-sites epic #605)

`RideKind.CompactRoller = 197`, `Player/CompactRoller`. It is a 2.6 t tandem of the common class
(Hamm HD 12, Bomag BW 120): two 1.2 m steel drums 0.72 m across, a 1.7 m wheelbase, 10 km/h. Its
numbers are in `Avatar/CompactRollerLayout` (tier 0).

## Driving

- **It steers by bending in the middle**, as the wheel loader does. The yaw is the rear frame's.
  The kinematics now live in `Avatar/FrameSteering` (`v·sin φ / (Lf + Lr·cos φ)`), shared with
  `WheelLoaderLayout`, whose numbers are unchanged (pinned in `CompactRollerLayoutTests`). On full
  lock (±0.5 rad) it turns on a 3.3 m circle, measured and computed alike.
- **A hydrostatic drive**: the throttle and the brake take it forward and back to 10 km/h. Let go
  and it stops within 1.5 m.

## The vibration

- **The work toggle** (`dig_mode`: C / pad B, the excavator's and the loader's) sets the drums
  vibrating, or stops them. In VR it is a red button on the console right of the wheel
  (`XrCabControls` "roller", `Kind.Poke`). No new binding.
- `Vibration` ramps 0 to 1 over `VibrationRamp` (0.6 s), and back.
- **The hum**: `SiteSfx.RollerDrum` synthesises a 55 Hz thump with harmonics, two weights beating
  slowly, and the frame rattling at each stroke. `CompactRoller.DrumLoop` bakes it into a 2 s seamless
  loop (`Dsp.Loop`), which the sound player lists by itself. Each rig has an `AudioStreamPlayer3D`
  "Drums" whose volume follows the vibration, on every peer.
- **The tremble**: `Rideable.CameraShake` (new; 0 for every other ride) is
  `Vibration × ShakeAmplitude` (0.0035 rad). `FootPlayer.ShakeRideCamera` applies it in all three
  ride cameras: cockpit, first person and chase. It uses the on-foot shake's two incommensurate
  wobbles a side, scaled by the camera-shake setting (`GameSettings.ScreenShake`, which nothing read
  before). **Never in VR.** `FootPlayer.RideShake` is what a check reads.
- The engine revs up with the vibration (idle 1000, 2800 rpm), and the gear shows "V".
