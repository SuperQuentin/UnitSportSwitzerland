# Mobile airstairs (#417, epic #423)

- **What**: `Player/Airstairs.cs`, RideKind **126** (a mount of its own, not a `HeavyCatalog` entry:
  it needs none of the heavy driveline, only a slow truck and a platform). Drawn by
  `Avatar/AirstairsMeshBuilder.cs` (`AirstairsLayout`: one set of numbers for the mesh, the hull and
  the deck; `AirstairsRig` rebuilds the stairs' mesh in place only when the platform moved 2 cm).
  A low forward-control cab, the flight from the ground behind the chassis up to a platform over
  the cab (2.4-5.6 m), a canopy, a bridge plate in front. Admin picker tab **Airport**.
- **Driving**: 25 km/h forward, 8 reverse, 2.2 m/s² (it climbs a 10% apron at 0.8 throttle; 1.3 did not), brake then reverse (as a car's automatic), bicycle-model
  steering. On the move the platform rides at 2.5 m. Nothing new is replicated: the platform's
  height travels in `Anim.X` (`WritePose`), parked in `VehicleState.Flags` (cm).
- **The deck** (`AirstairsMeshBuilder.Deck(h)`, `walk-aboard`): the flight is one ramp from 4 cm under
  the ground to the platform's back edge, flush (the walk has no step-up), the platform, side rails
  (the flight's tilted with it), and the **bridge plate**: a ramp from the platform's front edge down
  7 cm to the lip. Never "aboard" (its aboard box is under the ground): nobody is carried by stairs,
  the walker just collides with them (decks are in the on-foot mask). `Decks` is rebuilt when the
  height moves 5 mm, and `FootPlayer.Deck` rebuilds that set's bodies when its deck changed.
- **Where to dock, from the aircraft's deck alone** (`Vehicles/AirstairsDock.cs`): a door's sill is
  its `DeckPart.DoorStep` box (`A320Deck`: out through the skin). Its top, its outer edge and the way
  out of the cabin are all that is used, so any walkable `Flyer` docks the same way (AN-124 #419,
  freighter #420, kneeling included: the sill is read where it is drawn now). Docked: the truck
  faces the door, the lip **20 cm inside the sill's edge**, the platform **4 cm over the sill**: the
  plate's slope then crosses the sill's top just inside its edge, so there is no lip either way (a
  platform 1 cm under the sill would stop the walk dead, a plate ending on the sill's edge left a
  2 cm step). The hull boxes stop 0.75 m short of the lip: docked, the platform's front is inside
  the aircraft's parked box.
- **Docking while driving** (`FootPlayer.Airstairs.cs`): let go (no pedal, no steer, under 1.5 m/s)
  with the lip within 3.5 m of a door's docked spot, facing within 40° of square to it and the sill
  in the platform's range: it is held, slid (0.9 m/s) and turned (0.5 rad/s) into place, the
  platform rises at 0.45 m/s; "Airstairs docked at door N". Driving off undocks.
- **Parked** (`VehicleBody.Airstairs.cs`): every peer looks for the sill at its lip twice a second
  and eases the platform to it (an aircraft kneeling or settling moves it), so docked parked stays
  docked with nothing replicated. The height is kept in its state for whoever takes it.
- **Taxiing away** (`PushAirstairs`, the stairs' authority, asleep or not): a walkable aircraft moving
  over 0.3 m/s with the stairs' foot, middle or lip inside its footprint (parked box and wing box,
  +1.2 m) shoves them sideways away from its centreline at max(2.5, 2 × its speed) m/s until clear
  of its span; their position is published at 20 Hz meanwhile. A client lets them settle by physics
  after; the dedicated server keeps them where they are (it has no ground). The aircraft's driven
  hull (fuselage only) never touches docked stairs, so it is not held.
- **API for stands (#422)**: `AirstairsDock.DockedTo(planeState, door)` gives the `VehicleState` of
  stairs docked at that door of a parked aircraft (level, on its gear); `AirstairsDock.PlaceAt(vehicles,
  planeState, name, doors...)` places them through `VehicleManager.Place` (server or offline), by
  default two at an A320's L1 and L2 (`A320Doors` = 0, 2).
- **Getting in**: by the cab door, E from outside always (`Rideable.DrivenFromInside` false), even
