# Vehicles carried inside vehicles: holds, tie-down, relative sync (#418)

- **A carrier opts in by filling `VehicleDeck.CargoBays`** (`DeckBuilder.CargoBay(authoredCentre, size)`,
  size = across, height, length; the floor is the box's bottom) in a deck of its `Rideable.Decks`.
  Nothing else: the hold's floor, walls, tailgate and ramp are ordinary `DeckBox`es of the same deck
  (`walk-aboard`). A ramp that only exists while open is a `DeckPart.DoorStep` of door *k*, the
  tailgate a `DeckPart.DoorShut` of door *k*; the carrier's door bits (`BusDoors`: `Airliner.DoorsOpen`,
  a parked one's flags) switch them, so a vehicle drives out only through an open door or ramp.
  Give the hold an `Aboard` box too if people should walk in it.
- **What fits** (`Vehicles/CargoFit`, tier 0 `CargoFitTests`): the ride's `ParkedBox` size (hull bounds,
  measured from the model) plus 10 cm on every side within the bay, nose or tail first. A Citaro
  (2.65 × 3.26 × 12.6 m measured) fits an AN-124's hold, not a Transall-sized freighter's.
  In a bay = the vehicle's ground point over the bay's floor plan, at most 0.5 m under the floor.
- **Driving** (`Player/FootPlayer.Hold.cs`): a ground vehicle (`IsVehicle`, not a `Flyer` or `Boat`,
  not an NPC) builds the decks of carriers near it (`ScanDecks(holdsOnly)`, the walker's `DeckSet`s,
  their hulls excepted). Each frame (`CarryInHold`, before anything is drawn from it): **carried
  first, then looked at** (a carrier at 60 m/s has left last frame's spot behind; tested the other way
  round a teleport or a fast carrier dropped the car out of the hold), then in a bay it fits: `DeckOn`
  = the carrier's key, `DeckSection`, and the section's motion since the last frame carries it, as a
  walker is carried; its own ride physics runs relative to the floor. `DeckPos`/`DeckYaw` publish the
  spot; every other peer draws it from its copy of the carrier (`PlaceOnDeck`, unchanged). The body is
  drawn tilted with the floor (`_holdTilt` in `PoseRideVisual`, carried to others in `BodyPose`).
- **Tie-down**: stopped (< 0.5 m/s) in a hold with the handbrake held 0.3 s (a two-wheeler: the brake)
  it is tied (`TiedDown`): no ride physics, put exactly at its spot in the carrier's frame every frame.
  The throttle (or, not a bike, the brake pedal: at a standstill that is reverse) lets it go.
- **Parked in a hold**: getting out captures `VehicleState.Carrier` (key), `CarrierSection`,
  `CarrierPos`, `CarrierYaw` (velocity zero). `VehicleBody.Hold.cs`: no physics, `ProcessPriority` 5;
  every peer sets its transform from its own copy of the carrier each frame (`FollowCarrier`), the
  authority publishes the place for the server. Carrier and cargo get mutual collision exceptions.
  A carrier that changes hands (its pilot got out: a parked one, a new key) is found again by where
  the vehicle stands (`FootPlayer.CarrierAt`, 4 Hz while lost). Getting back in (`ResumeHold` in
  `EnterVehicle`) puts it at its spot in the carrier now, still tied down.
- **Process order**: carrier 0, carried vehicle 8 (driven) / 5 (parked), people aboard either 10
  (`DeckPriority`), so a walker in a bus in a hold is carried after the bus moved.
- **Carrier side**: `WatchGuests` excepts players in a bay (grown 1.5 m) from the hull; a flying carrier
  calls `IgnoreGuests` too (`HasHolds`). **Trap** (`FootPlayer.Overlap.cs`): the overlap easing apart
  took back that exception once "clear" and the A320 flew into the car in its hold; a carrier and what
  rides in or on it (`DeckOn`) are never eased apart.

- **Boats on trailers** (#463): a boat trailer's cradle is a hold for one kind of boat (`CargoBay.Only`,
  `Takes`) on a hold-only deck (`VehicleDeck.CargoOnly`, `DeckBuilder.BuildCargo`). The boat is never
  driven in: it is parked there (claimed, then parked with `Carrier`), and parked out again into the
  water (`player/trucks-buses`). Strapped (`VehicleManager.OnTrailer`) it is drawn level and is not
  enterable. A carrier's ground rays skip its cargo (`FootPlayer.Cargo`, `VehicleBody.Cargo`, and
  `GroundQuery` past anything `InHold`). A carrier key may be `"?"`: found where it stands (a trailer
  the server has not named yet).
- **The AN-124** (#419) is drive-through: the bay runs hinge to hinge, vehicles drive up the nose ramp and

  knelt; a ramp whose slope depends on a state is two `DoorStep`s on derived door bits
  (`An124Deck.DeckDoors`). A carrier's driven hull must leave the bay's ends open (a belly slab, not the
  fuselage), or a long vehicle's level hull box meets it on the ramp before it is excepted (1.5 m).
- **Limits**: a vehicle driving out of a *moving* carrier keeps only its own speed (no carrier
  velocity added); trucks with trailers and articulated buses are carried by the cab's section only;
  carried rides are ground vehicles (no planes, boats in holds); the hold platform of the test carrier
  is not drawn.
