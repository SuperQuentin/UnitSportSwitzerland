# The cars in an underground car park are vehicles (#558, PR 3 of 3)

The bay cars of a block's basement car park (the `FurnitureType.Car` pieces in a `RoomType.CarPark`
room) are real parked vehicles: aim at one, it wakes, get in, drive it up the ramp and out through
the garage door. It is the pattern of the hall forklift (`vehicles/forklift`, #630) with a car park's
numbers: nothing is sent, the server derives the slot from its own plan.

- **Pure half: `HallCarRule`** (`src/Interiors/HallCarRule.cs`, tier 0: `HallCarTests`). The owner
  name `<building>_c` (a hall forklift's is `<building>_h`, a yard's `E_N_Index`, a street car park's
  `E_N`, so the four never meet; node `veh_slot_<building>_c_<piece>`), `Pick` (which ordinary road
  car stands in a bay: a stable hash of the building and the furniture index into
  `DormantVehicles.ParkedKinds`, the street car parks' own list, so a bay never changes car when
  another is added) and `NoseYaw` (the one place the yaw convention is written down: a vehicle's nose
  is -Z in its node frame, the piece's front is +Z, so the node is turned half a turn past the piece).
- **`HallCars`** (Godot): `IsBayCar(layout, piece)` (a car on the floor of a car park room: not the
  cars of a showroom or a service bay, which stay props), `LocalFrame`, `SlotOf(layout, index, origin)`
  (LV95 and altitude 3 km down under the building, ordinal = furniture index, never sent).
  `InteriorMeshBuilder.IsCarvedOut(layout, piece)` leaves a bay car out of the merged mesh and its
  collision, as a loose pallet and a hall forklift are.
- **`ParkedCars`** (one node per interior, `HallCars` under the `InteriorNode`): one `MultiMesh` per
  look (kind, shared with the street car parks' `DormantLooks`, built once per session), one shared box
  shape per look and one `DormantBody` per bay with its slot. A car park of 40 bays is a dozen draw
  calls and 40 bodies, built once with the interior; nothing runs per frame. Aimed at, a body wakes
  its slot through `VehicleReach` -> `DormantVehicles.Wake`, offline and online alike.
- **Server wake**: `ServerWake` sends an owner ending `_c` to `ServerWakeHall(car: true)` (shared
  with the forklifts): the asker must be inside that very building (`InteriorManager.SpaceOf`), the
  slot comes from `GetOrCreate(building)` and `HallCars.SlotOf`, and a client names a furniture index
  and nothing more.
- **The prop never shows under the real car**: `DormantVehicles.OnVehicleAdded` (the node
  `veh_slot_..._c_N` entering the vehicles) calls `ParkedCars.Woke`, which zeroes the instance and
  disables the shapes on **every** peer: the join snapshot, a broadcast spawn and a late joiner alike,
  because it keys on the node existing, not on a message. `ParkedCars._EnterTree` also hides a car
  whose vehicle exists (or whose slot this peer knows is awake), so an interior built after the wake
  never draws it.
- **A woken car that is driven away: the bay refills (decision, #558 risk 2).** The slot is awake
  while its vehicle exists; getting in removes the vehicle node (the player is the car now), which
  starts `_gone`; after `DormantVehicles.RespawnSeconds` (180 s, `--dormant-respawn` for a check) the
  server, if the bay is clear of vehicles and nobody is within 40 m of it (`Clear`), puts the slot to
  sleep: the `Slept` RPC redraws the car on every peer (`ParkedCars.Slept`), and the owner is dropped
  from `_awake`. The server keeps the woken slots of `_hallCars` itself, because a bay car is no tile's
  slot (`Find` looks in `_slots`). Until then the bay is empty on every peer that saw the car taken. A
  car left standing in the bay keeps it (its node exists). Known limit, the same as a street car
  park's: a peer that joins (or builds the interior) while the bay is empty but not yet restocked
  never saw the car go, draws the sleeper, and waking it makes a second car (`ServerWakeHall` lets a
  wake through when no vehicle node of that name exists); it is a bay refilled early for that peer.
- **Driving out (#558 risk 1).** A woken car is never an empty vehicle driven by the server: the player
  who gets in takes it over (`EnterVehicle`), and from then on it is the player's body crossing the
  garage door's `DoorLink` exactly as the ramp check's car does. `tools/hallcarnetcheck.sh` drives the
  woken car from the ramp's foot up the ramp and out of the door.
- **Loot** is unchanged: a bay car is still a `FurnitureType.Car` container by position, so a woken or
  gone car leaves a searchable spot. Cars of a car park are 30 % of the loot's car rolls; not touched.
- **Checks.** Tier 0: `HallCarTests` (naming, the hash, the yaw). Quick: `--flatcheck` (`BayCars`: every
  bay car has a slot named for its building, the same every time, the nose through LV95 and back, the
  whole hull of the car it wakes as inside the room and its 2.5 x 5 m bay). Net:
  `tools/hallcarnetcheck.sh` on `fixture:garage` (`HallCarProbe`, `--hallcarcheck A|B`, roles by chat
  like `LiftSyncProbe`): A walks in, aims at a bay car, gets in, drives it up the ramp and out, leaves it
  in the street; B, at the far end of the car park, sees every sleeper drawn, then the sleeper go and the
  vehicle stand in its bay, the car climb the ramp and leave, and the bay refill after the respawn wait.
  Windowed (`WINDOWED=1`) both save `test_output/hallcar_*.png`.
