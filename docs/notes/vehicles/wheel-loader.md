# The wheel loader (#612, part of the building-sites epic #605)

`RideKind.WheelLoader = 195`, `Player/WheelLoader`, built on the excavator's pattern (#611): extra
state of three angles, worked in a mode, replicated in the pose and packed in the flags.

## It steers by bending

The front frame — the lift arm, the bucket and the front wheels — swings about a hinge in the
middle; the rear frame with the cab and the engine follows. The vehicle's yaw is the **rear
frame's** (the cab is on it, so the camera and the cockpit need nothing special), and the rear axle
runs along it, so it travels with `Slip` 0 (π reversing), like any car.

- `WheelLoaderLayout.YawRate(v, φ) = v·sin φ / (Lf + Lr·cos φ)`: frame-steering kinematics with
  the hinge between the axles; the articulation eases toward the stick (±40°, 0.8 rad/s).
- `TurnRadius(40°)` is 4.5 m, and `--loadercheck` drives a quarter circle on full lock and measures
  **4.5 m**, turning the right way. A sign error would turn it the other way; the check asserts which.
- The drawn front frame is `WheelLoaderFront` ("Front"), turned about Y by the articulation, its
  wheels spun by its own node; the rear wheels are the heavy rig's.

## Work mode

The excavator's toggle: `dig_mode` (C / pad B / a VR console button). In it the right stick and the
arrows are the arm's — the same actions as the excavator's right stick: `arm_boom_up/down` lift the
arm (back is up), `arm_bucket_curl/dump` roll the bucket back or tip it — and **it still drives**: a
loader fills its bucket by driving into the heap, so only the right stick changes meaning. The
camera keeps the mouse (`ApplyStickLook` skips while working). VR: two levers right of the seat
(`XrCabControls` "loader", `Kind.Hold`), work mode on the console.

The lift runs −0.62..0.95 rad (the bucket's pin from the ground to **4.4 m**, over a lorry's side),
the bucket −0.95 (dumped)..0.75 (rolled back); rest is carried low and rolled back. Each stops where
it is let go.

## Replication and parking

`WritePose = (Articulation, Lift, Tilt, signed speed)`; a copy keeps its own `WheelLoader`
(`VehicleOf`). Parked: lift, tilt and articulation, eight bits each, in `VehicleState.Flags`.
`Handshake.Protocol` 21 (the excavator took 20).
