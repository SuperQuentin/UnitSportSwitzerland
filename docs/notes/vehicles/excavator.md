# The excavator (#611, part of the building-sites epic #605)

`RideKind.Excavator = 194`, `Player/Excavator`, shaped on the forklift (#583): a slow machine whose
extra state is a few numbers, eased by nothing, replicated in the pose and packed in the parked
flags. It is the game's **first crawler**: nothing before it had tracks or skid steering.

## The heading is the cab's

The upper structure slews on the undercarriage, and the cab, the driver, the cockpit and the camera
all slew with it. So **the vehicle's yaw (`RideMotion.Yaw`) is the cab's** and the tracks keep a
heading of their own (`TrackYaw`); `Slew` is the difference. It travels **along its tracks**, not its
nose, and that needed no new physics: `RideMotion.Slip` is the angle between where a ride points and
where it goes (a drifting car's), and `FootPlayer.RidePhysics` already moves the body along
`heading.Rotated(Slip)`. So `Slip = −Slew` (+π reversing). The drawn undercarriage (`ExcavatorArm`
"Under") turns back by the slew, so it stays where the tracks are while the house turns.
`--excavatorcheck` drives it slewed a quarter turn and asserts it moves across its cab
(cos 0.00); a mirrored sign would drive it along the cab.

## Driving and digging

- **Drive mode**: the tracks. Throttle forward, brake back, steer **turns on the spot** (the tracks
  counter-rotate, `ExcavatorLayout.Tracks`): 1.6 m/s, 0.55 rad/s. Its grousers are rows of bars
  slid along each run by its track's speed (modulo their pitch), so the tracks visibly run.
- **Dig mode** (`dig_mode`: **C / pad B**, the clutch's and the crouch's, which mean nothing in a
  crawler; VR: a button on the left console): the tracks hold and the two sticks work the arm in the
  **ISO pattern** of a real one. Left stick: slew (X), stick out / in (Y). Right stick: boom (Y, back
  is up), bucket (X, left curls). Keyboard: WASD are the left stick, the arrows the right. Each joint
  runs at its rate while held and **stops where it is let go** (`Excavator.Work`, after the step,
  from `FootPlayer.WorkArm`). The slew turns the vehicle's yaw, so the camera slews with the cab.
- **The right stick is the arm's while digging**: `ApplyStickLook` returns early, the mouse still
  looks round. Everywhere else it is the camera's, as before.
- **VR**: the cab's two joysticks (`XrCabControls` "excavator"), each as **two `Kind.Hold` levers**
  side by side, because a grip holds one axis today: left slews and runs the stick, right raises the
  boom and curls the bucket. `vr-action-map` has the rows; one two-axis grip per joystick is the
  target. The right stick itself stays R3's action pad.
- Decided with the user: a drive / dig **toggle** rather than extra keys always on, or a held button.

## The arm

`ExcavatorLayout` (Godot maths only, linked into the tests): a 20 t machine (Liebherr R 920 / CAT
320 class), boom 5.7 m, stick 2.9 m, bucket 1.45 m. Joints: boom −0.75..1.05 rad from the
horizontal, stick −2.55..−0.45 and bucket −2.4..0.6 from the line of the part before. `Edge` is the
cutting edge's reach: 10.7 m level, 5.5 m deep, over 6 m high (`ExcavatorLayoutTests`). The arm has
**no collision**: a hull out at the bucket would stop it digging into the very ground it is for.

Each part is a pivot node rotated about its own X (`ExcavatorArm`), never rebuilt; a pose that has
not changed is not reassigned. Node space is the authored frame turned half round, so a part along
−Z rises for a positive turn.

## Replication and parking

- **Driven**: `WritePose = (Slew, Boom, Stick, Bucket)` in `Anim`; a copy is drawn from those alone
  and keeps its own `Excavator` (`VehicleOf`, as the airstairs and the forklift do). A pose of all
  zeroes is "not written yet" and keeps the rest pose.
- **Parked**: `ExcavatorLayout.Pack` puts the slew and the three joints in the 32 bits of
  `VehicleState.Flags`, eight bits each over its travel (1.4° of slew, under 0.012 rad a joint);
  zero is the rest pose. `CaptureVehicle`, `EnterVehicle` and `VehicleState.CreateRide` read it.
- `Handshake.Protocol` 20.

## The cab

The cab is in the middle of the house, not on its left as on a real one: `HeavyCabin` builds a
cockpit across the vehicle's middle, and its face is pulled back behind the dials (the forklift's
trick) so no dash hides the bucket. The driver holds a small wheel, because the heavy rig poses its
driver from the cockpit's wheel; joysticks in the hands are a later touch. Two joystick consoles
stand either side of the seat, where the VR levers are.

## The mini excavator (#614)

`RideKind.MiniExcavator = 196`. It is the same `Excavator` class built with `mini: true`.
- **Its numbers.** Everything the ride, the arm node and the check read comes from an `ExcavatorSpec`:
  `ExcavatorLayout.Spec` (the 20 t machine, its constants unchanged) or `MiniExcavatorLayout.Spec`.
  The mini is a 2.7 t class machine: 1.5 m over rubber tracks, a 2.3 m boom and a 1.25 m stick,
  about 4.6 m of reach and 2.6 m of dig depth, quicker joints, 4.5 km/h.
- **Its body** is `MiniExcavatorMeshBuilder`: a short-tail house under an open four-post canopy, the
  boom on a swing bracket in front of the operator. `ExcavatorArm.Create(spec, meshes)` poses it as
  it poses the big one.
- **The dozer blade** is on the undercarriage, so it turns back with the tracks under a slewed house.
  `Blade` is its arms' angle: 0 puts the edge on the ground, `BladeMax` 0.6 lifts it 0.33 m, and
  `BladeMin` -0.15 cuts 7 cm in. The plate is counter-rotated so it stays upright as it rises.
  `blade_raise` / `blade_lower` work **driving or digging** (a mini pushes soil as it drives):
  - keyboard: Shift / Ctrl;
  - pad: RB / LB, the gear paddles, which a crawler has no use for;
  - VR: a lever low and outboard of the left joystick, aft to raise (`XrCabControls` "miniexcavator").
- **On the wire**, the pose's four floats are all taken. The blade rides in the bucket's float:
  `ExcavatorSpec.PoseW` = bucket + 8 × one of 32 blade steps. The bucket never leaves ±4 rad, and
  the big one's W stays the bucket exactly.
- **Parked**, the flags pack slew, boom, stick, bucket and blade in 7, 8, 8, 6 and 3 bits. The big
  one stays at 8, 8, 8, 8 and none, bit for bit as before. `Handshake.Protocol` is 22.
- **On a building site**, `MachineRole.MiniExcavator` now parks as one (`DormantVehicles.SiteKind`).
- **Checks**:
  - `--excavatorcheck mini[,shots]`: the same script on the mini, plus the blade at its rate to both
    stops, drawn at the ride's angle, kept in the flags, and still working while driving. Shots go
    in `test_output/miniexcavator/`.
  - A mini round in `tools/excavatornetcheck.sh`: B draws A's arm and blade (0.358 against 0.360),
    and keeps the blade once parked.
  - `MiniExcavatorLayoutTests` (tier 0, 12) cover the reach, the blade's heights, the flags and the
    pose round trips, and the big one's packing unchanged.

## Not done

- No digging: the ground does not change (#620), and the bucket carries nothing (#615). The blade
  pushes nothing either.
- No boom swing (offset) on the mini: its arm stays on the house's centre line.
- No hydraulic whine or track squeal: the engine is the diesel profile and the throttle follows the
  hardest-pushed lever (#617 gave a reversing beeper to reuse).
