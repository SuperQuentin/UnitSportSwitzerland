# The telehandler (#614, part of the building-sites epic #605)

`RideKind.Telehandler = 198`, `Player/Telehandler`. It is a 13 m class machine (Manitou MT 1335,
JCB 535-125): a 2.9 m wheelbase, 24-inch tyres, the cab on the left, the boom along the middle from

## Steering modes

`roof_toggle` (O / D-pad left, the car's soft-top switch, which a telehandler has no use for; in VR
a dash button) cycles front → four-wheel → crab. The dash shows F, 4 or C where a gear would be.
`TelehandlerLayout.Motion` is a bicycle with both axles steered, and gives the yaw rate and the
middle's travel angle (the motion's `Slip`):

| mode | rear wheels | full-lock circle | travel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Front | straight | 4.2 m | a little inside the nose |
| FourWheel | opposite | 2.1 m (half) | along the nose |
| Crab | the same way | none | at the wheels' angle (34°), no turn |

All three were measured equal to the kinematics (`--telehandlercheck`). The front wheels steer
through the rig (`HeavyWheel.Steer` 1); the rear ones are `TelehandlerBoom`'s, posed by
`RearSteer(front, mode)`.

## The boom

- It pivots at the back of the chassis (`BoomPivot`). `Lift` runs from -0.22 (the forks on the
  ground ahead of the front wheels) to 1.15. `Extend` runs the inner section out 0 to 4.3 m. `Tilt`
  is the forks' pitch from level: the carriage is **self-levelling** (turned back by the lift), as
  a real one is. Full stretch puts the carriage 9.8 m up (`Carriage(lift, extend)`).
- **Work mode** (`dig_mode`: C / pad B, a VR dash button) keeps it driving, as on the loader:
  - lift is `arm_boom_up/down` (↑↓ / right stick Y);
  - tilt is `arm_bucket_curl/dump` (←→ / right stick X: curling tilts the forks back);
  - extend is `shift_up/down` (Shift / Ctrl, RB / LB), the forklift mast's paddles;
  - each runs while held at its rate (0.22 rad/s, 0.6 m/s, 0.5 rad/s) and stops where let go.
  The right stick is the boom's in work mode (`ApplyStickLook`).
- **VR** (`XrCabControls` "telehandler"): the boom's joystick right of the seat as two hold levers
  (aft lifts, aft tilts back), an extend rocker beside it (forward runs it out), and work mode and
  the steering mode as two dash buttons.
- The forks carry nothing yet: pallets on them are #615.
