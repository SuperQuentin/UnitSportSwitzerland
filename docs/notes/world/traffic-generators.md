# Traffic generators and destinations (#971)

- **What** (`World/TrafficGenerators`, `World/Traffic.Generators`): a `TrafficEndpoint` is a point on a road (LV95) with a
  heading into the network. A **generator** makes `PerMinute` cars there heading that way (`--traffic-rate X` scales every
  rate); a car that finds no room (none within 8 m, none coming up behind within 30 m; it starts no faster than it can brake
  for one ahead) waits in the generator's **spawn queue** (up to 40) and starts as soon as there is room, one a tick. A
  **destination** takes the cars driving out past it (against its heading): they leave the world there. **Shares** (the
  user): each a weight and a target, a destination's id (the car follows the shortest way found to it: `WayTo`, a
  Dijkstra over the lane graph as `NextRoad` lets a car go, cached per generator; `Vehicle.Planned`, `NextRoad` takes its next
  leg) or a direction along the generator's road (`AlongHeading` 0, `AgainstHeading` -1: at random from there). None: all
  along its heading. Endpoints snap to the nearest road edge within 30 m (`OnRoad`), again when the graph is rebuilt.
