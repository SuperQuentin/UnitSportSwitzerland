# The sun in the sky (#1479)

- **`World/SunDisc`** (added by `ClientWorld` beside `MoonDisc`, under the real sun only): a quad square to the
  ephemeris sun (`DayNight.Sky.Sun`) just inside the far plane, placed at `FramePreDraw` for the current camera,
  depth as the moon's (`sky_depth`): ridges, trees and buildings hide it. One direction for the sun, the moon and the
  stars (`DayNight`'s sky frame); the globe's sun (`Atmosphere.TrueSunWritten`, `Globe.Axes`) agrees to 0.002°.
- **The look** (`shaders/common/sun_look.gdshaderinc`, shared with the space sun in `atmosphere.gdshader`): a
  limb-darkened core at the true 0.27° radius (never under `MoonDisc.MinScreenShare` of the view, so it reads like
  the moon), a corona that widens and warms through more air (`warm`: 0 high, 1 on the horizon), a starburst along
  the screen's axes (0.15 on the ground, gone once the disc is big on screen, as through the telescope; full in space).
  Parameters are `sl_`-prefixed: a shader's uniform of the same name (`warm`) broke the include.
- **The sky no longer draws the light** under the real sun (`DayNight`: `SkyMode.LightOnly`): the Cartoon sky's
  disc was a 20° soft glow along the light, no core, lifted 3° at sunrise (#1479), and at night the moon's
  shading (#1798). Under the fixed sun of checks it still does.
- **Space**: `SunDisc` fades out with `Atmosphere.Space`; the atmosphere draws its sun (occluded by the Earth's ball).
- **Light shafts** (`SunShafts`, a child quad; `shaders/sun_shafts.gdshader`): each pixel marches 28 taps toward the
  sun's screen place over the depth buffer and counts open sky; occluders cut beams. Strength 0.035 high, 0.3 on
  the horizon, none in space, faded off screen; the sun's place worked out per view (VR). "Sun rays" setting
  (`GameSettings.SunRays`, off by default on phones and Quest).
- **Glare on stars**: `sky_point.gdshader` hides points within ~5 % of the view's height of the sun on screen
  (back by 25 %), from `world_true_sun`: a telescope's narrow view brings what is near it out.
- **Bloom**: Cartoon's is the corona (no engine glow, so the world never washes out). Realistic keeps its glow
  (threshold 1.2) on the new core; not tuned (Realistic is in development).
