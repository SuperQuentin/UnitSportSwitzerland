# Lift: thermals, ridge lift, updrafts (#1225)

`src/World/AirLift.cs` (pure, `AirLiftTests`), the sun it reads `src/World/SunPath.cs` (pulled out of
`DayNight`, the same numbers to the bit), the game's side `src/World/AirLiftField.cs`. Plan:
`docs/plans/adventure-stamina.md` 6-7.

## Rule

- **One query**, `AirLift.At(e, n, alt, now, IAirLiftTerrain)` -> the air's velocity (world axes, almost
  always only Y): thermals + ridge lift + `AirLift.Volumes`. A function of LV95 position, the height grid,
  the cover and the shared clock: the same on every peer, nothing summed per frame.
- **Thermals**: a candidate per 400 m cell (jittered by a hash), strength = sun.Y x aspect (0.4 + 0.6
  n.sun) x cover (bare 1, forest 0.6, water/ice 0, unknown 0.8) x life (a 6-10 min breath of its own),
  none under 0.25. Core up to 3.5 m/s with a falloff `exp(-(d/r)^2)` (the first `exp(-2x^2)` was so narrow
  a circling canopy lost it), a -0.8 sink ring at 1.5 r, top 300 + 900 x strength m faded over the last
  15 %. Cells cached (LRU 256) per terrain.
- **Ridge lift**: a wind of the day from a hash of the day (W/SW 60 %, Bise 25 %, Föhn 15 %, 3-8 m/s);
  lift = wind x the ground's climb over 40 m upwind x a fade to 200 m above the ground; the lee sinks half.
- **Volumes**: `Set(key, ...)` for persistent ones (a burning campfire: radius 4, 25 m, 6 m/s, kept in step
  with `PlacedObjects` and `CampfireClock` once a second by `AirLiftField`), `Add(e, n, alt, radius,
  seconds, now)` for a temporary one (the rewards plan's bottled gust), `Remove`, `Prune`.
- **Who flies in it**: `FlightEnv.Air`; `FootPlayer.FlyPhysics` asks `AirLiftField.AirAt` once a physics
  step for the own canopy, wingsuit or pigeon only (`AirNow` keeps it for probes). `Canopy` adds it to its
  target velocity, `Wingsuit` flies relative to it, `PigeonFlight.Controls.Air` carries a gliding pigeon.
  Planes, helicopters and remote flyers: zero.
- **Off** in a Battle Royale match and with the server's `--lift off` (`Permissions.LiftOn`, from
  `Arrival.Rules`); independent of `--stamina`.
- **The hour**: `WorldClock.HourAt(now)` when the world clock runs, else the sky's, else noon;
  `AirLiftField.HourOverride` for checks. The clock is `ClockSync.SimNow` (`core/three-clocks`).
- **Cues**: the variometer (`Audio/Variometer`, `SfxSynth.VarioBank`, Settings > Audio). The circling
  birds, rising specks, cumulus puff and campfire shimmer of the plan are not built yet.

## Measured

`--flycheck thermal --time 13 --chunks fixture:crag`: a paraglider circling at 0.7 of the stick gains
96 m in 90 s (the plan hoped for 150: the canopy sinks ~2.3 m/s in that turn, so a 3.5 m/s core nets about
1 m/s; the check asks 80). `--time 22`: no thermal, -115 m. `--flycheck campfire`: 3.5 m/s climb over a
fire's updraft (crossed at 9 m/s: 2 m gained, not the plan's 15 m, which would need hovering).
`--flycheck pigeon-thermal --time 13`: a pigeon gliding in circles gains 32 m in 20 s without flapping.
