# Aircraft parked at the airports (#422, epic #423)

A320s with airstairs docked, an AN-124 and the military freighter stand at real stands at Geneva
(LSGG) and Zurich (LSZH), for anyone to take. They come back a while after they are taken. There is
no AI traffic.

## Data: `airports.json` (the preprocessor's `--airports`)

- **Sources**:
  - swissTLM3D has the airports: `tlm_areale_verkehrsareal` `Flughafenareal`. Only Genève and
    Zürich are mapped as such; the airfields are other areas.
  - swissTLM3D has the paved movement area: `tlm_bauten_verkehrsbaute_ply` `Rollfeld Hartbelag` and
    `Hartbelagpiste`. Holes are ignored (`GeoPackageReader`), so the area under a dock counts as paved.
  - swissTLM3D has **no stands**. They come from OpenStreetMap, read from the Geofabrik PBF with
    `PbfReader`: `aeroway=parking_position` (a lead-in line, sometimes a node, with its `ref`),
    `apron` (named at ZRH: "Sector 7", "W"…), `taxiway`/`taxilane`, `runway` (centreline, `ref`,
    `width`), and the `aerodrome` relation for the ICAO code.
  - The tiles' buildings (`.bldg`, rasterised to 2 m) mark ground that is not free.
  - 2026-10-03 extract: GVA has 162 OSM stands, ZRH 255. Both have 0 tagged cargo.
- **The plan** (`tools/TerrainFormat/AirportPlanner.cs`, pure, `AirportTests`):
  - **Heading**: the nose is the lead-in line's end farther from the nearest taxiway. That end must
    be at least 15 m farther, or the line is a drive-through and is dropped (GVA 121-127). A node
    faces away from the nearest taxiway.
  - **Position**: the nose gear (`NoseGearZ`) stands on the stop point. The stand's E/N is the
    aircraft's node origin.
  - **Fit**: the footprint is nose to tail by wingtip to wingtip plus 3 m. Out on the wings, only
    the stretch round the wing root counts. At least 95 % of it must be paved and free of
    buildings, and it must not overlap a footprint already chosen.
  - **Choice**: the cargo apron gets the AN-124, then the freighter, roomiest stands first (farthest
    from the next stand). The cargo apron is one named cargo/fret/fracht, else every stand. Then
    comes a row of up to six A320s. It is built from the seed that gives the most A320s, then the
    tightest row. A row is one apron, its nearest stands facing within 30° of the seed, spaced at
    least 0.8 × span: business-jet stands are 15-20 m apart and are skipped.
  - **Gate**: airliners park only where a usable runway is at least 2.5 km long.
  - **Sizes**: the planner's copies of the game's numbers (`A320`, `An124`, `Freighter`), from
    `*Layout.NoseZ/TailZ/NoseGearZ` and `AirlinerCatalog.Span`. Change them together.
- **Runways** (`AirportPlanner.Profile`): the OSM centreline is sampled every 10 m on the built
  tiles, along the centreline and 1 m inside each edge. It records the steepest 100 m and the worst
  bump, which is the largest distance from a 200 m chord. A runway is usable when it is at least
  1.5 km long, no steeper than 2 % and has no bump over 0.6 m. Measured, all usable, **no terrain
  smoothing needed**:

  | Runway | Length | Steepest | Worst bump |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | GVA 04/22 | 3583 m | 1.07 % | 0.22 m |
  | ZRH 16/34 | 3244 m | 0.79 % | 0.29 m |
  | ZRH 14/32 | 3151 m | 0.65 % | 0.18 m |
  | ZRH 10/28 | 2500 m | 0.79 % | 0.27 m |
- **Result** (`test_output/airports/stage.txt`):
  - GVA: AN-124 at 19, freighter at 58, A320s at 61-66 (the row facing 317°).
  - ZRH: AN-124 at T46, freighter at W40, A320s at D06-D14 (facing 244°).
  - 3-7 s for the whole country, most of it reading the PBF.
- **Commands**:

  ```
  TerrainPreprocessor --out <chunks> --tlm <gpkg> --osm <switzerland-YYMMDD.osm.pbf> --airports [--airports-file f]
  ```

  It reads only the manifest, `.terr` and `.bldg`. The PBF comes from `swiss_data.py osm`, or
  straight from `https://download.geofabrik.de/europe/` (522 MB).
- **Licence**: `airports.json` holds positions derived from OpenStreetMap, so it is ODbL like the
  `.road` overlay (`tools/osm-odbl-licence`). It stays on the server and is never streamed.

## Runtime

- `World/AirportStands` (`Systems.Airports`, created in `ClientWorld`/`ServerWorld` beside
  `SteamerBerth`) reads the index once, from `IChunkSource.LoadAirportsAsync` or from
  `--airports <file>`.
  - `LocalChunkSource` reads `airports.json`. The decorators forward the call.
    `NetworkChunkSource` reads only its local source.
  - `FixtureChunkSource` plans its course's airport.
  - It is not an `AssetKind`: only the deciding peer needs it.
- **Who decides**: the dedicated server, or a client with no peer, as `SteamerBerth` does. A stand is
  registered when its tile enters the rings (`TileEntered`). A review runs every 3 s, nothing per
  frame. It places through `VehicleManager.Place`: owner 0, so the server is the authority at rest.
- **Names**: `veh_stand_<code>_<ref>`, for example `veh_stand_LSZH_D09`. The stairs are that name
  plus `_0` and `_2`.
- **Filling a stand**:
  - The aircraft goes on the ground at the stand's heading, gear down (`PackFlags`).
  - An A320 has L1 and L2 open. Airliner doors live in `Flags` bits 13-16, not `DoorsOpen`.
  - An A320's stairs are placed at the next review by `AirstairsDock.PlaceAt(plane.Capture())`. On
    the dedicated server that happens at once. Offline the client waits until the aircraft is posed
    and still: its body settles about 3° round on its gear, which put the L1 lip 0.6-0.7 m off and
    out of docking range.
- **Refill**: a stand whose aircraft is gone (taken, wrecked and cleared, or cleared as lonely) waits
  `RespawnSeconds`. That is 300 s by default; checks set `--standrespawn s` on the server.
  - It refills only with a player within 2.8 km, nobody within 300 m, and no other vehicle within
    half a span + 3 m. Its own old stairs are ignored for that test.
  - The old stairs, wherever they were pushed, are cleared first. The stand fills at the next
    review, because a node of the same name must be gone before a new one is spawned. A wreck still
    lying there holds the name until housekeeping removes it.
- **Bug fixed on the way** (`VehicleBody` spawn): a server-placed aircraft was drawn facing north on
  every client. Its replicated `Tilt` stayed identity, because the dedicated server never flies it,
  and remote copies pose from `Tilt`. A flyer now starts with `Tilt` = its yaw.

  (`--standrespawn 4`). The client's copies dock their stairs to the server-placed A320s.

  --at E,N`) writes `test_output/airports/apron|an124|freighter|runway-<style>.png`.

## Not done

- No jet bridges, tugs, ground vehicles or AI traffic. No cargo apron is tagged in OSM at GVA or
  ZRH, so cargo means roomiest.
- OSM stand headings are inferred. A stand mapped against the convention would face the wrong way.
- Only `Flughafenareal` airports. Airfields (Bern, Lugano, Sion…) have no `airports.json` entry.
