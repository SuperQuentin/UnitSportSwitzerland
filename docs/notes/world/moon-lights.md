# The Moon colonies' lights from afar (#1946)

Plan: `docs/plans/moon-surface.md` section 9. The colonies: `moon-colonies`, `moon-industry`, `moon-rovers`, `moon-ruins`.

- **From orbit** (`World/Moon/ColonyLights`, `shaders/colony_lights.gdshader`): one mesh of points for every colony and
  outpost (245 at present): a few window points round each module, eight edge lights round each pad, the runway's edge
  lights every 150 m, the ice mine's four floods, the mass driver's ends, the radio telescope's hut; the pit station one
  failing red light; the Cold War base its dome's glow and a third of its windows. Each point is a quad whose four
  corners share it: the vertex shader lays it ~3 px across on screen whatever the distance, pulls it 0.4 % toward the
  camera over the globe's coarse cells, draws it only where the sun is down at it (its up against the sun), brighter
  nearer (a third at the Earth's distance), and drops it within 6-14 km, where the colony itself is built (8 km).
- **Body-fixed** (`BodyGlobe.AddBodyFixed`): the points are in the Moon's fixed frame (metres from its centre). Under the
  globe's root that is the Moon's frame when the anchor is elsewhere (and the root shrinks it about the camera); with the
  anchor on the Moon (lunar orbit) the holder is turned from that frame into the anchor's world at each shift. The sun's
  direction comes from `BodyGlobe.Sun`, written when it moves. Shown while the globe draws.
- **In the telescope from the Earth** (`shaders/moon.gdshader`, `MoonDisc.NewMaterial`): the Moon's globe is only drawn
  within 10 Moon radii, so from the Earth the sky's disc is what the telescope sees. Each colony is a direction in the
  moon's own axes and a strength (staffed 1, the Cold War base 0.45, the pit none) in `colonies`/`colony_count`; the
  fragment shader draws a spark on the dark part, at least 1.3 px (from `fwidth` of the normal) or the colony's own ~1 km
  when the zoom shows more. Only the near side's are ever seen: Moon Village in the earthshine, Artemis Base on the south
  limb; ILRS is on the far side.
- **Check**: `--moonsites artemis:night,moonvillage:night` (the bodies' clock at a night there, the sun ~20° down; the
  camera 40 km off and 18 km up; `site_<id>_night.png`). From the Earth: `--telescopecheck --systems ui,physics,sky --sun
  real --date 2027-08-06 --time 21.5` (a waxing crescent, Moon Village just past the terminator; the probe's "moon well
  up" and steadiness bars fail on that low evening Moon: they are the probe's, not the sparks').
