# Traffic on the earth world's leaves (#1636, G2 C6)

- **What**: under `--frame earth`, `Traffic.LeafRoads` (`EarthLeaves.RoadsOf`, set by `ClientWorld`) makes the lane, rail,
  walk, ride and tram graphs come from the roads of the leaves round the view (`World/Traffic.Leaves.cs`), not from LV95
  tiles. Everything downstream (cars, signals, trams, walkers, riders, trains, endpoints) is the flat world's code.
- **Placing records** (`World/TilePlace`): every graph builder takes `TilePlace.Of(frame, tile)` per tile and asks it for
  `World(x, y, z)`, `Key(x, y, z)` and `Out(heading)` / `Turn(dir)`. An LV95 tile goes through exactly the old expressions
  (`TileToWorld`, `Key(id.Global)`, the unturned heading): the flat world's graphs are unchanged. A leaf goes through its
  anchor (`CubeKey.Anchor`) in doubles, its headings turned into the world. A new builder must do the same, never
  `seg.Lv95` / `tile.Id.Global` / `origin.TileToWorld(tile.Id, …)`.
- **Join keys**: on leaves `TilePlace.EarthKey`: geodetic latitude and longitude in 0.5 m steps, laid out like the LV95 key
  (high word south, low word east), so `Incident`'s 3 × 3 cells and the walkers' meeting grid read the same. Height-free,
  frame-free (graphs built before and after a re-anchor join), across cube faces. `TilePlace.KeyOfWorld(frame, p, onLeaves)`
  keys a world point; `LaneGraph.OnLeaves` says which kind a graph holds.
- **Leaf tiles without copies**: `LeafStore.RoadsOf` decodes the roads section alone (the terrain's 96 decoded leaves stay);
