# `/time` where the player stands (#1801)

The world keeps **one clock, on Swiss civil time** (`WorldClock`, `DayNight.Hour`); the real sun is
computed for each place (`SunPath`, from the camera's LV95 place). Under `--frame earth` a player
thousands of km away saw `/time set day` put the clock at 08:00 Swiss, which can be night there.
`World/LocalHours` (pure, unit-tested) reads `/time`'s words where the player stands; the clock and the
wire are unchanged.

## What the words mean

| Word | Home (≤ 250 km of Bern) or the fixed sun | Away, under the real sun |
|---|---|---|
| `sunrise` / `sunset` | today's at Bern (`SunPath.RiseOrSet(date, rise)`), the fixed day's 06 / 18 | the real ones at the place: upper limb on the horizon (−0.833°), `SunPath.RiseOrSet(date, rise, e, n)` |
| `noon` | 12:00 | local solar noon: Bern's highest sun (golden-section search) moved by the longitude, 4 min a degree |
| `midnight` | 00:00 | noon + 12 h |
| `day` | 08:00 | sunrise + 2 h, held to at most half the way to noon (the fixed day's 6 → 8) |
| `night` | 21:00 | sunset + 3 h, held to at most half the way to midnight (the fixed day's 18 → 21) |
| `hh:mm` | Swiss time | the player's zone: UTC + round(longitude / 15°), no summer time |

- **Home** is a great-circle radius from Bern, 250 km: all of Switzerland (Geneva 130 km, Müstair
  190) and its border towns. Inside it every word gives the very value it gave before #1801
  (`LocalHoursTests.Geneva_keeps_every_named_hour`, `--localtimecheck home`). The radius is a cut, not a
  blend: at 250 km Bern's sunrise and the local one differ by ~20 min at most.
- **The fixed sun** (checks, fixture and fun worlds) is the same sun everywhere, so its hours and its
  zone are Switzerland's wherever one stands.
- **Polar day / night**: `sunrise` and `sunset` answer "The sun does not rise here today (polar night).
  Try noon or midnight." (or "midnight sun") and leave the clock; `day` falls back to noon, `night` to
  midnight. Never NaN.
- **The zone word** (`ClockZone.TryParse`): `/time set <hour> [zone]`, `/time [query] [zone]`, `/time
  <hour> [zone]`. `here`/`local` (the default: the zone you are in), `swiss`/`ch`/`cet`/`cest`,
  `utc`/`gmt`, `utc+8`, `utc-3:30`, `gmt+5.75` (UTC−12..+14). A bare `+8` is not a zone: it would be read as
  an hour. Names ignore the zone (they are the sun's where you stand). `add` and `speed` take none.
  Swiss time's offset is the date's at noon (CET +1, CEST +2).
- Replies away from home show both faces: "It is 12:30 UTC-7 (21:30 Swiss time)". At home they are
  unchanged ("It is 21:30").

## Who reads which place

- **Offline**: `DayNight.Place`, the camera's LV95 place the sky is read at.
- **Online**: the server reads the admin's place from the body it already keeps
  (`ChatManager.PlaceOf` → `FootPlayer.Global`), resolves the hour and sets its one clock, which goes to
  everyone as before (`WorldTime`). No protocol change. The console has no place: Swiss time.
  `/time query` is answered by the player's own client from its own place.
- The server's sun model and date are its own (`SunPath.Model`, `Calendar.Today`, `--date`): a client
  forcing `--sun fixed` against a real-sun server gets the server's reading.
