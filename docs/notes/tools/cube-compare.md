# The cube comparer and the rigorous LV95 inputs (G2 B3 PR 0, #1507)

Every B3 stage is checked against the LV95 build of the same ground (`docs/plans/global-tiling-g2.md`, B3).
Code: `tools/TerrainPreprocessor/CubeCompare.cs` (files, frames, kinds), `CubeCompareCore.cs` (pure: `Spread`,
`Tally`, `FeatureMatch`, `LineIndex`, `Agreement`, `ShiftFit`, `CompareBars`; `CubeCompareTests`),
`GeoInput.cs` (`GeoInputTests`).

## `--cube-compare`

```
TerrainPreprocessor --cube-compare --chunks <LV95 dir> --cube <cube region dir>
    [--kinds heights,cover,holes,water,roads,buildings,trees,pistes,fields,photo] [--compare-stride N]
    [--compare-leaves N] [--jobs N]
```

- Reads `.cubl` leaves (every section) and G1's `.cubb` tiles (heights alone; a `.cubl` leaf wins). A kind no
  leaf holds yet is **skipped with a line saying so**; exit 0 clean, 1 on a broken bar, 2 on bad arguments.
- **Positions**: LV95 records to ECEF by `SwissGeodesy.ToEcef(MinE + x, MaxN − z, y)` with their own height
  (an LHN95 altitude), leaf records by their leaf's anchor. Vectors are compared in each level-9 cell's
  anchor frame, one cube bundle at a time (its tilt against the local vertical is under 2.5 mrad there).
- **Rasters** (heights, cover, water, piste slots) at every leaf lattice vertex (every `--compare-stride`-th):
  the vertex's rigorous LV95 position, the LV95 value there, bilinear for heights, nearest for categories.
  Heights under LV95 still water are counted apart: the `.terr` holds the bed there (#298).
- **Draped features** (road vertices, surveyed trees) are compared as height above their own build's ground:
  the `.terr` bilinear for LV95 (its draping), the leaf mesh (its triangles split along (i, j)–(i+1, j+1))
  for leaves. Buildings and signals are not draped: absolute.
- **Matching**: by id where there is one (EGID, OSM and LWB ids for pistes and fields), else mutual
  nearest neighbour in plan (5 m; surveyed trees 1 m). A record counts only where both builds have ground:
  an LV95 record in a leaf the region holds, a leaf record over an LV95 tile with a `.terr`.
- **Per kind**: heights (bias, fitted horizontal shift, percentiles); cover and water (agreement per class
  of the LV95 build, still levels); holes (quad centres, each side's nearest on the other); roads
  (centreline vertices to the other build's line of the same class both ways, height above ground,
  length per class, junctions and signals matched); buildings (EGID, then nearest; vertices pair by pair);
  trees (surveyed matched, scatter and planted counted by kind); pistes (slot type agreement, OSM ids);
  fields (ring vertices to the LV95 outline by id). Photos are not compared here (B3 PR 7 resamples them
  in Godot).
- **Bars** (`CompareBars`, the plan's): 2 cm (p99) on vertices, plus 2×10⁻⁴ of the length for what is
  placed by distance (+1×10⁻⁴ for the LV95 grid's 80-190 ppm); categories ≥ 99.5 % per class with
  1,000 samples or more; scatter counts ±1 %; nothing alone over 0.5 %. **Heights** against `.terr`
  cannot meet 2 cm (7.2 cm quanta on a 1 m grid averaged from the 0.5 m source, against the source
  itself on the ~1.2 m lattice): their bars are |mean| ≤ 2 cm, the fitted horizontal shift ≤ 2 cm
  (`ShiftFit`: dh = c − ∇h·s by least squares on slopes 0.05-1.5, |dh| < 0.5 m) and p50 ≤ 7.2 cm.
- **Memory**: LV95 tiles and leaf heights in small LRU caches, each thread keeping its last tile (a lock
  per vertex cost more than the comparison); run with `DOTNET_gcServer=0` beside other jobs (server GC
  grew a scratch copier past 10 GB on 25 leaves).

## Rigorous geographic inputs in the LV95 build (`GeoInput`)

Every latitude and longitude the LV95 build reads (OSM: overlay, airports, pistes and lifts, venues,
givers, castles, fields; IGN's BD TOPO; Wikidata) enters through `GeoInput`: `SwissGeodesy.ToLv95` at the
**ground of the built tiles** (`GeoInput.Over(<chunk dir>)`: the `.terrc` heights every 50 m, a few metres
off at worst, a tenth of a millimetre in plan), else at a height the source gives (Wikidata's elevation),
else the Plateau's 500 m ellipsoidal. Boxes and filters go back through `GeoInput.ToLatLon`. So the LV95
build is an exact baseline for the cube build, which places the same points on the Earth from their
latitude and longitude. `SwissProjection` is left to the game's GPX, MapCore and MapSetup (B3 PR 8), and
G1's diagnostics in `CubeCheck`/`CubeFeatures`/`CubeStage` (what the old LV95 path did).

- **Why the ground**: an LV95 point moves ~3 cm per km of ellipsoidal height it is converted at; over
  the D: region (ground 370-2,320 m) the Plateau's height alone leaves p95 2.0 cm, max 4.5 cm.
