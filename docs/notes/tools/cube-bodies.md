# Other bodies' pyramids: `--cube-body` and the colour bake (#1849, H7c of `docs/plans/planets.md`)

The Moon's (and later Mars') coarse levels, in the Earth's format (G1's tiles, bundles, a manifest), on the body's own
cube (`CubeShape.Moon`: the 1,737.4 km sphere, face 0 on the near side's centre, the poles the centres of faces 4 and 5;
`tools/cube-tiles` has the format, `CubeShape` the placing).

## Heights: `TerrainPreprocessor --cube-body`

```
dotnet <tp>/TerrainPreprocessor.dll --cube-body moon --dem <whole-body DEM .tif> --cube-out <dir> [--cube-levels 0-5] [--jobs 4] [--cube-check]
```

- **The DEM** (`CylRaster`): one band of int16, uint16 or float32, uncompressed strips, classic TIFF or BigTIFF, simple
  cylindrical over the whole body (2:1, 180° W to 180° E, 90° N to 90° S), GDAL's SCALE/OFFSET metadata and NoData
  applied. That is how USGS ships LRO LOLA's `Lunar_LRO_LOLA_Global_LDEM_118m_Mar2014.tif` (92,160 × 46,080, int16 in
  0.5 m over the 1,737.4 km sphere, 8.5 GB) and MGS MOLA's 463 m mosaic. A missing sample takes the mean of the valid
  ones round it.
- **A height is a function of its lattice point**, as `--cube-global`'s: the point's planetocentric latitude and
  longitude (what the DEMs are gridded in; on Mars' ellipsoid it differs from the normal's by up to 0.3°), the mean of
  bilinear samples over its footprint. Two faces holding one edge point compute the same bits, which `--cube-check`
  proves. The maths live in `CubeBodyHeights` (linked into the tests, `CubeBodyTests`).
- **One face at a time**: the DEM is read as a window of rows and longitudes round the face (`CubeBodyHeights.WindowOf`,
  margins for the widest footprint), each row once and in file order, kept as the file's 16-bit samples. LOLA's
  windows: ~1 GB for an equatorial face, ~2.6 GB for a polar one (every longitude).
- The manifest names the body (`"body": "Moon"`) and its orientation; the Earth's manifests carry no body.
- Build it out of the worktree (`no-builds-inside-the-project`): `dotnet build tools/TerrainPreprocessor -c Release -o <scratch>`.

## Colour: the Godot bake with `--body`

```
python tools/body_colour.py <colour .tif> <colour.jpg>          # Godot's Image reads no TIFF
<godot> --headless --path . -- --globealbedo <dir> --body moon --colour <colour.jpg>
```

- The Moon's colour is NASA SVS's CGI Moon Kit `lroc_color_poles.tif` (27,360 × 13,680 RGBA, LZW, ~400 m a pixel):
  LROC WAC's colour, Hapke-normalised, so it carries no shadows of its own (the LROC WAC *morphology* mosaic does: its
  low sun's shadows would be drawn twice under the game's own light). One image a tile (`CubeAlbedo` with one "month"),
  WebP, the Earth's sizes (`GlobeAlbedoBake.SizeFor`).
- The bake refuses a pyramid of another body (the manifest's `body`).
