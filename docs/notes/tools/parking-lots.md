# Car parks laid out, not painted (#499)

- **What it replaces**: a car park used to be `SurfacePattern.ParkingBays` and nothing else —
  `CoverPalette` packed the pattern code into vertex-colour alpha and
  `shaders/body/terrain.gdshaderinc` painted a 2.5 x 5.0 m grid of lines on the terrain. TLM records
  no orientation for a surface pattern and a tangent cannot be recovered from the screen-space
  normal (`never-derive-surface-tangent-from`), so that grid ran on **world axes**: in any lot not
  square to north the bays cut across the rows (`land-cover` already said so). Fitting an axis needs
  the polygon, which is why the layout had to move into the pipeline.
- **`ParkingPlanner`** (`tools/TerrainFormat/ParkingPlanner.cs`): **pure** — no Godot, no I/O, no
  RoadGen — so the network stage, `ProceduralWorld`, the `parking` fixture course and the unit tests
  all run the same code. In TerrainFormat rather than RoadGen because the game references only
  TerrainFormat (the `LandingPlanner` precedent). Takes an LV95 ring, the roads beside it, an
  `IParkingGround` (ground + what already occupies it) and an optional `Frontage`.
- **The frame**: everything is laid out in the lot's own `(u, v)` about its centroid — `u` along
  `Lot.Axis`, `v` across. The axis is the bearing of the **bordering road** with the highest
  `priority` when one lies within 30° of a principal axis (a lot is built square to its street),
  else the long side of the **minimum-area rectangle** — whose sides are always collinear with a
  convex-hull edge, so the candidates are the hull's own bearings. Ties break on the lower bearing,
  so the axis never depends on ring winding or the starting vertex (both pinned in tests).
- **Modules**, widest that fits the usable depth (extent less two 0.3 m insets): two-sided
  5 + 6 + 5 = 16 m, one-sided 5 + 6 = 11 m, 45° off a 3.5 m one-way aisle 4.2 + 3.5 = 7.7 m,
  parallel along a kerb 2.0 + 3.5 = 5.5 m. Below that the lot is **rejected** and keeps the old
  shader grid, which is still better than bare tarmac.
- **Bands are anchored to the lot's lower `v` edge** and a band is laid only when the **whole** of
  it fits. The first version stacked outward from the centroid, which put a row's aisle outside a
  9 m lot — a row of bays nothing could reach. The anchor is a real boundary of the polygon, so a
  vertex moved on the far side shifts no bay.
- **A cell becomes a bay** when all four corners and its centre are inside the ring, unoccupied
  (walls from `Facades`, road corridors from the block's own segments + 2 m), the ground is known,
  and it is level to 0.35 m. A row holds one plane, so a lot on a slope **terraces per row**.
- **Planters replace the end bays**, they do not need spare ground beside them: a row with at least
  `IslandAfterBays + 2` clear cells spends its first and last on a kerbed `APRP` bed, and the bay
  next to one is flagged `Shaded`. The first version hunted for 2.5 m of free ground past the row
  end and so produced planters only when the bay grid happened to leave slack.
- **Entrance**: on the bordering road with the highest priority (score = priority × 100 − distance),
  a throat from the carriageway edge to the first pad, a `GiveWayLine` where it meets the road, and
  on a **paid** lot a `TicketBarrier` 4 m in with a `StopLine`, a `TicketKiosk` at the driver's
  window and the blue P. Paid or free is hashed from the lot key, with the road's priority tipping
  the odds (a lot off a busy road is the one that is metered in practice). A lot with no mapped road
  gets rows and no throat — TLM maps plenty of those.
- **The bay by the way in is left free** (`NearEntrance`), so an arriving player has somewhere to
  put their own car; the two nearest the door (or the entrance) are `Disabled`, 3.5 m, with a blue
  roundel. A trolley shelter stands **in** a bay, flagged `Blocked` — which is what real supermarket
  lots do, and the only thing that works in a lot laid out to its own boundary.
- **The lot key** is the centroid to the decimetre, FNV-1a (`ParkingPlanner.Key`). Never
  `string.GetHashCode()`: it is randomised per process and would flip paid/free every run.
- **Getting the polygon to the stage**: the stage cannot see cover (`CoverStage` runs *after* it)
  and must read nothing written later, or `--rewrite`'s byte-identical rebuild is lost. So
  `ParkingAreaExtractor` runs in the **road stage**, which already holds the GeoPackage open, and
  dumps the rings to `<chunks>_temp/parking_raw/<tile>.park` (`RawParking`, modelled on `RawRoads`;
  tab-separated text, invariant culture). A ring goes to the tile holding its **centroid**, once,
  so the stage sees the whole polygon and lays out one layout across a seam. The hand-traced
  `cover_overrides.json` rings (#84, Riddes house 15) come through the same path and so get real bays.
- **The stage** (`tools/RoadGen/Rewrite/TileRewriter.Parking.cs`): `PlanParking` runs before the
  tiles are assembled, needs the final lines and the ground, and files each piece by the tile it
  falls in. A pad quad that straddles a seam is **halved recursively** until its corners share a
  tile or it is under 4 m, so nothing overhangs its tile by half a row. Region line in the stage
  stats: lots laid out, bays, pads, planters, walks, fixtures, markings, and why the rest were
  rejected.
- **Arrows are shared, not copied**: `ArrowShapes` and `Arrow()` moved from
  `TileRewriter.TurnLanes.cs` to `PaintEmitter`. Those outlines were traced from the real SSV 6.06
  drawing after two attempts from memory came out wrong (`turn-lanes`) — a second copy was not on.
  `PaintEmitter.DisabledBay` is new: a 1.2 m blue roundel, which is what reads at a car park's scale.
- **The pattern comes off in `CoverStage`** (`LaidOutLots`), which runs after the network stage and
  already loads the finished `.road`: cells under a `ParkingPad` become the new
  `CoverClass.ParkingPaved` — the same tarmac, **no** `SurfacePattern`. Without it every laid-out
  lot shows real bays *and* the world-aligned grid bleeding a cell past its edge
  (`surface-patterns`). A rejected lot keeps its class and its grid. The same pass plants one
  broadleaf per planter into the tile's `.trees` (not as a prop), so lot trees get the ordinary tree
  LOD, thinning and pooled collision.
- **Checks**: `ParkingPlannerTests` (31 cases: bays inside the ring and non-overlapping, the module
  per depth, the axis against winding/vertex order/a bordering road, no bay on a building or a road,
  terracing, the entrance on the main road, paid vs free, disabled and free bays, arrows on one-way
  aisles only, planters and their trees, shelters and the walk, and a **pinned layout**);
  `RoadGen --format-check` covers the `PARK` round trip; `ParkingFixtureTests` run the whole chain
  over the `parking` fixture course with no terrain data at all.
- **Not done**: separate entry and exit throats on a big lot (one throat serves both ways); motorcycle, truck and bus bays exist in the format but the planner
  only writes `Car`; multi-storey anything; a lot's own lighting; `Frontage` guesses the door from
  the nearest wall point and the floor area from how much wall stands within 60 m, because the stage
  has only `.bldg` walls — #496's `BuildingTypes` would answer both properly and should be used
  once it is available to the stage.
