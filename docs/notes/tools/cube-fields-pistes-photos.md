# Fields, ski slopes and photos on work frames (G2 B3 row 7, #1507)

Row 7 of the B3 table (`docs/plans/global-tiling-g2.md`): the farm fields, the ski slopes and the aerial photos
written into a cube region's leaves. The pieces they stand on are in `cube-frames`; the leaf formats in `cube-tiles`
(`Fields`: outlines as anchor (x, z); `Pistes`: records, `Slot`/`Aux` on the leaf lattice; `Photo`: an encoded image
over the leaf's (u, v) square, row 0 south). The lifts' `ropeways.json` is a region-wide file (row 8); the ropeway
lines themselves are TLM lines in the roads (stages 2 and 4b).

## Fields (`--cube-fields`, `tools/TerrainPreprocessor/CubeFieldStage.cs`)

```
TerrainPreprocessor --cube-fields --cube-out REGION [--fields LWB_DIR] [--osm-pbf PBF] [--gwr data.sqlite] [--cube-box ...]
```

- The LV95 build's sources, codes, ids and gating (`LwbReader`, `FieldCodes`, `OsmFields`), read once: LWB rings in
  LV95, OSM rings kept in latitude and longitude (`OsmFields.Load` takes a projection; the LV95 build passes
  `GeoInput.ToLv95`, unchanged).
- Per frame each field touching the frame's query box goes onto the plane (OSM exactly, `WorkPlane.ToPlane`; LWB from
  LV95 at the frame ground's height, `CubeFrames.FromLv95`), is prepared there (`FieldGeometry.Prepare`: 0.5 m,
  200 m²) and written whole into every core leaf its bounds touch, as the LV95 build writes it into every tile it
  touches; ring points as anchor (x, z) of the point on the leaf's ground.

## Ski slopes (`--cube-pistes`, `CubePisteStage.cs`)

```
TerrainPreprocessor --cube-pistes --cube-out REGION --osm-pbf PBF [--cube-box ...]
```

- One copy of the rasteriser: `PisteStage`'s rules (line buffer by distance, area inside and its depth to the outline,
  the edge ramp, which slope wins a vertex, records, `PackAux`) run on a `PisteStage.IPisteGrid`.
  `Lv95PisteGrid` is the LV95 tile's 1 m grid, the arithmetic as before (byte-identical). `LeafPisteGrid` is a leaf's
  lattice: a vertex at its plane point (`LatticeToPlane`), plane boxes taken into the lattice, rings filled by the same
  scanline in lattice coordinates (edges cut every 10 m first), distances in plane metres. No LV95 raster is
  resampled.
- The slopes are read once in latitude and longitude (`PisteStage.Load` takes a projection) and moved onto each
  frame's plane exactly. An area's groom phase is measured from its own first point (frames agree to a millimetre);
  the LV95 grid keeps its fixed origin. Terrain numbers per slope (altitude percentiles, sun, steepness) come from the
  leaf's heights above sea level, the gradient from the neighbours' plane positions.

## Photos (`--cubephotos`, `src/World/Globe/CubePhotoBake.cs`, a Godot tool mode)

```
<godot> --headless --path . -- --cubephotos REGION [--photos LV95_CHUNKS] [--size 512] [--quality 0.85] [--cube-box ...] [--mosaic PNG]
```

- As `GlobeAlbedoBake`: Godot's `Image` decodes the LV95 build's SWISSIMAGE tiles (`photo_E_N.jpg`, 512² a km) and
  encodes each leaf's JPEG. A pixel's colour is a function of its own place: its centre on the leaf's lattice, on the
  leaf's ground (bilinear heights), to LV95 by `SwissGeodesy`, bilinear between the source pixels' centres across tile
  edges. So leaves meet without a seam of their own (`--mosaic` puts a 3 × 3 block side by side).
- A leaf any of whose pixels the source misses gets no photo (counted). A `--cube-box` run keeps the other leaves'
  photos. The run checks each JPEG decoded again against its samples.
