# The road stage on work frames (G2 B3 PR 2, #1507)

The LV95 build's road extraction, tunnel holes and car parks, and BD TOPO's French roads, made cube-native
(`docs/plans/global-tiling-g2.md`, B3 row 2). The pieces it stands on are in `cube-frames`; the leaf format
in `cube-tiles` ("Leaves with content"). Code: `tools/TerrainPreprocessor/RoadSpace.cs`, `CubeRoadStage.cs`;
tests `FrameRoadsTests`; checks `--cube-compare` (`cube-compare`).

## One copy of the maths: `IRoadSpace<TTile>`

`RoadExtractor`, `TunnelCarver`, `ParkingAreaExtractor<TTile>` and `FranceRoads` take their plane, units,
ground and tile loop from one interface; positions are (E, N) metres on the space's plane, heights above
sea level, as before.

| | `Lv95RoadSpace` (the LV95 build) | `FrameRoadSpace` (a work frame) |
|---|---|---|
| plane | LV95 E, N (grid metres) | the frame's `WorkPlane` (true metres, 3 ppm) |
| sources in | as they are; lat/lon through `GeoInput` on the built ground | `WorkFrame.FromSource` (TLM at its own LHN95 Z); lat/lon by `Plane.ToPlane`, exact |
| ground | `Lv95Ground` over the batch's chunk grids | `FrameGround` over the region's leaf heights (`RegionHeights`) |
| tile loop | `PolylineClipper` at the 1 km grid | the leaf of each point (its geodetic normal's cube cell); a cut solved on the face index (secant) or by bisection across a face edge, then bracketed on the leaf to the last bit |
| record frame | `(E − MinE, h, MaxN − N)` floats | `LeafTransform.PlaneToLocal` in the leaf's anchor |
| holes | the 1 m quads (`HoleFormat`) | the leaf's lattice quads (`LeafHoles`), centres by `LatticeToPlane(i + ½, j + ½)` |
| carving from | written tiles only | any leaf (a portal in the next frame still opens this frame's quads) |

The constants stay in metres (4 m drape spacing, 0.1 m joins, the portal strip: centres up to half of
`ChunkFormat.SpacingM` in front of the portal, 1.5 m in, half the bore + 0.3 m wide), so the strip is the
same on either lattice. Distances along a line (the TLM keys' `FromM`) are plane metres: true metres in a
frame, LV95 grid metres (80-190 ppm long) in the LV95 build.

## `--cube-roads`

```
TerrainPreprocessor --cube-roads --cube-out REGION [--tlm GPKG] [--route-keys SQLITE] [--cover-overrides JSON]
    [--cube-bdtopo] [--cube-box minE,minN,maxE,maxN] [--cube-temp DIR]
```

- Runs over the leaves of `REGION` that have heights (the `--cube` stage's), one frame at a time in the
  driver's order, lines read 1 km round a frame (`TileRegion.RingM`), car park rings 500 m round
  (`ParkingReadM`: a ring's area centroid, its home, need not lie on a leaf it touches).
- Writes into `REGION`'s `.cubl` each core leaf's **`Holes`** (final, as the LV95 build's `.holes`), and
  into the temporary `.cubl` in `<temp>/roads_raw/` (default `<REGION>_temp`) the raw kinds the network
  stage (4b) starts from: **`Roads`** (the raw `RoadTile`, no Network flag), **`RoadKeys`** (one per raw
  segment) and **`Parking`**. The LV95 build keeps the same in `roads_raw/` and `parking_raw/`.
- `--cube-bdtopo` adds BD TOPO's `troncon_de_route` (the Géoplateforme WFS, a request per frame over its
  query box): French segments go into the raw roads with no key (decided with the user: 4b networks them
  with the Swiss ones). Without `--tlm` only BD TOPO's roads are written (France alone, Brest).
- `docs/data/cover_overrides.json` (the hand-traced Riddes lots) is read by default, as the LV95 build does.
- A `--cube-box` run rewrites the stage's kinds of every leaf in the frames it touches (`LeafWriter`): leaves
  of those frames outside the box lose theirs. Run whole frames.
- `--roads-only --no-network` (LV95) writes the raw roads alone, the reference to compare with.

## What 4b takes from here

- The raw `Roads` section of a leaf is what `RawRoads.Write` writes for a tile, in the leaf's anchor frame;
  `RoadKeys` is aligned with its segments (`LeafRoadKeys.Decode`), `FromM` in true metres: the OSM overlay's
  intervals (LV95 grid metres) must move to the plane's metres with it.
- `Parking` rings are on the leaf mesh in the anchor frame (`LeafParking`); `LocalToPlane` brings them back.
- A segment crossing a leaf edge is cut there, its two pieces sharing the cut point; across frames the
  two frames' cuts agree to well under a millimetre.
