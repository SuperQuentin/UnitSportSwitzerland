# Work frames, leaf transforms, the ground and the leaf writer (G2 B3, #1507)

The cross-cutting pieces every cube-native build stage stands on (`docs/plans/global-tiling-g2.md`, B3,
PR 1), and the first stage on them: `--cube` writes its heights into `.cubl` leaves. Formats in
`cube-tiles` ("Leaves with content"); the plane and the source projections in `WorkFrame.cs`,
`SourceProjection.cs` (B2, #1523). Tests: `LeafTransformTests`, `FrameBuildTests`.

## The driver (`tools/TerrainPreprocessor/FrameDriver.cs`)

- `FrameDriver(wantedLeaves, marginM, seaLevel)`: the level-9 cells holding wanted leaves, as
  `FramePlan`s in **(face, y, x) order**: every stage runs frames in that order (the seam's owner rule
  depends on it). Wanted leaves come from source coverage: `FrameDriver.LeavesUnderLv95` is G1's
  100 m probe lattice under LV95 km tiles (swissALTI3D, the foreign DEMs' tiles).
- A plan: `Core` (its wanted leaves, (y, x), what the frame writes: one `.cubl` bundle), `Margin`
  (wanted leaves outside the cell within M metres on the plane, breadth-first through
  `CubeKey.Neighbours`, so across face edges and round cube corners: context, never written),
  `Frame` (the `WorkFrame`), `Outline` (the cell on the plane, a point at every leaf corner),
  `Transform(leaf)` (cached `LeafTransform`), `QueryBox(source)` (the outline pushed out by M in eight
  directions, projected into the source's coordinates, plus a metre), `Region(source)`.
- `LeafRegion`: `TileRegion`'s contract (`ISourceRegion`: `MinE..MaxN`, `Count`, `Touches(box)` true
  within the margin of a wanted leaf, edges included, thread-safe), so `GeoPackageReader.TileRows`
  takes either unchanged. Each leaf is an 8-point outline in the source's coordinates, distances in
  metres by the source's scale at the region's centre (`MetresPerUnit`: LV95 0.9999, Lambert-93
  1.0009, latitude 111 155 m and longitude 77 178 m a degree at Geneva), 1 km buckets, `SlackM`
  0.5 m added (an LV95 point moves 3e-5 m a metre of height; outlines are at sea level). **X is the
  source's first coordinate**: for `GeographicSource` that is the latitude, the reverse of a
  GeoPackage's (x = longitude); a geographic reader must swap.

## The transforms (`tools/TerrainFormat/LeafTransform.cs`, Godot-free)

- `new LeafTransform(frame, leaf)`: plane (x east, y north) + height above sea level against the
  leaf's anchor frame (x east, y up, z south) and its fractional lattice (i, j). G1's scheme: the chain
  is rigorous at 17 × 17 control points (every 32 vertices, 39 m), the ground point S and normal U
  in the anchor frame (S + h·U is exact along the normal), the plane point (height-free: the plane is
  the ellipsoid from above), the sea level; bilinear between; **the outer rows rigorous**, so a shared
  edge's lattice points map to the same plane point and ECEF point to the bit from either leaf
  (tested across a face edge and at a cube corner).
- `SwissSeaLevel` is an `IGriddedSeaLevel`: the smooth Bessel part and the LV95 position are
  interpolated, CHGeo2004 is read at the point, so its kinks at the 20 km grid lines stay sharp
  (interpolating the undulation itself would be off ~1.5 mm there). `Split` + `Grid` is `Undulation`
  to the bit.
- API: `LatticeToPlane`, `PlaneToLattice` (Newton on the bilinear patches), `UndulationAt`,
  `LatticeToLocal(i, j, hEll)`, `LatticeToEcef`, `PlaneToLocal(x, y, hSea)`, `LocalToPlane`,
  `LocalToLattice`, `Jacobian`, `DirectionToLocal/ToPlane`, `HeadingToLocal/ToPlane` (degrees
  clockwise from north). Inverses are valid over the leaf and a few metres round it: ask the leaf
  that holds a point.
- Measured (`LeafTransformTests`, Geneva, Sion, a frame east, a face edge, across it, a cube corner,
  Brest): plane → local within **0.057 mm** of the chain (the ellipsoid's sag over a control cell,
  L²/4R), lattice → plane 0.018 mm at worst (cube corner), headings 0.002″; the plane-to-leaf turn
  reaches 0.18° a frame away.
- Cost (one thread): a transform **3.3 ms a leaf** (289 control points + 2,052 rigorous edge
  undulations); per point the rigorous chain plane → local is **1.7 µs** (the undulation alone 0.9 µs),
  the transform's plane → local 0.8 µs, lattice → plane 0.13 µs: a leaf's 513² vertices 451 ms
  rigorous against 35 ms.

## The ground (`tools/TerrainFormat/Ground.cs`)

- `IGround.Sample(x, y)` → height above sea level or null, the stages' drape.
- `Lv95Ground(grids)`: TerrainSampler's code moved (x, y = LV95 E, N; the tile west or south on a
  boundary); `TerrainSampler.For` returns it. Byte-identity: PR #1527.
- `FrameGround(frame, leaves)`: the leaf under the point (its normal's cube cell), the point's lattice
  position (`PlaneToLattice`), the mesh's ellipsoidal height as drawn (quad split along
  (i, j)–(i+1, j+1)) less `UndulationAt`; on an edge or over a missing leaf or NaN corner, the
  neighbours. Points on edges shared by two frames read from both frames agree to 0.005 µm
  (synthetic) and to under a µm over the D: region (`--cube-check`).

## The leaf writer (`tools/TerrainPreprocessor/LeafWriter.cs`)

- `new LeafWriter(dir, frameLevel, stageKinds, seams)`; `WriteFrame(cell, sections)`: the seam pass,
  then **one `CubeBundle.UpdateLeaves` per frame per stage** (the stage's kinds replaced, every other
  kind bit for bit). `WriteManifest(dir, leafLevel, frameLevel, sources)`: the region's
  `cube_manifest.json` from what is on disk: its level, every leaf with `kinds` and `bytes` and the
  heights' range, the region's `kinds` (both left out of pyramid manifests).
- **Seam: an owner per shared vertex.** A lattice vertex on a leaf's edge is held by 2 leaves (4 at a
  corner, 3 at a cube corner, `LeafSeam.Holders`). Its owner is the holder first in
  `LeafSeam.FrameOrder` (frame face, y, x, then leaf y, x) among those that have the kind; the others
  copy its value. Frames run in that order, so an owner is always in this frame (memory) or an
  earlier one (disk). Only the boundary rows are held (`BoundaryValues`); a leaf that copied is
  decoded again, patched, encoded. A frame rerun alone takes its earlier neighbours' values; **the
  frames after it must be rerun** to take its new ones.
- Lattice kinds known (`LatticeKind`): `Heights`, `Cover`, `Water` (stride 2). Pistes need their own
  adapter (PR 7).

- `--cube` runs on the driver and writes `Heights` sections through the writer (`.cubl`, one bundle
  per level-9 frame); the coarse pyramid keeps `.cubb`. `--cube-check`, `--cube-dump` and
  `--cube-swiss` read `.cubl` (G1's `.cubb` when a region has none).
- swissALTI3D at 2 m (the corridor, `data/swissalti3d_2m`): `SwissAltiSource(dirs, cache, 2)`, 500²
  cells centred at base + 1 + 2i, cached as `E_N_2m.f32` (1 MB); `CubeHeightSource.Swiss` is a list,
  finest first (0.5 m, then 2 m where 0.5 m has none).
- `--cube-check` adds: shared edges counted across frame and face edges; the frame ground against the
  sources; with `--cube-terr DIR` against the LV95 `.terr` at the same place (on leaves without the
  water stage, lake beds left out: the LV95 build holds beds there, those leaves the surveyed surface).
  Once `--cube-water` has run, the leaves hold the beds: the rows against the sources leave out points
  under a leaf's `Water` section and print how many (#1568); points on edges across frames
  from both frames' grounds (1 µm). G1's height-field collision rows are informational (it misses
  2 cm; collision is trimesh patches, C2).

## What the later stages take from here

- Read a source through `plan.QueryBox(source)` and `plan.Region(source, margin)`; a line stage
  (roads) wants a margin of about a leaf, as `TileRegion.RingM` was a tile.
- Work on `plan.Frame.Plane` in metres; drape with `FrameGround` (heights from the frame's own and
  its margin leaves: read them from the heights stage's `.cubl`); write positions with
  `plan.Transform(leaf).PlaneToLocal`, headings with `HeadingToLocal`.
- Lattice kinds (cover, water, pistes): compute each leaf vertex from `LatticeToPlane(i, j)` (never
  resample an LV95 raster), write through a `LeafWriter` with the kind's `LatticeKind` seam.
- A feature crossing a frame edge is cut by each frame at its own leaves; per-leaf counts differ
  from LV95 tiles by construction (the comparer, PR 0, matches by id).
- The water stage's one union-find over the region (PR 6) must see every frame's labels: it cannot
  run frame by frame as written here.

## Holes, as the road stage writes them (B3 PR 2, `cube-roads`)

What the water stage (PR 6) and the game (C1) read:

- **Where**: the region's own `.cubl` (not the temporary one), a `LeafKind.Holes` section on each leaf
  with a portal, none elsewhere; written by `--cube-roads` after the heights (`LeafWriter`, stage kinds
  `[Holes]`: a rerun replaces them).
- **What**: the lattice quads left out of the ground mesh at tunnel portals, `LeafHoles`: quad (i, j) is
  the one whose south-west vertex is lattice vertex (i, j) (i along u, j along v, 0..2^bits − 1),
  index `i + (j << bits)`; payload bits u8, then Brotli of the count and the sorted indices as delta varints.
- **Which**: the quads whose centre (`LatticeToPlane(i + ½, j + ½)`) lies in a portal's strip, the
  LV95 build's rule in metres (`TunnelCarver`: up to 0.5 m in front of the portal, 1.5 m in, half the
  bore + 0.3 m either side). A strip near a leaf or frame edge is cut by the leaves that hold its quads,
  each written by its own frame, so a hole across leaves is whole (each frame carves from its margin).
- **Not a lattice kind**: a quad belongs to one leaf, so there is no seam; a vertex next to a hole keeps
  its height (the mesh just drops the quad), as with `.holes`.
- About ~1.2 m quads against the LV95 build's 1 m: the D: region's 767 portals' holes have centres
  within 0.9 m (p99) of the LV95 ones, 7,398 leaf quads against 10,447 LV95 ones.
