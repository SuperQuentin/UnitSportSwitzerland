# Junction planner (#818)

Plan and the user's decisions: `docs/plans/junction-layout.md`. Junctions become streets, transitions and cores; this note
records what is built, phase by phase. Runs behind `RoadGen --rewrite|--test-region ... --junctions new` until the switch.

## Phase 1: mouth sections from the data, within the room

- **Model** (`tools/RoadGen/Layout/`): `Section` = an arm's strips left to right as seen driving in (side bands included),
  `CarriagewayLeft` from the arm's axis; `Strip(Kind, Width, Way, Moves, Id)`, ids stable along the arm (planner-added:
  left pocket 100+, left-turn bike lane 110, right pocket 120, hatch 130). `Section.Normal` reads a street's ATR2 attributes
  (sides as the street planner cut them at that end, the paint copy's bike lanes).
- **Data** (`TileRewriter.NeedsOf`, shared with the pocket layout, byte-identical): own lanes, OSM `turn:lanes` or inferred
  lanes, wanted pockets, speed, bike layout, bike box. `MouthPlanner.Wanted`: no lane or pocket for a banned turn; lanes
  3.0-3.5 m where lanes are added, the street's own widths where not; a one-way street out gets no arriving lane.
- **Planner** (`TileRewriter.Layout`, per block): (1) wanted sections; (2) exits lined up as if room were everywhere;
  (3) pocket sizes per arm (`PocketSizes`, right pocket at most `RightEarlier` past the left's storage), the street shared
  with the junction at its other end (its exit hatch keeps `TurnExit + TurnRejoin`; too short for both: **merged**, #325);
  an exit shorter than `TurnExit + TurnClear` feeds no pocket; (4) room per arm (`MeasureRoom`: every 2.5 m out to the
  longest transition, each side to the first building, other line, ground step > 1.2 m or tile edge, 20 m max);
  (5) rounds: align from the base sections, fit with the size search, align again from the fitted lanes, fit again; an exit
  that cannot hold its hatch (fit step "7 the pocket feeding this exit") drops its feeder's pocket, all again.
- **Room order** (`MouthPlanner.Fit`): 1 bike right-turn lane, verge and buffer, 2 island, left-turn bike lane, 3 lane widths to 2.75 / bike 1.25, 4 right pocket,
  5 sidewalk to 1.5 m, 7 the pocket feeding this exit, 7 left pocket, 8 painted bike lane. Size choice (step 6) comes
  first in effect (the user). Room is checked per station against what the transition still holds there (`Stretch`):
  houses 68 m out limit only a long storage. Where the street already reaches past its room (sidewalks to the facades),
  as far as the street does.
- **Through riders before turning ones** (the user, Oct 13): where the arriving side has neither a painted bike lane nor a
  path and the straight-on exit carries one on (a departing bike lane or path), the mouth adds a through bike lane (id 115),
  lit or not; never one leading into nothing. At the lights always: every bike lane ends at its advanced line or bike box.
- **One rule for the three bike moves** (the user, Oct 13): a bike lane beside the car lane making the move (left and right
  ones beside the car pocket; the right one only in layout b, where the kerbside lane does not turn right), where the street
  the move goes into carries riders on, or at the lights (an advanced line or a bike box ends it). So a left-turn bike
  lane may exist without lights now (#711 had it lights-only; `JunctionRule.LeftTurnBikeLane` is the old layout's).
  Differences left: no bike box without lights; the order they go when room is short (lights: right first, left after the
  island; without lights right then left, first of all; through last).
- **Bike stops at the lights, the same rule for the three moves** (the user, Oct 13; #351 had it for left-turners only):
  an arriving bike lane ends at an advanced line where the street its move goes into carries riders on (a departing bike
  lane or path, `Section.CarriesBikesAway`), else in a bike box (`Strip.Stop`, `BikeStops`; a kerbside lane straight on
  and right goes by its straight-on move). Built in phase 4. Short of room the left-turn bike lane goes
  early (step 2); the through one **survives when possible**: it goes with the painted ones, last (step 8). J1 N gets one;
  J3 E keeps it with the short pockets; J3 N has room for a pocket or the bike lane: the pocket goes (LTR, as designed).
- **Bike lane beside a right pocket** (the user, Oct 13): without lights always between it and the through lane (layout b);
  at the lights (b) only where the traffic arriving from the arm the pocket turns into cannot turn left (one-way out,
  forbidden, no lane shows it), else kerbside (a), painted or added. Replaces #351's coin flip (`NeedsOf.BikeBetween`, kept
  for the old layout). The signal plan follows the layout (a kerbside lane gets its bike signal); it no longer
  forces (b). In (b) the bike lane goes straight on only; riders turning right get **a bike right-turn lane at the kerb**, right
  of the pocket (id 125), where there is plenty of room: it is the first thing the room order gives up (step 1); then
  they **share the right pocket** (`Strip.SharedWithBikes`: a bike connector from it in phase 5, no bike symbol in it). J1: kerbside on every arm (every arm turns left).
- **One pocket rule** (the user's generalization, Oct 13): `Layout/PocketRules` holds the left and right pocket as one rule
  with a table (move, side, speed condition, sizes, ids, most lanes from data, room step). What a pocket displaces follows
  from its side, not its move: a centre-side pocket pushes the through lane out (alignment, split, merge with the far exit);
  a kerb-side one meets the kerbside bike lane. `Lengths`, `NoPocket`, `MouthPlanner.WithoutPocket` are generic.
- **OSM `turn:lanes` are the truth** (the user, Oct 13): `DataLanes.Of`: the lanes beyond the carriageway's own are the
  pockets, taken from each end (left-turning at the centre, right-only at the kerb, up to 3 each: double rights too), at
  any speed and on multi-lane approaches (they used to be folded); the room order never drops them (`keepPockets`, and
  `keepHatch` on the exit one feeds; "does not fit" is reported instead), a too short street keeps their shortest size.
  The old layout keeps `Assign` (byte-identical). Sion: right pockets 14 -> 17.
- **Lanes and sidewalks narrow per side** (steps 3 and 5): only on the side that lacks room (J3 N's departing lane keeps
  its 4 m).
- **The exit hatch is not dropped by the ladder**: it lines the through lane up (a jog of a lane without it). A split
  lead-in share stays at most half (all on the far side would jog the oncoming traffic as much).
- **Checks**: `--junctions new` prints a region line (pockets, splits, hatches, steps taken) and a comparison with the
  pocket layout (pockets in both / planner only / pocket layout only, with places); `--debug-junction E,N` prints every
  arm's normal, wanted and mouth section, room, openings, split and steps. The test region reports the planned lanes
  against its design (`ReportPlans`; `PlannerDesign` holds where the planner is meant to differ: none now). Tier 0
  `JunctionPlannerTests`.
- **Measured** (Oct 13 2026): test region every designed approach as planned. 20 Sion tiles (LV95 2505-2509 / 1136-1139,
  the Oct 2026 `test120` copy: no OSM speeds or signal nodes): 1,017 junctions, 3,100 car arms; left pockets in both 27,
  planner only 6, pocket layout only 4. The old-only: a pocket toward a one-way street nobody can turn into (2506288,1137747:
  an old bug), exits whose sidewalks already reach the facades (2508497,1139784: the old check ignored the sidewalks).
- **Pitfall**: a dropped pocket must be planned again from the wants (`NoLeftPocket`), not cut out of the section: its
  lanes were sized for the pocket and the section was moved by its split.

## Phase 2: the core (connectors, mouths, kerb corners, outline, lines)

- **Model** (`Layout/JunctionCore`, `Layout/CorePlanner`, `Layout/ArmAxis`): each arm's axis is its link's alignment from the
  node (LV95, a point a metre); inside the core an arm is taken straight along its tangent at the mouth. `Connector`: from an
  arriving lane to a departing one (ids, offsets, width easing from its lane's to the exit lane's along the curve only),
  `CoreCorner` (arc, vertex, chord, gore), `CoreMouth`, `CoreLine`. Plans only: nothing is written to tiles yet.
- **Which lanes join**: every allowed move of every arriving lane (`ArmPlan.Toward`): left into the lane by the centre line,
  right into the kerb lane (counted from the kerb), same-turn lanes lane to lane, through lanes from the centre line out;
  bike lanes into the exit's bike lane, else the kerb side of its kerb lane.
- **Radii** (the user, Oct 13): every turn **as wide as fits** between the mouths; the mouths come from each turn's least
  radius (car 6 m on the inner edge, truck 10 m where a Major road is involved), so the order is: mouths at the least radii,
  then every connector widened, then narrowed back where its corner's kerb would start outside a mouth. A bike lane turning
  with a car lane is **concentric inside the car's turn** (its least radius the car's less its width: the kerb at ~4.5 m).
- **A turn is a deflection, not a move name**: a connector turning 30 degrees or more sets the mouths at its least radius; a
  straighter one (a bend through, a "right turn" of 5 degrees at a fork) fits between them, as an arc where its lines meet
  inside both mouths, else a smooth S. An arc only where the lane lines meet near the junction (within 40 m).
- **One envelope rule for every corner** (the user's generalization, Oct 13): the kerb is the least rounding (legs of at
  least `TightKerb` 1.5 m) that keeps every connector edge point paved (a point in the sidewalk's wedge is paved when no disc
  of the radius inside the wedge covers it: nested, so found by halving). A corner no connector passes stays tight with no
  rule of its own; a left turn swinging into a narrow arm rounds it too. Buildings beside the corner (side bands behind the
  arc) hold the radius smaller and narrow the turns, under their vehicle's radius if need be.
- **Mouths**: pushed out until every turn's least-radius arc and both corners' kerbs start inside them (the mouth is where the
  kerb leaves the straight edge); monotone after two rounds (on a curved arm the kerb's meet moves with the mouth); at most
  40 m, and half the street where another car-road junction stands at its far end.
- **An S holds its lane while a kerb runs beside it** on the side it moves toward (to the corner on the driver's right), then
  moves at 1:5 at the most; a kerb between arms nearly in line (175 degrees and more, or kerbs meeting far off) is an S between
  the two kerbs, so a lane moving sideways along it stays inside. Gores (arms under 20 degrees apart) push their mouths to
  where the kerbs between them meet.
- **Lines through the core** are connector edges, chosen by `JunctionRules`: the main road's centre line (between its two
  outer-left connectors) where the priority plan carries it; edge guides without lights (the rightmost connector's right
  edge, dashed where a road joins on that side); at the lights the left-turn guide (inner edge) toward an exit island or beside
  a left-turn bike lane, and the line between them; same-turn pair lines; the through guide past an exit island.
- **Multi-node cores** (phase 5): a junction whose street to another car-road junction is under 30 m is left out of the
  checks (Sion: 565 of 1,017 junctions; TLM splits many crossings into nodes metres apart).
- **Checks** (`--junctions new` prints `junction cores`): kerbs starting outside their mouth, connectors leaving the outline
  (10 cm tolerance), tight corners against #700's, paved area against today's cap **plus its ribbons out to the new mouths**
  (the cap alone is a fraction of it). `--debug-junction` lists mouths, corners (radius, floor, the connector that rounds it)
  and connectors (radii least/widest/fill, S holds, OUTSIDE); `--junction-svg` draws the real geometry over today's cap.
- **Measured** (Oct 13 2026): test region 13 cores, every check 0, J7's tight corner as #700. Sion 452 single-node cores,
  2,772 connectors: kerbs outside their mouth 20, connectors outside 21 (gores under 20 degrees, arms whose order flips
  between node and mouth on curved links); tight corners: both 1, planner only 25 (near-straight corners whose 5-degree
  "right turn" needs no rounding), #700 only 7 (a left turn passes them); area x1.19 median (p10 1.10, p90 1.40).
