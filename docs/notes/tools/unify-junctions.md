- **Fix** (approach 1 of the handover): the kerb arcs `TurnLanes.Corners` records in town (`townArcs`, `CornerArc`)

  matched by distance and missed). The kerb then runs from A's sidewalk start in to A's mouth, round the arc, out to

  ring corner (its failed attempt is not counted as a rejection).
- **Mirror strips**: `Corners` skipped a corner with neither an approach nor an exit widening, so the departing side
