# Junction priority: main road, Wartelinie, signs (#121)

- **Planner**: `tools/RoadGen/Junctions/PriorityPlanner.cs` decides per junction in plan view;
  `Rewrite/TileRewriter.Priority.cs` turns the plan into paint (teeth, the main road's centre
  line through the junction), point props (signs) and the ATTR yield bits
  (`YieldAtStart`/`YieldAtEnd`, `road-format-v3`). Shared sizes: `tools/TerrainFormat/RoadSigns.cs`.
- **Main road**: arms rank by TLM `verkehrsbedeutung` (or OSM highway), then owner (federal,
  cantonal), then width class. The pair ranking highest (the straighter one on a tie) carries
  through, only if every other car arm ranks strictly below it, and only if it is a priority road
  (importance, a cantonal road, or a 6 m class and up). Otherwise the junction is
  right-before-left: no markings, no signs. Tracks, paths and squares never get priority or a
  treatment; a roundabout ring is the main road at each of its nodes; motorway merges and
  diverges are not junctions here.
- **Treatment of a yielding arm**: a Wartelinie (row of white triangles, base 0.50 m, height
  0.60 m, gap 0.25 m, apex toward the approaching driver) across its approach lanes (all lanes on
  a one-way approach) 0.25 m back from the main road's edge, only on paved arms; a 3.02 "Kein
  Vortritt" sign on the approach's right, 2 m behind the teeth. The main road gets a 3.03
  "Hauptstrasse" per direction (inside localities before the junction, outside after; left out
  where only 3 m lanes join) and its centre line carried across the mouth when both main arms are
  two-way, paved, wide enough for one (`PaintEmitter.MinCentreLineWidth`) and turn less than
  60 deg. A straight centre line simplifies to its two ends.
- **Sign sizes** as `RoadSigns` states them, with the code's references (SSV SR 741.21): plate
  side 3.02 0.90 m (0.60 small, inside localities), 3.03 0.50 m (0.35); lower plate edge 1.5 m,
  2.2 m on a sidewalk; carriageway edge to plate 0.30 m inside localities, 0.50 m outside; 76 mm
  pole. A sign that would stand in a carriageway or on a structure is rejected and counted.
- **Runtime**: `RoadSignBuilder` draws pole and plate into the road mesh and gives the pole
  collision (bridge-deck body); `RoadPaintBuilder` draws the teeth from `RoadPaintGeometry.Teeth`.
  `LaneGraph` carries the yield bits to `Traffic`: a car on a yielding edge looks from 40 m out,
  slows, and stops 2 m short of the end while a main-road car would reach the junction within 6 s,
  is in it, or a player stands in it. Cars on yielding roads do not wait for one another.
- **6-tile test region**: 487 junctions; main road 72 (14.8 %), 30 with a yielding road,
  roundabout 25, right-before-left 304; 54 yielding arms, 53 Wartelinien (113 teeth), 30 main
  centre lines; signs 18 x 3.03 and 48 x 3.02 placed, 13 rejected (5 carriageway, 8 structure).
- **Checks**: `dotnet run --project tools/RoadGen -c Release -- --priority-check` (21 checks: T and
  crossroads plans, teeth, signs, centre line, runtime teeth orientation). Screenshots: a T
  junction and two crossroads in Riddes (teeth, 3.02 on the right, 3.03 diamonds, centre line
  through). `--trafficcheck --traffic 120 --at 2583450,1113720`: up to 2 cars giving way at once,
  now printed (`giving way N`). Multiplayer: `--garagecheck drive` straight at a 3.02 pole from
  15 m: the car stops with its centre 2.16 m from the pole, and the observing client's
  `netsmooth_track.csv` ends at the same distance.
