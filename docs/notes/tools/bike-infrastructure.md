- **Candidates** (raw lines, before the junctions read them): paved at-grade `Major`, `Road`, and

  ramps. **Outside towns only**, no lane where a `Lane`/`Track`/`Path` runs within 40 m and 20°
  for 70 % of the line (in towns a footpath runs beside every street: the rule removed 40 % there).
  OSM `cycleway=lane|track` per side overrides (`OsmOverlayReader`).
- **Lanes** (Radstreifen, SSV 6.09): width to the line's axis 1.50 m in town, 1.80 m outside
  (Kanton Bern AH Veloverkehr 2021 §4.3; ASTRA Handbuch Veloverkehr in Kreuzungen 2021 Abb. 301;
  measured to the line's middle, ZH Standards Veloverkehr 2023 p. 37). Centre line kept when the
  car lanes keep 2.75 m (ZH "Schmalfahrbahn"); else **Kernfahrbahn** (no centre line), lanes what
  a 3.5 m core leaves, at least 1.25 m (GR Anhang A §2.6). The guides ask a 4.5 m core and only
  inside localities (SSV Art. 74a al. 2: lanes on both sides outside need a centre line): the
  looser rule is the user's choice, so 6 m roads get lanes. One-way: one lane right of travel,
  a 3 m car lane left. No white Randlinie beside a lane. Line 0.15 m yellow 3 m / 3 m.
- **Paths** in towns (urban stations of a street that wants one, both sides of a two-way street,
  right of travel on a one-way one): five layouts, user's spec, outward from the kerb:
  1 path at sidewalk height, yellow dashes to the sidewalk; 2 = 1 behind a 1.0 m grass verge;
  3 path halfway down (6 cm), sloped kerbs both sides; 4 = 3 behind a verge; 5 = 4 with a 0.8 m
  grass buffer to the sidewalk too. Path 2.0 m (ASTRA). Grass takes the path's height. A kerb
  touching the path is a straight slope over 0.30 m (ASTRA slanted kerb "A", 6 cm / 30 cm);
  other kerbs vertical. The layout is hashed per **stroke** (links chained through each node with
  the straightest partner under 30°, keyed by the smallest TLM uuid), so a long street keeps it;
  where the corridor to the facade cannot hold layout + 1.5 m sidewalk, the next smaller one of
  the same level (5→4→3, 2→1), then none. A street with paths on ≥ 50 % of its urban stations
  drops its painted lanes; else it keeps the lanes and gets no path.
