# The base land cover on work frames (G2 B3 PR 5a, #1507)

swissTLM3D's area layers and the hand-traced overrides at every leaf vertex: the first half of row 5 of the B3
table (`docs/plans/global-tiling-g2.md`), done before water (row 6) because the water pass starts from the Water
class. Code: `tools/TerrainPreprocessor/CoverExtractor.cs` (`ICoverSpace`), `FrameCoverSpace.cs`,
`CubeCoverStage.cs`; tests `FrameCoverTests`. Format: `LeafCover` (`cube-tiles`).

## One copy: `ICoverSpace`

`CoverExtractor.RasteriseLayers` keeps the layers, their order, the classifiers and the overrides; a space
stamps each ring by `CoverExtractor.Stamped` (Water is never overwritten).

- **LV95** (`Lv95CoverSpace`): the scanline on the 1 m grid, as before (byte-identical).
- **A frame** (`FrameCoverSpace`): each ring onto the plane (`WorkFrame.FromSource` at its own height; a
  hand-traced ring at the ground), cut every 10 m, clipped to a box 2 m wider than each leaf it may cover,
  taken into that leaf's lattice (`PlaneToLattice`) and filled row by row like the LV95 scanline (half-open
  rows, the vertices between crossing pairs). No LV95 raster is resampled.

```
TerrainPreprocessor --cube-cover --cube-out REGION --tlm GPKG [--cover-overrides JSON] [--cube-box ...]
```

- **After the network stage** (PR 5c): `--cube-cover-network` (below). The trees: 5b.

## After the network stage: paving, tunnel roofs, laid-out lots (PR 5c)

`CoverStage.PaveStreets`, `RoofTunnels` and `LaidOutLots` take an `ICoverLattice`: the cells, each one's position
(x, z) and ground (y) in the frame the road records are in, the cells near a box (a superset, tested by position),
the cells in a box (`InBox`, the junction caps' reach) and the urban density at a cell.

- **LV95** (`Lv95CoverLattice`): the 1 m grid in tile-local metres, its loops as they were (byte-identical).
- **A leaf** (`LeafCoverLattice`): every vertex in the leaf's anchor frame at its own ground (`LatticeToLocal`), where
  the network stage writes its records (4b); the cells near a box through `LocalToLattice`; the urban density at the
  vertex's LV95 position (`UrbanField` over the LV95 build's buildings, until stage 3 writes them per leaf).

```
TerrainPreprocessor --cube-cover-network --cube-out REGION --facades LV95_CHUNKS [--cube-box ...] [--lv95-roads LV95_CHUNKS]
```

- Each leaf with a `Cover` and the network's `Roads`; the others of a bundle keep their cover. The planters are
  counted; their trees come with 5b. `--lv95-roads` (a check) runs the passes on the LV95 build's own network
  roads moved into each leaf, so the passes can be compared apart from the network stage's differences.
- **By construction**: the LV95 pass paves a tile from its own (clipped) segments only, so a street just across a
  km line never paves this side; a leaf does the same at its edges. A junction cap paves its axis-aligned box, and a
  leaf's anchor axes turn against the LV95 grid (by the meridian convergence): the box differs at its corners.

## Trees (PR 5b)

The four kinds of `CoverExtractor`, their rules shared (`ScatterChance`, `ScatterTree`, `RowTree`, `SurveyedHeight`,
`CoverStage.TreeClearance`, `CoverStage.PlanterHeight`: the LV95 build calls them in its old order, byte-identical),
placed on leaves by `--cube-trees` and written as each leaf's `Trees` in its anchor frame.

```
TerrainPreprocessor --cube-trees --cube-out REGION --tlm GPKG [--cube-temp DIR] [--cube-box ...] [--lv95-roads LV95_CHUNKS]
```

- **Scattered** (wooded classes): every vertex a leaf owns draws its chance, the class's density per hectare over
  the vertex's area (`ScatterChance`: the LV95 build's 0.0004 a square metre, kept per area), from a seed of its
  **global lattice vertex** (`TreeSeed.Of(face, i, j)`, SplitMix64), then its jitter (±1 m) and height.
- **Planted rows** (orchards, nurseries): the frame's plane every `PlantingSpacing` metres; a row tree's seed from
  its grid node.
- **Surveyed** (`tlm_bb_einzelbaum`): each point onto the plane at its own height, on the leaf mesh.
- **Planters**: the network's islands (4b), heights from their LV95 position.
- **Road clearance**: the leaf's raw and network segments, `TreeClearance` rounded up to whole metres (the LV95 mask
  stamps a disc of whole 1 m cells round points truncated to its grid: the threshold a lattice turned against it
  cannot repeat to the tree).
