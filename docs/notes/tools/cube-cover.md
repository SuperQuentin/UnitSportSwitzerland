# The base land cover on work frames (G2 B3 PR 5a, #1507)

swissTLM3D's area layers and the hand-traced overrides at every leaf vertex: the first half of row 5 of the B3
table (`docs/plans/global-tiling-g2.md`), done before water (row 6) because the water pass starts from the Water
class. Code: `tools/TerrainPreprocessor/CoverExtractor.cs` (`ICoverSpace`), `FrameCoverSpace.cs`,
`CubeCoverStage.cs`; tests `FrameCoverTests`. Format: `LeafCover` (`cube-tiles`).

## One copy: `ICoverSpace`

`CoverExtractor.RasteriseLayers` keeps the layers, their order, the classifiers and the overrides; a space
stamps each ring by `CoverExtractor.Stamped` (Water is never overwritten).

- **LV95** (`Lv95CoverSpace`): the scanline on the 1 m grid, as before (byte-identical).
- **A frame** (`FrameCoverSpace`): each ring onto the plane (`WorkFrame.FromSource` at its own height; a
  hand-traced ring at the ground), cut every 10 m, clipped to a box 2 m wider than each leaf it may cover,
  taken into that leaf's lattice (`PlaneToLattice`) and filled row by row like the LV95 scanline (half-open
  rows, the vertices between crossing pairs). No LV95 raster is resampled.

## `--cube-cover`

```
TerrainPreprocessor --cube-cover --cube-out REGION --tlm GPKG [--cover-overrides JSON] [--cube-box ...]
```

- Over the region's leaves with heights, frame by frame, rings read with no margin (as the LV95 tiles);
  each leaf's `Cover` through the `LeafWriter`'s cover seam. `docs/data/cover_overrides.json` by default.
- **Not here yet**: street paving, tunnel roofs and laid-out car parks (they read the network stage's final
  roads and the buildings' facades: after 4b), and the trees (5b).
