# The tiling check: junctions the same wherever the tile edges are (#1660)

The user's rule: "junctions should be generated the same, no matter where the tile edges are". `RoadGen --tiling-check` is the
bar for it: the network stage on the same raw lines under several tilings of one plane, every record compared.

```
RoadGen --tiling-check --chunks DIR [--temp DIR] --box minE,minN,maxE,maxN
    [--tilings "x,y,angle,cell;..."] [--block-m 4000] [--halo-m 1000] [--verbose] [--no-smooth]
```

- **Input**: an LV95 build's raw input (`DIR_temp/roads_raw`, its keys, `.terr`, `.bldg`, car parks, the OSM files), every raw
  tile within 3 km of the box. Nothing is written: each tiling's tiles stay in memory.
- **The runs**: the raw pieces are joined back across their km cuts (`Rewrite/LineJoin`: ends meeting on the edge between two
  tiles within 5 mm, the same segment record, keys agreeing where both have one) and cut again at each tiling's cells
  (`Geometry/GridUnits`: an origin, an angle, a cell size; each cell's frame a plane-aligned 1 km square centred on it and
  snapped to 2 m, exactly like `FrameUnits` for a leaf). The stage runs on them through `RegionData.On` (the LV95 plane, so the
  OSM, canton, gantry and tram inputs are the LV95 build's), in blocks of about `--block-m` and a halo of about `--halo-m`.
  The ground is the LV95 `.terr`, the walls the LV95 `.bldg` moved into each cell's square.
- **Default tilings**: `0,0,0,1000` (the km grid, the reference), shifted by 250 m east, 500 m north, (334, 666) m, turned
  28.5° in 610 m cells (like level-14 leaves), turned 45° in 400 m cells from (100, 200).
- **Compared** in the box, each tiling against the first (`Diagnostics/RecordMarks`, shared with `CubeTestRegion`'s leaf
  compare): junction records (class, area within 2 % or 1 m²), signals (plan size, canton look, poles' colour, overheads,
  gantries), stop lines, poles (flags), approaches (`LANE`: every lane's kind and moves), canton signs; all matched within
  5 m, placed within 5 cm. Paint: painted length (lines: a dashed line's dashes) and area (triangles) per type on a fixed 25 m lattice, within 1 m
  and 0.05 m² (a line offset from a curved centreline steps at a cut by its offset times the turn there, as at every seam). Centrelines: every reference vertex to the other tiling's lines, within 1 cm. `--verbose` lists each difference with
  its distance to either tiling's nearest cut. `[tilingcheck] RESULT: ok` when every bar holds.
- **Cost**: the traffic-lights test region (31 tiles) about 4 min per tiling at low priority, ~1 GB.
