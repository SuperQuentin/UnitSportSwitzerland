## Rules (SSV, SR 741.21)

- Art. 51: white on green leads to motorways / expressways (4.31), white on blue to destinations mainly by main roads (4.32),
  black on white by minor roads (4.33); at most three lines.
- Art. 52: advance signs 150-250 m before the junction outside towns, 20-100 m inside; 4.38/4.39 show a lane split.
- Art. 53: **Einspurtafeln 4.41 (main roads) / 4.42 (minor)** over the carriageway before junctions on multi-lane roads, the
  arrow on each lane's middle; fields coloured as Art. 52.
- Art. 70: lamps may stand side by side over the carriageway (red left, amber, green right): the horizontal head.
- Art. 71: heads repeated over their lane, or only over the carriageway where useful; lower edge 4.50-5.50 m.
- No SSV article, open VSS text or cantonal handbook names a head built into a sign (VSS norms paywalled): inferred legal
  from Art. 53 + 70 + 71, and Geneva builds it.
