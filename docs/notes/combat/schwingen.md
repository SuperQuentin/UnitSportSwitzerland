# Schwingen, Swiss wrestling at the Älplerfest (#1269 part 6)

`src/Combat/Schwing*.cs`, `src/Player/FootPlayer.Schwing.cs`, `src/Avatar/HumanMeshBuilder.Schwing.cs`; the plan is `docs/plans/adventure-festivals.md`
section 6. It copies the fist fight's shell (`fist-fight`: an invite, marks on a line, forfeits, a pose field the dance layer draws, a side-on camera) and
not its strike rules. Built on the festival framework (`festival-framework`): the game's server method pays through `FestivalService.Award`.

## What is pure (tier 0, `SchwingRulesTests`, `SchwingBotTests`)

- **`SchwingMatch`** (10 Hz on the server). Each wrestler has **balance** and **grip** (0-100). Intro 2.4 s, Live (3 min; the Schlussgang 6), Ground, Done.
  Leaning (the move stick, in the wrestler's own frame: in, out, left, right) against a rival who leans too wears both grips 8/s; a lean alone pushes the
  rival's balance down 3/s; balance comes back 6/s when nobody pushes, grip 3/s.
- **Throws**: Kurz, Übersprung, Hüfter, Brienzer, Gammen, Wyberhaken (`SchwingRules.Throws`: weight, brace lean, counter lean, in the defender's frame).
  A throw costs 10 grip (15 needed), has a **0.35 s telegraph** and a 0.9 s cooldown. At its end the defender's lean at that moment answers it
  (`SchwingRules.Answer`): the counter lean = **Countered** (the thrower loses 18 balance), brace held + the brace lean = **Braced** (the thrower loses 10,
  the defender 5 grip), anything else lands: balance - weight x (0.6 + 0.4 x the thrower's grip) x 1.2 when the defender leaned in. Under 20 balance = **Down**.
- **Bodenarbeit**: the thrower is on top, the tug meter starts at 50, each press (at most one per `TugGap`, 0.08 s, per wrestler) moves it 7 towards the presser's end.
  100 = both shoulder blades down: the thrower wins **if its grip is above 0** (the hand still on the trousers), else it is gestellt. 0, or 6 s with the
  meter at 50 or under = the one underneath twists out (both up at balance 60, the thrower 10 grip down). 6 s with the thrower ahead pins.
- **Result**: win 10, gestellt (the clock ran out, or a pin without grip) 9 each, loss 8.5 (`SchwingRules.Score`). Batzen and party points: win 30 / 100,
  gestellt 20 / 70, loss 12 / 40, x1.5 in the Schlussgang (`Reward`). Three won bouts a day reach the game's daily cap (120).
- **`SchwingBot`**: `SchwingSkill` by `BotDifficulty` (answer chance 0.35 / 0.6 / 0.85, reaction 0.28 / 0.2 / 0.12 s, aggression, tug gap), `Answer`, `Attack`, `Wander`.

## The server: `SchwingManager` at `World/Schwing`

- **Off a festival**: `/schwing <player or bot>`, `/schwing bot` (the nearest free bot within 20 m), `accept`, `decline`, `leave` (an invite of kind `schwing`, so
  the toast and F2 work too); refused unless both are on foot, up and within 12 m. A bot accepts at once. Nothing is paid. E at a player stays the fist fight.
- **At the ring**: the Älplerfest's **sawdust ring** (`SchwingRing`, 14 m across, 20 m north of the site's middle) is open from the day the def's `schwingen`
  game opens (day 5). E at it (`RequestJoin`; pad Y; the VR pad route) joins the queue, E again leaves. Two humans in the queue wrestle; one alone for 8 s is
  given the nearest free bot within 400 m (`FreeBot`, the bot is set down beside the human). From festival day 9 a ring bout is a Schlussgang. A ring bout
  pays through `Award(peer, "aelplerfest", batzen, why, "schwingen", points)` once it is over, to each human: never to a bot, never for a bout under 20 s of play, never to a forfeiter.
- **Wire** (protocol 70, no other bump): client to server `RequestJoin`, `RequestLeave`, `Act(bout, lean, brace, throw)` (budget 20 a second, from a wrestler within 9 m of the
  bout's centre), `Tug(bout)`; server to the two wrestlers `Begin`, `State` (on change and at 10 Hz while live, reliable: the payload is 15 small ints; the plan said
  unreliable), `Thrown(bout, by, throw, answer)` (answer 0 = the telegraph starts), `Ended`, `TellQueue`. Refusals log `[schwing] #n refused ...`.
- **`ActFor` / `TugFor`** are the server methods the RPCs and a bot call alike (`bots/bot-capability-map`).
- A wrestler who disconnects, gets into anything or is 18 m from the centre forfeits.

## Bodies and poses

- The marks are 0.9 m apart on the line from A to B; each client sets its own body on its mark and faces the rival; the server sets a bot's.
- **`FootPlayer.SchwingPose`** (`[Export]`, OnChange; part of `InDuel`, so no items, emotes or interactions): the server works out each wrestler's pose
  (`SchwingMatch.PoseOf`) and sends it in `State`; a human's client writes it on its own body, the server on a bot's. Every peer draws it with the dance layer
  (`HumanMeshBuilder.SchwingMoves` = 6000 + pose; `DanceMove.Schwing*`): grip, four leans, brace, lying (flat: the body's down rotation), kneeling on top,
  bridging underneath, stumble, the winner's brush, the loser's dusting, and six throws whose first 35 % is the wind-up the rival answers. A throw plays from when
  the copy saw its pose change, over a second, so both peers show the same frame to within the network delay.
- No mesh is rebuilt per frame: the figure is posed by the dance layer on its skeleton, the ring is made once per site.
- Model viewer category **Schwingen** (every stance, every throw); sounds `SchwingSounds` (grunt, slap, thud, bell) are static banks, so the sound player lists them.

## Not built, and the plan's differences

- The Anschwingen is not a bracket: two in the queue wrestle, and every ring bout pays. The Schlussgang is the longer bout from day 9, not "the two best", and no
  Schwingerkönig title or bull's bell yet. Spectators see the poses and hear the sounds but get no HUD (the ring has no `Watch` feed).
- The Schwingerhosen (`ItemId.Schwingerhosen` = 500, Alpine set, Bottom slot) can be worn but are not required and not yet sold or found.
- Gripping the belt for real in VR, and a Schwing challenge on the Activities screen, are later.
