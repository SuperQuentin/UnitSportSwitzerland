# Data usage: what streaming costs, and the settings that cap it (#63)

- **Settings → Data** (every platform; `DataRows` in `SettingsScreen`):
  - **Data use:** Standard or Low data (`GameSettings.Data`, `--data low`).
  - **Tile cache size:** `CacheGb`, which sets `NetworkChunkSource.MaxCacheBytes` live. Defaults are 2 GB on PC and 0.5 GB on a phone (`UsePhoneDefaults`).
  - **Downloaded tiles:** the cache's size on disk and the data streamed this session, with **Clear** (`NetworkChunkSource.ClearCache`, never a `.part`; it re-measures the live cache through `NetworkChunkSource.Active`).
  - Phone only: **Low data with data saver** (`AutoLowData`) and **Ask on mobile data** (`WarnMetered`).
- **Low data:** `LodPolicy.FromSettings` takes the Low table capped at `GameSettings.LowDataRings` (5). Full tiles (about 2 MB on disk, more with buildings) are then only within ring 1 (roads to 2, buildings to 1), with 5 KB coarse companions out to ring 5 and the horizon past it. `GameSettings.LowDataActive` is Low data chosen, or the data saver on with auto.
- **Android:** `DataWatch` (under `GameShell`) reads `AndroidBridge.NetworkState` every 5 s: `ConnectivityManager.isActiveNetworkMetered` and `getRestrictBackgroundStatus() == 3` (data saver on). A change of data saver raises `GameSettings.NotifyChanged`, so the terrain re-reads its rings mid-session.
- **Metered warning:** `GameShell.Join` asks once per session: "Use Low data", "Join anyway" or "Never ask" (`Modal.Choose`), with `StreamEstimate`'s numbers.
- **Counter:** `ChunkStreamer.SessionBytes` is static across every server joined. The log prints `[stream] N MB received this session` every 10 MB, and the perf overlay shows it.

## Measured (Oct 2026)

- **Setup:** a fresh desktop client (empty `--chunks` and `--cache`) joins a server with the real terrain, at 15 rings, 60 km horizon, Medium detail, standing at the spawn, for 150 s.

  | | Before #63's yard fix | After |
  |---|---|---|
  | Standard | 480+ MB (capped by the server's 3 MB/s) | 300 MB |
  | Low data | 291 MB | 80 MB |

- **What the bytes were:**
  - Most were `DormantVehicles`' yard pass. It loaded every entering tile's `.bldg`, plus its 2 MB full grid whenever the tile had any building at all.
  - On a client the yards now come from `TileFurnished` (buildings built near the player, both files already in the RAM cache), and only for tiles where `SiteYards.HasSite` finds a site.
  - The server keeps the eager pass, because it has local data and must know every slot to wake it.
- **How to find who fetches a tile:** a stack trace at the top of `CachingChunkSource.Load*Async` (the synchronous part still has the caller). One in `NetworkChunkSource` only shows `MoveNext`, because the caller is gone after the first await.
