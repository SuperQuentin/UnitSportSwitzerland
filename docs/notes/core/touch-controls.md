# Touch controls: the phone's on-screen controls (#63, by mode since #1043)

- **What it is:** `Core/TouchControls` is a CanvasLayer (layer 13) that `ClientWorld` adds next to the prompt bar when `TouchControls.Wanted` (`Platform.IsMobile`, not headless). It plays a **virtual gamepad** on device id 8 (`XrPad` is 7): every control sends a pad button, a trigger or a stick, so every action works through the pad bindings `PlayerInput.RegisterActions` already has. Actions with no pad button (the map, the bag, hotbar slots, More entries) are sent as `InputEventAction`. **Touch adds no action**: a new action needs no touch binding, only a pad one, and shows up in More (or as a pill) by itself.
- **Design** (reviewed): https://claude.ai/artifact/HiCytzteG2xeFGEQynZDAF.

## Layout: `Core/TouchLayout` (pure, tier-0 tested)

- **The mode picks the controls** (`TouchLayout.For(mode, flags)`): 2 to 4 under the right thumb, in the same places for the whole mode. `ClientWorld.TouchNow()` gives the mode (`TouchState`): the same branches as `Prompts()`.
  - Foot: Jump, Crouch; Use (drag-through) and Aim with an item in hand; Run with the Run-button setting.
  - Spectator / creative flight: Up, Down; Walk in the spectator camera.
  - Drive: Gas and Brake pedals, Horn; Gear ± and Clutch with a hand-shifted box; Work for a machine with a work mode. Ride (bikes): Pedal, Brake, Tuck.
  - Snow: Jump, Brake, Schuss. Dig (excavator digging, loader / telehandler working) and Crane: a second, fixed stick (the pad's right stick). Fly / Hover: a rocker on the triggers (up RT, down LT, springing back; a plane's throttle lever holds itself), Fire when armed, flaps on an airliner, Boost on a UFO. Glide: one action (open the canopy, flap), Dive and Drop for the pigeon. Fight: Punch, Kick, Block, Jump. Aura: one big Next move. Passenger: no stick, only look and pills.
  - Top bar (all modes): II (Start), More, MAP, CHAT (an Enter key), CAM (R3); Get out (Y) in a vehicle; BAG when the hotbar is hidden.
- **Millimetres, not pixels:** sizes and offsets are mm from the corners (`TouchKey.Mm`), turned into canvas pixels from `DisplayServer.ScreenGetDpi()` and the viewport's final transform, inside `DisplayServer.GetDisplaySafeArea()`. A desktop `--mobile` run counts the window as a 68 mm tall phone. A tablet keeps the clusters in its corners.
- **Sizes:** primary 13 mm, others 8 to 12 mm, pedals 12 × 26 / 17 mm, top bar 7 × 6 mm, every control +2 mm of hit margin; the stick ring 12 mm radius. The old pad's buttons were about 5 mm.
- **The hotbar** on a phone: `TouchLayout.Hotbar` places it (7.5 mm cells, the bag's cell first), bottom centre but pushed off the buttons' side by 49 mm × size; `InventoryUi.PlaceForTouch` takes `TouchControls.HotbarPlace`. On a 16:9 phone it would otherwise run under Aim and Crouch. Aim and Run stand above its row.
- **Size setting** 0.75 to 1.2 (`TouchLayout.MaxScale`): past 1.2 a 62 mm tall 16:9 phone has no room for the hotbar beside the buttons.
- **`TouchLayoutTests`:** every mode and flag set on 16:9 to 22:9 phones (62 to 75 mm) and a 4:3 tablet, both hands, every size: no two controls overlap, all on screen, the hotbar clear of the buttons, nothing in the stick zone at size ≤ 1.

## The layout editor (#1048)

- **Two ways in, one `Core/TouchEditor`:** More › Edit layout edits the mode in play over the live game (the overlay feeds it its touches and draws nothing meanwhile); Settings › Controls › Touch layout (`Core/TouchLayoutScreen`, layer 45) edits any mode with a ◀ mode ▶ stepper (`TouchLayout.Fullest` flags) over a still backdrop, the game's overlay standing aside (`TouchLayoutScreen.IsOpen`). Its toolbar sits on the stick's side, where no mode has a control.
- **Edits:** drag to move, the corner handle to resize (0.6 to 1.6×), Hide (the control leaves the screen and is still in More, a "Show …" button brings it back), Reset (this mode's own and the shared overrides of its controls), Cancel, Done.
- **Overrides:** `GameSettings.TouchOverrides`, mm from the control's corner and a size factor (`TouchOverride`), keyed `Mode:id` (this mode only) or `*:id:Shape` (`TouchLayout.SharedKey`: every mode with that control, Jump on foot and in a fight, the top bar, the pedals). A mode's own beats the shared one. "Moves: all modes / this mode only" picks which is written. `TouchLayout.Apply` puts them on `For(mode, flags)`; `GameSettings.SaveTouchOverrides` writes that key alone.
- **Refused drops:** `TouchLayout.Conflict` lays out every mode and every `FlagSets` variant that has the control, on this screen: a drop covering another control (Jump on the Run button that only the Run-button setting shows), leaving the screen or running under the hotbar puts the control back and says why.
- **Labels fit their key** (`TouchControls.FitFont`): "Handbrake" in an 8 mm key.

## Pills, More, labels

- **Hints name these controls (#1054):** `InputDevice.Touch` (`core/key-hints`): "Accelerate with [Gas]", "Steer with the stick". The drive layout has a Handbrake key and the ride layout a Hop key (A), because the ride cards name them.

- **Labels:** a mode key takes the text of the first prompt bound to its control ("Hold to wind up a cast", "Brake: snowplough"); a text too wide for the key goes under it.
- **Pills** (cyan, right edge, above the keys, below the top bar): every prompt with no control of its own, the interact one (`interact_mount`) first, at most 4, then a second column. A pill keeps its slot while its prompt lasts and lingers 0.4 s after. It presses the action's first pad button, else its trigger, else the action. Some prompts have a home instead and never become pills (`TouchControls.Homed`): Inventory, Map, ToggleMode, Spectator, Quick menu, Help, Menu, "Travel", "Camera".
- **More** (`Core/TouchMore`): the `ControlsHelp.Groups` of the mode (and of the ride: Trucks and buses, Airliners...), plus Travel, Creative, Spectator camera, Quick menu, Emotes, Drop item, Journal, All controls. Rows already on screen, and stick axes, are left out; a row whose keys are one `{action}` (cruise) counts as that action. Long rows are cut at the colon. A tap sends the action (a 0.6 s hold for a "(hold" row); a wheel (emotes, quick wheel) opens and the next finger aims it and lets go. A long press **pins** the row to the mode's pills (`GameSettings.TouchPins`, "Mode:action", two a mode). The sheet sits under the top bar, over the buttons' half; the stick stays live beside it.
- **New prompt for it:** "Start engine" (engine_toggle) in a vehicle whose engine is off. The vehicle readout (`PlayerFeel`) moves to the top left on a phone and names no keys.

## Feel

- **Look:** any touch that is not a control, the hotbar or the stick zone is a look drag. It sends `InputEventMouseMotion { Device = TouchControls.Device }` scaled by `GameSettings.TouchLookSpeed`, so the camera turns 1:1 with the finger. Camera code tests motion with `PlayerInput.IsLookMotion`: the captured mouse or the overlay's drag, **never** the mouse Godot emulates from a touch (`InputEvent.DeviceIdEmulation`), which would turn the camera twice. Use, Fire, Aim and pills **drag through** to the look: aim while firing.
- **The look drag goes out in window pixels** (`GetFinalTransform().BasisXform`). Godot scales a parsed mouse event from window to canvas, as it does the touch. Sent in canvas pixels, it was divided by the UI scale: half speed on a phone.
- **Stick:** floats where the thumb lands in the lower 70 % of the left 42 % (mirrored left-handed). Past 1.15 of its ring it holds L3 (run, fly boost, `StickRuns`); the pad's sprint latch keeps the run until the stick is let go. **Run lock:** a chip 9 mm above the ring; let go there and the stick stays forward and running; the next touch in the zone takes it back.
- **Gas lock:** slide 5 mm off the top of a Lockable pedal and let go: it stays down until it or Brake is touched.
- **Tap to use** (setting): a look touch under 0.2 s and 2 mm taps RB with an item in hand, and there is no Use key.
- **Hotbar:** a tap sends `slot_N`; a 0.5 s press (a ring fills) selects it and sends `drop_item`. The BAG cell opens the inventory.
- **Inventory panel** (overlay hidden, Godot's touch-to-mouse): a press held 0.45 s still on a slot is the right click (`InventoryUi.TouchPanelMouse`); a stray tap or drag off the panel puts the carried stack back instead of dropping it.
- **When it shows:** while the pointer is held (`MouseCapture.Mode != Visible`) and no text field has focus. A real pad's button or a push past 0.5 hides it until the screen is touched again. Hiding releases every held control; showing again re-lays out (settings may have changed). Touches count as the pad for `LastDevice`.
- **Haptics:** a light tick on each press, and `PlayerInput.Rumble` on the phone's motor (`Input.VibrateHandheld`, the VIBRATE permission).
- **Settings → Controls** (mobile only): Touch look speed, Button size, Button opacity, Left-handed, Run by pushing the stick past its ring, Use items by tapping the world.

- **The pointer mode is `MouseCapture.Mode`, never `Input.MouseMode` (#1020).** On Android, Godot 4.7 drops every mouse-mode change once a finger has touched the screen (`DisplayServerAndroid._mouse_update_mode` returns early unless the last pointer was a mouse). `MouseCapture.Mode` remembers what the game asked for; `MouseModeDisciplineTests` fails on a raw `Input.MouseMode`. A phone captures as Confined, so the mouse Godot emulates from a touch never clicks "use item". `--touchcheck` cannot see this: desktop applies the mode.
