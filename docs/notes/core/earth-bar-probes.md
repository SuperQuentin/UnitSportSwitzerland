# G2 D's bar: the re-anchor, long-drive and long-flight probes (#1710, D0)

The flip to `--frame earth` (D2, plan `docs/plans/global-tiling-g2-d.md` section 5) is proven by rows that did not
exist before D0. All three run under `--frame earth` only, and judge re-anchors through one helper.

- **`Core/ReanchorWatch`**: what a re-anchor must not show. `Watch(name, node)` samples a node's Earth position (ECEF)
  every frame in game time (a time-warped flight, `--fixed-fps`); across a frame interval holding a re-anchor the
  middle sample is compared with the line through its neighbours, and fails past `JumpTolM` plus twice the same error's
  95th percentile elsewhere (the worst is a leg's turnaround: 55 cm on `straight`, which would hide a pop). `WatchGround(at)` asks the ground under a point again by its Earth position in the new
  frame (`GroundTolM`, 2 cm). `Shots` (windowed only): a 160 × 90 grey copy of every frame, the change across a
  re-anchor against its four neighbours (a spike over 3x and 2 grey levels is a pop). The viewport's texture is the
  frame drawn before this `_Process`: the re-anchor shows one frame later. `ProcessPriority` is `int.MaxValue` (after
  the shifter's `int.MinValue` and every mover).
- **`--reanchorcheck`** (`Core/ReanchorCheck`): a camera driven at 30 m/s then flown at 80 m/s 300 m up, back and
  forth along legs, four rigid boxes asleep by the start (the shifter's teleport) and a plain marker (a node the shift
  moves); boxes and marker must not move over the run, the camera and the ground must not jump. On a fixture course
  along its longest reach (`ClientWorld.ReanchorCheckFor`), `--originshift 400` for many re-anchors in a short run;
  `tools/reanchorcheck.sh` windowed on the D: region at Geneva. `--reanchorinject <m>` puts the camera and the marker
  that far aside at the 3rd re-anchor: 0.3 m fails three ways (marker moved, both tracks jumped 0.19 m), the proof
  the check sees a pop; never in a map row.
- **`--longdrivecheck`** (`Player/LongDriveCheck`): a `RaceRoute` reads roads only 4 tiles round where it is built,
  so the drive is chained legs: each toward a point `--legm` (3 km) on toward `--to` (LV95 notation; the leg target
  is `GlobalPos` maths), the next built 1.2 km before the current one ends, from just ahead of the car, and a new
  `AutoPilot` takes over on the move. Fails on a stall (60 s, less than 50 m won), a fall (1 m under the ground for
  0.5 s, or out of the car), a jump. No `--to`: one leg, the longer way along the road (the quick row on `hairpin`).
  `tools/longdrivecheck.sh`: Geneva → Nyon, the Geneva patch's north end (the D: region is patches, not a corridor).
- **`--longflightcheck`** (`Player/LongFlightCheck`): an A320 launched 3 km over the start (`DebugLaunch`), the light
  sim's autopilot (`--airliner sim`, set by the probe if not given) on its knobs: heading toward Brest every second
  (the chord's level part at the aircraft is the great circle's start), 10 km cruise, 1,500 m from 220 km out.
  `Engine.TimeScale` = `--flightwarp` (4) for the cruise, 1 on the way down. Every half real second: the ground
  under it drawn (`Globe.DrawnLevelAt`: the visible globe tile's level, `LeafLevelDrawn` for a leaf the earth world
  draws, -1 for a hole; 3 s fails), 8 far points at 70 % of the horizon (at most 150 km; 10 s fails), 300 m over the
  pyramid (`Net.PyramidGround`). At the end the destination's leaves must be drawn under it: the script passes
  `--leaves .../brest_cube` (the game opens one leaf dir), so Geneva is pyramid and fill only.
- **Runs**: quick rows on fixtures (`@realtime`: the leaves stream on threads); full rows on D: through
  `tools/lib/barprobe.sh` (skipped with RESULT ok when the data is missing, quiet user://, its log checked for
  `[frame] earth`); the long ones carry `@timeout=3700` (`tools/test.sh`: a row longer than `TEST_TIMEOUT`).
