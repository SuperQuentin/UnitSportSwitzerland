# Aircraft cockpits (#421): the A320, the military freighter, the AN-124

- **View**: the airliners are in the cockpit cycle of `cockpit` (V / R3: chase → cockpit with the body →
  without → chase; VR toggles the body). `FootPlayer.HasCockpit` includes `Airliner`;
  `UpdateFlightCamera` hands over to `UpdateCockpitCamera` at `AirlinerRig.EyeFrame` (the captain's
  `HumanMeshBuilder.DriverEye`, recline 0.2), same free look, seat and FOV settings; the look rests at
  −0.14 rad (`AirlinerCockpitPitch`). The HUD's airliner lines go in the cockpit (the screens show
  them) unless `CockpitHud`. `--ride a320|freighter|an124,secs,out.png` parks one for pictures.
- **`Avatar/AircraftCockpit`** (child of every `AirlinerRig`: owner, remote, parked), laid out by a
  `CockpitLayout` per type (authored numbers from `A320Layout`/`FreighterLayout`/`An124Layout` and the
  builders' `CockpitPanelHalf`). The builders keep only the consoles, pedestal, panel and glareshield
  boxes; every moving control, screen and lamp is the cockpit's:
  - controls (nodes, `NodeTurn` = the authored turn flipped about Y): the captain's sidestick (A320;
    the F/O's stays put, they are not coupled) or both yokes (column pitch ±0.12 rad, wheel ±1.1 rad),
    thrust levers (−8° idle .. +32° TOGA, −18° reverse, `CockpitInstruments.LeverAngle`), flap lever,
    speedbrake, gear lever, parking brake, tiller (on the ground), rudder pedals (±5 cm with the
    stick's roll), overhead toggles: battery, APU, seatbelts, landing lights, a master per engine;
  - the pilot (`HumanMeshBuilder.AppendPilot`): the outer hand on the stick / yoke horn, the inner on
    the levers, the balls of the feet on the pedals; a body mesh cached per quantised pose (stick in
    tenths, levers in eighths, reverse; 256 max), the head separate; `PilotShown` = someone in seat 0,
    `View` hides the head (Body) or everything (Bare) for the local pilot only;
  - lamps (material swapped on change): master warning (red, flashing) and caution (amber) each
    side, three gear lights (green down, red moving or broken), autopilot, seatbelt signs (A320 cabin
    every 4 m, freighter hold, AN-124 upper deck), a warm flood over the panel when powered.
- **Instruments** (`InstrumentCanvas`, a `Control` in a `SubViewport`): one atlas of 128 px cells,
  nearest filtering, unshaded quads with a UV window each. Glass (A320): PFD (attitude with a 5° ladder,
  speed tape 2 px/kt with the Vmo band, altitude tape, VS, heading tape, FMA = thrust detent or
  `SPEED HDG ALT`, red STALL/OVERSPEED/GEAR), ND (heading arc, track, a 2 km ground grid that turns and
  slides as the map), E/WD (N1 dials, EGT, FF, FOB, flaps, memos), SD wheel page, ISIS, an FCU strip on
  the glareshield. Steam (freighter, AN-124): ASI, ADI, altimeter (two hands and a drum), VSI, HSI in a
  basic T each side, four N1 dials, EGT bars, fuel, flaps. Pure arithmetic in tier 0
  (`CockpitInstruments`, `CockpitInstrumentsTests`): IAS from TAS (ISA), tape marks, needle angles, the
  warnings' rules, detents, fuel flow, EGT.
- **Cost rules** (`perf-no-per-frame-allocations`): the viewport renders only within 30 m of the
  active camera (checked 4×/s) and only when the `Readout` (a record struct of rounded ints) or the
  map cell changed, at most 20×/s (`UpdateMode.Once`); numbers' strings are cached

- **What every peer sees**: speed, height, attitude, heading, track and VS come from the drawn frame's
  motion (position deltas smoothed; heights over the sea through `AircraftCockpit.WorldAltitude`, the

  pose now also carries: W bit 20 power (battery or an engine), 21 autopilot, 22-23 the red warning
  (1 stall, 2 overspeed incl. flaps, 3 gear: low under 230 m, slow, sinking, gear not down; the owner's
  model decides), and over the stick in Z the fuel % and engines running (`StickSpan`, < 2^24).
  Cautions (fuel < 10 %, parking brake with the levers forward, an engine out in the air) are worked
