# The UFO: a flyable saucer with a tractor beam (#861)

## Rule

- **A vehicle `Flyer`** (`Player/Ufo.cs`, `RideKind.Ufo` = 201, appended; the next other mount is 202),
  in the R menu's Mounts tab (`Rideable.All`, greyed online for a non-admin) and in the model viewer
  under Aircraft (the Rides set). Left where you get out, as a `VehicleBody`. Its state is
  `Ufo.State`, a `UfoFlight.State` (like an airliner's `State`). Its motion is `Player/UfoFlight.cs`,
  pure maths linked into the tier-0 tests (`UfoFlightTests`). Keep it free of nodes.
- **Arcade hover, no aerodynamics.** There is no gravity and no stall in flight.
  - Letting go of the climb keys holds the height.
  - The stick wishes for a horizontal velocity in the heading frame, forward and sideways alike:
    cruise 30 m/s, boost 70 m/s, at 14 / 28 m/s² accel and 22 m/s² braking.
  - Climb is 10 m/s, 20 m/s with boost.
  - It turns on the spot to the look (`LookSteers`, 2.4 rad/s), as the helicopter does.
  - A lightly damped spring tilts it into its acceleration (ω 5.5, ζ 0.28, ≤ 0.38 rad), plus a steady
    lean with the speed. It wobbles past level when it stops.
  - It flares near the ground: it never sinks faster than 2 m/s + 0.9 m/s per metre of free height.
  - Under 4 m, slow and not climbing, it settles onto its legs. Otherwise a craft mounted on the
    ground hovered 2.7 m up for ever: `ApplyRide` lifts a grown capsule by the radius difference.
- **Landing.**
  - The legs come down under 7 m of free height (descending or slow) and fold above 11 m.
    They are always down for a touchdown.
  - Touching the floor under 6 m/s flat with nobody climbing is a landing. The pads grip and it can
    still turn on the spot.
  - The ramp opens by itself 0.8 s after landing and shuts on lift-off. While the ramp is down it
    climbs only slowly, with no horizontal speed.
  - **G / X (`car_door`)** is the hatch. On the ground it opens or shuts the ramp. In the air it puts
    the legs up or down (the choice is forgotten on landing).
- **Free height** (`Ufo.Below`, `FootPlayer.UfoStep`) is a ray down that counts **static** bodies
  only (`TractorBeams.GroundUnder`). What the beam holds, a car or a player under it, is nothing to
  land on. With every hit counted, the craft settled onto its own cargo and followed it down.
- **The body** is a capsule of radius 3 m and height 6 m, with its origin at the legs' feet, so it
  reaches 2.2 m under the belly even with the legs folded. Crash at 24 m/s.
  - Parked, its collision is the hull box only (`ParkedBox`, from 2.25 m up), so a person walks
    under the belly.
  - E gets in at the ramp's foot (`EntryPoint`). Getting out on the ground puts you at the ramp's
    foot (`Rideable.ExitSpot`, `FootPlayer.ClearStandAt`), else beside it (`FindExit`).
  - Two seats under the dome (`Seats`). The pilot is drawn (the `Hanging` pose, hands on overhead
    grips); a passenger goes through the passenger system.
- **Intents** (`new-feature-bots`): `FlightInput` has `Boost`, `Hatch`, `Beam` and `Lights`. The
  last three are edges, built from key events in `FootPlayer.HandleUfoInput` and consumed by the
  next step.
  - A script or a bot uses `FlyControls` plus the held switches `Ufo.SetBeam`, `SetLights`,
    `SetRamp(bool?)` and `SetLegs(bool?)`. A null value means automatic.
  - **Scripted heading**: `ViewYaw` is the absolute heading (Godot yaw, `-DegToRad(compass)`).
    A `FlightInput` with `ViewPitch` null (a script) and `ViewYaw` 0 keeps the current heading.
    `Effort` boosts for a script.
  - An empty saucer (`Piloted: false`, a parked `VehicleBody`) sinks gently onto its legs, beam off.

## The tractor beam (`Player/TractorBeams.cs`, `TractorBeamMath.cs`)

- **Shape.** A cone hanging **straight down** (world down, not the wobbling craft's) from the hatch
  (`Ufo.HatchOffset`, the axis at the belly).
  - 45 m long, 1.4 m across at the hatch, half angle 0.35 rad (about 17 m across at 40 m).
  - Things are drawn up at 3.2 m/s and in to the axis (1.2 /s, at most 4 m/s). They are held
    **4.5 m under the hatch**, bobbing, carried along with the craft's velocity.
  - Held there they are 2.3 m clear of the capsule. At 2.2 m a car inside the capsule shoved the
    craft up and the pull followed it to 2 km. At 3.3 m the car was a floor it landed on.
  - Let go (beam off, or out of the cone), things are lowered at 2.5 m/s to the static ground, then
    released.
- **Who moves what (server-authoritative like every moving body).**
  - Every peer reads the beams from what it already has: each pilot's replicated ride pose
    (`Ufo.BeamOn(Anim)`, via `PlayerSnapshot`, built once a tick in `TractorBeams.Active`).
  - The `TractorBeams` node (in `ClientWorld`, `ServerWorld` and `TestWorld`) moves each registered
    thing **only on the peer that simulates it** (`IBeamable.BeamMovable`, its multiplayer authority):
    - the server moves what it owns (placed and woken vehicles, server-owned items);
    - a client moves what it parked or dropped.
  - The thing publishes its place as usual (`NetPlace`), at the 0.05 s rate while held, so every
    other peer sees it rise.
  - No new RPC: the beam's state travels in the pose bits.
- **The seam for other features** (cows, #863): implement **`IBeamable`** (`BeamPosition`,
  `BeamMovable`, `BeamGrounded`, `BeamMove(velocity, dt)`, `BeamRelease()`). Call
  `TractorBeams.Register(this)` in `_EnterTree` and `TractorBeams.Unregister(this)` in `_ExitTree`.
  While held, `BeamMove` comes every physics step: move kinematically and ignore your own physics until
  `BeamRelease`. `TractorBeams.At(tree, point, except)` gives the beam a point is in, for anything
  that lifts itself, as a player does.
- **What it lifts.**
  - **Dropped items**: `DroppedItem.Beam.cs` makes them kinematic while held, then static.
  - **Vehicles standing in the world** (`VehicleBody.Beam.cs`): its `_PhysicsProcess` is held by a
    0.25 s lease, so a stopped service lets go by itself. Nothing longer than 12 m, nothing hitched,
    nothing in a hold. On the dedicated server it rests where it is let down.
  - **Dormant vehicles**: the pilot's own peer asks them awake with `DormantVehicles.Wake`, using a
    cylinder query four times a second. Once awake they are the server's, and the server lifts them.
  - **A person on foot standing still** (`FootPlayer.BeamPhysics`, run on that body's own peer, as a
    blast's push is):
    - lifted; walking frees them, and they are let down at 2.5 m/s, steering with the walk;
    - unhurt, with no collision with the lifting saucer meanwhile;
    - someone still within the cone when they stop walking is taken again.
  - **Not done**: pallets (`PalletService` pallets are not bodies; a "take, carry in the pose, drop"
    would be needed), props, and cows (#863).

## Look and sound

- **The model** is `Avatar/UfoMeshBuilder.cs`, posed by `UfoRig.Show(Look, dt)` from `Ufo.LookOf(Anim)`
  on every peer:
  - a 9.7 m lens hull (smooth lofts, so the cartoon cel light rolls over it), a glass dome;
  - 16 rim lamps chasing (shared unshaded materials per colour and level, swapped only on a level
    change), an underside glow ring and an `OmniLight3D` with the lights on;
  - three legs that swing in and telescope under the belly, a ramp hinged at the hatch, a hatch glow
    when open;
  - the beam: an additive unshaded shader cone (`UfoRig.BeamMaterial`, rings running up it, fading
    down its length, cut off by the ground's depth) and a `SpotLight3D`.
- **The mothership** is `MothershipRig` (`UfoMeshBuilder.Mothership()`, 121 m, a turning ring of
  lights, an optional docking-bay `Beam`). It is scenery and an event prop, not a mount.
- **Pose on the wire** (`Ufo.Pack`):
  - X: the hum;
  - Y: 1 − legs;
  - Z: 1 − ramp;
  - W: bits for the beam (1), the lights off (2) and landed (4).
  An all-zero pose is a parked saucer: legs down, ramp open, lamps on, quiet.
- **Parked state.** A parked one keeps whether the ramp is shut and the lamps are off in
  `VehicleState.Flags` (`PackFlags`).
- **Sound** (`Audio/UfoSounds`, static, so it is in the sound player) is played by the rig's own
  `AudioStreamPlayer3D`s on every peer:
  - a hum loop pitched 0.75 to 1.45 with the speed (quiet when parked: the hum follows the spool);
  - the beam's drone;
  - a servo loop while legs or ramp move;
  - a touchdown thud (`LegsBank`).

## How to check

- `tools/test.sh unit` (`UfoFlightTests`, `TractorBeamTests`).
- `<godot> --headless --path . -- --flycheck ufo --world flat`: ramp opens, lift-off, cruise 30, boost
  70, legs folded, stop and hold height, beam on/off, land with the ramp open. Windowed, with
  `,out.png`, it also writes `out_braking.png` and `out_beam.png`.
- `<godot> --headless --path . -- --beamcheck [out.png] --world flat`: a person, a car and an item are
  lifted and held, the person walks free, and all are let down unhurt.
- `--models,test_output/models --modelsonly UFO --style cartoon` (and `Mothership`), and `--sounds,check`.
- `tools/ufonetcheck.sh` (net tier: a fixture server and two clients). B sees the legs, ramp, hum,
  beam and lamps, and is lifted and let down. A sees B rise. A parks it and B sees it parked.
