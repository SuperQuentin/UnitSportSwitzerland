# `/movespeed`: an admin's movement speed factor (#1485)

`/movespeed [<factor> | normal] [player]`. Not `/speed`, which is the simulation's speed (`Core/SimClock`).

- **The factor** (`Player/MoveSpeedCommand`, pure, `MoveSpeedCommandTests`): any positive finite number, no upper
  cap; `5`, `0.5`, `x20`, `20x`, a comma as the decimal point; `1`, `normal`, `reset` or `off` is normal speed.
- **On the body** (`FootPlayer.MoveSpeed.cs`): `SpeedFactor` scales `WalkSpeed` / `RunSpeed` (so footsteps, bob,
  FOV and the airborne launch rule follow), the crawl while downed, a prop-carrying walk, the walk's acceleration
  (times the factor when above 1, so a high factor is reached at once), both swim strokes, the dive and the rise,
  free climbing and creative flight. Not vehicles, the equipment rides (skis, wingsuit, paraglider, parachute) or
  the spectator camera, which has its own wheel speed (2 m/s .. 100 km/s, held to the altitude, #1796: `creative-mode`).
- **Whose factor**: the local player reads the static `FootPlayer.OwnSpeedFactor` (kept off the body, so a respawn
  keeps it; none in a Battle Royale match, `Permissions.InMatch`); every other body reads its own field: a server-run
  bot's, or the server's record of a peer's. The record is what `StaminaWatch` reads: a body above x1 is not logged
  for an implausible climb.
- **Who may**: offline the player (`HandleLocal`), as `/spawn` and `/tp`. Online it is past `ChatManager`'s single
  admin check: anyone else gets "'/movespeed' is an admin command". `RefusedInMatch`: nobody playing a round
  (`Core/Rounds`). The completer offers it to admins only.
- **Online**: foot movement is the owner's, so the server sets its record and sends the factor to the owner
  (`SpeedFactorSet`); the other peers see the body move through the usual snapshots, and nothing on the server snaps
  it back (there are no movement checks). `ChatManager.SetMoveSpeed(peer, factor)` is the one server path: the
  command, a bot (`/movespeed 3 <bot name>`, `BotDirector.Named`), and the reset when an admin loses the rights
  (`OnAdminChanged`). Leaving a server resets the own factor (`Permissions.Reset`).
- **Checks**: `--chatcheck` (offline set, query, refusals, reset); `tools/movespeednetcheck.sh` (plain players
  refused, A at x8 and x1 as B sees A's copy, a bot by name).
