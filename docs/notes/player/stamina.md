# Stamina: one budget for the muscles (#1225)

`src/Player/Stamina.cs` (pure, `StaminaTests`), the meter on the body `FootPlayer.Stamina.cs`, the max
upgrades `src/Adventure/StaminaUpgrades.cs`. Plan: `docs/plans/adventure-stamina.md` 1-4.

## Rule

- **Spent by muscles only**: climbing 9 /s (x the stick's share, at least the 1 /s of holding; x1.3 on
  loose rock and scree), a climb-jump 18, sprinting in a fight 12 /s (`InCombat`: `InDuel`, or hurt by a
  player in the last 6 s), the sprint stroke and a sprinted dive 8 /s, a pigeon's flaps 5 /s (10 boosted).
  **Free**: sprinting out of a fight, gliders (wingsuit, parachute, paraglider), mantles, slides, wall
  jumps, kicking off a face (the overview's "Decisions taken").
- **Regen** after 1 s without a spend: 25 /s on the floor, 35 seated, 15 riding, 25 a pigeon walking or
  perched; nothing in the air, climbing or swimming. **Winded** at 0 until 25 % is back: no climb entry,
  no climb-jump, no sprint stroke, no flap, no sprint in a fight.
- **Max** = 100 + 10 x steps (0..10) + 25 while `Fed`. Food: energy bar +50, chocolate +30, hot chocolate
  `Warm` 240 s (climbing x0.75), fondue `Fed` 300 s (full at once). `ItemDef.Stamina/Buff/BuffSeconds`;
  food is refused only when neither health nor stamina would change; the toast says both.
- **When it counts** (`StaminaApplies` = `Stamina.Applies(...)`, one place): the Adventure layer on for
  the body (`AdventureOn`: the service exists and, on a client, not `Permissions.InMatch`; the server asks
  `AdventureService.InMatch` for its bots), not creative, the server not `--stamina off`
  (`Permissions.ServerStamina`, from `Arrival.Rules`), no `/stamina infinite`. Otherwise `Infinite`: every
  spend succeeds, the ring is hidden.
- **Owner only, never replicated**: ticked once a physics step (`TickStamina`, beside `TickHealth`).
- **Upgrades**: the `"stamina"` section (`StaminaProgress { Steps }`) and the reward kind `"stamina"`
  (`"+N"`, 1..10, clamped at 10; `"+0"` and garbage refused) registered on the authority by
  `StaminaUpgrades.Attach` (from `AdventureService._Ready`, which also calls `Provides("stamina")`).
  `Grant` checks `Active(peer)`, writes, touches, then raises `StaminaRaised`. The owner reads its steps
  from `SectionSync` (`StaminaUpgrades.MySteps`); a raise refills the ring.
- **`/stamina [infinite | on | full]`**: on this screen (`ChatManager` before sending), free offline, an
  admin's online (an honest client's rule).
- **The server watches** (`Net/StaminaWatch`, 10 Hz, replicated state only): a climb gaining more than
  `Stamina.PlausibleClimbM(max)` since standing logs `[stamina] implausible climb <name> <m>` once. No
  correction.
- **HUD**: `ui/stamina-ring` (and its VR wrist copy); the panting breath (`SfxSynth.PantBank`) when winded.

## How to check

`tools/test.sh unit` (`StaminaTests`), `--climbcheck` (the costs on a real face, empty on scree at the
predicted height, the reward raising the max), `tools/climbnetcheck.sh` (`--stamina off` arrives).
