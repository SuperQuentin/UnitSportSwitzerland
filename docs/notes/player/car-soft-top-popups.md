# Car soft top and pop-up headlights

- **Car soft top and pop-up headlights** (`Car.Headlights`/`RoofOpen`, `CarRig`, `CarMeshBuilder`; #48).
  **L / D-pad →** headlights on every car (pop-ups rise with them), **O / D-pad ←** soft top on every
  `BodyShape.Roadster` (NA6CE — the NA MX-5 —, NB8C, MR-S); the D-pad sides only mean items on foot.
  Toggled in `FootPlayer._UnhandledInput` like the engine, only when `_ride is Car` (a remote copy's
  `_ride` is null, so its copy of the handler does nothing). The rig eases them (top 2.2 s: glass winds
  down into the belt, then the cloth squashes back onto its hinge behind the seats; pods 0.6 s) and
  snaps on its first frame, so a car built with its top down does not fold in front of you.
  **Sync**: bit flags in the car's pose `W` (1 brake, 2 lights, 4 roof) — `Anim` is taken as-is,
  never blended, so small integers survive; no new synced property. **Parked**: `VehicleState`
  carries `lights`/`roof` in its spawn dictionary (missing keys read false) and the parked rig shows
  them; nobody can switch a parked car, so nothing more is replicated. GPX replay cars turn their
  lights on at dusk (`DayNight.Night > 0.35`). The Initial D cars are right-hand drive, so the open
  cockpit's wheel is on the right. Checks: `--avatars 3 out.png --cartops [--focus N] [--view deg]`
  (the four states side by side), `--synccheck` (car stage presses L and O on the NA6CE; fails if the
  mirror's switches ever differ on a fresh frame), `tools/switchcheck.sh` (server + two clients:
  driven and parked; needs terrain chunks, not yet run).

## Hydraulics bounce (#464)

`CarBody.Hydraulics` (only the XP90 Yaris so far): **O / D-pad ←** (the soft-top key, which does
nothing on a hard top) toggles `Car.Bouncing`. **Visual only**: `CarRig.ApplyHydraulics` hops the
body (`HopPeriod` 0.62 s, 0.32 m, nose and tail leading in turn, easing in and out over a hop);
the wheels and the physics never move. Each landing thuds (`SfxSynth.LandingBank`, pitched down),
and while it bounces the rig loops the clip at `CarRig.BounceClipRes`
(`res://assets/audio/yaris_bounce.ogg`) **if that file exists**: drop any clip there, no code change.
**Sync**: pose `W` bit 64 (`PoseBounce`, above the throttle's three bits), so every peer runs the
same hops and sound from its own copy. Not carried by `VehicleState`: a parked car stops bouncing.
Kept across a preset change (`SetCarSetup`).
