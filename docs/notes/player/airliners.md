# Airliners: heavy aircraft (#414; light sim #415, A320 cabin #416)

- **What**: `Player/Airliner.cs` (a `Flyer`) over the pure model `Player/AirlinerFlight.cs` with the
  numbers in `Player/AirlinerCatalog.cs` (A320 now, RideKind **125**; the AN-124 and the military
  freighter's numbers are there and tested, their kinds come with #419/#420). Drawn by
  `Avatar/A320MeshBuilder.cs` from `Avatar/A320Layout.cs` (one set of numbers for the model, the
  cabin's deck and the seats), moved by `Avatar/AirlinerRig.cs`. Admin picker tab **Aircraft**.
- **The model** (tier 0, `AirlinerTests`): a point mass with a polar per flap setting (lift at zero
  alpha, the stall's lift, the flaps' drag; values chosen so the book speeds come out), gear and
  spoiler drag, per-engine spool and thrust lapse, ISA density from the altitude (`FlightEnv.Altitude`).
  The nose **weathercocks** into the airflow, so the heading follows the bank by itself (no rudder).
  **The stick is the A320's normal law** on every type in Arcade and on fly-by-wire types in Sim: it
  commands a pitch rate and a roll rate; let go, the flight path is held (and the bank up to 33° in
  Sim; Arcade rolls wings level). Protections: alpha (and **alpha floor**: full thrust whatever the
  levers say), pitch +30/−15°, bank 67°, speed above VMO. Without them (#415, conventional types in
  Sim) a stalled wing drops the nose and a wing.
- **On the ground**: wheels with rolling friction, brakes with anti-skid (`BrakeMu` 0.38), the nose
  wheel steering with the tiller (75° at walking pace) blending to the pedals' 6° by 25 m/s, rotation
  about the main gear once the elevator has air (limited short of the tail strike with the
  protections), ground spoilers when rolling with the levers at idle, reversers when Ctrl is held at
  idle (they stop it, never back it up). Nobody at the controls: brakes set. The gear lever is locked
  down with weight on the wheels.
- **Touchdowns**: judged by the last airborne sink: above 3 m/s damage, 4.6 the gear gives way
  (belly slide, scraping damage), 7 a crash; 9° of bank costs a wingtip, 23° is a crash. Damage goes
  to the vehicle's health (`Airliner.TakeDamage`, `FootPlayer.FlyPhysics`).
- **Measured** (A320, 64 t, sea level, `AirlinerTests`): stall 146 kt clean / 103 kt FULL; take-off
  roll 1.11 km to 149 kt at TOGA with 1+F; 90 s later 800 m up; hands-off level 90 s within 8 m; a 3°
  approach at Vref touches down at 1.6 m/s and stops in 490 m on brakes and reversers. AN-124 rolls
  1.34 km to 144 kt, the freighter 0.66 km to 117 kt.
- **Keys** (`PlayerInput`, `FootPlayer.Airliner.cs`): Shift/Ctrl and RT/LT the thrust levers, the stick
  pitch and roll (steering on the ground), Space/A brakes, F7/F6 and RB/LB flaps, G/X the gear (in
  the air; on the ground it says it is locked), `/` and D-pad ← the speedbrake (0, ½, full), `.` the
  parking brake (keyboard only), L landing lights. Nav, beacon and strobes are automatic.
- **Handling setting**: `GameSettings.Airliner` (Settings -> Vehicles "Airliner handling", `--airliner arcade|sim`).
- **Collision**: it collides as its fuselage's constant section (`Airliner.HullBoxes`, the flyer
  exception in `FootPlayer.FitHull`), short of the tail cone so a rotation never drags it. The wings
  clip like the light plane's when flown; parked they are a box at the root's height (`ExtraBoxes`)
  and the fuselage box reaches the ground (`ParkedBox`).
- **Network**: `Anim` = (spool, lever, the stick quantised into one float, bits: flap lever, gear
  lever, gear broken, speedbrake, ground spoilers, reverse, lights, doors, parking brake). Remote
  copies travel the parts at the aircraft's own rates from the levers (`AirlinerRig.Show`). Parked:
  the same bits in `VehicleState.Flags` (`PackFlags`/`UnpackFlags`).
- **Sound**: `EngineProfile.Turbofan` (`EngineSynth.Jet`: fan tone 700 Hz–2.4 kHz with its second
  harmonic, a low rumble, broadband roar growing with the thrust), quieter from the cockpit.
- **HUD** (until the cockpit's screens, #421): kt, ft, fpm, N1, flaps, gear, speedbrake, brakes, STALL,
  OVERSPEED, TOO LOW GEAR.
- **Check**: `--flycheck a320 --world flat` (quick): a scripted pilot (`AirlinerCircuit`) on the real
  actions with analog strengths: take-off, gear up, climb to 600 m, a 180° turn, approach with the
  flaps and gear as the speed allows, flare, brakes and reversers to a stop; fails on a hard landing,
  damage or a crash. Runs at `Engine.TimeScale` 4.
- **Light sim** (#415, `--airliner sim`, all in `AirlinerFlight`, `AirlinerSimTests`): spawned cold and
  dark; **Z** runs the start: battery, APU 10 s, then each engine 35 s from its bleed air (A320: one
  running at 45 s, both at 80 s; thrust only from engines running), the APU off once all run; Z again
  shuts down. **Fuel**: 45 % at spawn, burnt per engine from its thrust (A320 TOGA 8.25 t/h), the mass
  falls with it, empty tanks flame the engines out; stopped on the ground with them off (not starting)
  it refuels at 150 kg/s. **Autopilot + autothrust** (Y): engaged on the heading, altitude (to 100 ft)
  and speed it flies; while on, the stick turns the selected heading (0.35 rad/s) and altitude (20 m/s),
  the levers the speed; a full stick for 1 s disconnects. Banks at most 25°, ~2000 fpm. **Trim**
  (Home/End, conventional types only): hand-flown they have no path law, the nose seeks the trimmed
  angle of attack (static stability, 0.7/s; the stick 2.5× the arcade rate), roll is neutral, they stall.
  **Limits**: flaps or gear past their speed take damage. The parked state keeps "engines running"
  (flags bit 18). HUD: APU %, START ENG n, ENGINES OFF, AP targets, FUEL, TRIM. Check:
  `--flycheck a320 --world flat --airliner sim` (the start, then 25 s on the autopilot: held within 16 m).

  `Driverless`: stood up from its seat it stays the player's aircraft and flies on hands off (the law
