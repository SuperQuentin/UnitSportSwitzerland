# Free climbing on desktop and pad (#1225)

`src/Player/FootPlayer.Climb.cs`, the rule in `Player/Climbable.cs` (pure, `ClimbableTests`). Plan:
`docs/plans/adventure-stamina.md` 5.

## Rule

- **On wherever the Adventure layer is, or in creative mode, never in a Battle Royale match**
  (`FootPlayer.ClimbingAllowed` = `Stamina.ClimbingAllowed(AdventureOn, Creative, Permissions.InMatch)`).
  Elsewhere (a race server, a fixture world without `--systems ...,adventure`) the movement is the old one:
  mantle, wall jump, `XrClimb`.
- **What can be climbed** (`Climbable.Face(body, normal.Y, cover)`): a face with normal Y in
  [-0.15, 0.6] (53° to a slight overhang; 0.6 is `XrClimb`'s too); terrain of any cover but `Glacier`,
  `Snowfield`, `Water` (unknown cover counts as rock); any `StaticBody3D` (walls, piers, dams, fixture
  blocks) unless it is in the group `FootPlayer.NoClimbGroup` (`no_climb`: placed objects, built pieces,
  gadgets, stations, pallets add themselves). Never a vehicle, a moving body, a player or a tree trunk
  (`ClassifyClimb`: a `TreeColliders` child; the terrain is a `ChunkNode`'s unnamed body).
- **Entering**: pushing the stick into a climbable face (`ClimbPushDot` 0.6), in the air at once (after
  the mantle had its chance), on the ground after 0.25 s and only where the face rises past `MantleMax`
  over the feet (a garden wall still needs Jump to mantle). Not winded, not sliding, not downed. An NPC or
  a bot needs `FootActions`' `Climb` (`BotIntent.Climb`). A let-go or a kick-off cannot re-grab for 0.6 s.
- **On the face** (`ClimbPhysics`, after the mantle, before the swim): one chest ray (1.25 m up) along
  `-normal` a step (`Core.RayQuery`); the body is held off the face by a spring, `StandOffFor(n)`: the
  capsule's foot 5 cm off the rock, so on a slab the chest is about a metre out (a fixed 0.37 m made the
  feet scrape and climbing run at half speed). Moved with `MoveAndSlide`. Up 1.2, sideways 1.4, down 1.6
  m/s, scaled by the stick; Jump = climb-jump (1.9 m in 0.4 s, 18 points), Back + Jump = kick off (4 out,
  3 up, free; the base-jump rule then opens the wingsuit on Jump with 12 m of air), Crouch = let go.
- **Top-out**: no face at the chest (or the face lies back past 0.6) = a lip: the mantle
  (`TryBeginMantle`) is tried reaching 0.45, 0.8 and 1.2 m beyond the body (`TopOut`): the 1 m height grid
  rounds a ledge's edge off into a slope, and the mantle's 0.45 m landed on it and refused. Until it
  succeeds the hips' ray holds the body and it climbs on.
- **Empty**: the hands slide 0.3 s (`ClimbSlipped`, a grunt), then a fall. Down onto standable floor ends
  the climb. Any ride ends it (`ApplyRide`). Items holstered (`ItemController.UsablePlayer`,
  `HeldItemVisual`, the phone), no footsteps, no wading.
- **The pose** (no new field): `PoseKind = PoseWallClimb` (8), `Anim = (speed along the face, limb phase,
  climb-jump 0..1, 0)`, `BodyPose = ClimbBodyPose(n, yaw)`: the figure turned to the face (first person
  keeps the node on the view) and leant about the chest by asin(n.Y). Drawn by `ApplyClimbFigure` with the
  skinned figure's bones (`avatar/wall-climb-pose`); a remote advances the phase from `Anim.X`.
  `IsClimbing` reads the pose on a remote. Protocol 51.
- **Controls hint**: the first climb of the session, 5 s (`ClimbHintDue`, `PlayerFeel`).
- **VR**: `XrClimb` keeps its grip-and-pull and its `PoseClimb` carrier pose (open question 7), and spends
  `FootPlayer.SpendXrClimb` (1 /s, 9 a metre) where the meter counts, its own 15 s elsewhere.
