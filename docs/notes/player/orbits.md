# Orbits: the air, gravity and the UFO in space (#1480, #1430 H5)

Plan: `docs/plans/ellipsoid-handover.md` section 5. The geodesy under it: `core/swiss-geodesy`.

## The air: `Core/Atmosphere`

- **The US Standard Atmosphere 1976**: seven layers of constant lapse rate in geopotential altitude to
  86 km, the standard's own table from 86 to 150 km (log-linear between rows), no air above 150 km.
  Temperature, pressure, density, `Sigma`. Altitudes are geometric metres above sea level.
  Plain C#, `AtmosphereTests` against the table (0 to 150 km, to 0.1 %).
- **Airliners and their instruments** (`AirlinerFlight.Density`, `CockpitInstruments.Sigma`) keep their
  troposphere formula **bit for bit below 11 km** (tested) and read the standard above. Their formula
  works on geometric altitude where the standard works on geopotential: 0.24 % thicker just above
  11 km than just below. The old one was 60x too thin at 40 km and stuck at 3.6·10⁻⁶ kg/m³ for ever
  above 42 km.

## Orbits: `Core/Orbit`

- **Doubles in an inertial frame (ECI)**: ECEF's axes at sim time 0. ECEF = ECI turned about Z by
  `Orbit.RotationAngle` = ω·t, t the shared sim time (`ClockSync.SimNow`), so every peer and every time
  scale agree. `DVec3`, `OrbitState(R, V)`, `Energy`.
- **Gravity** is the point mass GM/r² (`Orbit.Gravity`, `Planet.Gravity`). The ground game keeps 9.81.
  No J2, no moon, no sun.
- **Velocity Verlet** (`Orbit.Step`) under gravity plus a constant thrust over the step: symplectic,
  time-reversible (a negative step goes back). `OrbitTests`: a 400 km circular orbit closes after one
  period within 10 m at 60 Hz and at 8x speed over ten orbits; energy drift < 10⁻⁶ (10⁻¹⁴ seen).
- **ECEF ↔ ECI** adds or takes off ω × r (318 m/s eastward at Bern for a body standing still).
  `Circular` aims a circular orbit along a direction projected on the horizontal; `Apsides`, `Period`.

## The UFO in space (`Ufo.SpaceFly`, `UfoFlight.SpaceStep`)

The seam below is `Player/SpaceOrbit` since the shuttle (#1492, `player/shuttle`) flies it too: an
`ISpacecraft` has a `SpaceOrbit`, its body sets its `Place` before each step (`FootPlayer.SpaceStep`,
`VehicleBody.StepFlyer`), and `SpaceOrbit.Step` returns the body's velocity and the orbit's track.

- **Above the Space handover** (60 km climbing, out below 50 km: `Curvature.SpaceRegime`, on the
  craft's own altitude) the saucer's state is an `OrbitState`. Each physics step:
  - the body's place comes in as `Ufo.Place` (`SpacePlace`: frame, world point, altitude, sim time), set
    by `FootPlayer.UfoStep` and by `VehicleBody.StepFlyer` for an empty one on the server;
  - the orbit steps under GM/r², the pilot's thrust and the 1976 air's drag (against the ground-relative
    velocity, `BallisticCoefficient` 230 kg/m²: 11 m/s² at 7 km/s and 60 km);
  - the body is sent where the orbit says (`velocity = (target − here) / dt`), so **Jolt only has the
    contacts**: a body found more than `ContactSlack` (1 m) from where it was sent is taken as the truth
    and the orbit restarts from it (`Contacts` counts them).
  - **Compare in world space through the shift, never in ECEF**: the move from the flat ground frame to
    the first Space anchor is rigid and leaves a point 400 km up ~10 m off its true ECEF place (the LV95
    and WGS84 verticals differ by 2.7 cm per km of height). Compared in ECEF, that was a contact, and
    the orbit restarted at the wrong speed and decayed.
- **The space drive** reuses the hover's controls (no new key, pad button or VR way):
  stick = thrust along the heading frame (3 m/s²), climb / descend keys = along the up (12 m/s²: beats
  gravity's 9.6), boost = three g (30 m/s²) on both. Nothing held is a coast. The saucer still turns to
  the look; legs fold; it tilts into its thrust; the hum follows the thrust.
- **Back below 50 km** it is the arcade hover again. Falling out of orbit at kilometres a second, the
  hover's dampers brake in proportion past twice the boost speed (`UfoFlight.FastFrom`, `FastBrake`):
  2 km/s comes down to cruising in about ten seconds. Unchanged at any speed the hover reaches by itself.
- **The HUD** shows `ORBIT km/s, height, Pe, Ap` (over the ground under it) instead of speed and
  clearance (`PlayerFeel.AppendOrbit`).
- **`/orbit <km>`** (100 to 100,000 km): offline free, online an admin's: the server checks
  (`ChatManager`), then `OrbitForced` (protocol 92; named to sort after `NotifyKicked`, whose number old clients are kicked by) on the pilot's client, which runs
  `FootPlayer.PutInOrbit` (circular, over where it is, the way it heads). A bot's body runs it on the
  server.
