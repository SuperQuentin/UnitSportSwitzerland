# Home-trainer training: a GPX on the roads, the trainer's watts, its grade as SIM (#901)

- **What**: the phone's **Training** app (home tile). It does:
  - **Import GPX...** (the OS picker, `FileDialog.UseNativeDialog`; the last 8 kept as "Ride again");
  - the trainer: **Search** (a 12 s scan), a found device to connect it, **Reconnect** the
    remembered ones (by id, no scan), and **Easier** / **Harder** (the trainer difficulty, ±10%,
    50% by default, as Zwift's);
  - **Start training** / **Stop training**.
  Plan: `docs/plans/home-trainer-rides.md`.
- **Code** (`src/Trainer/`):
  - `TrainerLink` (node, the only code touching RideLink): the engine, a `QueuedDispatcher`
    drained in `_Process`, `Watts` / `Cadence` / `HeartRate` (stale after 3 s), `FeedGrade`,
    `Flat`, `user://trainer.json` (`TrainerPrefs`: difficulty, devices, recent GPX).
  - `TrainingSession` (node): load → snap → travel → mount → ride → finish.
  - `CourseProfile` / `GradeFeed` (pure, `CourseProfileTests`).
  - `TrainingProbe` (`--trainingcheck`).
- **Following**:
  - The GPX is snapped by `TrackMatcher`, so it carries the road-surface heights. When it will
    not snap, it is ridden as recorded. One `RoadNetwork` (the course's box plus 1 km) serves the
    matcher and the junctions.
  - What the pilot rides is the **way** (`WayPoint`s: LV95, height, road node or -1, course point
    or -1): the course, 3 km at a time (more when under 800 m are left), or roads off it.
  - The way becomes a `RaceRoute` **2.5 m wide**, ridden by `AutoPilot.For` (the lean pilot of
    races and NPCs). It steers, and brakes only for a bend; a brake from it cuts the pedals.
    At 6 m the racing line cut a junction's kerb into the corner.
  - **Run-up**: every way starts 8 m behind its first point. A race's speed profile is 0 at its
    first point (a standing start); a rider held to that crept for 18 s.
  - Progress is the nearest way point, searched forward from the last one, so a course that
    loops back never jumps ahead. `Along` is the course metre of the last course point ridden.
- **Junctions** (`JunctionChoice`, pure, `JunctionChoiceTests`; not the road generator's junction
  planner):
  - On `RoadNetwork`'s walking graph (`IWalkGraph`: a node per road vertex, heights kept).
  - A junction is a node with three roads or more within 2.5 m of the way, the next one within
    250 m.
  - Each road out of it has a bearing measured 15 m in (negative: left). The road the rider came
    by, a road doubling back (beyond ±160°), and roads within 12° of each other are dropped: a kerb's
    curve leaves tangentially, sharing the road's first nodes.
  - **Choose** (the steering, A/D, the stick; or the phone): the road one step left or right of the
    one the way takes, at the next junction. Asked before one is in sight, it waits for it.
  - Off the course, the way goes straight on at every junction (`Straightest`). Past 20 m from where
    it left, wherever it passes within 3 m of the course going its way (±45°), it becomes the course
    again.
  - **Rejoin** (PgUp): the shortest way along the roads (`RouteTo`, never straight back) from the
    road ahead to the course within 3 km after where it was left, arriving the course's way (±60°).
  - **U-turn** (PgDn): `PlaceAt` the rider stopped and turned round on the nearest road node, then
    the road back, straight on.
  - A dead end: the way ends there and the pilot stops; the HUD says to turn round.
- **Power**: with measured power arriving, `Bicycle.RiderWatts` is the reading and the throttle is
  held. Without, the keys pedal (throttle, brake, sprint) as on any ride. The session forces the
  Sim profile and restores it at the end.
- **Grade**:
  - The way's `CourseProfile.GradeAt` (the course's own heights on it, the road's off it), over a
    40 m window. Point to point, GPS height steps read a steady
    4% as −12..+20%.
  - Times the difficulty, clamped to ±25%, rounded to 0.1%.
  - Sent as `SetSimulationAsync` with the bike's Crr 0.005, at most once a second and only on a
    ≥ 0.2% change (some trainers queue writes: a write per frame is felt as the hill arriving
    late).
  - Flat again at a stop and at the finish.
- **ERG** (off by default, every ride starts in SIM; `\` or the phone's ERG button):
  - On at the 10 W step above the last ~3 s of power (`RecentWatts`), as `SetTargetPowerAsync`;
    `[` / `]` (or the phone's -10 W / +10 W) step it by 10 W, 50..1500 W.
  - No grade is sent meanwhile; off, the grade feed is reset, so the road goes back at once.
  - Not offered without a trainer or with one whose FTMS features have no power target
    (`ErgWhyNot`). A target the trainer clamps is shown and kept (`AppliedValue`).
  - The game still rides on the measured watts: a climb in ERG is slow, as in Zwift.
- **Start behind the phone**: Start calls `Teleporter.TeleportTo` while the app is open, so the
  trip runs behind its screen and the phone folds away on arrival. The body is then put on the
  start facing the course, then on the bike.
  - **Gotcha**: the phone's dark backdrop eases out only in its on-foot camera step. A mount
    within the 0.4 s fade left it over the riding camera for good, so `ApplyRide` drops it at
    once (`DropPhoneBackdrop`).
- **HUD** (`TrainingHud` canvas, layer 11, text set on change at 4 Hz):
  - the course;
  - watts, rpm, bpm (or "no trainer: the keys pedal");
  - speed, the road's grade and the trainer's;
  - km done / total, climbing left, time;
  - ERG and its target, else the trainer's grade;
  - off the course (and the Rejoin key), the turn asked for, the next junction's roads with the
    one taken in brackets, a dead end ahead.
  - At the end, a toast with the time and average power.
- **Build**: `tools/RideLink/Game.props` (imported by the csproj) sets the Windows TFM
  `net8.0-windows10.0.19041.0` and `TRAINER_BLE` when `GodotTargetPlatform` is windows.
  - It is not in the csproj because Godot's `--import` rewrote the csproj and dropped that
    `TargetFramework` (the `.csproj.old` it leaves behind is the sign).
  - Linux server and Android: no Bluetooth; the keys pedal, or `--trainer-sim`.
- **`--trainer-sim`**: RideLink's simulated rig (a trainer, a strap and a power meter) at 230 W,
  which is not the bike's own 180 W, so the check can tell the two apart.
- **Check**: `--trainingcheck --trainer-sim --world fixture --chunks fixture:hairpin` (quick).
  - A GPX of the fixture's first 600 m, the rig, the app opened on the phone, Start.
  - Finished in ~54 s at 230 W, on the rig's watts every sample, worst 3.4 m off the line
    through the hairpins at 63 km/h.
  - 7% down sent as −3.5%, and decoded the same by the rig's trainer.
  - Windowed it writes `test_output/training_1_app.png` and `training_2_ride_*.png`.
- **Check**: `--trainingcheck junctions --trainer-sim --world fixture --chunks fixture:junction --traffic 0`
  (quick). A course east along the main road past the T:
  - the T found 121 m ahead with its roads; left taken (the kerb's curve, -35°), 60 m up the side
    road, off the course;
  - turn round, Rejoin: 0.1 km back onto the course east of the T;
  - ERG on at 240 W, +10 W, off: the rig's trainer decodes each (`Mode`, `TargetWatts`);
  - finished, worst 1.6 m off the way.
  - `--traffic 0`: a car queued at the T stopped the bike dead (the ride does not ghost through
    traffic yet).
- **Not yet**: riding through traffic (a car in the way stops the bike), arrows drawn on the road,
  loading roads beyond the course's 1 km margin (off there, the way ends), the elevation profile,
  the FTP test's `PowerMeasured` (#903), ANT+ (no USB stick yet). RideLink's WinRT path has not met a
  real trainer.
- **Devices**:
  - turn at the next junction: the steering on all three (A/D, the left stick, the VR stick), or the
    app's Left / Right;
  - Rejoin PgUp, U-turn PgDn, ERG `\`, ±10 W `[` `]`: keys (every letter is taken). A pad and VR
    use the same buttons in the Training app while riding (no pad button is free on a bike).
  - The app is the phone's own navigation: keys and mouse, pad, VR poke.
- **Bots**: n/a, a client-side training of the local player.
