# Passengers (#158): riding along, driverless vehicles, taking the wheel

- **The model**: a vehicle being driven lives inside one `FootPlayer`, its **host**, which runs the
  full ride physics (`RidePhysics`, a truck's sections and all). Riders are other players with
  `RidingWith` = the host's peer id and `SeatIndex` (replicated, OnChange). The host's own
  `SeatIndex` is 0 at the wheel, above 0 when it sits in its own vehicle **driverless**. Seats are
  `Rideable.Seats` (`Avatar.SeatAnchor`, read once per kind from the drawn model, the server builds it
  headless): a car's two front buckets and two bench places (roadsters and midships two), a truck's
  driver and cab passenger, every bus seat (`heavy-cabin`), a motorbike's rider and pillion.
  Flyers, bicycles, skis, the horse: no seats.
- **Server** (`Vehicles/PassengerService` at `World/Passengers`): hands out seats (the free one nearest
  the asker; refused when too far, moving > 3 m/s, a bus with its doors shut, or full), keeps who
  rides with whom in boarding order, and moves the vehicle from host to host. `VehicleManager`'s
  per-peer vehicle count goes with it (`TransferDriving`), or the new host could not park it.
- **Getting in**: E beside someone's vehicle (`DrivenVehicleInReach`, nearer than any parked one) asks
  for a seat; not a bus, which is walked into and sat down in from the aisle (#162, `walk-aboard`). The first person into an empty vehicle still drives it (the old claim).
- **Riding**: the passenger's node is set to the host's position every frame on **every peer**, not
  from its own interpolated stream; its body is disabled; its figure (`SeatedFigure`: back on the
  seat, hands on the thighs, feet on the floor; astride on a pillion) is a **child of the host's
  rig** at `SeatedFigure.FrameOf`, so it moves with the body's pitch and kneel exactly. A
  passenger's copy is processed after its host's (`ProcessPriority` 10), or it trails a frame
  (measured 7–9 cm at 47 km/h on a third peer). Camera: from the seat's eye with a free look that
  stays where it is put, or behind the vehicle (V). No items in a seat. In a walkable vehicle E stands
  up into the aisle (`StandUp`) instead of getting out. HUD: the vehicle's speed, and
  "nobody at the wheel: F takes it".
- **The driver gets out (or bails) at any speed with people aboard**: not handed to a passenger, not
  parked. `HostLeaving` sends the state; the server makes the **first rider** the host, sat where it
  was (`TakeVehicle`, `SeatIndex` > 0), and the car rolls on with **no input**: no pedal, the wheel
  let go (`RidePhysics`), into the ditch or down the hill. The others are `Moved` to the new host.
  A motorbike cannot: its pillion is put off (`Eject`) and it is parked as before. A host that
  leaves the game: the same, from the server's copy (`VehicleStateOfCopy`).
- **Taking the wheel** (`take_wheel`: F / RB, the trick keys, which a passenger never uses): only
  when nobody holds it. The host itself sat driverless just moves over (`WheelTaken`); a rider asks
  the server, which asks the host to `GiveUp`: its state goes to the rider (`HandOver` →
  `TakeVehicle` seat 0) and the old host rides on in its seat. Nobody takes it automatically.
- **Out**: a passenger at ≤ 3 m/s, beside its seat (a bus: at its doors). With nobody left aboard,
  a vehicle is parked as it always was. A wreck throws everyone out.
- **Interest**: everyone aboard one vehicle sees everyone else aboard it (`InterestService.Together`,
  with the race rule).
- **Check**: `--passengernet a|b|c [pw]` on three clients of a loopback server (a with
  `--admin-password`; `--generated-world` without terrain data): a drives with b and c aboard and
  jumps out at 45 km/h; the car rolls on with b as host and slows; c takes the wheel from b, drives,
  stops and gets out; b, alone, takes it and parks. Every peer checks the seats, that each
  passenger is 0.000 m from its host, the figures on the rig and the driver drawn only while there
  is one. b saves `test_output/passenger_seat_view.png` and `passengers_outside.png`.
  `--avatars … --cockpit [--heavy N] --fill` puts somebody in every seat.
- **Limits**: a hand-over blinks the vehicle out for a round trip (the old host stops simulating
  before the new one starts). No seat changes but through the wheel. The first-person passenger has
  no mirrors. Planes and helicopters take no passengers.
