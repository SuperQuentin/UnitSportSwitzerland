# A region's border leaves are real only in part

- **A built region's border leaves hold NaN heights where its sources stop.** The cube build covers the leaves its
  LV95 tiles touch, so the tiles' edges cut the leaves (at the face's 28.5° twist). On B4's D: region, 434 of the
  462 border leaves (of 2,412) have NaN vertices, about half of each on average. The real/generated boundary
  therefore runs inside leaves, not along leaf edges.
- So anything that pairs "real" with "generated" (a blend, a fill, a mask) works per lattice cell, not per leaf. The
  fill blend (`Leaves/LeafBlend`, #1510 PR 3) takes the real ground as the 8-vertex cells (~9.5 m) whose every vertex
  has ground, in quadtree blocks (`RealLeaf`), and merges the fill into the rest of a part-real leaf. With 32-vertex
  cells (38 m) the border was a visible staircase, scalloped round every corner by the hand-over; at 8 it is the
  source's own straight edge.
- The flat world never met this: its real set was whole LV95 tiles.
- How to look: `BlendCheck --leaf-blend --out <dir>` writes a shaded relief of 3 × 3 leaves round border leaves, the
  fill tinted (`terrain/judge-terrain-blend-shaded-relief`).
