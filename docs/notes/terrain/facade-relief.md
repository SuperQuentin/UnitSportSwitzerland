# Facade relief: depth from the facade UVs (#683)

> This file was generated by Claude.

- **No geometry, no new data.** `shaders/body/building.gdshaderinc` reuses what `windows` already
  bakes: UV (metres along the wall, storey coordinate), UV2.x (whole storeys) and CUSTOM0 (the
  wall's horizontal tangent, storey height). Every style gets it (PS1, Cartoon, Realistic share the body).
- **Recessed windows:** the glass sits `window_recess` (0.22 m) behind the facade. `recess_trace`
  follows the view ray from the facade into the opening box (u along, v up, d in); the first face
  hit is the glass or a reveal (jamb, head, inner sill). The glass point feeds `room_behind` and
  `stained_glass`, so the room parallax starts at the glass, not the facade. Reveals take their
  own normal (`n`), so every style's sun shades them; they darken toward the glass and catch a lit
  room's spill at night. `recess_sun` shadows the glass and reveals from the head and jambs.
- **Around the opening:** a stone sill's front with its shadow below; on ~55% of houses (hash of
  the 24 m cell, like the festive lights; storey under 3.6 m, under 6 storeys) louvred shutters
  folded back, one paint a house, and ~15% of their dark windows shut (shutter at the glass).
- **Walls:** a string course at each floor slab with a shadow line under it, a darker plinth
  (`plinth_height`, 0.45 m) with a light chamfer.
- **Distance:** the recess depth scales with the window grid's `legible`; thin features fade by
  `fine_detail(size, px)` (gone under ~1 px), `px` = metres a pixel spans on the wall.
- **Not yet:** depth on roofs (tiles), quoins at corners, the sill's top face from above, doors.
- **Check:** `tools/style-shots.sh` views `house` and `chase` (`--style ps1|cartoon|real-`).
