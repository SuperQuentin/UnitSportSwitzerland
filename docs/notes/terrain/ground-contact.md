# Buildings meet the ground (#1417)

The preprocessor seats every solid rigidly on the median ground of its footprint and never flattens the
terrain, so on a slope a base floats or sinks and a door sill is one point against ground that moves.
Two game-side fixes and a probe; the terrain-side fixes are #1419 (below: outline-sampled base, building pads; then the
joint town ground solve, the smoothed road profile and the road blend's reach fade, `tools/town-ground`).

## The probe

`<godot> --headless --path . -- --groundcheck --chunks <terrain_chunks> --systems ui [--ground-tiles E,N;E,N]`
(`src/Terrain/GroundCheck.cs`, real tiles, in no tier; report in `test_output/ground/groundcheck.txt`, RESULT `ok`
when a tile was measured). Default tiles: Sion 2597,1119, Lausanne 2538,1152, Zermatt 2623,1095. For each ring stride
1/2/4/10 (the drawn ground is the stride-1 grid `Decimate`d, bit-identical to the mesh) it prints:

- walls: the base pieces' gap histogram (< 5 cm, 5 cm-1, 1-3, 3-10, > 10 m), pieces over a gap with no footing,
  gapped buildings with no footing at all, footings whose foot ends above the drawn ground (a slot), off-base bottoms;
- doors: sill against the ground 0.8 m in front at both jambs and the middle, buried over 10 cm, floating over 15 cm
  after the stoop, the three worst as `--at E,N`.

## The LOD finding

Rings 1 and 2 (stride 2, 4) load the stride-1 grid (`needsFullGrid` while stride < 10), so footings and door sills were
sized on ground the ring does not draw: up to 1.85 m of slot under a footing. The coarse ring loads the stride-10 grid, so
its footings matched, but `ComputeDoors` gets no grid there (sill = base + 0.8 m): on Lausanne 507 buried and 522 floating
of 1073 doors. Footings now follow the ring (`RingGround`, `Build(..., ringStride)`). Doors are unchanged by ring on
purpose (the door set, slots and count, must not depend on the ring): at stride 4 they are still off (Lausanne 120 buried,
191 floating), at stride 10 untouched. **Far-ring doors are the next terrain-side job.**

## Footings (`BuildingMeshBuilder.Footings.cs`)

- Every piece is footed on its own: the old rule (any piece over 3 m, no footings at all) is gone.
- Up to `FootingMax` (3 m) a footing, up to `PlinthMax` (12 m) a plinth wall (same quads, darker masonry,
  `Footing.Plinth`); a piece higher than 12 m is a data error and stays as it is.
- `BottomEdges`: a horizontal wall bottom off the base (wall above, none below, up to a storey over the base) is footed up
  to `OffBaseMax` (1.5 m, under walking clearance, so an upper storey over a passage stays open).
- A piece goes as deep as the deepest dip of the ground between its ends (three samples) plus `FootingBelow`.
- Collision uses the same pieces on the grid it is built with; built once per tile.

## Doors (`BuildingFootprint.ComputeDoors`, local `Settle`)

Ground is read `FrontReach` (0.8 m) in front at both jambs (half the width, at most 1.5 m), in the middle, and in the wall plane.

- Ground over 10 cm above the sill: the sill is raised to it if the rise is at most `MaxLandingRaise` (0.9 m) and the wall
  keeps `MinDoorHeight` under the eave; else the wall is penalised 3 and an extra door is dropped. The raise carries inside
  by itself: every entrance copies `DoorY` from the spot, and `DoorLink` frames the portal from it while the interior floor
  is `EntranceY` in its own space, so outside and inside meet at the new sill.
- Lowest front sample over 15 cm under the sill: a stoop of `ceil(drop / 0.3)` steps, at most `MaxStoopSteps` (3), each at
  most `MaxStoopRise` (0.3 m) (`DoorSpot.Steps/Rise`); a drop over `MaxStoopDrop` (0.9 m) penalises the wall 4.
- Vehicle doors (barn, garage, bays) are left alone. A null grid changes nothing, so the golden door baselines hold.
- Mesh: `AppendDoor` via `StoopBoxes`; collision: `StoopFaces` beside `LinkFaces`. Model viewer: `Terrain > Ground contact`.

## The road-to-facade strip (#1419, `GroundStrip.cs`)

`--groundcheck` also prints, per tile and ring stride, `strip stride S`: the lattice cells between a road ribbon's edge and a
facade, with a slope histogram (< 15, 15-30, 30-45, 45-52, > 52 degrees) and the lattice edges that step over 0.3 m. A cell is in the
strip when it is outside every at-grade ribbon (`RoadEmbankment.EdgeOffset`, sidewalk and verge included; no rail, ford or stairs)
and every footprint, and its distance to the nearest ribbon edge plus to the nearest facade is at most 25 m. The ground is the one
players stand on: the road blend at clearance 0 over the stride-1 grid, then every S-th vertex. Pure code in `GroundStrip.cs`
(unit tests `GroundStripTests`). The Sion/Lausanne/Zermatt tiles are rebuilt into a scratch dir with the preprocessor
(`terrain` pass from the `.xyz.zip`s of the tile and its 8 neighbours, then `--features-only --tlm --route-keys --buildings --gwr`).
