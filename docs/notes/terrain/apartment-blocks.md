# Apartment blocks: stairwells, elevators, flats, a shared basement (#557)

Before, a block of flats was planned as one big house round a single stair core. Now
`InteriorGenerator.Apartments.cs` plans it the way a Swiss *Mehrfamilienhaus* is built.

- **Who** (`ApartmentTypeFor`): every `BuildingKind.Apartment`; an `Other` building over three
  storeys or 200 m² (the old plans already made those flats); 60 % of `Commercial` blocks of three
  storeys or more, by a roll of the key, as **shops under flats** (`BuildingType.MixedUse`). Never a
  bank. `BuildingType.Apartments` / `MixedUse` (9, 10) mark the plan; a footprint the planner cannot
  fit (too narrow, too shallow for a stair) falls back to the old cored plan.
- **A stairwell per front door.** Every door on the front wall (slot 0 always, extras if far enough
  apart: the stairwell width plus two thin flats, more for mixed blocks) gets one: a core room
  (lobby on the ground floor, landing above, the cored plan's own switchback stair and walkway,
  `Stair`), and from three levels up (basement counted) an elevator column beside it: lobby
  in front, cabin (`RoomType.Elevator`) facing the lobby, back landing. Room indices of a
  stairwell are the same on every floor. Wells slide flush to an end when the sliver beside them
  is too thin for a flat; a one-stairwell block that would get two slivers puts the stair at one
  end, one flat a floor (a three-family house). Doors on other walls open into whatever room
  takes them, circulation first, never a bathroom or a bedroom (`AptEntrances`, `StreetRank`).
- **Regions and flats.** Round each stairwell, regions touching circulation along one wall (`Way`s:
  the landing's stretch of wall away from the stair runs). A side wider than 13.5 m gets a corridor
  off the back landing (`CorridorSide`), flats front and back of it; a block more than 12 m deeper
  than its stairwell gets a corridor from the back landing to the back facade (`DeepBack`), flats
  both sides. Each region is cut along its way into flats of the block's own size
  (55–115 m², `|flatsize` roll); a piece under 22 m² is a box room off the landing.
- **A flat from its front door** (`FlatRooms`, by shape and by which walls are facades):
  *T* (as wide as deep: entrance, then bathroom/WC/box room against the landing wall, a hall across,
  living room + kitchen at a facade end, bedrooms), *spine* (deep: a hall straight in, rooms either
  side, living room and kitchen across the far facade), *gallery* (shallow, or facades only at the
  two ends: a hall along the door wall, living room at one facade, a bedroom at the other, wet rooms
  in the dark middle), *studio* (under 38 m²), *linear* (under 4.4 m wide). Every flat has a bed, a
  kitchen and a bathroom. `ConnectFlat` grows the doorways from the hall; a hall opens into the
  next with no door, the kitchen half the time into the living room (open kitchen). `Strip` gives
  every room its minimum first and shares what is left by weight; when even the minimums do not
  fit, the item easiest to lose goes (`FlatItem.Keep`).
- **Every floor of flats is the same plan**: one seed (`|flats`) for all of them, as the plumbing
  of a real block stacks. A mixed block's ground floor is shops instead: the sales floor at the
  street, the stockroom and WC behind, a door onto the lobby; a street door that lands in it is
  its own.
- **The basement is shared**: a corridor joins every stairwell's back landing across the whole
  width (the stairwells meet there and in the car park). A strip behind the stairwells at least
  9.5 m deep is the **car park** (`CarPark`: a row of 2.5 × 5 m bays across from its doors, two
  if deep enough, painted out, a pillar every three bays, cars in 70 %, bike racks); otherwise the
  biggest region that takes a row and an aisle, in a block of 300 m² or more. The rest, cut along
  the corridor: a laundry per stairwell (two machines, two dryers), the shelter, the boiler room
  (`TechRoom`), and storage compartments (`Cellar` with `StorageCage`s, wooden slatted cages that
  hold loot). Driving in is #558.
- **Format**: `FloorPlan.Flights` (several stairs a floor; `AllFlights()` everywhere a flight was
  read), `LiftPlan` (cabin rectangle, door side, floors served, the call button and panel
  positions), `InnerDoorPlan` (a flat's front door: floor, hall room, side, centre, `Locked`).
  New rooms `Elevator CarPark TechRoom Corridor`, pieces `Pillar StorageCage Mailboxes BikeRack`.
  Plan version 17 (#501 took 16).
- **Validator**: reachability now runs from every street doorway through doorways, up and down
  every flight and along every elevator, over the whole building (a block's upper floors are
  stairwells that never meet); a hole must cover its flight; each elevator has a cabin with a
  doorway on every floor it serves; each inner door hangs in a doorway.

## The elevator (`InteriorManager.Lifts.cs`, `LiftRide`)

- **State** is a `LiftRide(From, To, Start)` per (plan, lift), server-owned; idle is a ride from a
  floor to itself, doors open there. From it and the server clock (`ClockSync.ServerNow`) every
  peer derives the doors on each floor (close 0.9 s, travel 1.4 + 0.6 s a floor, open 0.9 s) and
  where the cabin is. Pure, unit-tested (`LiftRideTests`).
- **A ride is a teleport.** On arrival the server (`TickLifts`) takes everyone it sees in the cabin
  on the floor it left (`PlayerBody`, the relayed transform) and sends each `CarryLift`; the client
  moves itself straight up or down (`FootPlayer.RideLift`: same spot, at rest). So a group rides
  together, and whoever stepped out while the doors were closing does not. Remote players see the
  body jump with the relay, which inside a building nobody notices.
- **Calls**: `RequestLift(key, lift, floor)` from the landing's call button or from the cabin; the
  server checks the sender is in the building and at that elevator, and ignores a call while a ride
  is on or the cabin is already there. State goes to the peers in the building (`SetLift`), and a
  client entering asks (`RequestLifts`).
- **Doors** (`InteriorNode.Inside.cs`): two steel panels a floor, in the wall between the cabin's
  face and the landing's, sliding apart; a box collider while shut so nobody walks into a shaft
  whose cabin is elsewhere. The frame, the call button, the floor indicator, the cabin's panel and
  its handrail are baked (`InteriorMeshBuilder.LiftDressing`). A chime as the cabin arrives.

## Flats' front doors (`InteriorManager.InnerDoors.cs`)

- A `DoorLeaf` (the street door's) in each flat's doorway, swinging into the hall, solid while shut.
  Open/shut is server state (`RequestInnerDoor` → `SetInnerDoor` to the building), asked for on
  entering (`RequestInnerDoors`).
- 40 % are **locked** (`InnerDoorPlan.Locked`, `InteriorGenerator.LockedShare`). E opens the lockers' dial
  (`LootService.PickOther`, a third mode beside containers and BR crates); the combination is
  `InnerDoorLock.Combination(building, door)`, the server checks it (`RequestInnerPick`), unlocks
  it for everyone and swings it open. Unlocked doors are kept in memory: a server restart locks
  them again.

## Controls (every device)

| What | Keyboard | Pad | VR |
|---|---|---|---|
| Call the elevator (at its call button) | E | Y | Y, or grip the call button |
| Choose a floor (in the cabin) | E, then arrows / Enter / mouse | Y, then D-pad / A | Y or grip the panel, then point and pull the trigger at the list |
| Open / shut a flat's door | E | Y | Y, or grip the door |
| Pick a flat door's lock | E, then the dial (A/D, mouse) | Y, then the stick | Y or grip, then the stick |

Esc / B / E again closes the floor list. Prompts through `InputHints`.
