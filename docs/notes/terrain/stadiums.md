# Stadiums: a catalog row each, one builder for all (#885)

`src/Terrain/Stadiums/`: `StadiumSpec` (a row), `StadiumCatalog` (the rows), `StadiumBuilder` (the
sweep), `StadiumCheck` (`--stadiumcheck`). Plan and later phases (stage, lights, effects):
`docs/plans/stadium-concert.md`.

## Why a catalog, not detection

- The data cannot give a bowl. swissTLM3D has the pitch (`Sportplatz` in `tlm_bauten_sportbaute_ply`,
  our `CoverClass.SportsField`) but no stand or roof; swissBUILDINGS3D draws stadiums anywhere from one
  filled solid (Wankdorf: 246 x 190 m, 38 m, kind Annex) to **nothing at all**: Stade de Genève is an
  "Offenes Gebaeude" in the TLM name index, and our tiles have no solid at its point. A sports field
  alone is no stadium either: every village has one.
- So, like the IKEA stores (`ikea`), a stadium is named by position: a row with its centre spot, and
  the tile holding that spot builds the whole bowl. A pure function of the tile id, the same on server
  and clients, nothing sent.

## Adding a row (what Geneva's took)

1. **Centre spot and axis**: the centroid and principal axis of the pitch's `SportsField` cells in its
   `cover_E_N.cover` (raw deflate after a 20-byte header, 1001 x 1001, x east, z south from the NW
   corner). Geneva: 7070 cells, 104.4 x 67.8 m, 1.35 degrees west of north.
2. **Outline and depths**: the swissimage aerial,
   `https://wms.geo.admin.ch/?SERVICE=WMS&VERSION=1.3.0&REQUEST=GetMap&LAYERS=ch.swisstopo.swissimage&STYLES=&CRS=EPSG:2056&BBOX=<E0>,<N0>,<E1>,<N1>&WIDTH=960&HEIGHT=960&FORMAT=image/jpeg`
   at 3 px a metre: the seating front (the grass edge), the roof's outline and opening; each side's
   depth is the roof's outer edge from the seating front.
3. **Heights, tiers, capacity**: published figures (data sheets, Wikipedia, StadiumDB) and photos; tune
   the rows until `--stadiumcheck`'s seat count is within 12 % of the real capacity.
4. Find the name: the geo.admin search API
   (`api3.geo.admin.ch/rest/services/api/SearchServer?searchText=Stadion&type=locations&sr=2056`).
5. Data buildings in the bowl are clipped by `StadiumClip` as their tile is decoded (`LocalChunkSource`,
   `NetworkChunkSource`): every triangle whose centre is inside the facade outline goes, so mesh,
   collision, doors, perches and interiors all see the clipped tile, on the server and every client.
   Indices never change (a building wholly inside stays in the list with no triangles). At Geneva a
   corner of the La Praille mall (#132 of `buildings_2498_1115`) stood in the north-west concourse.
6. Add the row to `StadiumCheck.Rows` with the outline and capacity it is held to.

## How the builder works

- The seating front is a rounded rectangle; stations go round it (seat blocks of about 14 m with a
  1.2 m aisle between, a gate in the middle of each side in `Gates`, 6 arcs a corner). Every tier, the
  back wall, the roof and the facade are swept out along the stations' normals, so the bowl closes
  round its corners by construction. Each side's facade depth blends round the corners by the
  normal's squares.
- A row is one tread (garnet, alternate rows a shade darker) and one riser (darker) per segment; no
  seat is modelled. The collision is one ramp per tier and segment on the nosings (30.7 degrees in
  Geneva, under the 52 degree floor angle), plus walkways, walls, back walls, facade, roof top.
- A gate drops the rows under its headroom: a tunnel under the stand from the facade's opening to the
  pitch, roofed at `GateHeight` above the higher of the pitch and the ground at its mouth (the street
  round Geneva stands 1-3 m over the pitch, which buried the first gates). Each tier has a concrete
  soffit a riser under its ramp, so the concourse sees concrete, not the rows' backs. The space under the stands is open: the concourse.
- One prop-material mesh (`StyleKit` `Prop`, vertex colours raw linear), joined to the tile's
  building-site mesh in `ChunkManager` and drawn with the buildings layer (ring 3: no far LOD yet); the
  collision joins the site faces in the both-sided road cells. Geneva: 12.4 k triangles, 2 k
  collision faces.

## Checking

- `--stadiumcheck --systems ui` (quick tier): outline against the aerial, seats against the capacity,
  triangle budget, steepest walkable slope, the north gate clear and roofed, the owning tile.
- Model viewer: `--models --modelsonly stadium`. In the world:
  `--chunks <main>/terrain_chunks --at 2498751,1114890 --origin 2498751,1114890 --shot-queue <file>`
  (world 0,0 is then the centre spot, x east, z south).
