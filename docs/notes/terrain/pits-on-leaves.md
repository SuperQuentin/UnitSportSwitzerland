# Building-site pits on the earth terrain (#1653, G2 C7)

The flat world's pits (`construction-sites`, #619) cut each LV95 tile's ground. Under `--frame earth`, each leaf
cuts its own sites' pits, in its drawn mesh and in its collision patches alike.

- **Where they come from**: `LeafStand.Make` plans the sites on the leaf's stand-in tile (`SitePlans.For`,
  `SiteShellBuilder.Build`) and keeps `Pits`, in the tile's frame. `PitsInAnchor()` moves them into the leaf's anchor
  frame, where its lattice is (a shift by `LeafStandIn.Offset`).
- **Any mesh** (`PitTerrain.CutMesh`): the flat `Cut` works on a square lattice of `quad` m (a row skip, `Sampler`'s
  profile). A leaf's lattice is ~1.19 m and not square to its anchor frame. So the general cut looks at every triangle
  whose box meets a pit's box. The lining follows `MeshProfile`: the ground those triangles make, bending at every
  triangle edge the segment crosses. The walls' tops then meet the clipped ground's edge exactly. The flat `Cut` shares
  the same core (`CutCore`), byte for byte as before.
- **Drawn** (`EarthWorld.Mesh`): cut after the vertex colours and normals, before the pattern points are baked
  (`LeafPattern.Bake` runs on the cut vertices).
- **Collision** (`LeafCollision.PitFaces`): the lattice cells whose box meets a pit's leave the patch's grid. Their
  triangles, cut and lined (`PitTerrain.CutFaces`), go in as faces with the roads and buildings, sorted into patches by
  their middle. They face the way the drawn mesh does (front faces up, walls into the pit).
- **The shells' faces both ways round** (`LeafStand.BothWindings`): slabs, flights, scaffold lifts and footbridges
  collide in the flat world's road cells, which have `BackfaceCollision`. A leaf's patch is one-sided. Without the
  second winding, a body walked through a basement's flight.
- **`InPit`** (`EarthWorld.InPitAt`): within 0.5 m of a pit's rectangle on the leaf under the point, and no lower than
  1.5 m under its floor (in the anchor frame), as `ChunkManager.InPitAt`.
- **Trees** were already left out of the pits (`EarthWorld.Furnish`).
- **`--pitcheck` under earth** checks the sites the leaves plan (`PitCheck.FindOnLeaves`, through `EarthWorld.StandOf`),
  not the LV95 tiles' sites. On the `site` course they differ: the block's ramp comes up at the other end of its lane,
  and the shell's box is turned a quarter, a tie in the plan broken by 10⁻⁵ of a turn. Heights go through the site's frame
  (`Dug.Y`), because the world's Y is not the leaf's.
- **Open** (#1685): a pit that reaches past its leaf's edge is cut on its own leaf only. The neighbour leaf's ground
  covers that part of the pit. The flat world's `PitSeam` keeps pits off a tile's edge, but a stand-in tile's edge is
  ~200 m past its leaf's, so that rule never fires at a leaf edge.
- **Checks**: `PitTests` (a sheared lattice cut exactly round the pit, the walls' tops on the ground, nothing kept far
  from it); `--frame earth --pitcheck --chunks fixture:site --traffic 0` (the check-map row). Shots: `--pitcheck shots`,
  windowed, `test_output/pit/`.
