# Road railings (#126)

- **Kinds**, planned by `tools/RoadGen/Network/RailingPlanner.cs` right after the walls
  (`road-embankments-walls`) into the v3 `LPRP` layer, numbers in `tools/TerrainFormat/RoadRailing.cs`:
  - **On a fill wall's crown** (every generated one): posts in the crown's middle, face toward the
    road, foot = crown top. Guardrail beside Motorway..Minor, fence (railing, 1.0 m, two rails)
    beside a Lane or an Urban street. TLM walls (variant 1) get none: they are not drawn.
  - **Above a drop**: Motorway..Minor at grade, every 2 m per side, where the raw ground 5 m out
    from the edge lies more than 3 m under the road (a fill slope taller than a person) and no
    other line lies in between. Face 0.5 m out from the edge, foot = the road's height. A side
    with a wall (either kind) near its face line gets none from this rule.
  - **Median**: a one-way Motorway/Expressway's inner side (left of its traffic), where the first
    line found within 4 m of its edge is another one-way motorway or expressway carrying traffic
    the other way. #117's shift leaves 1.4 m between the paved edges. Each carriageway writes its
    own `MedianDouble` beam 0.2 m off the median's middle, so the two beams stand back to back
    with no pairing to agree on, and a carriageway whose partner diverges just stops.
- **Runs**: one-station gaps closed, a gap wherever another line comes under the rail (driveway,
  junction arm; junctions are already gaps, the segments stop at the junction polygon), < 12 m
  dropped, points simplified (plan within 5 cm, foot within 3 cm). Bridges keep their parapets
  and get nothing here.
- **Prop convention**: points = the rail's road-side face line, y = foot, 4th value = top above
  the foot (0.75 guardrail, 1.0 fence); the road on the RIGHT of the point order, posts on the
  left (`PostSetback` 0.12 m behind the face, stored as `Thickness`); `Param` = post spacing (4 m
  guardrail, 2.5 m fence).
- **Runtime** (`src/Terrain/RailingBuilder.cs`): guardrail = the W-beam band (0.31 m, folded 5 cm
  toward the road once) plus 0.1 m box posts; fence = a top and a middle rail plus 6 cm posts;
  posts sink 0.8 m below the foot to meet the slope. Drawn into the road mesh (one draw call),
  collision = one vertical strip along the face from 0.3 m under the foot to the top, in the
  two-sided bridge-deck body with the walls. `RaceLine.Widen` counts every linear prop (walls and
  railings) as a wall obstacle, so the racing line keeps off them.
- **6-tile test region** (Riddes + above Sion): 29 guardrails on walls (0.71 km), 21 above drops
  (0.72 km), 12 median beams (3.80 km: every inner-side station of the 1.9 km of at-grade A9
  found its partner), 4 fences (0.05 km); 301 points, 0.96 KB/tile, 17 ms/tile planning. Rebuild
  byte-identical. Mesh cost estimate: ~2 triangles per metre of guardrail for the posts (8 per
  post, 4 m) plus 4 per point for the beam: ~12k triangles over the 6 tiles, most on the A9 tiles.
