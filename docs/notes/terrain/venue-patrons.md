# Venue patrons (#1244, part 6 of #1232)

Guests at a venue's tables, stools, machines, counters, settees, bar games, dance floor and showing halls, and on its
terrace. Plan: `docs/plans/venue-patrons-ambience.md`. Decoration, like `BowlingDeco` (#1323): real bot bodies indoors
are #1330's.

- **Client-side, nothing on the wire.** `VenuePatrons` (`src/Interiors/Patrons/`) is a node in each built interior
  (`InteriorManager.Create`, beside the staff) and in each tile's terrace root (`VenueTerraces.Build`). Every peer draws
  the same guests from the plan and the shared clock; a peer may show a change a few seconds after another (below).
- **Spots** (`PatronSpots`, pure): read off the pieces as their meshes draw them (`InteriorMeshBuilder.Dining`/`Venues`/
  `Arcade`, `TerraceProps`): a `TableSet`'s and `BanquetteSet`'s chairs (facing pairs), a `HighTable`'s four stools,
  each `BarStool` (facing its bar), a `SeatingBank`'s seats (`InteriorGenerator.RowsOf`, as `CinemaSeats`), the player's
  place at each arcade machine (two at a `Racer`, an `AirHockey`, a `DanceMachine`), two at a `PoolTable` / `DartBoard`
  (the oche 2.37 m out) / `TableFootball`, a `Settee`'s back bench and wings, one dancer per 1.2 m cell of a
  `DanceFloor`, a queue of up to 5 at a counter (`OrderCounter`, `KebabCounter`, `ConcessionStand`, `BoxOffice`,
  `ShoeCounter`, `PastryCase`, `PrizeCounter`), two at a terrace's bistro set, six at a beer bench. Only in venue rooms
  (`VenueOf(RoomType)`): a flat's table has no guests. A standing place must be clear of other pieces (rugs and the
  dance floor aside) and inside its room, or it is left out (a queue stops at the first table in its way).
- **Schedule** (`PatronSchedule`, pure, `PatronScheduleTests`): each spot group's time is cut into windows as long as
  its stay (`WindowOf`, shared-clock seconds: a dinner 600, a coffee 360, an arcade game 120, a queue customer 40); a
  window is taken with the venue's `Busy(venue, hour)` at its start, for 65-100 % of it, by a party of 1-4 (mostly 2).
  Busy curves: Beiz/pizzeria 12:20 and 19:30, café 9:00 and 15:30, fast food noon, bar/pub from 16:00 to 02:00 (peak
  21:30), club 22:00-04:00 (peak 01:30), bowling and arcade afternoons and evenings, cinema 13:00-00:30. A spot is full
  about `Busy` of the time. The hour is the game's (`WorldClock.HourAt`); stays are real seconds (a 24-minute day would
  make a dinner a minute and a half). `--patronhour <h>` forces the hour (shots, checks).
- **Changes only out of view**: a spot's guest arrives or leaves when the camera cannot see it (outside the frustum or
  45 m off); on screen it waits. A player standing or sitting on a spot clears it at once. First built, everything is
  as the schedule says.
- **Cinema halls (owner's rule)**: a showing hall's audience (25-70 % of its seats, times the cinema's busy-ness) is
  drawn per 15-minute showing, but taken only while **no player is in the hall's room** (`InteriorNode.BeamBodies`); a
  hall with its screen off is empty, also only once nobody is in it. The screen state is `CinemaBooths.StateOf` (its
  default before anyone touches the panel).
- **Queues**: one customer served every `ServeOf` seconds (box office 22, kebab 45...); the rest step forward in view
  (eased), the served one steps aside 0.85 m and goes once out of view (or after 8 s); a newcomer joins the back only
  out of view.
- **Terraces**: `TerraceWeather`: nobody from 23:00 to 07:00, nobody while it snows (`SnowService.Today`), all of them
  from 14 °C at 500 m, none under 4 °C (`DayWeather.TempAt`). Lausanne on a cool day: about a third.
- **Drawing**: the nearest 32 are `SkinnedFigure`s posed at 15 Hz within 10 m, 5 Hz beyond (`PatronPoser`: seated
  `SeatedFigure.Joints`, standing `HumanMeshBuilder.PoseJoints(Standing)`, arms by a two-bone reach, head pitch and
  roll; dancers `PoseStride` with a `DanceParams` at 124 bpm). The rest are still copies: a `MultiMesh` per pose
  (chair, stool, standing) and look (8), baked once from a posed figure. A tile's terrace patrons sleep while the camera
  is over 120 m from all their spots.
- **Loops** (`PatronPoser`): fork to mouth and cutting (Dine), both hands to the mouth (Snack), sips and talking hands
  (Coffee, Drink), popcorn and a glance up (Watch), stick and buttons, flippers, wheel, drums, hoop, mallet (Arcade, by
  piece), the phone out (Queue), talk and a cheer (Settee), the bend, strokes and shot / the waiting cue (Pool), three
  darts and a pause / a sip (Darts), bent over the rods (TableFootball). `EventIn` gives a move's moment for its sound.
- **Frame time**: a full multiplex hall (4 halls, ~300 seats each), a full restaurant and a full club stayed at
  8-11 ms a frame on an M-series Mac, as an empty hall (9.3 ms).
- **Sound** (`VenuePatrons.Sound`, `Audio/VenueAmbience`): see `audio/venue-music`'s ambience line.
- **Getting there for a look**: `--enter <venue name | building key>` or `/enter` (`core/commands`).
- **Bots**: decoration only; a row in `bots/bot-capability-map` (World). #1330 makes real guests.
- **Not here**: guests walking in and out, food and glasses in hands, staff that serve, guests reacting to players,
  remote players' sitting spots on other clients beyond `BeamBodies`, the figures dimmed with a hall's house lights.
