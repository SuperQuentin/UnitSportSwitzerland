**Pinball detail** [S]: backbox 73 W x 73 H x 25 D; total height **193 cm** (widebody 63 W, WPC ~197 cm);
playfield angle **6.5 deg**; glass 109.3 x 53.3; lockdown bar ~90 cm, flipper buttons ~86 cm. Pitch
0.75-0.8 m side by side, 1.4 m deep plus 1.0 m player space.

## Layout

- **Rows**: uprights along walls, backs to the wall, screens out, cabinets side by side at ~0.7-0.8 m
  each; **back-to-back island rows** in the middle (two rows share a back, 1.5-1.8 m deep with stand
  space); racing twins along the longest wall; rows perpendicular to the main aisle [E].
- **Zones**: redemption/kids with a prize counter near the entrance (best staff sight line); video row;
  racing row; **pinball row** grouped; simulator/VR corner; dance pads in the open centre; claw machines
  at the window facing out; change machine in sight of the door [E].
- **Aisles**: planning guides 1.5-1.8 m main, 0.9-1.2 m between machines [S/E]; some municipal codes
  require 1.68 m (one side) / 2.29 m (both sides), and 1 machine per 3.7 m2 as a legal maximum [S].
- **Density**: a 140-185 m2 arcade holds **20-30 machines** (14-16 per 100 m2, ~6.5 m2 per machine with
  aisles) [S]; dense 18-20 per 100 m2; barcade with seating 8-10 per 100 m2 [E].
- **Where they are**: bowling concourses (8-30 machines: OZ Bowling Dielsdorf, La Praille), cinema lobbies
  (6-15 near the toilets), family entertainment centres, malls, motorway services [S/E]. JungfrauPark
  Interlaken Game Zone: retro cabinets (Sega Rally, Daytona USA, Wing War), pinball, air hockey, table
  football, VR arena [S].

## Swiss context

- Money-gambling "Spielsalons" are heavily regulated or banned in cities such as Zurich [S], so arcade
  halls are **rare**. What exists:
  - **Pinball bars and clubs**: Pinball Lounge Bar, Rheingasse, Basel (7 machines, mid-1980s to 2018);
    Flipperclub Regio Basel, Munchenstein (54 machines, Gottlieb/Williams/Bally); Extraball, Solothurn
    (~170 machines); a club in Schaffhausen; a private pinball museum in Zurich [S].
  - **Event arcades**: Maison25 Zurich "Arcade" nights (drink included); Pixel Arcade Room, Zurich, an
    80s team game with Pong/Pac-Man/Tetris [S]. Simulator venue: Racing Unleashed, Zurich Airport [S].
  - Elsewhere in Europe: Game Ninety World, Nivelles (~50 Japanese cabinets + pinballs); Memory Club Lyon
    (~20 arcade + 7 pinball) [S].
- **Generator rule**: spawn arcades mainly as (a) corners inside bowling/cinema/FEC, (b) **pinball bars**
  (6-30 pinball machines + bar + seating), (c) a rare mid-size retro hall near tourist spots. Never a
  gambling hall.

## Ambience [E, anecdotal sources]

- **Light**: dark room, ambient **20-80 lux**; screens and marquees give the light; neon tubes (pink,
  cyan, purple) as wall art; black ceiling with exposed duct, uplights; star/geometric carpet in dark
  blue, purple or teal with bright squiggles (blacklight-reactive in some); checkered floor in barcades.
- **Screens** loop attract mode: demo play, high scores, flashing "PRESS START", colour cycling.
- **Sound**: dense overlapping loops, ~80-90 dB hum: "INSERT COIN" voices and attract jingles, coin
  clatter, ticket-printer chatter, prize clunk, flipper thwack, pinball bells/chimes and multi-ball
  music, engine roar, gunshots, synthwave/80s track at moderate volume.
- **Time**: afternoons kids and brighter; evenings bar crowd with extra neon; close 22:00-01:00.

## Exterior

- **Storefront**: glazed shop front 4-8 m wide on a ground floor, dark glass door, black or deep-colour
  fascia, name in pixel font or round "bubble" lettering with neon trim (pink, cyan, yellow, green),
  chasing bulbs round the sign, window with cabinets stacked screens-out to the street, vertical blade
  sign [E].
- **Generic icons only** (no trademarks): joystick with red ball and button cluster, pixel invader-style
  alien, pixel heart, coin, pinball with spring lines, maze ghost-like character, pixel stars.
- **Barcade**: dark brick or black render, hanging neon "PINBALL" / "BAR", chalkboard, benches outside.
- **Mall / FEC**: open-front arcade with a glowing arch entrance and floor markings.

  1.2 x 2.2 x 0.8; foosball 1.4 x 0.75 x 0.9; skee-ball 0.6 x 3.2 x 2.0; DDR 1.5 x 1.1 x 2.1; punch
  0.9 x 1.5 x 2.0; photo booth 1.0 x 1.5 x 2.2.
- Row pitch 0.7 m, pinball pitch 0.8 m with a 1.0 m standing zone; aisles 1.5-1.8 m main, >= 1.0 m
  between rows; density 14-16 per 100 m2 (hall), 8-10 (pinball bar with seating).
- Venue types by place: bowling/cinema corner 4-30 machines; pinball bar 6-30 + bar; FEC and tourist hall
  20-30+.

## Caveats

dimensions are for modern bodies; classic EM cabinets are similar width, shorter backbox.
