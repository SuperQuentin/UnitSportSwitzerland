# Venues and shop units: their own street door and a glazed front (#1292)

Plan and the owner's decisions: `docs/plans/venue-own-door.md`. See-through glass is #1321.

- **Doors of their own** (`BuildingFootprint.OwnDoors`, `DoorSpot.Unit`, beyond the door budget, each its own slot):
  - a block of flats with shops on its ground floor (a mixed block, or flats holding a dining venue,
    `DiningShops`): the footprint asks the apartment planner where its units fall
    (`InteriorGenerator.StreetUnits`, as `PlansGarageRamp` does for the ramp) and lays a 1.2 m door in the
    middle of each unit's street wall; the venues take the unit doors nearest their points. A unit door
    builds no stairwell and makes no wing's front (`TryBlock`, `TryWings` skip it), so the plan with the
    doors is the plan the footprint asked about; it opens into its sales floor (`AptEntrances`, `UnitRank`).
  - anywhere else (a house, an office, a school, a shop building) every dining venue gets a door on the
    main door's wall unless its point is 4 m nearer another (`StreetPull`), slid a quarter metre at a time
    off the doors there (`SlideStep`). Even a building with one door: the planner puts the stair behind
    the main door, so the café beside it gets its own (owner, Q3).
  - not where the planner gives a venue no room: industrial sites, churches, landmarks, hall venues,
    sheds, garages, annexes.
  - the venue's sign, terrace, awning and music go with its door (`ComputeDoors` leaves the main door's
    `Venue` 0 when the venue has a door of its own).
- **Shop units**: a street shop wider than 14 m is cut into units of about 8 m (`Shop`, `SplitOver`,
  `UnitWidth`), the first off the lobby, each further one through a door off the one before.
- **The room behind** (`DiningUnits`): the room the venue's own doorway opens into is its guest room
  (`EntryRoom`); outside a block of flats it is walled off to the front's span (`WallToSpan`, `Carve`),
  what is left at either end (at least 2.2 m) keeping its use, behind a door from the guest room only if
  nothing else reaches it; never walled below 30 m² (`SpanKeep`). `Entrances` cuts a venue's doorway in a
  room a venue can take (`GuestCapable`) before any other.
- **The front** (`VenueFront`, `VenueFronts` per tile on `TileFurnished`, one frame mesh and one glass
  mesh a tile): `DoorSpot.Span` wide round the door (by kind outside a block of flats: café, kebab, fast
  food 5 bays, restaurant, pub, bar 7, club 9; a unit's width in one), cut short of every other door on
  the wall; 1.1 m bays, a stone plinth to the 0.4 m sill, glass to `DoorSpot.FrontTop` (2.6 m under the
  storey's ceiling), a painted fascia (`FasciaH`) the name stands on. Panes: `shaders/shop_glass.gdshader`,
  `room_behind` (now `shaders/common/room_behind.gdshaderinc`, `ROOM_SHOP`: no curtains, no fake
  mullions) as wide as the front, tinted to the venue (`Tint`), warm at night. Model viewer:
  `Interiors / Venue front`.
- **Windows inside at the bays** (`FrontWindows`, after the outline fit): the wall the doorway is in takes
  one window per bay, same place, sill and top. A doorway on a shaped room's angled edge keeps its windows.
- **Toilets** (owner, #1292): where space is short a washroom shrinks: a women's and a men's WC side by
  side (`ToiletSet.Pair`) before the single WC, and the big gendered set off a guest room only where the
  room keeps 6 m across (`GenderedKeep`). See `public-toilets`.
- **Plan version 40.**

## Checks

- `--diningcheck`: every venue by its own door into its guest room, its windows at the bays (a café in a
  school, a restaurant in an office block, a bar in a block of flats with one door among the cases).
- `--doorcheck`: unit doors counted apart from the budget, shop doors, fronts at least `MinSpan`; the
  baseline gained 238 unit doors, no door moved.
- `--venuecheck --chunks <dir> --venuedoors <name>`: prints each venue's own door, its front and the room
  it opens into. Trois Verres (Geneva): slot 5 on the school's west wall into its Gaststube.
- `--shopsurvey --chunks <dir>`: unit widths, venues by building kind, own doors (locked, into the wrong
  room, rooms narrower than their front), ms a plan, ms a tile's doors and the units' share. Measured
  (Oct 28, 17 tiles of Geneva and Lausanne): street units 9.8 → 8.0 m median (771 of them); 1,633 own doors
  (693 a venue's), 4 with no doorway inside, 61 into a room not theirs; 842 glazed, 114 rooms more than
  1 m narrower than their front (by 2.4 m median). A tile's doors 2.2 s on average on the worker, 0.55 s
  of it the blocks' units (`StreetUnits` plans up to the ground floor only, `GroundOnly`; a wing plan whole,
  as it is kept only if all of it validates).
- **Never locked**: `MoveEntries` (#1290) keeps a unit's own doorway as it keeps the main door's
  (`UnitDoors`): its room goes back to its rectangle rather than its door reading as locked (603 → 4).

## Known limits

- A room narrower than its front: the footprint sets the span before any plan exists, and a planner's
  room by the door can be narrower (a school's end room: Trois Verres' Gaststube is 3.8 m behind a 7.8 m
  front), so the glass also fronts the rooms beside it.
