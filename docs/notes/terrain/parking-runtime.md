# Car parks at runtime (#499)

- **Nothing new is streamed.** A laid-out lot rides in the tile's existing `.road` v3 layers, so a
  car park costs one more surface in the road mesh and no extra node or file. How it is *built* is
  in `parking-lots` (tools).
- **The pad** is `AreaPropType.ParkingPad`, drawn by `PavementBuilder` beside the #123 turn-lane
  strips it already drew, in its own `LotAsphalt` — a shade lighter and greyer than a carriageway,
  so a lot reads from the air as a lot and not as a very wide road. Flush, `Height` 0: its collision
  is the heightfield's, because the road blend holds the ground under it (`HoldUnderPavement`,
  extended to `ParkingPad`) exactly as under a ribbon.
- **The ground round the pad (#603)**: the hold pins only the lattice points *inside* the pad, so
  the terrain triangles across its edge rose to the raw ground one cell out and, in a lot dug into
  a slope, cut through the first row of bays as green wedges. `RimAroundPavement` gives every pad
  (and turn-lane `Pavement`) a road-like embankment from its outline: at most the pad's height out
  to one lattice diagonal, then the cut slope; the fill slope below; 3 m reach. `PadBlendTests`.
- **Planters** are `AreaPropType.ParkingIsland`, drawn and collided by `IslandBuilder` — the
  roundabout-island code, which already does a grassed kerbed bed with a rim face. Only a roundabout
  has the flush `Variant 1` mini form, so `mini` now tests the type as well. The blend holds the
  ground under one through `HoldUnderIsland`.
- **Walks** are ordinary `AreaPropType.Sidewalk` patches: `RoadStreetBuilder` and
  `StreetAreas.IsSolid` carry them unchanged from #119.
- **Bay lines, arrows and the disabled roundel** are `PNT2` paint on the pad, drawn by
  `RoadPaintBuilder` with no change at all: `PaintShape.Triangles` was already generic, so
  `PaintType.DisabledBay` needed only the colour it carries.
- **What stands in a lot** is `ParkingBuilder` (`src/Terrain/ParkingBuilder.cs`), from the `PPRP`
  point props: `TicketBarrier` (a housing with a 3 m boom in 50 cm red-and-white bands, `Variant 1`
  = up), `TicketKiosk`, `CartShelter` (four posts, a canopy and a back wall over one bay),
  `ParkingSign` (the blue 4.17 on a pole). Boxes in the PS1 house style, appended to the road mesh
  like the #121 signs. **The boom is deliberately not solid**: it is a stick at windscreen height
  that a car drives through today, and stopping a car with it means a moving body and an opening
  animation, which belongs with whatever eventually makes a barrier open.
- **The bay list** is the `PARK` section, read as `RoadTile.Parking`. It exists so anything that
  wants a parking bay can ask for one instead of guessing — which is what `AfricaTwinEgg` does by
  hand today (it hunts the cover raster for a parking cell, because no real bay existed). #499 does
  **not** change the egg. If it ever reads `PARK`, the bay must be an *extra* source and its
  "2.5 m bay on the far side of the lane" fallback has to stay: TLM maps no car park at Riddes
  house 15, which is the very case the egg was written for (`africa-twin-egg`, and the hand-traced
  rings in `cover_overrides.json` exist because of it).
- **Bay order is a wire contract**: a dormant vehicle's slot ordinal is an index into `PARK`
  (`dormant-vehicles`), so reordering bays would move every parked car in the region. Pinned in
  `ParkingPlannerTests`.
- **The fringe between the outermost bay and the lot edge** keeps its cover colour but loses its bay
  pattern, because `CoverStage` rewrote those cells to `CoverClass.ParkingPaved` (`parking-lots`).
  A lot the planner rejected is untouched and still shows the old world-aligned grid.
- **Checks**: `--chunks fixture:parking` is a big-box store's lot off a road turned 14° off east —
  so a world-aligned grid would visibly cut across the rows — with `ParkingFixtureTests` asserting
  the bays, the pad under every one of them, the planters, the walk, the shelter and that no bay is
  square to the world.
