# The game's own producers build cube leaves (G2 B5, #1509)

The generated world, the fill's domain and the fixture courses build cube leaves in memory (`CubeLeafContent`), beside
the LV95 tiles they still serve. The game reads none of them until C1 draws leaves, behind `--frame earth`. Plan:
`docs/plans/global-tiling-g2.md`, B5. Code in `src/Terrain/Leaves` (Godot-free) and `Fixture/FixtureLeaves`.

## The helpers

- `LeafFrames`: a leaf's level-9 `WorkFrame` and its `LeafTransform` (3.3 ms to make, 512 kept), also a leaf's
  transform in another leaf's frame (`TransformIn`, for a stage's halo). `LeafFrames.Swiss` stands on LHN95.
- `SourceOnLeaf`: a producer's own coordinates (LV95, a course's metres) on one leaf. A vertex's source position is
  bilinear between control points every 16 vertices, each taken rigorously; it is exact on control lines, so
  neighbouring leaves give a shared edge vertex the same position. A record's point goes the other way rigorously
  (`Local`). A source's notation is two-dimensional: a point is placed by its latitude and longitude at sea level,
  whatever its height, for the lattice and the records alike.
- `LeafLattice`: heights (ellipsoidal by the leaf's undulation), cover and water (every second vertex) from
  functions; a costly ground every `stride` vertices, bilinear between.
- `LeafSplit`: a polyline cut where it crosses a leaf edge, by bisection on the leaf a point lies in; pieces share
  their ends.
- `SourceTileToLeaf`: a tile's records (LV95 or a course's tiles) moved into a leaf: road records through RoadGen's
  `LeafRecords.Map` (its `IMap`, public since B5), building triangles and trees point by point.
- `LeafFillDomain`: the fill's domain as great-circle discs (40 km) round the spawn, every place reached and the real
  leaves; the same shape on every face, it only grows.

## Fixture courses (`FixtureLeaves`)

- A course is placed at a latitude and longitude (`CourseSource`: its metres on the plane tangent at the start).
  `FixtureLeaves.At(source)` puts it where its LV95 start is.
- **Its metres are true at its own height**: a plane's metres are the ellipsoid's, so 1,200 m up a course would be
  2×10⁻⁴ longer (half a metre on the hairpin). `CourseSource` scales by R / (R + h) at the start's ground.
- Ground every 8 vertices (~10 m), bilinear, as the tile source's 10 m lattice; a `Terrain` function at every vertex.
  Roads are the course's lines cut at leaf edges. Car parks, painted lines and blocks come from the tile source's
  own planners, each record filed by its first point or middle. Trees and fields come from the course.
- Ski slopes on leaves since C7 (#1649); the fleet is no leaf data: it is laid by the course's placement (`terrain/fixtures-on-earth`).
- `FixtureCourseTests`: every course's leaves round-trip; every road point lies in its leaf with the ground within 2 m
  (ski lifts are cables, left out); the start stands on the course's ground; a course placed 180 km away keeps its
  metres (2×10⁻⁵); leaf cuts share ends.

## The generated world (`GeneratedLeaves`, `GeneratedLeafRoads`)

- Heights are the generator's function at every vertex (`ProceduralWorld.HeightIn`, its lattice cache). Cover and
  water come from its tile products (the classifier fills 10 m squares of a tile whole): the 1 m cell and 2 m sample
  nearest each vertex. Trees and buildings are filed into the leaf they stand in. 24 tiles' products are kept.
- `Build(leaf)`: the generator's own leaf. The blend against real leaves is C1's `LeafBlend` on the built lattice
  (`terrain/earth-world`, PR 3): a convex mix, so B5's additive `correction` seam is gone.
- Roads: RoadGen's stage on the leaf's work frame (`FrameUnits`), halo 2 leaves, one leaf at a time. The raw input is
  the generator's keyed segments of the LV95 tiles under a leaf, cut at its edges, each keyed by its line and the
  metre it starts at. The ground is the generator's at a plane point; the walls are its buildings on the plane.
  The leaves of a work frame share its units, raw roads, walls and RoadGen's `Facades`/`UrbanField` (64 tiles, 2
  frames), on up to 3 states, so 3 leaves are built at once (#1699). `UrbanField` computes densities in 256 m blocks
  of nodes where a line asks, not per tile (a frame's tiles are overlapping 1 km leaf squares). A village leaf
  ~1.7 s (was ~5 s; flat's village tile ~0.9 s), a rural one 0.1-0.5 s; the first of a process adds the generator's
  river network (2-4 s).
- `BlendCheck --leaf-roads-time [--at E,N] [--side N] [--digest file] [--parallel N] [--flat]`: each leaf's time
  round a point (the brsquad village by default), and a SHA-256 of each leaf's roads, written to the file or compared
  with it: build a baseline on `main`, then a speed-up must give `RESULT: ok` (also on N workers). `--flat` times the
  flat world's generated tiles over the same ground.
- `BlendCheck --leaves`:
  - heights are the function within 0.03 mm;
  - shared edges are equal within a frame, and within 1 cm across one;
  - every tree and building lies in exactly one leaf;
  - cover and water come from the tiles;
  - the codec round-trips.
- `BlendCheck --leaf-roads [--side N]`: round the biggest village near the anchor. Ends across leaf edges meet
  (0.00 cm); junctions and signals match the LV95 build of the same block (0.3 cm p95). That village has only 3
  junctions and 1 signal.

## The ratchet

The producers read LV95 tiles through `TileId.Global` and `TileRect.Covering` (TerrainFormat), and through
`FixtureChunkSource.CourseOf`/`TilesUnder`, so the flat-LV95 ratchet does not grow.
