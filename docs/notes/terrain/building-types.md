# Building types (churches)

- **Building types** (`src/Interiors/BuildingTypes.cs`): the data has no "church". swissBUILDINGS3D
  draws a nave and its bell tower as two solids, and so does the procedural world, so each used to
  get its own door and interior. Entering from the street usually meant the tower, which gave a
  5 m square room 16 m tall. `BuildingTypes.For(tile)` groups solids into typed `BuildingGroup`s
  as a **pure function of the `.bldg` tile**, cached per tile object. Server and clients compute
  it from the same bytes, so it is never sent and the format is unchanged.
- **Church** = a hall (Sacral; or Other/Civic when the tower is the Sacral one, because the GWR
  point often lands in only one solid) of at least 40 m², plus up to two towers that are within
  1.5 m, tall (at least 12 m and 2.2 times their longest side), roughly square, rising 2 m above
  the hall, aligned within 15°, and sharing at least 1.5 m of wall. A lone Sacral hall is a church
  of one. Groups never span tiles.
- `PlanBox` is the shared plan view (min-area oriented rectangle, and the **eave**: the median top
  of the *vertical* wall faces, so gables and spires don't count). `BuildingFootprint` uses the
  same box, which keeps doors where they were.
- **Doors**: every member keeps its own door, sized as a Sacral door and never within 0.6 m of
  another member's box (it would open into the nave).
- **Interior**: planned once under the **primary** (the nave) key. `InteriorManager.PlanKey` maps
  any member's door to it, so the player's space, loot and the stored JSON are all the church's.
  `InteriorLayout.Entrances` has one entry per door; the player arrives at, and leaves by, the
  door they are at. Rules are in `InteriorGenerator.Church.cs`:
  - Frame Z runs along the nave, street end at −Z.
  - The nave is one room with `Span` storeys; each tower base is a `Porch` with an arch.
  - The tallest tower that fits gets a switchback stair, 3.0 m (or 2.7 m) storeys at 47° or less,
    up to a two-storey `Belfry` with a bell.
  - Nave furniture: chancel `Dais` (collision ramp at its front), altar lifted onto it, cross,
    lectern, pews facing the altar, and the pastor rat (`FurnitureType.PastorRat`, the "Dorime" rat,
    box-built in `InteriorMeshBuilder`) beside the altar, or at the step if the dais has no room (#241).
    Chapels planned by the generic Sacral rules get one too. Beside it the church radio
    (`FurnitureType.ChurchRadio`, `RadioByRat`, layout v12); the rat and the front-pew people are
    figures since #370 (`docs/notes/items/church-radio.md`).
    The pew row nearest the altar is a `FrontPew`: chibi people in cat-ear headbands with pixel
    kawaii faces (6 eye and 5 mouth maps, one 17x17 grid), built in `InteriorMeshBuilder.Congregation.cs`,
    varied by a hash of the pew's position so every peer builds the same people.
- **Stored plans**: `InteriorLayout.Group` holds `BuildingGroup.Fingerprint`, and `Matches`
  compares it. Bump `InteriorGenerator.ChurchRules` to re-plan churches only.
- **Exterior** (`BuildingMeshBuilder`): every member is dressed as Sacral. The nave gets one tall
  storey, so the shader's window row becomes church windows. The tower gets no window grid, and
  its faces above the eave are roof colour, which gives a slate spire.
- **Check**: `--interiorcheck[,shot.png]` plans churches, prints each one's LV95 position and
  writes `Church_<key>.svg`. Live, it enters by the tower door, walks the first flight, stands in
  the belfry, leaves by the nave door and re-enters. On the generated world the first church is
  at about 2583302/1113286, next to spawn.
