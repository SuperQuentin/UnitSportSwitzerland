## Controls

The excavator's arm actions in the ISO crane pattern (`new-action-three-devices`):

| What | Keyboard | Pad | VR |
|---|---|---|---|
| climb / take the remote / let go | E | Y | Y, or grip at the ladder or the box (as E) |
| slew left / right | A / D (`arm_slew_*`) | left stick X | cab: the left joystick's lever; remote: the left stick, not turned by the head |
| trolley out / in | W / S (`arm_stick_*`) | left stick Y | cab: the left joystick's second lever; remote: the left stick |
| hoist up / down | ↑ / ↓ (`arm_boom_*`) | right stick Y, back raises | cab: the right joystick, aft raises; remote: the right stick's Y (`XrPad` feeds it as the axis while `CraneWorking`) |
| hook on / set down | H (`couple`) | D-pad ← | cab: the poke on the left console; remote: right stick ← (`couple`, as when mounted) |
| hook camera | V (`camera_toggle`) | R3 | gap: a headset cannot be handed another camera |

- The prompts: at the foot, centred ("Climb the crane", "Take the crane's remote"); working it, the

## The hook

- **Hooked on** (`couple`) when the hook block's underside is within 0.6 m over a pallet's top and
  0.9 m of its centre (`CraneControl.Hooks`). Let down onto a pallet, the hook stops on its top,
  which is inside that window.
- **The pallet hangs from four slings**, 1 m long, its underside `CraneControl.SlingHang` (3.05 m)
  under the hook's origin, its runners along the jib. Until the slings are taut it stays where it
  stood (`Hold.Rest`, `CraneControl.HungAt`): no jump on hooking.
- **Set down** (`couple`) when its underside is within 0.3 m over what is under it

- The take and the drop are `PalletService`'s own requests: an operator's hook is a carrier like a

- **The operator steps the pose** (`CraneControl.Step`) and sends it unreliably at 10 Hz (`Send`);

  40 m up while its jib is still in view.
- **Let go** (`AskRelease`, or the peer leaving): the server stamps the pose and the simulated time

  starts, then eased into the schedule over the cycle's first tenth (the hook's way down to the

- **A pallet on the hook** is the hold's `Carrying` (`Pallets.Carried`), set by `PalletService`
  when it grants an operator a take, cleared on a drop, sent to all (`Carry`). A crane let go with
  a pallet on its hook holds it, off its schedule, until somebody sets it down.
- `PalletService`'s `Forks` (what is on the carrier) and `InReach` (measured from the hook, the
  pallet a sling's length under it) have a crane branch (`CraneService.HookOf`).
