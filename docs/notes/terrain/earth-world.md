# The earth world: `--frame earth` (#1510, G2 C1's second half)

The game on the region's built cube leaves (B4) in true Earth positions, behind `--frame earth` (`GameSettings.Frame`).
`flat` stays the default until G2's step D flips it. Plan: `docs/plans/global-tiling-g2.md`, C1 "Second half"; the
decisions were made with the user on 2026-11-08.

## What there is (PR 1, #1576: bare ground)

- **The switch**: `--frame flat|earth`, stored as `GameSettings.Frame`.
  - Online too since #1513 (PR #1610): the server on leaves (`net/server-regions`), with the fill and the generated world round them since #1682: `Earth/GameLeaves` builds the same leaves on a client and the server (the generator anchored at the default spawn, the globe's pyramid, a domain growing round every focus), so `--generated-world --frame earth` serves a generated world on leaves. `tools/fillagreenetcheck.sh` (`--fillagree E,N`): a client and the server agree on 25 fill heights to 1 mm, on the generated world and across B4's edge. Leaves are asked nearest a focus first; the spawn and the join wait up to 180 s while leaves load. A fill leaf is slow to make (6-50 s with roads, #1699): the generated-world net rows still time out under `TEST_FRAME=earth` (`tools/lib/twoclient.sh`).
  - A fixture course stands on its own leaves offline (PR 3); online it falls back to `flat`, as the server does.
- **The leaves**: `Leaves/LeafStore` (Godot-free).
  - `Open(dir)` indexes every `.cubl` bundle; `Get(key)` reads and decodes a leaf on its first ask and keeps the last 96.
  - `Cached(key)` never touches the disk; `Keys`/`Has` are the real-leaf set.
  - `TerrainPaths.FindLeafDir()`: `--leaves <dir>`, else the chunk folder's `_cube` sibling.
- **The frame**: the ENU frame on the tangent plane at the start (`new WorldOrigin(FrameAxes.Enu(ecef))`).
  - `OriginShifter { Earth = true }` re-anchors it every 3 km (`GroundMode`) to the tangent anchor under the camera, turning with the Earth (#1512, C3: `core/floating-origin`, "The earth world's ground turns").
  - One frame at every altitude: Space re-anchors every 20 km, no handover.
  - Gravity is the anchor's up: at most 0.027° off the ellipsoid's under the camera.
  - The shaders' curvature is refraction's lift only (`Curvature.RoundGeometry`, `world/earth-curvature`).
- **`Earth/EarthWorld`** serves `IWorldGround` (`chunks.Ground`'s twin; `ClientWorld.Ground` picks one).
  - Leaves within 900 m along the ground of the camera or an anchor are loaded on 3 workers and committed under a 4 ms budget; they are dropped 300 m further out.
  - **Not under a camera in Space** (#1631): above 60 km (`CameraCeilingM`, the Space regime's entry) the camera is no
    focus; the globe draws the ground. A queued leaf no focus wants any more is skipped when a worker reaches it, and a
    request that comes back with nothing frees its slot, so `Idle` holds again. Before, a fast climb or orbit over the
    fill queued hundreds of leaves (~2 s each with roads, one at a time) and the ground under the landing waited
    behind them for minutes. `Status` says what it is busy with (a shot's timeout line prints it).
  - A leaf's mesh is its collision's (`LeafCollision.Prepare`, road blend at clearance 0): what is drawn is what is stood on. Its look: "The ground's look (C6)" below.
  - Each leaf is placed by one rigid transform from its anchor (`LeafToWorld`) and placed again from the frame at each shift, never accumulated.
  - Queries compose in doubles: world → ECEF → the leaf it lies in → that leaf's anchor frame → its mesh (`CubeGeometry.MeshHeightAt`) → back.
  - Water comes from `CubeWater` (ellipsoidal level, every second vertex) with the flat world's wave scale, cover from the lattice byte, tunnels from the leaf's road bores.
  - `TileEntered`/`TileUnloaded` carry `GroundTile.FromLeaf(key)`. Furnishing and replacement events come with PR 2 and PR 3.
- **Collision**: C2's `CollisionPatches` round the collision anchors (look-ahead 10 s, at most 600 m; none 200 m up).
  - Each patch is a `StaticBody3D` holding a `ConcavePolygonShape3D`, placed by its leaf's transform.
  - `HasCollisionAt` asks whether the patch under the point stands.
  - The patches carry roads and buildings already (C2), so a body bumps into a building before PR 2 draws it.
- **`Globe`** (`Earth = true`, `Leaves`):
  - drawn at every altitude, placed in the anchor frame;
  - its quadtree goes down to level 14 from the leaves' heights (every 8th vertex);
  - commits under a 3 ms budget.
  - The far view at every altitude (#1629, C6): `terrain/earth-far-view`.

## Furnishing (PR 2, #1613)

- **The stand-in tile** (`Leaves/LeafStandIn`, Godot-free, `LeafStandInTests`): a leaf as an LV95-shaped tile, so
  today's builders, the door index, interiors and the tile events run on it unchanged.
  - Its frame is the leaf's anchor frame shifted 500 m along x and z (C2's road-blend square): the leaf lies in the
    middle of the 1000 m tile. A `ChunkNode` drawn there is placed by `EarthWorld.StandInToWorld(leaf)`.
  - Its name is a `TileId` no LV95 tile has: E = 100,000 + face · 20,000 + X, N = 100,000 + Y (`Id`, `TryLeaf`).
    `BuildingKey`/`DoorKey` built on it name a leaf's buildings, stable across runs (interior layouts are stored
    under it). D re-keys them with C5's `BuildingId`. Every seed hashed from such a key (plan, shop, bank, cellar,
    locks, loot, a site's crane: `Core.Fnv`) already hashes as from the building's `BuildingId` (#1658):
    `Buildings` registers each solid's id with `Fnv.Alias`, so the same house seeds alike in any leaf build. The flat
    world's seeds are untouched (its LV95 keys never match), so flat and earth houses differ until D.
  - Its ground is a 1 m `ChunkGrid` rasterised from the leaf's bare lattice (the same triangle split as the mesh,
    within the height quantum, 7 cm); off the leaf, the nearest height on the row. Water: each 2 m sample takes the
    leaf's nearest water sample, as an anchor-frame height.
  - Records are copied and shifted (`Roads` via `LeafRecords.Map` and `RoadTile.WithId`, `Buildings`, `Trees`).
- **`LeafCollision/LeafStand`**: made once per leaf with its collision (`LeafCollision.Prepare`, every peer alike):
  the grid, the shifted records, the building sites and the doors (`BuildingFootprint.ComputeDoors` on the grid).
  The collision now carries what C2 left to C1: garage links, stoops, the sites' shells, yards and offices, and the
  buildings' footings on the grid.
- **`EarthWorld.Furnish.cs`**: on the worker that loads a leaf, the flat world's near-tile build on its stand-in:
  roads and paint (cells when lean), lamps and sign names, building cells with occluders and box proxies, site
  shells and cranes, trees (none in a pit), water (the still layer and the streams). Committed into a `ChunkNode`;
  the doors go into `DoorIndex` under the stand-in name through the node's whole transform
  (`DoorIndex.SetTile(id, Transform3D, …)`); `TileFurnished`/`TileUnfurnished` are raised on the earth ground and
  on the flat streamer's (the dressing systems listen there).
- **The drawn ground** is the bare lattice blended at `VisualBlendClearance` (0.35 m under the roads), from the same
  bands as the collision's (`LeafCollision.Bands`); heights and collision stay at clearance 0.
- **Interiors**: `WorldOrigin.StandIns` places a stand-in tile (`TileTransform`, `TileToWorld`, `TileLocal`), and
  `LeafCollision/StandInChunkSource` loads its grid, roads, buildings and trees by name (`ClientWorld.WorldSource`,
  given to `InteriorManager` and `VenueTerraces`). `GroundTile.Lv95Tile` of a leaf is its stand-in name.
- **Left out** (PR 3 or later): ground cut at tunnel portals and site pits (the leaf's holes are still cut), piers and
  stadiums (their catalogues are LV95), castles and venues matched by LV95 position, C5's per-leaf ids, rewards and
  fields.

## The fill and the fixture courses (PR 3)

- **`Leaves/EarthLeaves`** (Godot-free) is what `EarthWorld` stands on (`ILeafSource`; `LeafStore` is one too):
  - with the generator (`Systems.Generated`, `GeneratedFill` or no local terrain): the region's leaves, and the fill on
    every leaf of `LeafFillDomain` (a 40 km disc round the start, the region's disc, grown by `Reach` wherever a focus
    goes) that the region does not hold, built on the workers by B5's `GeneratedLeaves` and `GeneratedLeafRoads`;
  - a part-real border leaf (`terrain/border-leaves-partial`) is merged: its real cells with everything on them, the
    rest the fill's (cover, still water, trees and buildings standing in it; its roads stay the region's);
  - a fixture course (`--chunks fixture:<course>`) on its own leaves (`FixtureLeaves`), all there is; the earth world
    no longer falls back to `flat` on one;
  - produced leaves are kept 64 at a time; a fill leaf takes ~0.5 s of a worker, ~2 s with roads (RoadGen's stage, one
    leaf at a time).
- **`Leaves/LeafBlend`**: the flat fill's blend on the face's global lattice, a convex mix all the way
  (`M = (1 - W) G + W R`, then `h = (1 - F) M + F C`, C the real surface continued from the nearest real cell over
  60 m). The real ground is `RealLeaf`'s 8-vertex cells with ground everywhere, as quadtree blocks; their heights are
  kept to the bit and a fill vertex on a real leaf's edge copies it. What stands on the fill moves with the blend's
  change under it; still water is dropped where the blend tilts its bed past 1.5 %. B5's additive `correction` seam
  in `GeneratedLeaves.Build` is gone. Real leaves on another cube face are not seen (none in Switzerland).
- C5's per-leaf generators (`core/earth-ids`) need no hook here: a produced leaf has the same sections as a real one,
  and PR 2's furnishing calls them on every leaf `EarthWorld` loads.
- The globe's last level (L14) reads `EarthLeaves.HeightsOf`: over the fill, the fill's heights (the generator every
  8th vertex, blended), so the real relief the fill replaces never stands up through it (it did, in ribbons, when the
  globe read the region alone). Its coarser levels over the fill are still the real relief (follow-up).

## The ground's look (G2 C6, #1634, #1632)

- **The style kit's terrain material** (`EarthWorld.GroundMaterial`, `MaterialRole.Terrain`, restyled with the
  rest; `ClientWorld` adds it to the fog list): every style and its standalone variants, as a flat tile's.
  - The mesh carries what a flat tile's does: `CoverPalette` colours in **linear** (`SrgbToLinear`; raw sRGB read
    as linear was the "mint lawns, white banks"), blended with differing 4-neighbours (`ColourAt`, the flat
    builder's boundary blend at stride 1), the pattern code in alpha, at the vertex's **altitude** (the anchor frame
    is on the ellipsoid: height less the geoid, `SwissGeodesy.FromEcef(anchor).H`), and C3's pattern points
    (`LeafPattern`, baked in the stand-in frame) for Cartoon's paint and Realistic's textures.
  - **`gives_way = false`** on the leaves' material: the globe sets `world_fill_hide = 1` under earth, and the
    terrain body dissolves anything off the flat world's real-tile mask, which leaves are never on. Without it
    every leaf fragment was discarded and the globe's blurred tile showed instead.
- **Lakes cut at leaf edges** (`Earth/LeafWaterClip`, `LeafWaterClipTests`): a leaf's 2 m water samples stopped
  up to a sample short of its edge, so the lake bed showed in lines between leaves. The samples now reach two
  samples past the leaf (`LeafStandIn.WaterMarginSamples`) and the still water is clipped by the leaf's four edge
  planes, each through the edge's two corners and the vertical over the first in a fixed order, so both
  neighbours build the same plane (within a millimetre): no gap, and no translucent overlap. The streams in the
  roads are built apart (`WaterMeshBuilder.Build(..., channelsOnly)`) and not clipped.
