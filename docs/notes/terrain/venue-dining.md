# Restaurants, cafes, bars, pubs and terraces (#1232)

> Research for #1232, Oct 2026: tagged [S] sourced, [E]/[H] estimated, [D] derived. Constants built from [E] numbers stay tunable.

Swiss architecture literature was thin: typology details are [E], checked against a few heritage sources.

## How many exist

- **Companies** (NOGA 56): ~23,300 in 2022, ~19,000 restaurants/snack/tea-rooms/gelaterias; 19,426 (2014),
  18,420 (2008), 17,658 (1995). GastroSuisse ~21,000 members, Gastgewerbe ~24,900 businesses, 260,000 staff [S].
- **business-monitor.ch** (28,265 firms): 22,484 restaurants/cafes/snack/ice cream, **2,175 bars**, 1,762
  caterers, **301 discos/clubs**; bars German 1,205 of 17,268, French 717 of 9,154, Italian 253 of 1,774.
  Leaders Zurich ~4,330, Bern ~2,950, Vaud ~2,860 [S].
- **Trend**: 2018-2022 closures 1,437-1,795 a year vs openings 1,526-2,072, but the rural Dorfbeiz is dying [S]:
  a village has 0-1 traditional inns. **Shares** [E]: 25-30 % Gasthaus/Beiz, 15-20 % Italian/pizzeria, 8-10 %
  Asian, 5-8 % kebab, 4-6 % burger/fast food, 3-4 % tea-room; bars ~8-10 % of restaurants.

## Archetypes (public interior incl. bar; ground floor adds kitchen, WC, storage)

- **Gasthaus / Beiz / Gasthof** (default; 2-3 storey gabled house on a street or square, flats or rooms above):
  Gaststube 40-90 m2 (25-60 seats) + **Saeli** 30-60 m2 (20-40 seats, club nights, weddings, optional stage) +
  kitchen 20-40 + Buffet 6-10; ground floor 120-250 m2 [E]. Vestibule with hooks; perimeter benches + free
  tables; **Stammtisch** (6-10 seats) near the Buffet; Buffet (till, taps, glass rack) with a swing door to the
  kitchen; WC at the back or half a flight down; cellar; **Kachelofen** in alpine/central regions; ceiling
  2.4-2.8 m with beams. Dark fir/oak panelling to 1.2-2.4 m, cream plaster, parquet or tile, heart-cut chairs,
  dark red/green bench pads, antlers, guild plaques, flags [S dark wood]. Props: jass cards, Stammtisch sign,
  menu stand, clock, sometimes dartboard, Toeggelikasten. 2200-2700 K, wheel chandelier, small-pane windows
  0.8 x 1.2 m with shutters. Variants: Hotel-Gasthof (+ Speisesaal 60-120 m2), Berggasthaus, Romandie Auberge.
- **Cafe / Pinte (Romandie)**: Cafe Romand Lausanne (1951 oak counter) and Pinte Besson are heritage [S]. 40-120
  m2, counter 4-8 m zinc/wood, banquettes, bentwood chairs, marble tables 0.6-0.7 m, mirrors, blackboard menu,
  Markise terrace [E]. **Brasserie / station buffet** 120-300 m2, ceiling 3.5-4.5 m, tile, brass rails, glass
  pass to an open kitchen, 60-150 seats, 3000 K [E].
- **Pizzeria / Osteria**: 80-200 m2, **wood-fired dome oven** (base 1.2-1.6 m, chimney) visible from the dining
  room, checked cloths, wine shelves, tile floor, terracotta, green/white/red signs, Ticino vine pergola,
  take-away window, green/red awning, lit sign box [E].
- **Fondue Stuebli / Carnotzet / chalet**: carnotzet = small rustic vaulted cellar, wood panelling, stone, 10-40
  seats [S]. Sheep heads, cow bells on beams, raclette heater with a half cheese, copper caquelons, red-white
  checks, antlers, skis [S]. Benches with sheepskins, ceiling 2.2-2.4 m, slate floor, candles 1800-2400 K,
  50-100 lux; exterior wood chalet, carved balcony, heart cut-out shutters, firewood stack [E].
- **Ticino Grotto**: ex rock cellar (vents "fisdire"), forecourt with **granite tables and benches under
  chestnuts**, boccia lane, Merlot in boccalino [S]. Forecourt 150-400 m2 gravel, 12-30 granite tables (slab
  2.0-2.5 x 0.7-0.8, bench slabs 0.35), 1-3 stone buildings (kitchen 12-25 m2, room 20-40 m2, rock cellar),
  gneiss piode roof, vine pergola, dry-stone walls, string bulbs [E].
- **Tea-room / Konditorei**: pastry vitrines at the entrance, marble tables 0.6-0.7 m, bentwood or velvet
  chairs, chandeliers, panelled Salon upstairs (Schiesser Basel c. 1900; Spruengli gold lettering) [S]; 60-200
  m2 incl. 8-15 m2 sales counter, 20-60 seats, striped awning [E]. Cafe-Baeckerei 40-80 m2, high tables.
- **Kebab / Imbiss** 20-60 m2, glass counter with vertical spit, 2-6 small tables, photo menu, LED 4000 K, hood.
  **Burger / chain** 80-250 m2, order counter, bench modules, drive-thru on town edges. **Asian** 60-150 m2,
  lacquered wood, red/black/gold, lanterns, aquarium, round tables for 8-10 [E].
- **Pub** (754 OSM): 80-200 m2, dark wood, brass taps (6-12), snugs, TVs, dartboard, pool, 2400 K; many OSM pubs
  are really bars or Beizen. **Cocktail bar** 40-120 m2, long bar, 1800-2200 K, velvet, brass, low sofas. **Wine
  bar** 40-100 m2, bottle walls, high tables with 4-6 stools, bar 4-7 m; Caveau with barrels in Lavaux/Valais [E].
- **Student / rock bar** 80-250 m2, bar 8-10 m, stage 3 x 2 to 6 x 4 m, black walls, posters, Toeggeli, pool,
  pinball. **Sports bar**: TVs, high tables, darts, neon logos. **Club** (302) 150-800 m2, cloakroom, 10 m bar, DJ
  booth, dance floor 60-300 m2, haze, LEDs, windowless, basement or ex-industrial [E].

## Terraces and outdoor dining

- **Basel-Stadt rules** [S]: only in front of the venue; **no** carpets, platforms, fences, screens, third-party
  ads; loose matching furniture; one-colour parasols; **plants max 1.0 m**; open by 12:00; **quiet from 23:00**,
  no music. Zurich: police + (since 2008) building permit. So loose furniture on public land; podiums, planters,
  windbreaks only on private land.
- **Pavement terrace** [E]: strip 1.5-2.5 m deep (up to 5 m on squares), pedestrian passage **>= 1.5 m (1.8-2.0
  typical)** by the kerb, 1.0 m clear of the door, 6-30 seats (squares 50-120); **2.2-2.6 m2 per seat**, bistro
  rows 1.3-1.5 m, square layouts 2.0-2.4 m centres.
- **Gartenwirtschaft**: 150-600 m2 gravel or grass under chestnut/plane/linden, Festbank rows 1.6-1.8 m apart
  (1.6-1.8 m2 per seat), aisles 1.2 m, hedge, string lights, Ausschank hut, WC hut, play corner [E].
- **Parasol** 3 x 3 or dia 3.5-4.0 m, one per 2-3 tables, base 40-90 kg, clearance 2.3 m. **Markise** at
  2.5-3.0 m, projection 2.5-4.0 m, shopfront wide (3-8 m), name on the valance. Also: planters 0.4-0.5 x 1.0,
  A-frame 0.6 x 1.0, patio heaters 2.2 m, string lights 2200 K, parklet decking 1.5-2.5 m deep.
- **Season** [E]: factor 0 Dec-Feb, 0.5 Mar and Nov, 1.0 Apr-Oct (alpine resorts all year); furniture stacked
  off-hours. P(terrace) 0.55 restaurants/cafes/bars, 0.35 kebab, 0.9 beer garden/grotto.

## Building and exterior [E]

- Most venues on the ground floor of a mixed-use building; old-town fronts 4-8 m wide x 15-25 m deep; ~30 % on
  **corner sites** (chamfered door, windows two sides, wrapping terrace). Cellar via a steep stair behind the
  bar or a pavement **Kellerklappe**. Free-standing: roadside Gasthof, lakeside, mountain, Bahnhofbuffet. Roof
  flue. Facade: cream/ochre/red/white plaster, half-timber (NE), chalet wood (Valais, Oberland), granite (Ticino).
- **Signs**: wrought-iron bracket (Wirtshausschild) 1.0-1.5 m arm at 3-4 m with a painted panel 0.6-0.8 m
  (horse, lion, deer, bear, ox, sun, crown, cross, star) [S]; painted facade lettering; backlit box signs
  1.5-3.0 x 0.5-0.8; window neon "Bar" / "Open"; blade sign 0.6 x 1.0; "Tagesmenue" blackboard. **Menu case**
  0.5 x 0.7 m, locked, lamp, at 1.5 m by the door. Flower boxes, shutters (green, red, brown), Ruhetag sign.
- **Names** [S, one ranking]: Roessli (1), Loewen (3), Kreuz (4), Sternen (5), Hirschen (6), Krone (8), Sonne (10);
  Engel, Adler, Ochsen, Baeren, Post, Bahnhof, Traube. Romandie: Cafe du Commerce / de la Gare / de la Poste.
