# Windows

- **Windows**: `BuildingMeshBuilder` bakes facade UVs (metres along the wall, storey
  index) from the *triangle* normal; the shader draws the window grid from those. Storey
  height comes from GWR `GASTW` (69% coverage), else wall height / 2.9 m. Barns, garages,
  tanks and anything under 3 m opt out with uv.y < 0.

## Rooms behind the glass

> This section was generated by Claude.

- **Interior mapping** (#59): CUSTOM0 carries the wall's horizontal tangent (x, z) and the storey
  height in metres (`MeshData.Frames`, RGBA float). `room_behind` in `ps1_building` follows the
  view ray from the glass to the first plane of a box room: one room per window column, depth
  3–5.5 m, colours, floor, furniture and a picture from the window's hash. UV2.y stays free (#56
  uses it).
- **Only a hint gets through**: seen plainly, a fake room is obviously fake. The room is softened
  toward its average colour; half the windows have a net curtain, the others side curtains; and a
  strong sky reflection sits on top, stronger at grazing angles.
- A storey taller than 5.5 m (a church nave) gets leaded coloured glass instead of a room.
- **Occupancy**: `occupied_box` / `occupied_axis` (up to 8, from `InteriorManager` via
  `ChunkManager.SetOccupancy`) light more windows of a building with players in it, and draw a
  figure walking about some of its rooms. See `door-portals`.
- Exterior and interior windows do not line up yet: #60.
