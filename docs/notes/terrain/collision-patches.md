# Collision patches round bodies (G2 C2, #1511)

G1 found a separate height field misses 2 cm (`cube-tiles`), so under `--frame earth` collision is the mesh's own
triangles, cut into patches round bodies, on client and server alike. Plan: `docs/plans/global-tiling-g2.md`, C2.
Code in `src/Terrain/LeafCollision`. The flat world keeps today's collision. Nothing here is hooked up yet: C1's
second half wires the provider to the earth streamer, and C4 wires it to the server's world.

- **A patch is a level-16 cube cell**: 128 × 128 of a leaf's 512² lattice cells (~150 m), 16 a leaf, named by its
  `CubeKey` (`CollisionPatches.PatchLevel`; `LeafCollision.Split`/`PatchesOf`).
- **`LeafCollision.Prepare(leaf, neighbourRoads)`** readies a leaf once:
  - the lattice vertices in its anchor frame (`CubeGeometry.Vertices`);
  - the road blend (`LeafRoadBlend`, clearance 0);
  - the road and building faces, sorted into its patches by their middle's lattice position.
- **`Patch(key)`**:
  - its ground triangles, split as the drawn mesh along (i, j)–(i+1, j+1), front faces up, hole quads left out;
  - plus its road and building faces.
  - Faces are in the leaf's anchor frame; the owner places the body with the leaf's transform.
  - `features: false` gives the ground alone.
- **Road and building faces** come from the builders the LV95 tiles' collision uses, run on the leaf's roads section:
  - roads: bridge decks, walls, railings, islands, signs, masts, car parks, signals, sidewalks and kerbs;
  - buildings: their shells.

  Garage links, stoops and offices need the doors the LV95 ground grid gives, so they are left to C1.
- **The road blend on a leaf (`LeafRoadBlend`)**:
  - `TerrainMeshBuilder.ComputeRoadBlend` runs unchanged on a 1001² grid at 1 m in the leaf's anchor frame, the
    leaf's records shifted by 500 m (a leaf is ~610 m).
  - The leaf's own roads and its eight neighbours' (moved into its frame) are blended each, then merged, keeping the
    tightest band. Vertices on a shared edge are clamped alike from either side.
  - A vertex takes the band bilinear from the four cells round it, or the nearest cell's.
  - The mesh C1 draws should take the same bands at `VisualBlendClearance`.
- **`CollisionPatches`** (the provider, pure, unit-tested):
  - `Update(bodies)`: every patch within `CollisionReach.RadiusM` (300 m) of a body or of its look-ahead point is
    built on a worker, nearest first, 2 at a time.
  - A patch is held until every body is past `ReleaseM` (400 m). An airborne body wants none.
  - Distances run on the ellipsoid: the chord to the patch's centre less its circumradius, found by a breadth-first
    walk over cube neighbours, so across face edges too.
  - The owner takes `TryTakeReady` (faces, null for no ground) then `TryTakeReleased`.
  - `CollisionReach.Wants(distanceM, aheadM, airborne, holding)` is the rule in metres; the tile form calls it.
- **Building-site pits** (#1653): the lattice cells reaching a pit leave the grid and come back cut and lined, as
  faces (`LeafCollision.PitFaces`); the site shells' faces go in both ways round (`terrain/pits-on-leaves`).
- **`LeafCollisionSource(leaves)`**: the builder the provider takes. Prepared leaves are kept (32); the leaves come
  from whoever holds them.
- **One preparation per loaded leaf (#1626)**: a leaf prepared a second time can differ. A border leaf's merge with the
  fill depends on the fill's domain at that moment, and its edge blend on the neighbours' roads that are there.
  - When patches were cut from one version and the height query read another, a patch stood 27 m off the query.
  - `EarthWorld` now pins a loaded leaf's preparation (`Pin`/`Unpin`): every patch of it is cut from that one, and the
    trim never drops it. `Forget` (#1655) drops the pins too.
  - A body cut from another preparation is built again, with an `[earth] ... built again (#1626)` line.
  - A body holds its ground as shape 0 and its roads and buildings as shape 1 (`EarthWorld.IsGroundHit`).
    `--earthcheck` fails on a ray that lands on a leaf's *ground* off the query, and `tools/earthcheck.sh border`
    starts where the bug was found.

## Check: `--cubeharness <region> --cubepatches [--cubeat lat,lon]`

The steepest full leaf, a middling one and the leaf at `--cubeat` (Geneva's centre by default). For each:

- the time to prepare it;
- all 16 patches as Jolt trimesh bodies, with the build time including one physics frame, the memory, and the
  triangles;
- 3,000 vertical rays against the ground-only patches, compared with the blended mesh (bar: under 1 % over 2 cm).

Then the provider round a body standing at the last leaf's centre. The report goes to
`test_output/cubeharness/<region>-patches.md`.
