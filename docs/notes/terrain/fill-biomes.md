# The earth fill's biomes outside Switzerland (#1809)

Plan and phases: `docs/plans/fill-biomes.md`. Past the generator's own map (`swiss_relief.gz`, 550 x 430 km round
Switzerland) the fill was the map's edge extruded: green grass, conifers and farms in the Sahara, on Greenland, over the
sea. Since phase 1 the finished fill leaf is put in its biome. Code: `src/Terrain/Biomes`, Godot-free.

- **`FillBiome.At(lat, lon, altitude, insideMapM)`**, the chain:
  1. the **Swiss guard**: 20 km or more inside the generator's map, `Swiss`, dithered across those 20 km by value noise;
  2. **land cover**: `LandCover`, empty until phase 3;
  3. the **photo guess** `Guess(PhotoStats.Sample, lat)`;
  4. `Swiss`, the generator, where no grid is embedded.

  Then `ByAltitude`: over the latitude's snow line Ice, over its tree line alpine Tundra (not a desert). The lines are
  piecewise linear tables; 2,900 m and 2,100 m at 46°, the generator's own.
- **`PhotoStats`**: `photo_stats.bin.gz` (1.28 MB, embedded in the game, BlendCheck and the tests), a 0.2° grid
  (1800 x 900). Per cell:
  - the sRGB of the greenest month;
  - the snowy months: luma over 0.62 and `R - B` under 0.06, since bright sand is warm and snow is not;
  - a sea flag: water in 9 or more months, either bluer than green or black (Blue Marble's lakes).

  The grid is read bilinearly, the point warped by ~0.25° of value noise so the edges are ragged and not 0.2° cells.
  - **Rebuild**: `<godot> --headless --path . -- --biomephotobake <out.bin.gz> [--bmng D:/UnitSportSwitzerland/data/global/bmng]
    [--cell 0.2]` (`World/Globe/BiomePhotoBake`, ~75 s, the project imported first). Then copy it to
    `src/Terrain/Biomes/`.
  - **Not `GlobeGround.Classify`'s water**: its "dark is water" (luma under 0.12) took the Amazon, the Congo and Borneo
    for sea in most months.
- **`BiomePass.Apply`**, run last by `GeneratedLeaves.OnPyramid`, so the altitude is the pyramid's (ellipsoidal minus
  the leaf's undulation; past Switzerland that undulation is the Swiss grid's edge, tens of metres off EGM2008):
  - it decides a biome every 2 vertices, with latitude, longitude, undulation and the guard at control points every
    16 vertices, bilinear between, longitudes unwrapped across the antimeridian;
  - a leaf whose every control point is 20 km inside the map returns at once, untouched (`Stats.Untouched`);
  - **cover**: `Ground(biome, class)`: rock, scree, ice and water stay; the rest becomes `Sand` 42, `DryGrass` 43,
    `Tundra` 44, `LushGround` 45 (`CoverPalette`, exempt from its 2,900 m snow line), `Glacier`, or stays. Built cover
    (paving, gardens) stays only with a kept farm;
  - **canopy**: in the wet tropics (80 %) and the taiga (half) value-noise patches of ~480 m turn the open ground to
    `Forest`. Trees are scattered on them, one per 4 x 4 vertex block of the global lattice at most (35 %, ~150 a
    hectare), at a hashed vertex: broadleaf 18-32 m, conifers 10-20 m;
  - **trees**: the generator's kept by share (desert 1/50 as low shrubs, steppe 1/6, savanna 1/4, tundra 1/30 dwarf
    shrubs, ice and sea none) by a hash of their nearest global lattice vertex; the tropics broadleaf, the taiga conifers;
  - **farms**: kept whole by a hash of their ~300 m cell (256 vertices): none in desert, tundra, ice or sea; 1/6 steppe,
    1/4 savanna, 1/5 tropics and taiga;
  - **still water**: the generator's rivers dried in the desert (their beds stay, wadis).

  Every decision is a function of the global lattice: neighbouring leaves agree. The arithmetic is +, -, *, / and floor
  only (no trig), so Windows and Linux peers agree too.
- **What it does not do yet**: no relief change (dunes), no new models (palms, cacti, acacias, birches), no animals
  rule (the occasion cows still graze the desert), the globe still paints its own open ground green or white (phase 2,
  after #1802), and the footstep snow over `CoverPalette.SnowLine + 125 m` is still the Alps' everywhere.

Checks: `FillBiomeTests` (the guess at known places, the guard, the lines, the classes, a guarded leaf left to the bit,
a desert leaf); `BlendCheck --fill-biomes [--pyramid DIR] [--side 3] [--out DIR] [--spots a,b]` builds 3 x 3 fill
leaves on the pyramid at a desert (the user's `LV95 1829400,-515818`), steppe, tundra, ice, jungle and sea spot, checks
each one's main biome and that Lavaux and the Salève are unchanged to the bit, and writes top views (generator | biome).
In the game, `--frame earth --origin E,N --at E,N --globe off` shows the fill alone (the LV95 notation of each spot is
in the check's output).
