# Steinstossen: the Unspunnen stone put (#1269 part 3)

`src/Occasions/Festivals/Stein*.cs`; the plan is `docs/plans/adventure-festivals.md` section 3. The first game of the **Älplerfest**
(`FestivalCatalog.Aelplerfest()`, `aelplerfest`, 14-24 August, Höhematte in Interlaken, then Schwägalp, Schwarzsee, else the region's largest town),
open from day 1. It uses the framework's seams only: `FestivalService.Award` for Batzen and board points, the running list for the day and the
site, the `Games` facet, `FestivalOccasion.Decorate` for the ground. Other games add their own `FestivalGame` and day row to the same def.

## What is pure (tier 0, `SteinRulesTests`)

- **Classes** (`SteinRules.Classes`): the 83.5 kg Unspunnen stone (par 4.2 m, 30 Batzen, 120 points at most), a 40 kg (8.5 m, 14, 60) and an 18 kg (15 m, 6, 24)
  stone for a first go. Three puts a day (`AttemptsPerDay`, counted by the framework's `Played["stein:<day>"]`); every put pays.
- **A put is six server-time stamps and the camera's pitch** (`SteinThrow`): lift down, lift up, three steps, the push. `Resolve(seed, class, throw)` is
  everything the server decides:
  - **Lift**: `Meter(seed, class, held)` is a triangle wave 0-1 (1 = peak), period `1.1 + 0.019 * kg` s (2.7 s for the big stone), phase from the attempt's
    seed (FNV-1a); `LiftQuality` is 1 from 0.95 up and 0 under 0.55, which is a **foul** (the stone slips, 0.3 m, nothing paid, but it was a put).
  - **Run-up**: a 90 BPM drum starts at the release; beats 1-3 are the steps, beat 4 the push. Each press is graded with the concert pogo's windows
    (`PogoBeat.Window`, perfect 0.07 s, good 0.14 s): perfect 1.0, good 0.72, miss 0.3.
  - **Power** `0.2 + 0.8 * (0.4 lift + 0.45 steps + 0.15 push)`; **angle** is the camera pitch clamped 5-75, with `Technique` (1 inside 38-42 degrees, 1.2 % of the
    speed lost per degree outside; at this speed physics alone would favour ~33 degrees, so the band is a technique rule); **distance** is a ballistic flight from
    1.9 m (`Range`, `Position`, `FlightTime`) at `FullSpeed(class) * power * technique`, where `FullSpeed` is solved so that a perfect put at 40 degrees is exactly the par.
  - **Pay**: `rating = min(1, metres / par)`; Batzen `round(rating * MaxBatzen)`, points `round(min(1.05, rating') * MaxPoints)`. Three perfect puts a day (90) fit the
    game's daily cap (100), and the stone alone could reach the festival's 600 cap in 7 days.
- `Implausible(stamps, begun, now)`: six finite stamps in order, 0.1 s apart at least, none from before the attempt, the push not in the future (0.35 s latency allowed)
  and not stale (2 s), the lift not held over a minute. A bad grade is a skill, not a rule: late is a Miss.
- `BotAttempt(skill, rng, seed, class, begun)`: a bot's presses (the lift on a peak, the drum, the angle), every error scaled by `1 - skill`.
- `SteinGround.Of(site)`: the stone lies 12 m to the left of the site's middle, thrown along one of eight compass directions picked by the site's coordinates; pure, so the server and
  every client agree with nothing sent.

## The wire (`SteinService`, protocol 61): `World/Adventure/Festivals/Stein`

Client to server: `RequestBegin(class)`, `RequestThrow(attempt, stamps, pitch)`. Server to client: `TellBegin(id, seed, class, begun)`, `TellNo(why)`, `TellLanded(...)` (to the thrower
and every peer within 300 m of the stone). Reliable, 8 requests a second per peer. Server methods carry the rules: `ServeBegin(peer, class)` (festival running and the stone open, an adventuring
human with puts left, the body within 7.5 m of the stone), `ServeAttempt(peer, attempt, stamps, pitch, trusted)` (the attempt is the peer's and used once, the stamps plausible, the body still at
the stone, then `Resolve`, `FestivalService.Award(peer, festival, batzen, why, "stein", points)`, and the landing sent), `ServeBot(bot, skill, rng)`. A put's Batzen and points land in the framework's
section and the party board, so the milestones and the Journal's Festival tab follow with no work here. Offline the client plays the server (peer 1).

**The honest limit**: a client could send perfect stamps (the meter's seed is known to it, as it must draw it). The server bounds them in time (arrival) and pace, keeps the daily limit and the cap,
and no put can pay more than the class's maximum; a cheat gets at most 30 Batzen x 3 a day, inside the game's 100 cap.

## Deviations from the plan

The player is not moved onto a run-up (the steps are beats, not strides; the server needs the body within 7.5 m of the stone); the push angle comes from the camera pitch and a technique band; the ground is
not the flattest cell within 150 m but a fixed spot beside the site's middle; no ambient bots stand at the ground and `/stein <bot>` is not built; the Journal's per-game best is the framework's (points).
