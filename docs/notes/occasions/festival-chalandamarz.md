## What is pure (tier 0, `ChalandaTests`)

`ChalandaRules`, a function of the site and the server clock alone (server, clients and props agree with nothing sent):

- **The beat**: 120 per minute (`SecondsPerBeat` 0.5), beat n at server time `n * 0.5`, so every player and every party member has the same beat; `Grade(stamp)` grades a ring
  against the nearest beat with **`PogoBeat`'s windows** (Perfect 70 ms, Good 140 ms, else Miss); `BelievableStamp` (not 0.3 s ahead of the server, not 2 s old, finite).
- **The ground**: a loop of radius 45 m round the site's middle; the Treichel stand on its west side; seven stations counter-clockwise (45 degrees apart, 34 m), the stand again as
  the finish (`Path`); `OnLoop` (within 12 m of the circle), `Left` (more than 40 m beyond it); length about 275 m, par 1.4 m/s (about 196 s), `MaxSeconds` max(300 s, 2.5 x par), a
  finish quicker than 8 m/s (`SprintSpeed`) is no march.
- **Walking**: a ring counts only for a body seen to move at least 0.3 m within the last 1.5 s (`MinStep`, `MoveWindow`).
- **Score**: Perfect 3 points, Good 2, Miss 0; a ring on a beat another marching party member also rang on earns each of the two `SyncBonus` 2 (once per beat); the march pays
  `15 + points / 10` Batzen up to the day's 100 (a perfect, fully in-step march just reaches it); every finish hands 2 `Engadiner Nusstorte` (the Errands' existing 436), the first of
  the day a `ChalandaTreichel` (`ItemId` 515, a cosmetic hand bell; the worn `Treichel` garment 467 is earned elsewhere and is not given).

## The server (`FestivalService.Chalanda.cs`, protocol 78)

One request, `RequestChalanda(what, stamp)` (0 start at the stand, 1 ring at server-time `stamp`), `ServeChalanda(peer, what, stamp)` is the server method (adventuring human, 10 requests
a second at most); `TellChalanda(what, a, points, text)` tells the owner (`Started`, `Rang` with the grade and the running points, `Synced`, `Station`, `Done`, `Lost`). A refusal
goes through `Tell` (`Refused`). Offline the local player is the authority and no RPC is used.

- **Start**: within 5 m of the stand (`PositionOf`); a start replaces a march that runs (the client itself rings instead of starting while it marches).
- **Ring**: the stamp is the client's `ClockSync.ServerNow` at the press; the server grades it, never trusts a grade. A ring is a Miss with a line when it is on a beat already rung
  ("Once a beat."), off the loop's corridor ("Back onto the loop!") or from a body that has not moved ("Keep walking!"). A stamp from the future, stale or NaN is refused.
- **In step**: for a Perfect or Good ring on beat n the server looks at the other humans of the party (`CrewKeys`/`CrewPeers`) marching with their last good ring on the same beat n;
  each of the two gets +2 once for that beat and is told (`Synced`). The beat index comes from the stamp, not from arrival order, so latency does not decide it.
- **Tick** (`ChalandaTick`, every 0.25 s, from the festival `_Process`): follows the position; the next station within 7 m (the stand within 5 m for the last) is passed, in order, one at
  a time, so a station cannot be skipped; 40 m beyond the loop leaves the procession; over `MaxSeconds` ends it. At the stand after the seventh station `FinishMarch` pays through
  `Award` (the framework's caps, board and milestones), hands the items and tells the rhyme "Chalanda Marz, chalanda Avrigl, laschè las vachas our d'stalla!"; a finish quicker than
  the sprint limit (`FestivalService.ChalandaSpeedLimit`; `--chalandafast` lifts it for the checks, which teleport) pays nothing ("not a march").
- **Bots**: `ServeChalanda(botPeer, what, stamp)` counts (`ChalandaBotActs`) and pays nothing; `Award` refuses a bot.

## Deviations from the plan

- The Treichel is **not an item the player holds** while marching: the ring is the interact key at any time during the march; the hand bell is the souvenir of the first finish of the day.
- **The village loop is a ring of 45 m**, not a route through the real village's streets (no road routing for a festival site); the station banners mark it. There are no children NPCs in
  the procession (the party and the drum are the procession).
- **One game and token cap 100** (one day cannot earn more), milestones 5/10/10.
- Party points are paid when the march ends (the framework's `Award`), not ring by ring; a player alone scores no in-step bonus.
- The villages' coordinates are computed from their WGS84 with swisstopo's approximate formulas (about 1 m); the relocation rule places it elsewhere without them.
