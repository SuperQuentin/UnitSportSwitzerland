# Escalade (#1269 part 8, a calendar festival; issue #1351)

Geneva's festival of the night of 11-12 December 1602: the Marmite smashed with the saying, the Course de l'Escalade, the soupe.

## The festival

`FestivalCatalog.Escalade()` registers `escalade` from the catalogue's static constructor; its default `OccasionEntry` is in `OccasionConfig.Defaults` (priority 8, Atmosphere off, the sky is
the season's). Window `12-sat#2 .. +1`: the second Saturday of December and the Sunday after (11-12 December 2027, 12-13 December 2026). One site, the Place du Bourg-de-Four in Genève (radius
100 m); a region without Geneva gets it in its largest town (the relocation rule). All three games open on day 1. Token cap **300** (two days cannot reach 600; the framework checks that the games
can reach the cap); milestones 250/600/1100 points pay 10/20/40. Under `--festival test` the def is re-registered at the fixture's start (`FestivalService._Ready`).

## What is pure (tier 0, `EscaladeTests`)

`EscaladeRules`, a function of the site alone (server, clients and props agree with nothing sent): the Marmite at the site's middle, the pot 9 m east, the start line 12 m west, six gates on a ring
of 55 m (an arc of 6/7 of a circle, so the line and the first gate are apart) then the line as the finish; reaches (Marmite 5 m, pot 4.5, line 5, gate 6, crew 12); `CourseLength`, par
(5.5 m/s, about 60 s), `MaxSeconds` (four times par, at least 180), `Plausible` (nobody runs 12 m/s); `CoursePay` (20 Batzen, up to 40 more at 80 % of par fading to none at 150 %; points are 3 per Batzen);
`Pair(members)`: the youngest and the oldest by one total order (account creation, then key), so two people are never the same one and a lone player is both.

## The server (`FestivalService.Escalade.cs`, protocol 77)

One request, `RequestEscalade(target)` (0 Marmite, 1 pot, 2 start line), `ServeEscalade(peer, target)` is the server method (adventuring human, near enough by `PositionOf`, 10 requests a second at most);
`TellEscalade(what, a, ms, text)` tells the owner. A refusal goes through `Tell` (`TellRefused`). Offline the local player is the authority and no RPC is used.

- **Marmite**: the party members (`CrewKeys`/`CrewPeers`) within 12 m of it are the candidates; their `PlayerProgress.Created` picks the youngest and the oldest. Only those two can press. With two people
  the first press holds it for 6 s (`_holds`, told to both), the other's press smashes; one person smashes alone. Everyone near it who has not smashed today is paid through `Award` (30 Batzen, the
  pair 10 more, 100 points, the game's day cap 40) and gets three `MarmiteChocolate`; all are told the saying. Pressed again the same day: "You have smashed the Marmite today."
- **Soup pot**: every party member within 12 m of the pot who has not eaten that festival day is fed (`_fed`, memory), paid 8 Batzen and 20 points and given a `SoupeEscalade`; the ladler is told how many
  were fed, the others who ladled. Nobody hungry: "Everyone here has eaten today."
- **Course**: E at the line starts a run (`_courses`; pressing again restarts). `EscaladeTick` (every 0.25 s, called from the festival `_Process`) reads the runner's position and passes the next point
  of the path within 6 m, one at a time in order, so a gate cannot be skipped and the line only finishes after gate 6. At the line `FinishCourse` pays by the time against par; a time below the sprint limit
  (`EscaladeSpeedLimit`, 12 m/s; `--escaladefast` lifts it for the checks, which teleport) pays nothing ("not a run"); a run over `MaxSeconds` is closed ("Too slow").
- **Bots**: `ServeEscalade(botPeer, target)` counts (`EscaladeBotActs`) and pays nothing; `Award` refuses a bot, and the Marmite's pair counts humans.

## Deviations from the plan

- The torchlit Cortege is **not built** (the plan's Escalade row names only the Marmite, the Course and the soupe), nor a night of its own.
- **The Course is a loop on a ring**, not a route through the real old town's streets (no road routing for a festival site); the gate torch posts mark it.
- **The rewards plan's `Trials` do not exist**, so the Course is its own server tick; `adventure-together`'s pot is not used, the soupe pot is the festival's own.
- **Token cap 300** (two days).
- **The Marmite is never shown smashed**: a prop with no replicated state; the crash, the saying and the chocolate are the smash.
- The youngest/oldest are by **account age** (`PlayerProgress.Created`) among the party members near the Marmite, not the whole party.
