# Sechseläuten (#1269 part 8, a calendar festival; issue #1358)

`src/Occasions/Festivals/Sechselaeuten/` (namespace `UnitSport.Occasions.Festivals.Sechselaeuten`), the server half in `FestivalService.Sechselaeuten.cs`, the def in
`FestivalCatalog.Sechselaeuten.cs`; the plan is `docs/plans/adventure-festivals.md` section 8, the framework `festival-framework`, the sibling calendar festival `festival-escalade`.
Zürich's spring festival, the third Monday of April: the guilds light the pyre under the **Böögg**, a snowman whose head is stuffed with firecrackers, and the whole square
bets on how long it takes the head to burst.

## The festival

`FestivalCatalog.Sechselaeuten()` registers `sechselaeuten` from the catalogue's static constructor; its default `OccasionEntry` is in `OccasionConfig.Defaults` (priority 8, Atmosphere
off, the sky is the season's). Window `04-mon#3 .. +0` (one day: 20 April 2026, 19 April 2027, 17 April 2028). One site, the Sechseläutenplatz in Zürich (LV95 2683617 / 1246744,
radius 120 m); a region without Zürich gets it in its largest town (the relocation rule). Both games open on day 1. Token cap **200**; milestones 100/300/700 points pay 5/10/20. Under
`--festival test` the def is re-registered at the fixture's start (`FestivalService._Ready`).

## What is pure (tier 0, `SechselaeutenTests`)

- **`SechselaeutenRules`**: the light (`Lights(lastHour, hour)`: the sky clock crosses 18:00, never a first look); **`Draw(seed)`** = the burn time in minutes, log-normal with a median of 13
  minutes and sigma 0.6, clamped to 3..75 (a burn over 40 minutes is about one in thirty; the test draws 20 000 and pins the median, the tail and that every bucket is possible);
  twelve five-minute **buckets** (`BucketOf`, `Label`: "10-15 min", the last is "55+ min"); a bet is `Stake` = 10 Batzen; **`Share(bets, minutes)`**: each human bet weighs
  `stake x (4 if the exact bucket else 1) / (1 + distance in buckets)^2`, the pool (every human stake) is divided in proportion in whole Batzen that add up to the pool exactly (largest
  remainders, ties by key), a lone bettor gets the stake back, a bot wins nothing; the ground (the pyre at the site's middle, the Weibel 9 m east and 3 m south, six banners on a ring of 34 m).
- **`BooggRound`**: one round on the server. `Light(now, seed, secondsPerMinute)` draws the time and keeps it in a private field; **`Minutes` is -1 until the head has burst**; `ViewFor(key)` builds
  what a player is told (`BooggView`), in which the time and the moment of the bang (`DoneAt`) are 0/-1 until the bang. A test serialises the burning view and looks for the minutes and the bang
  time in the JSON.

## The server (`FestivalService.Sechselaeuten.cs`, protocol 78)

One request, `RequestSechse(kind, arg)` (0 bet on bucket `arg`, 1 grill a Cervelat, 2 take the purse), and one message, `TellSechse(json)` (the round's `BooggView` for that player, with a line to
show). `ServeSechse(peer, kind, arg)` is the server method (adventuring human, near enough by `PositionOf`, 10 requests a second at most). Offline the local player is the authority and no RPC is used.

- **A round** per running instance, in memory only: bets open from the end of the last round. Every quarter second `SechseTick` reads the sky clock (`FestivalService.Hour`); when it crosses 18:00 the
  round is **lit**: the burn time is drawn from `Random.Shared` (not the date) and kept in the round, `LitAt` and the burn's end are fixed on `ClockSync.SimNow`, bets close, everyone is sent the view.
  **Every game evening of the real day lights a new round** (a game day is ~22 real minutes): one minute after a bang the next round opens its bets.
- **The burn pace**: a minute of the burn is **6 real seconds** (`DefaultSecondsPerMinute`): the median burn bursts 78 s after the light, the longest (75 minutes) after 7.5 minutes. The sky-clock
  minutes would have made a median burn 12 seconds. `--sechsefast` (0.4 s) and `--sechsepace S` set it for checks; `--sechsedraw M` fixes the drawn time on a check's server.
- **The bet**: `ServeBet` spends 10 Batzen with `FestivalRules.Spend` (the section is touched, so the client's mirror follows), one bet per player and round, at the pyre (14 m); refused with the
  reason ("Bets are closed: the Böögg is lit.", "You have bet on 15-20 min.", "A bet is 10 Batzen; you hold 0."). **A bot bets** (`ServeSechse(botPeer, SechseBet, bucket)`) for the crowd: its bet shows in
  the count and on the board but has no stake and wins nothing.
- **The secret**: until the bang nothing carries the time. The view's `Minutes` is -1 and `DoneAt` 0, the server log says only "the Böögg is lit" (the result is logged after the bang: "the head burst
  after 13.4 min (bucket 3); pool 20 shared: ..."), no field of `RunningFestival` is added. The flames on the clients follow a fixed 15 s ramp after the light, so what is seen says nothing either.
- **The bang** (`BangRound`): the pool is shared by `Share`; each share is **Batzen already in play** (staked ones coming back), so it is credited straight to the player's instance
  (`CreditBatzen`: online it lands in the section, an over festival converts at once, an offline player is **owed** on the next load through the board's `Owe`) and is outside the cap;
  a player who is here also gets the play's pay through `Award` (5 Batzen and 30 points plus the share as points, the `wette` game's day cap 160). Everyone is sent the view with the time, the board
  (name, bucket, share of every bet) and a line of their own ("The head burst after 14.4 minutes. You bet 15-20 min: +10 Batzen from the pool of 10.").
- **The purse**: the Zunft's Weibel (a figure 9 m from the pyre) hands each player a purse of 40 Batzen once a day (`purse` game, `Award`, 20 points) so there is something to bet with.
- **The embers**: once the head has burst, E at the pyre grills a Cervelat (`ItemId.BooggCervelat` 510, food, warm 2 minutes), one a round and player.
- **A round still open when the day ends is void**: every stake goes back (`VoidRound`).

## Deviations from the plan

- **A minute of the burn is 6 real seconds**, not a sky-clock minute (that would have been 0.9 s).
- **The pyre is 7.2 m of logs with the 2.9 m Böögg on top** (10.4 m in all), not 10 m of logs.
- **The fit to the real burns is an approximation** (log-normal, median 13 min, sigma 0.6, clamp 3..75), not a table of the recorded burns.
- **A purse and a Weibel were added** (the plan has no source of Batzen for a festival whose only game is the bet); **a Cervelat** on the embers (`ItemId` 510).
- **Shares are not capped Batzen**: they are the staked Batzen coming back, credited outside the cap; the cap (200) counts the purse and the play's pay only.
- **Bots bet without a stake**, so a bot can neither win nor take a human's share.
- **Rounds are in memory**: a server restart in the middle of a round forgets it and the stakes (the day's other rounds are unaffected).
- Open question 9 (the secret's seed): the seed is `Random.Shared`; the result is **logged** on the server, not shown on the party board.
- **Not built**: the Zünfte's cortège (the guilds' parade on horseback), the Kinderumzug, the burnt head in pieces, a leaderboard of the day's best guesses, the real shape of the Sechseläutenplatz.
