# Désalpe, the cow drive (#1269 part 7)

`src/Occasions/Festivals/Desalpe/` (namespace `UnitSport.Occasions.Festivals.Desalpe`), the server half in `FestivalService.Desalpe.cs`, the def in

dress the cows in flowers, drive the herd down to the fair, vote for the finest herd. Batzen and party points go through `FestivalService.Award`.

## The festival

`FestivalCatalog.Desalpe()` registers `desalpe` ("Désalpe", 20 September to 5 October, 16 days: Val-de-Charmey, then Urnäsch, then the region's largest town, no
closing show) from the catalogue's static constructor; its default `OccasionEntry` is in `OccasionConfig.Defaults` (priority 8, Atmosphere off). Day table: the dress game
from day 1, the drive from day 3, the vote from day 4. Games: `schmuck` (day cap 60), `alpabzug` (120), `wahl` (40). Milestones 600/1600/3200 points pay 20/40/80.
Under `--festival test` the def is re-registered at the fixture's start (and the route is 45 m instead of 700 m, the fair empties after 6 s instead of 120 s).

## What is pure (tier 0, `DesalpeTests`)

- **`DesalpeRoute.Plan(seed, length)`**: a polyline from the alp to the fair (metres east / north of the site's middle), exactly `length` long, bending left and right; a function of the
  site alone (`DesalpeGround.SeedOf`), so the server, every client and the props agree with nothing sent. **Not the real road network yet** (the plan's 4-12 km `RoadNetwork.Path`).
- **`HerdFlock`**: the steering, stepped at 10 Hz. The Leitkuh (cow 0) follows a carrot 15 m ahead on the route at 1.1 m/s and **only while a driver is within 30 m of the herd**; she waits
  when a herd cow lags 20-40 m. The rest follow in slots behind her (2 m a row, alternating sides), 1.6 m apart. **Drivers push**: a cow inside a driver's flight zone (5 m on foot, 9 m
  mounted, found from the body's speed) moves away from the driver measured at the cow's shoulder, so a driver behind it sends it on and one ahead of it sends it back (the point of balance).
  At each of three junctions the (at most two) cows with the least herd drive (seeded) walk 20-30 m into the field and graze; they rejoin only when pushed back within 6 m of the road (so
  the driver must go round them from the field side); a cow 150 m from the Leitkuh is lost.
- **`DesalpeRun`**: grazing (dressable) → driving → arrived. It ends when the Leitkuh and every cow not lost stand within 22 m of the fair, 45 s after the Leitkuh arrived, or at three times par
  (par = length / 0.85 m/s). `DesalpeResult`: cows arrived, dressed, lost, seconds, score.
- **`DesalpeRules`**: a cow's dress is one byte (0 bare, else 1 + (shape - 1) x 6 + colour; three shapes, six colours); the bell is not chosen (the Leitkuh the big Treichel, every third cow a
  middle one, the rest a small one). **Score** = cows arrived x 50 + 10 for each arrived cow in a headdress + up to 100 for a drive under par; 1 Batzen per 8 points (a perfect drive of 14 is
  about 120, the game's day cap). Dressing a bare cow pays 4 Batzen and 10 points, once per cow, run and player; a vote 20 Batzen and 40 points.
- **`DesalpeWire`**: the snapshot, 3 bytes of header and 11 a cow (two floats, a yaw byte, a gait byte, the state): 157 bytes for 14 cows, refused when short, long, NaN or out of range.
- **`DesalpeVote`**: every herd that came down stands in the fair's ring; one vote per player per festival instance, never for a herd the voter drove; top three, ties to the herd that came first,
  fewer than three share the prize.

## The server (`FestivalService.Desalpe.cs`, protocol 73)

- One herd per festival instance (`DesalpeEnsure`, from the running list; a changed site starts a new one). `_PhysicsProcess` steps it every 0.1 s with the adventuring bodies' positions
  (`AdventureService.PositionOf`) and the farmhands, snapshots it to peers within 500 m (8 Hz while it walks and for 20 s after it arrived, 1 Hz otherwise), and tells everyone the state (a JSON
  `DesalpeView`: phase, run number, dress of each cow, elapsed, par, the ring) on every change.
- `ServeDesalpeDress(peer, cow, dress)`: the game open (day 1), the herd on the alp, a body within 9 m of the cow (a bot has none), 10 requests a second.
  `ServeDesalpeStart(peer)`: the drive open (day 3), the herd on the alp, a body within 35 m of the herd's start (the sign stands 14 m down the route). A drive with fewer than two people within 70 m
  of the start gets **farmhands** for the rest: server-run points that walk to the rear and the left flank, the odd one goes after a stray after 6 s. They push like a body and earn nothing.
  `ServeDesalpeBot(botPeer)` adds a hand of its own (at most four). `ServeDesalpeVote(peer, entry)`: the vote open (day 4), a human, a body within 40 m of the fair, a herd it did not drive, no vote yet.
- **Settling**: every human within 60 m of the herd at any step of the drive is a driver; when it ends each is paid `BatzenFor(score)` through `Award` (the framework's caps, board and milestones) and told
  `TellDesalpeResult`; the herd joins the ring; 120 s later (6 s under the test festival) a fresh herd stands on the alp wearing the same dress.
- RPCs: client to server `RequestDesalpeDress`, `RequestDesalpeStart`, `RequestDesalpeVote`; server to client `TellDesalpe` (reliable), `TellDesalpeSnap` (unreliable ordered),
  `TellDesalpeResult`, `TellDesalpeVoted`. Offline the local player is the authority and no RPC is used. `DesalpeWelcome` sends a joiner the state and a snapshot.

## The client

- **`DesalpeHerd`** draws the snapshots (eased: positions at 8/s, yaw 6/s, gait 8/s) as MultiMeshes: bodies without the field cow's small bell (`CowMeshBuilder.BodyWithoutBell`), heads pivoting at the neck,
  one Treichel per cow (three sizes by scale, swinging with the stride), and **one MultiMesh for each headdress in use** (the style material takes no per-instance colour, so a shape in a colour is a
  mesh; the groups are rebuilt when the dress changes, never per frame). A pose writes four float buffers, one engine call each; nothing is allocated per frame, no mesh is rebuilt. The Treichel rings
  from the cows near the camera while they walk (`DesalpeSounds.Treichel(size)`), now and then a moo.
- **`DesalpeGame`** (HUD and interaction, bottom-centre): within 4.5 m of a cow on the alp E puts on the next headdress shape and Use the next colour (the items let go of Use then:
  `DesalpeGame.TakesUse`); at the alp sign E starts the Alpabzug (the herder's call plays); in the fair's ring Use picks a herd and E gives the vote. While a drive passes near the player a panel shows
  cows with the herd, strays, the Leitkuh's progress and time against par; the result stays 7 s. Prompts come through `DesalpeGame.InteractionFor` (the outline round the cow) and `TryInteract`, both in
  the existing interaction chains.
- **Props** (`DesalpeModels`, dressed by `FestivalOccasion.Decorate` like the flag poles): the alp sign (14 m down the route from the herd's start) and the fair's ring (a round fence and a board of rosettes
  at the site's middle). Model viewer: Festivals › Désalpe (dressed cows of each shape, all 18 headdresses, the Treichel, the sign, the ring).
- **Devices** (no new key): keyboard E / LMB, pad Y / X, VR the same pad buttons through `XrPad`. Rows in `xr/vr-action-map`; grabbing the cow's head by hand is a `gap`.

## Not built

- The **real road** (`RoadNetwork.Path`, 4-12 km, 300-900 m down): the route is a seeded 700 m valley road; the gates (3-6 fence gates that open and shut) and the Leitkuh's carrot over real ground.
- Headdresses and Treicheln as **items** (craft, buy, hold to the head): a cow is dressed by cycling at it; no `ItemId` of 500-539 is used; the bell size follows the cow's rank.
- **The herding call** ("Hoo!" from the emote wheel); the herder's call plays at the start only.
- The **vote's prizes**: the top three get no extra Batzen yet and no "Schönste Herde" title (the standings are on the notice board and in the ring's state); the ring is memory only (a server restart clears it).
- A bot **brain** that walks a real bot to the sign (the farmhands are server points, not bodies); the quest step kinds.
- A herd taken over from a field herd (`Herds`): the festival's herd is its own staged herd at the alp.
