# Christmas market (#1269 part 8, the first calendar festival; issue #1348)

Stalls on a market square, a free taste at each one every day (the stall-hop), wares for Batzen, the Glühwein mug (2 dl, 10 %), the Samichlaus on 6 December.

## What is pure (tier 0, `ChristmasTests`)

  south row north; the kinds alternate (`StallKind` Gluehwein, Raclette, Gritibaenz, Ornaments) so each row offers all four wares. The Samichlaus stands 12 m east of the middle (facing
  the lane), the market tree 12 m west. A function of the site alone: the server, every client and the props agree with nothing sent.
- **Wares**: Glühwein mug `ItemId.GluehweinMug` 501 for 4 Batzen, Raclette (`ItemId.Raclette`) 8, Grittibänz 3, Christmas bauble `ItemId.ChristmasBauble` 502 for 12. 1 Batzen = CHF 1 when converted
  at the end, so the prices are about a real market's.
- **Stall-hop**: the first use of a stall in a day is a free **taste** (a stamp): 8 Batzen and 20 party points; all eight stamped in one day add the **Marktgang** (16 Batzen, 60 points). A day pays 80 Batzen
  at most (`DayMax`, the game's day cap), 600 over the festival is the framework's cap; a whole round of wares (27 Batzen) costs less than one day pays.
- **The Samichlaus**: on 6 December only, once a player: 30 Batzen, 80 points, a Grittibänz and two mandarins. Before the day he says he comes on 6 December, after it that he has gone.
- `FestivalRules.Spend(inst, n)` (in `FestivalBook.cs`): takes held Batzen, never from `Earned` (what the cap counts), refuses more than held.

## The server (`FestivalService.Christmas.cs`, protocol 75)

One request, `RequestMarket(target)` (0-7 a stall, `FestivalService.MarketSamichlaus` = 100); `ServeMarket(peer, target)` is the server method. It needs the market running, an adventuring human (a Battle Royale
round has none), the body within `StallReach` (5.5 m) of that stall (from `AdventureService.PositionOf`, which the server reads from the movement packets) and 10 requests a second at most. A stall not stamped
today is tasted: `Award(..., "stallhop", points)` pays through the framework (caps, board, milestones), `_stamps[(instance, peer)]` remembers the day's mask in memory (a restart only gives the day's cap again).
Later uses buy: `Spend`, then `AdventureService.Grant(peer, new Item(...))` puts the ware in the pack. The server tells the owner `TellMarket(day, mask, what, stall, text)` (a sync on joining, a taste, the
Marktgang, a purchase, the bag), and a refusal goes through the festival service's `Tell` (`TellRefused`). Offline the local player is the authority and no RPC is used.

## The Glühwein and the alcohol hook

`GluehweinMug` carries `DrinkMl = 200`, `Abv = 0.10` and `Buff = Warm` (240 s) on its `ItemDef`, like the beer and the Fendant: drinking it is the ordinary `ItemController` consume path, which calls
`AlcoholService.Drank` and so `ServeDrink(peer, ml, abv, fed, why)` on the server: 15.8 g (`AlcoholRules.GluehweinGrams`) in the drink log, the climb buff warm. **`--alcohol off`** (the server's rule, or
Settings > Gameplay > Alcohol offline) makes `ServeDrink` refuse, so the mug logs nothing and the buff and health still apply; `mild` halves the effects as for any drink. The **found** Glühwein (`ItemId.Gluehwein` 45, the
Christmas loot) is unchanged: an alcohol-free punch. The two share one icon.

## Deviations from the plan

- **Not a facet of `ChristmasOccasion`**: the plan's text says the markets are the Christmas occasion's new facet; the framework ties a festival to its own occasion entry (the id of the def), so the market is a
  festival occasion `christmasmarket` (11-25 .. 12-23, Atmosphere off) running beside `christmas` (11-01 .. 12-31), which keeps the sky. A player's "Off" for it hides the stalls only.
- **One market, not six**: a region shows the first of its six sites it holds (the full map: Basel; the western region: Montreux); the relocation rule applies as for the other festivals.
- **The "stall-hop errand"** is a festival game (`stallhop`, Batzen and party points through `Award`), not an errand giver's quest; the adventure bus gets `FestivalGamePlayed` and the milestones as for
  every game, which the errands plan can read. No NPC stall-keeper.
- **Batzen-priced ornaments**: the bauble is the one ornament (a held item, worth nothing but looks); no wearable or placeable decoration. The stalls are always dressed with their wares; the day's taste is a
  stamp, not a cup handed over.
- **The Samichlaus** stands at the market all festival; his bag is given only on 6 December (his prompt tells the date otherwise). No walk through the market or Schmutzli.
- Not built: Zibelemärit (plan question 6), a market sound bed (the sleigh bells play on the bag only), stalls as interiors, a `FestivalShop` for the festival's other wares.
