## Two festivals, one ground

`FestivalCatalog.Fasnacht()` (`fasnacht`, Luzern, `easter-52 .. +5`: six days from the Schmutziger Donnerstag, Weinmarkt) and `BaslerFasnacht()` (`baslerfasnacht`, `easter-41 .. +2`:
the three days from the Monday after Ash Wednesday, Marktplatz) are registered from the catalogue's static constructor; their default `OccasionEntry`s are in `OccasionConfig.Defaults`
(priority 8, Hats on, Basel's Atmosphere on for the Morgestraich only). A region without Luzern or Basel gets the relocation rule's town. Real dates: 2026 Luzern 12-17 February,
Basel 23-25 February; 2027 4-9 and 15-17 February; 2028 24 February - 29 February and 6-8 March (`FasnachtTests`). One site each (radius 90 m); token cap 600 for Luzern, **300 for
Basel** (three days cannot reach 600). Under `--festival test` both are re-registered at the fixture's start (`FestivalService._Ready`).

Why two defs and not one with two windows: `FestivalCalendar.Ended` reads only a def's first window, so a second window would convert Basel's instance (the year's) as over after Luzern's last day.

## The Guggenmusik (game `guggen`, day 1; `GuggenChart`, `GuggenRules`)

The alphorn game's rhythm engine (the pogo's timing windows `PogoBeat.Window`: perfect 70 ms, good 140 ms capped at 0.4 beat; a song per seed `HornWire.SeedOf`; the same session shape), three voices, 120-140 BPM,
eight bars of four-four after a four-beat count-in (about 15 s). **Loud and in sync is scored, not consonance**: no pitches to pick, one tap per note.

- Parts, seated in the order players sit down: **drum** (a hit on every beat, snare and bass drum alternating, one off-beat a bar; 40 notes), **trumpet** (stabs on the off-beats: always the "and" of 1 and 3, now and
  then of 2 or 4; pitches from the bar's chord, I-IV-V turns seeded), **sousaphone** (oom on beats 1 and 3, root then fifth; 16 notes).
- **`Grade(chart, part, taps)`**: a tap takes the nearest note nothing earlier took inside the good window (10 points perfect, 6 good); a tap that takes none costs 3, so holding the key down scores nothing.
- **`Sync(chart, scores)`**: of the beats on which two or more of the playing voices have notes, the share on which every one of those notes was hit (chart-relative: each player runs their own clock, nothing is aligned
  over the wire). **`Din(sync, voices)`** = 1 + 0.6 x sync x (voices - 1) / 2: alone 1.0, two voices in step 1.3, three 1.6 ("loud": more voices; "in sync": tight).
- **Pay**: Batzen = accuracy x 20 x din (nothing under 25 %, at most 32 a song); game day cap 80; party points = score x din.
- **Server** (`FestivalService.Fasnacht.cs`): `RequestGuggenStart` (a seat: at the stage, an adventuring player; whoever sits down within 45 s of the first joins the next voice, a fourth opens a song of its own),
  `RequestGuggenSong(string taps)` (the whole song's taps in milliseconds, strict wire `GuggenWire`: a bad string is refused whole; accepted 90 % of a song after the seat, within 40 m of the stage), `TellGuggenSeat`,
  `TellGuggenResult`. When all voices have sent theirs (or 25 s after the last would have ended, `GuggenTick`) the server grades each part, scores the sync and pays every human through `Award`. Bots:
  `ServeGuggenBot(peer, skill, rng)` sits down and sends `GuggenRules.BotTaps`; they raise the humans' din and earn nothing.
- **`FasnachtGame`** (HUD and interaction, bottom-centre): E at the stall / lantern / stage (`InteractionFor`/`TryInteract` in the existing interaction chains); in a song the part's notes scroll to a line, tap with
  `use_item` (LMB, the pad's trigger, the VR trigger through `XrPad`) or `jump`; the part's note sounds when tapped (drum snare/bass, trumpet, sousaphone, pitched from the chart), `Esc` leaves, walking 25 m from the
  stage ends the song. Strings are cached on the keys that change; the panel redraws only while a song runs.

## Deviations from the plan

- **Two defs** (`fasnacht`, `baslerfasnacht`) instead of one festival with two sites (see above).
- **Larven are items from a stall plus the festival's own mask**: four masks (506-509 = Headwear 5-8) are items; Headwear 9-10 (Fritschi, Ueli) are only the Hats facet's. Only 5 `ItemId`s were reserved (505-509), so no
  item per mask of the six.
- **No "town's lamps alone" shader global**: the new `world_lamps` multiplies every prop lamp (mask over 0.99), lit windows and building glow; interiors' lamps and signs are untouched.
- **The Guggenmusik is a tap game, no pitch**: the notes sound as they are tapped; the other players' instruments are not heard by others (only the player's own, like the alphorn: **a gap**).
- **Räppli are thrown anywhere** (the paper is cosmetic); the party score counts throws inside 160 m of a running Fasnacht.
- The Fasnacht Cortège, the Schnitzelbank and the Fasnachtsfeuer are not built.
