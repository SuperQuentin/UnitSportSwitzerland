# Player birthdays (#1423)

A player enters their birthday on the Account page; on that day the game celebrates it. Code: `src/Occasions/Birthdays/`,
`src/Ui/BirthdayPage.cs`, the hats in `src/Avatar/HumanMeshBuilder.Birthday.cs`.

## The date

- **Account page › Birthday** (`Ui/BirthdayPage`): day and month, the year optional (it only gives the age in the chat line),
  Clear; two switches: **Let other players see it's my birthday** (off by default) and **Wear a party hat on my birthday** (on).
- **Stored** in `GameSettings` (`BirthdayDay/Month/Year/Public/Hat/GiftYear`, the offline copy) and, signed in, in the account's own
  Nakama storage object `profile/birthday` (owner read and write only; `NakamaRest.ReadOwn/WriteOwn`). `MyBirthday.Pull` takes the
  account's copy on each sign-in (the gift year is the later of the two); every change is pushed. An account with no copy gets this
  machine's.
- **Rules** (`BirthdayRules`, pure, `BirthdayRulesTests`): the same day and month; a 29 February falls on the 28th in a year with
  none; a year between 1920 and now, a 29 February year must be a leap year. **One gift a calendar year**, whatever the date is
  changed to (`GiftDue`: the gift year is remembered), so moving the date to today gives the hat and the line but never a second
  gift. "Today" is `Calendar.Today` (`--date` moves it); the server's own date decides the chat line and the day's counters.
- `--birthday DD-MM[-YYYY]` pins it for one run and saves nothing (the checks), `--birthday-public` shows it.

## On the day

- **Title screen**: the account button's second line is "Happy 30th birthday, Anna!" in amber; the profile picture wears the party hat.
- **The party hat** (`Headwear.PartyHat`, 21): `OccasionHats` puts it on while the head slot is empty, before any occasion's hat;
  the switch takes it off. Replicated as every hat (`HeadwearId`).
- **The gift** (`ItemId.BirthdayGift` 551, its data the year): put in the pack by `BirthdayService.LookForGift` (every 2 s on the
  client, so midnight while playing works too), with the greeting. **Use opens it** (`ItemUse.Open`, the inventory's Open button):
  the year's collectible hat (`ItemDefs.BirthdayCollectibles[CollectibleIndex(year, 5)]`: crown, candle hat, cake hat, propeller cap,
  balloon hat, Headwear 22-26, one a year in turn), a party hat to wear any day, a cake, three rockets and CHF 30 (`GiftCash`) to
  the pocket; paper and a chime for everyone near (`ItemEventKind.BirthdayWish`, extra "gift").
- **The cake** (`ItemId.BirthdayCake` 552, `PlacedKind.BirthdayCake` 13): set down like a campfire (`Placeables`, the ghost; a table
  top is flat enough). **E within 2.2 m takes a slice** (`BirthdayService.TryInteract`, in `FootPlayer.TryInteract` seated, indoors and
  outdoors, its prompt and outline in `InteriorManager.Interacting` in the same order): +40 stamina and fed for 2 minutes when the
  meter has room (outside the Adventure stamina is infinite: just "Delicious"). Eight slices, anyone any number; the server keeps the
  count (in memory: a restart lays it whole again), every peer swaps the mesh (`BirthdayMeshes.Cake(left)`, cached per count, built
  when a slice goes, never per frame), and the eighth slice takes the cake away (`PlacedObjects.ServerRemove`).
- **The fireworks** (`ItemId.BirthdayRocket` 553, `ItemUse.Firework` like the 1 August box): only on the player's birthday, one show
  at a time (65 s), three a day; `ServeShow` sends `(start, seed, E, N)` to every peer and `Fireworks.QueueBirthday` plays
  `FireworkShow.Birthday(seed)` (a minute, 22 shells and six rings, no Swiss cross) at the spot, whatever festival switch the
  viewer has. Seed: `ShowSeed(name, day, n)`.
- **The Polaroid's birthday frame**: a picture taken (camera or phone) within 8 m of a cake prints confetti on the border and bunting
  in the margin (`PhotoStore.Print(frame, birthday: true)`), baked into the JPEG, so it travels like any print.

## With others (only when shown)

- **The chat line**: the client tells the server its birthday right after its name (`ClientWorld.OnWelcomed` → `SendMine`, the same
  ordered channel) and on any change; a celebrant who is new today is announced to everyone: "It's Anna's birthday: Anna turns 30
  today! Walk up and wish them a happy birthday." (no age without a year). At the server's midnight the counters restart and
  today's celebrants are counted again.
- **Wish a happy birthday**: E at a celebrant on foot (the fist fight's pointing, `FighterInteraction`/`TryEngageFighter`, before the
  challenge; a breathalyzer in hand still wins), within 4 m, once a day per pair; then E is the fight's again. The celebrant reads
  "Luca wished you a happy birthday! (3 wishes today)", the wisher a confirmation, everyone near sees paper over the celebrant's head
  and hears the chime. `/birthday` lists today's celebrants (with ages) and, for a celebrant, "12 people wished you a happy birthday today".
- **Bots** (`Bots/BotBirthday`, from `WanderBrain` after the UFO): a free-roaming bot within 40 m of a celebrant it has not wished
  walks up, wishes through `ServeWish` (the server path), waves for 2.5 s; gives up after 40 s.

## Network (protocol 88)

`World/Birthdays` (`BirthdayService`): `Tell(day, month, year, shown)`, `RequestWish(target)`, `RequestSlice(cake)`,
`RequestShow(e, n)` up; `SetCelebrants`, `CakeState`, `Sliced(owner)`, `Told(line)`, `Show(start, seed, e, n)` down. A joining peer
gets the celebrants and the eaten cakes (`SendTo`); `PeerLeft` forgets. Bots never get an RPC (`Note` skips ids ≤ 0).
