# Festival framework: festivals as occasions with a site, a day table, Batzen and a party score (#1269 part 1)

`src/Occasions/Festivals/` (namespace `UnitSport.Occasions.Festivals`); the plan is `docs/plans/adventure-festivals.md`
sections 1.1-1.3, the decisions `docs/plans/adventure-phase3.md`. Part 1 ships the framework and **no real festival**:
the Älplerfest and its games, fireworks, the Désalpe and the calendar festivals register their `FestivalDef`s in their own PRs.

## What is pure (tier 0, `FestivalCalendarTests`, `FestivalFrameworkTests`)

- **`FestivalDef`**: `Id` (= the occasion's id), `Title`, `Sites` (real first, fallbacks after), `Games` (`FestivalGame(Id, Title, DayCap)`),
  `Days` (`FestivalDay(From, Opens[], Blurb)`: a game opens on `From` and **stays open**), `Show` (`ClosingShow`), the default `Schedule`
  windows, `Milestones` (party points → Batzen), `TokenCap` (600). `Problems()` says what is wrong with a def; the tests walk the catalogue.
- **`FestivalCalendar`**: `SpanOf(from, to, startYear)` (the dates an instance covers), `Containing`/`Day` (1 = the window's first date),
  `Ended` (the day after the last date; an instance no window knows counts as over, so Batzen are never stuck).
- **Two schedule tokens** in `OccasionWeekdays` (so `OccasionSchedule` reads them with no change): `04-mon#3` (the nth weekday of one
  month, negative counts from the end: Sechseläuten) and `easter-46` (a day counted from Easter, `easter`, `easter+1`: Ash Wednesday;
  `OccasionWeekdays.Easter`, the Gregorian algorithm). Both take the `+N` tail (up to 13 days) and open once a year, so they have a start and an
  end; the monthly `fri#1` has no festival span. Tested against real dates: Sechseläuten 2026-04-20, 2027-04-19, 2028-04-17; Ash Wednesday
  2026-02-18, 2027-02-10, 2028-03-01; Luzern's `easter-52 .. +5` and Basel's `easter-41`.
- **`FestivalSites.Resolve`**: the first site of the def that the region holds, else the region's **largest town** with at least
  `MinBuildings` = 200 (`OccasionTowns.MinBuildings` x 10; ties by name) and the text "<Title>, held this year in <Town>", else null (not held).
- **`FestivalBook`** (the `festivals` section) and **`FestivalRules`**: per instance (`test-2027`) the Batzen held, `Earned` (what the cap counts),
  points, best per game, plays and Batzen paid per `game:day`, milestones paid, `Converted`. `Earn` cuts to the game's daily cap and the
  festival's `TokenCap`; points are never cut. `PayMilestone` is outside the cap and once per index. `Convert` pays an over instance at **1 Batzen =
  CHF 1** and marks it, so a second call pays nothing.
- **`FestivalBoard`**: a crew is its members' **sorted progress keys**; `Add` returns the milestones that play crossed, each with the members who
  contributed by then (guests included); `Owe`/`TakeOwed` for a member who was offline.

## Economy (the econ check)

1 Batzen = CHF 1, `TokenCap` 600 from games; the milestones pay on top but a def's total stays under a quarter of the cap (`Problems`), so a
festival is worth at most ~CHF 750. Against `assets/adventure/fares.json`: three half-fare cards (CHF 190) but not a fifth of a GA (CHF 3 995), and under twice
the starter quest catalogue's cash (CHF 440). `A_festival_is_worth_about_six_hundred_francs_and_never_a_travelcard` pins it.

## The occasion

- `FestivalOccasion : Occasion` (one per def, made by `OccasionRegistry.Get` for a catalogued id). Facets: Decorations, Atmosphere, Audio, Hats are
  cosmetic; the new **`OccasionFacets.Games`** (`1 << 6`, in `Gameplay`) is not: a player's Off hides the bunting and never the Batzen or the score, and
  `AllowClientOptOut` cannot switch the games off. `OccasionConfig.Defaults()` is where a festival's entry goes (priority 8: under the seasons for the
  sky, over the UFOs). The server's `Games` facet is what makes a festival run.
- **Decoration** so far: a ring of 16 `FlagPole`s round the site (`PolesOf`, hashed per tile through `OccasionDecor`, one tile owns each pole, shown
  in the model viewer under Occasions › Prop). Bunting, the beer tent and the game grounds come with the games.
- The resolved site is set on `FestivalOccasion.SetSite` (the server sends it); changing it re-dresses the world (`OccasionManager.NotifyContentChanged`).

## `FestivalService` at `World/Adventure/Festivals`

- **Authority** (the dedicated server, or the client offline as peer 1): every 5 s (and on `OccasionManager.Changed`) it reads the running
  `Games` occasions and the calendar into `Running` (`RunningFestival`: id, instance, day, length, dates, the resolved site), resolves sites
  (`InRegion` = the server's `ChunkManager.AvailableTiles`; towns from `OccasionTowns`, which the server loads itself), and tells every client
  (`TellFestivals`). A day opening raises `FestivalOpened(peer, key, festival, day)` on the adventure bus for each adventuring peer (and for a peer
  whose progress loads during a festival): the errands plan's world event. `FestivalGamePlayed` and `FestivalMilestoneReached` follow an `Award`.
- **`Award(peer, festival, batzen, why, game = "", points = 0)`**: the only way Batzen and points are earned; for the mini-games and the errands
  plan. Refused (0) for a bot (negative peer id), a peer out of `AdventureService.Active` (a Battle Royale round), a festival that is not on, or a
  game not open yet. Returns the Batzen granted after the caps. The game's own server method checks the body is at the ground first.
- **Party board**: `user://festivals/<instance>.json` (`WorldChoice.SavePath`; a check's own folder under `--adventurecheck`-style scratch), written every
  30 s and on exit. A crew is the keys of the party's humans at the moment of the play (`PartyOf`), so a party that re-forms with the same people finds
  its score; a solo player is a crew of one. Each member is sent its crew's `BoardView` when it changes and when its party does (checked every 2 s).
  A milestone pays every member who had contributed: online members in their section, offline ones through `Owed`, paid when they next load.
- **Conversion**: `ConvertFor(peer)` runs when a peer's progress loads and when a festival ends under an online peer: every instance that is over and
  not paid yet becomes francs in the account (`Bank.Credit(peer, n, "festival")`), once (`Converted`), with a card in the Journal. A player away
  for a year is paid the next time they come.
- **Wire (protocol 58)**: server → client `TellFestivals` (the running list as JSON), `TellBoard` (a `BoardView`), `TellConverted`, `TellRang`,
  `TellRefused`; client → server `RequestRing`, `RequestTestDate` (the last two only on a server started with `--festival test`). The section travels by
  the adventure service's `SectionSync`.
- **Journal**: `FestivalPage` (tab "Festival", order 35, `AdventureLog.RegisterTab`): where it is held, the day, the day table (open / tomorrow /
  opens day N, the closing show), the crew's score bar with its milestones and members, this player's Batzen (CHF worth, earned of cap, best per
  game), and a card per past festival. Refreshes on `Changed`, `BoardChanged`, `Converted` and `SectionChanged("festivals")`, never per frame.

## The check-only festival

`--festival test` (never a menu entry, never shipped): a `FestivalDef` `test` (five days from 10 March, a bell on day 1 and a gong on day 3, each
rung three times for a play: bell 40 Batzen + 100 points, gong 60 + 150; milestones 150/400/800 points pay 20/40/80), held at the fixture
course's start; its occasion entry exists only under the flag and is never written to `occasions.json`. The checks read the calendar with `--date
2027-03-11` and move it with `OccasionManager.OverrideDate` (server side: `FestivalService.AskTestDate`, only under the flag). Not a bot concern:
bots earn nothing.

## How to check

- Tier 0: `FestivalCalendarTests`, `FestivalFrameworkTests`.
- Quick: `--festivalcheck --festival test --date 2027-03-11 --world fixture --chunks fixture:flat --systems ui,physics,occasions,adventure` (windowed it also
  saves `test_output/festival_journal.png` and `festival_site.png`).
- Net: `tools/festivalnetcheck.sh`: two friends in a party, both members' points on one board, the milestones, the conversion online and on load,
  never twice. Everything is asserted on the remote peer.
