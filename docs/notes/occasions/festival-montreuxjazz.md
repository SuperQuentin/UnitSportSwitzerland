# Montreux Jazz Festival (#1269 part 8, [#1394](https://github.com/SuperQuentin/UnitSportSwitzerland/issues/1394))

`montreuxjazz`, sixteen days from the first Friday of July (`07-fri#1 .. +15`: 3-18 July 2026, 2-17 July 2027) at a small stage on the Place du Marché by the
Montreux quay: a jazz quartet, a seated lawn, the lake behind the band. Two games on keys that exist (the interact key at the stage-front rail, jump on the beat),
a Jazz Night on day 8 and a closing concert on day 16. The plan is `docs/plans/adventure-montreux-jazz.md`; the festival framework is `festival-framework`, the
concert system `concert/concerts`, the venue row `terrain/venues`.

## What is pure (tier 0, `MontreuxJazzTests`, `MontreuxJazzRulesTests`)

- **`MontreuxJazzRules`**: the constants (cap 400, 6 Batzen and 15 points a song, 10 and 25 for the bis, 20 and 40 for the finale, x1.5 on day 8), `NightOf(date)`,
  the venue's frame at a site (`FromFrame`/`ToFrame`, `StageFront`, `Rail`, `InZone`: within 35 m of the stage front and ahead of it, `AtRail`), the show's clock
  (`Moment(event, serverNow)`), `Counts` (80 % of a song) and `Meter` (the crowd meter of one call: Perfect claps of everyone on the ring's eight beats, one clap a beat each,
  the band returns at 12). Geometry reads a **site**, not the venue row, so a check holds the festival at the fixture's start and the zone follows.
- **`JazzSet`**: the set's shape as a pure function of the beat count, shared by the light desk, the server and the tests. Six sections of 4 bars and a dark bar make a
  song (25 bars, 40-75 s at 80-150 BPM); a set is N songs, the call (3 bars of dark: 2 beats of silence, the ring's 8 beats, 2 of tail), the bis (4 bars of chorus and 4 of
  a gentle peak) and 2 bars of rest, repeating for as long as the show is on. N is 2 on an ordinary night, 4 on the Jazz Night, 6 on the closing one (`SetSongs`). Songs are
  short on purpose: the show plays 20:30 to 23:30 of the game clock, three real minutes at the default day length.
- **`ConcertPlanner.Jazz(stadium, year, night)`**: the quartet's night, a pure function of the instance's year and the day. `Genre.Jazz` is appended to the enum and **never
  drawn by `Plan`** (tested over every venue and 300 seeds): only a venue with a `Season` forces it (`Tonight`). A 12 x 7 m deck under a 4.5 m roof, no screens, 33-35
  fixtures in one universe (6 spots, 6 washes, 4 floor beams, 2 hazers, the IR rig; no strobe, blinder or pyro), amber / deep blue / magenta, 80-150 BPM; the closing night
  adds two confetti cannons (`ConcertEvent.FinaleNight`).
- **The def** (`FestivalCatalog.MontreuxJazz`): games `listen` (day cap 60) and `bis` (40), both open from day 1, milestones 300 / 800 / 1500 points pay 10 / 30 / 60 Batzen
  (a quarter of the cap, the framework's limit), `ClosingShow.Concert`, and **`Relocates = false`** (new `FestivalDef` flag): a region without Montreux does not hold it, no town stands
  in (`FestivalSites.Resolve` returns null), unlike the Christmas market.

## The band, the crowd and the lights (`src/Concert/`)

- **`JazzInstruments`**: an upright bass, a saxophone, a piano with its stool, a drum kit; one mesh each, built once from boxes and tubes, shown in the model viewer ("Concert"), placed as nodes.
  The quartet is four idle skinned figures on pivots (`ConcertPerformers.BuildBand`): the sway is the pivot's roll per beat, nothing is rebuilt; the pianist stands at the keyboard (a seated pose
  does not exist), the band is hidden during the call and the rest.
- **The crowd** (`ConcertCrowd.Places` for jazz): rows facing the stage, 80 % taken, a centre aisle, none under the stage or in a crew zone, 350-700 people; `seated` is a uniform of
  `shaders/concert/crowd.gdshader` (the figure lowered 0.45 m, the legs folded), the hop damped to a quarter.
- **The light desk** (`LightDesk.JazzLook`): calm = a warm spot on the soloist over slow amber and blue washes; groove = the washes turn on the bar, the floor beams fan slowly; chorus = the
  washes up in the palette; peak = a gentle build over its four bars; never a strobe, never a blackout flash; phrases always in sync, the pump a third as deep; the call is dark with the crowd's
  wristbands twinkling amber.
- **Sound**: `ConcertSound` plays a quieter seated crowd for jazz (`JazzMurmur`, `JazzApplause`: softer, a longer tail), both in the sound player. **No music is committed**: the PA
  plays `CdLibrary.JazzUrls` (empty until the owner picks copyright-free tracks, burnt like `DefaultUrls`), a jazz night's setlist is only those, every other stage none of them
  (`ConcertMusic.SetlistOf`); with none the stage runs on the made-up songs, lights only. **Caveat**: the games' clock is the made-up grid; once real jazz CDs exist the lights follow the CD
  while the games stay on the grid, so `ServeMontreux` must then read `ConcertMusic.At` (a follow-up).

## The games (`FestivalService.MontreuxJazz.cs`, `JazzGame`)

- **Listening**: E (pad Y, VR grip through `XrPad`) at the rail 2.5 m ahead of the stage front marks the listener (the Jazz pass, item 511, comes with the first mark of a festival). The server
  samples every quarter second: a marked player standing in the zone (35 m, ahead of the front) adds the seconds; **when a song ends** (the dark bar) a listener with 80 % of it is paid
  `Yield(6, friends, u)` Batzen and 15 points (`TogetherRules`: +25 % a friend of the party within 60 m, up to x1.75; the same points; a stranger never counts as a friend), 12 songs a night at most
  (and the game's 60 a day). Leaving the zone drops the mark. The closing night's last song adds `Yield(20)` and 40 points. No drip: a lurker at 36 m, or one who steps out, earns nothing.
- **Encore**: the call after a set: a ring closes on every beat at the bottom centre (`JazzGame`, like `PogoQte`: jump is the clap, `FootPlayer.JumpTaken`), each press stamped with the client's
  `ClockSync.ServerNow` and judged on the server by `PogoBeat` on the show's beat grid (the stamp is trusted within 0.35 s of the server's own clock, else the arrival time is used). The meter
  sums the Perfect claps of everyone, party or stranger, bots too; at **12** the band returns and every human with at least **two** claps of their own is paid `Yield(10, friends)` and 25 points
  (a lone player cannot reach 12 on eight beats: two friends need 6 each). Deviation: the bis's *lights* always play (they are a pure function of the clock, nothing is broadcast); only the pay depends
  on the meter, and the toast says "the band is back" or "the crowd was too quiet".
- **Network** (protocol 86, see `net/protocol`): `RequestMontreux(kind, stamp)` client to server (kinds 0 mark, 1 clap, 10 a second at most through `Allowed`), `TellMontreux(what, a, b, text)` server to
  client (the meter on every clap, the song paid, the call's result). Everything is judged on the server from the shared clock; persistence is the `festivals` section only (the pass flag and the Batzen);
  per-song tallies are in memory (a restart gives the day's cap again, as the Christmas market does). A Battle Royale round refuses (`Active`).
- **Bots**: `ServeMontreux(botPeer, kind)`, counted in `MontreuxBotActs`, claps join the meter and are never paid (`bots/bot-capability-map`).
