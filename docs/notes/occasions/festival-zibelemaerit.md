## Deviations from the plan

- The plan says only "one day of stalls in Bern, a Christmas-market variant": built as such (the stall-hop of the Christmas market, four stalls, one day), plus the confetti fight the real market is known for.
- **The confetti eggs are thrown, not cracked on heads**: a throw is the Fasnacht's Räppli burst; there is no hit on another player and no damage (a PvP egg would need the combat system's hitbox rules).
- **No onion-wreath crafting or auction**: the braid is a held item bought for Batzen; no wearable wreath.
- **Mehlsuppe is not tied to 5 a.m.**: the kettle serves all day (a time-gated sale would hang the check on the game clock).
- **Token cap 52** (one day), milestones small (13 in all).
- Not built: a market sound bed, the Kinderumzug, the Bundesplatz's fountains, onion stalls as interiors.
