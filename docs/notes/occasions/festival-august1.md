## The server (`FestivalService.August1.cs`, protocol bump at ready)

One request, `RequestAugust1(target)` (0 lampion stand, 1 fireworks stand, 2 post, 3 light the box in hand), `ServeAugust1(peer, target)` is the server method (adventuring human, near enough by
`PositionOf` for the stands and the post, 10 a second at most); `TellAugust1(what, a, ms, text)` tells the owner, `ShowOwn(start, seed, e, n)` tells peers in range of a lit box.
A refusal goes through `Tell`. Offline the local player is the authority and no RPC is used.

- **Lampion stand**: one `ItemId.Lampion` a day (`_lamps`, memory). **Fireworks stand**: a `ItemId.FireworkBox` for 20 Batzen (`FestivalRules.Spend`), three a day.
- **The box**: `ServeLight` (night, 25 s cooldown) picks a seed (`OwnSeed(instance, n)`), a start 3 s ahead and tells every adventuring peer within 20 km; the client takes the box when its
  own copy of the rules allows it (`August1Game.TryLight`, `ItemUse.Firework`), the server checks again and refuses a forged one.
- **The walk**: E at the post at night with a lampion: the members are the leader and the party members within 12 m of the post who took today's lampion; **the village children make up
  the three walkers** (`Needed`) when there are fewer humans. `August1Tick` (every 0.5 s) follows the **leader's** position: a stop passes when the leader is within 8 m of it and every human
  member is within 30 m of the leader (else the party is told to wait, every 4 s); the post after the tenth stop finishes. Everyone is paid through `Award` (one paid walk a day each; the second
  is told "paid for today's"); dawn or `MaxSeconds` ends it, a loop at the run limit pays nothing (`--august1fast` lifts the limit for checks that teleport).
- **The lake show**: no new wire: `ShowOf` fixes `ShowStart`/`ShowSeed` in the running list when the sky crosses 22:00 on **every game evening of both days** (a show lasts 6-12 min; one
  on the books at a time), `Fireworks` plays it from the **nearest launch** (`LaunchFor`: the lake's barge, the fan turned to the water through `_rot`). Watching 20 s within 1.5 km of the
  site (the framework's rule) pays once a festival through `WatchedAugust1` (30 Batzen, 60 points).
- **Bots**: `ServeAugust1(botPeer, target)` is counted (`August1BotActs`) and pays nothing (`Award` refuses a bot, and a bot is no walker).

## Deviations from the plan

- **The walk needs three walkers, not three friends**: the party's humans and the village children make it up, so a solo player can walk; friends add 10 Batzen each. It is a festival game, not
  a quest of the errands plan (no NPC giver).
- **The loop is a ring of ten stops round the site**, not a route through a real village's streets.
- **The lakes' shows are one show at the nearest barge** (one is on the books and every launch point in the table is a candidate), not seven at once; the particle budget is one show's. The
  barge coordinates are approximate.
- **Watching the lake show pays only at the site** (the framework's watch rule reads the festival's first resolved site, one town); the fans at other lakes pay nothing.
- **Höhenfeuer are not an interaction**: nothing to light; they burn by the hash and the clock. The village fire is a prop.
- **The plan's "stamina plan's campfires"** is an `AirLift.Volumes` entry per fire within 3 km, not a `PlacedKind.Campfire`.
- **Own fireworks are bought for Batzen at a stand** (the plan's `FestivalShop` is not built; this stand is the only shop of this festival).
- The client's own `TryLight` mirrors the server's rule (night, cooldown) so a refused box is not used up; a server that disagrees (a different sky) still refuses, and the box is lost.
- The Swiss flag and the national song are not built; the "Feuerwerk" is silent for a peer whose Audio facet is off.
