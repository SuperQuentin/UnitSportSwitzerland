# Maps on the oblique stereographic (#1514, G2 C5)

Decided with the user (comment on #1514, Nov 5 2026): every in-game map lays the Earth flat with an **oblique
stereographic projection centred on the view**; only MapSetup's Swiss map (`Ui/MapCanvas` on `MapView`) keeps LV95,
its own projection. GPX tracks use the same projection centred on the track (Nov 8 2026; `gpx/gpx-ghost-racing`).

- **`MapProjection`** (`tools/TerrainFormat/MapProjection.cs`, Godot-free): EPSG method 9809 on WGS84 (the ellipsoid
  onto Gauss's conformal sphere, then its stereographic). x east, y north, metres from the centre; conformal, true
  north up and scale 1 at the centre, `ScaleAt` 1 + d²/4R² (60 ppm at 100 km), `ConvergenceAt` (0 on the centre's
  meridian). `Forward` closed form, `Inverse` iterates the latitude (1e-12 rad). `MapProjectionTests`: EPSG's worked
  example (RD New, to the mm), round trips within a µm over ±1000 km (Bern, Brest, Sydney, Reykjavik, the
  antimeridian), 10 m steps true through the scale, right angles kept.
- **`EarthMapView`** (`src/Ui/EarthMapView.cs`), the adventure map's view (`AdventureMap.View`, `IMapOverlay.Draw`):
  - the centre as latitude and longitude; `Pan` and `ZoomAt` move it and **re-centre the projection**, so the view's
    middle is always true north up at scale 1;
  - Earth places go through the projection: `ToScreen(GlobalPos)`, `ToScreenLatLon`, `ToLatLon(screen)`,
    `ScreenYaw(at, trueYaw)` (a heading from true north as one on the map, the convergence there added);
  - what is still LV95 until G2 D (the relief, the fog lattice, canton borders, POIs, pins, events) goes through
    `ToScreen(e, n)`: the projection's first-order fit to LV95 at the centre (central differences ±500 m), refitted at
    every move. Both are conformal, so it is off at second order only: under 250 m (half a pixel) at the widest zoom,
    2 px/km over ±225 × ±175 km (`MapProjectionTests`), nothing zoomed in. `ToLv95(screen)` (a click) is rigorous.
  - `DrawSampled` draws an LV95-lattice texture whole under that affine (one transform, no resampling; its UV spans
    the texture, as the fog shader wants). `Bounds()` is the LV95 box holding the view, for culling.
- **The player's arrow** comes in from true north (`AdventureMap.Me`, `ClientWorld` through `WorldOrigin.TrueYaw`) and
  is culled off the view: a polygon drawn thousands of km off screen came back as a dark sliver over half the map.
- **Battle Royale** (`BrMapDraw.NorthTurn`, set per match in `BuildMap`): the zone plane stays LV95 offsets from
  `AreaE/N` (wire, C4/D); over a few km the stereographic at the area is that plane turned by the LV95 convergence
  (scale within 200 ppm, a tenth of a pixel), so `Screen`/`Zone` turn by it, the map image and its outline are drawn
  turned (`Image` composes with the minimap's own turning transform), and the turning minimap subtracts it.
- **Slope map** (`World/Snow/SlopeMap`): its pixels are sampled on a true-north grid (the LV95 grid turned by the
  convergence at the skier) and the arrow is the true heading.
- **Check**: `--placescheck` windowed saves `test_output/places_map_projection.png` (the country at 2 px/km centred on
  Scuol, −2° convergence, fog lifted through `AdventureMap.ShowFog`): the relief's edges and the borders turned, true
  north up. `--navcheck`, `--locatecheck`, `--brcheck` cover the overlays and the BR maps headless.
