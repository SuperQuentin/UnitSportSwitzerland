# Menu skins (#1135, `Ui/Skins/`)

The menu pages wear a **skin**: colours, shapes and fonts read from a JSON file. A new look is a new file, never new
code.

- **The default is Low-poly Sport** (`assets/skins/sport.json`), chosen by the owner on 2026-10-23 from four
  directions on the "Menu Theme Directions" design canvas.
  - Dark faceted ground, sky blue `#4FD1FF` with an orange dash `#FF7A3D`.
  - Slanted buttons, fields, tabs and switches (StyleBoxFlat `Skew`).
  - Menu entries fill with the accent under the pointer or focus.
  - Titles in Saira Condensed ExtraBold, made oblique and in capitals; text in Saira.
- **Classic** (`classic.json`) is the dark glass and amber look the menus had before. It is also built in code
  (`MenuSkin.Classic`) as the fallback of every lookup.
- **Earlier tries the owner turned down**: Alpine Sticker (day) and Night Arcade (night). The first pass of this PR
  shipped them; they are removed.

## Where a skin applies

- `Screen` (every menu page), `Modal` and the loading screen take `MenuSkin.Theme()`.
- **The HUDs keep `UiTheme.Get()`**, the classic look, whatever the skin.
- The two themes define the **same variations** (`SkinTheme.AddVariations`), so `UiKit` builds for either and a label
  takes its colour from the theme above it, also when a page refreshes later:
  - labels: `DimLabel`, `FaintLabel`, `AccentLabel`, `GoodLabel`, `WarnLabel`, `BadLabel`, `SectionLabel`;
  - buttons: `MenuEntryButton` (not "MenuButton", which is a Godot class), `PrimaryButton`, `IconButton`,
    `TabButton`;
  - panels: `CardPanel`, `PagePanel`;
  - fonts and constants: `bold_font`, `display_font`, `display_uppercase` on `Label`; the juice numbers on `Juice`.
- `UiKit.Text(…, UiTheme.TextDim)` and the other role colours map to those variations by themselves (`UiKit.Variation`).
  - `UiKit.Bold`, `UiKit.Display` and `UiKit.DimFont` take the theme's face or colour once the control is in the tree.
  - A hard-coded `Colors.White` or `AddThemeColorOverride(…, UiTheme.X)` on a page does not follow the skin: use these
    instead.

## A skin file

- `id` (letters, digits, `-`), `name`, `font` (`system` or `saira`), `night` (a hint for Auto).
- **`colors`**, `#RRGGBB` or `#RRGGBBAA`:
  - every role in `SkinData.Roles`: ground, card, cardEdge, ink, inkDim, inkFaint, accent, onAccent, accent2, control,
    controlHover, controlPressed, good, warn, bad, hairline, shadow;
  - optional `controlEdge`, which falls back to cardEdge.
- **`shape`**:
  - `radius`, `controlRadius`, `outline`, `controlOutline`;
  - `shadowX`, `shadowY`, `shadowSize`, and `controlShadow` (cards and the main button cast it too);
  - `hoverScale` and `hoverTilt` (the `Juice`);
  - `skew` (0 to 0.5);
  - `menuFill` (entries fill with the accent);
  - `displayUppercase`.
- `SkinData.Problem` refuses a file with a missing role, a bad colour or a shape out of range. `MenuSkin` logs a
  warning and skips that file.
- Files are read from `res://assets/skins/`, then `user://skins/`. A player's own file may replace a built-in one by id.

## Settings

- Settings › Video › Menus offers:
  - **Menu look**: a skin, or Auto (a day skin from 07:00 to 19:00, else a night skin, by the world's clock in a
    world);
  - **By day / By night**: the two skins Auto uses;
  - **Reduce motion**: no juice and no title slide-in.
- `--menuskin <id>` forces a skin for one run (screenshots, checks).
- A page takes the skin that is active when it is built. Pages are built fresh on every push, so the next page shows
  a change.

## The title screen in Low-poly Sport

- **Logo**: UNITSPORT in the display face, then SWITZERLAND in the accent with a slanted `accent2` dash.
- **Entries**: slanted bars that slide in one after another (`SlideIn`, skipped with Reduce motion).
- **Occasion**: the occasion running now sits on a "Now on" card at the bottom right.
- **Footer**: the key hints and the version.
- The 3D valley (`TitleDiorama`) stays behind, under a fade in the skin's ground colour (`SideShade`).

## Checks

- `SkinDataTests` (unit): every shipped file reads, a missing role or bad colour is refused, shapes are checked, Auto
  and the fallback pick.
- Screenshots: `--uishot <png> 4` with `--settings`, `--solo` or `--multiplayer`, and a scratch `APPDATA` so the real
  settings stay untouched.
