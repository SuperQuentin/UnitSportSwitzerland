# Quick menu: a ring of the ways into everything (#840)

- **What**: `Ui/QuickMenu`, a `CanvasLayer` (layer 6) made by `ClientWorld` beside the emote wheel.
  Nine wedges, top first, clockwise: **Activities**, **Invites** ("n waiting", an amber dot),
  **Inventory**, **Vehicles**, **Map**, **Phone** (#889, the pad's way to take it out), **Emotes**, **Bird journal**, **Character** (#1127, the figure editor, mounted too), **Controls**. Each shows its key
  under it (keyboard only). A wedge that cannot be used now is faint and the middle says why when it is
  aimed (Activities offline, Vehicles in a match or not on foot, Emotes when the body may not emote); a
  pick of one flashes the reason (`InviteToasts.Flash`).
- **Drawing**: the ring is `Ui/Radial` (`Draw`, `DrawSector`, `Aimed`), shared with the emote wheel.
- **Picking**: an entry presses its action (`XrPad.Tap`, deferred, as the wrist menu does), so it opens
  exactly what its key opens. Activities and Invites push `ActivitiesScreen` on the shell; Emotes opens
  the emote wheel **latched** (`EmoteWheel.Open(latched: true)`: open until a click / A, no held key).
- **Controls** (`PlayerInput.QuickMenu` = F2 + pad Back, `InviteDecline` = Shift+F2):
  - Keyboard: hold F2, push the captured mouse (a virtual stick), let go; a click picks. A quick tap
    (< 0.25 s game time) accepts the newest invite if one is up, else leaves the ring open until a pick
    (F2 again, click, Enter) or Esc. Shift+F2 declines the newest invite.
  - Pad: **hold Back 0.3 s**, aim with the right stick, let go. A tap of Back is still the inventory: the
    quick menu catches pad Back in `_Input` while no panel holds `UiFocus` and taps `inventory` itself on a
    short release, so the inventory now opens on the release instead of the press. With a panel open
    (inventory, loot, shop), Back goes to the panel as before.
  - VR: Menu hold pulses Back (a tap: the inventory, as before); the wrist menu has Activities and Invites
    entries; the right hand aims an open ring as it aims the emote wheel.
- Not opened while a page, the loading screen or a modal is up (`ClientWorld.MenuOpen`), nor while a text
  field or a panel holds `UiFocus`. It holds `UiFocus` while open.
- **Hints**: "Quick menu" is in the corner key list everywhere (`ClientWorld` hints); F1 has a "Quick menu
  and invites" group.
- **Checks**: `--quickmenu [delay] [wedge]` opens it latched at boot, aimed at a wedge, for a screenshot
  (`--uishot <png> <s>`); `tools/invitecheck.sh` holds F2 and pad Back.
