# The stamina ring (#1225)

`src/Ui/StaminaRing.cs` and `shaders/stamina_ring.gdshader`, created by `PlayerFeel` on its HUD layer;
the VR copy `src/XR/XrStaminaRing.cs` (a child of `XrWatch` on the left wrist).

## Rule

- **Nothing on screen unless in use** (`ui/style-guide`): drawn only while the meter is not full or winded,
  fading out 1.5 s after it fills; hidden (not drawn) at full and where the meter does not count.
- **Where**: third person 34 px beside the right shoulder (`UnprojectPosition(chest + right * 0.5)`),
  pushed out of the centre 20 % of the screen (`ux-guidelines`: keep the centre clear); first person,
  behind the camera, or with no camera: low right of the crosshair.
- **One shader on a `ColorRect`**: the inner ring holds the first 100 points; upgrades add a thin outer
  ring in 10-point segments (up to 125 points of a circle), so the ring never changes size. The next
  climb-jump's cost is a lighter band at the end of the fill. Winded is orange **and** blinks **and** a
  panting breath plays (`SfxSynth.PantBank`): never colour alone. Uniforms set only when they change
  (static `StringName`s).
- **VR**: the same control in a 64 px `SubViewport`, on a billboard quad beside the watch dial, headset
  layer only; the viewport renders only while the ring shows.
- `--stamina-shot [points]` (windowed dev aid): holds the meter at that many points (62 by default), saves
  `test_output/stamina_ring_third.png` and `stamina_ring_first.png`, quits.
