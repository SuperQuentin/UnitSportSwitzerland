# Chat speech bubbles over players' heads (#862, `Ui/Bubbles/ChatBubbles`)

- **What**: a player who says something in the chat near you gets the films' comic bubble
  (`Ui/Bubbles/SpeechBubble`, the same drawing as the trailer's `Shot.Bubbles`) over their head
  for the line's reading time plus a second (`BubbleGeometry.ReadingTime`, at most 9 s), typed
  in and popped. One bubble per speaker: a new line replaces it, at most 8 at once (the oldest goes).
- **Which lines**: only `ChatKind.Say` lines a player typed. Commands (`/...`), system, admin,
  private and error lines never. Online the server says who spoke: `ChatManager.Say(peer, text)`
  broadcasts the line and the `Said(peer, text)` RPC, raised on clients as
  `ChatManager.Spoken(peer, text)`; offline `HandleLocal` raises it with `ChatManager.OfflineSelf`
  (you). `ClientWorld` resolves the peer to its body (`Players/<peer>`, or `LocalPlayer` offline).
  A bot that talks calls `ChatManager.Say` with its peer id (`bots/bot-capability-map`).
- **Shapes from the text**: a line in capitals (4 letters or more, none small) or ending `!!` is a
  shout (spiky), a line in parentheses a thought (cloud, the parentheses dropped); otherwise said.
- **When it shows**: within `ChatBubbles.Range` (36 m) of the camera, in front of it, its head
  on screen, and not your own head seen from inside it (first person: the camera within 1.2 m of
  your `HeadTop`). Hidden by distance or view, it keeps its time and comes back if you turn.
  The tail points at `FootPlayer.HeadTop` (eye height + 0.32 m; on a machine, its seat's eye
  height; a passenger, over its host).
- **Flat screen**: a `CanvasLayer` at layer 4 (over the world, under the prompts 11, the chat 15
  and the menus), lettering 2.6 % of the window's height, placed by `BubbleLayout` like the films'
  (on screen, none over another or over a speaker's head; a bubble pushed far away goes under the
  others). The body and the tail's end are **eased** toward the layout's spot (`BodyEase` 8/s,
  `TipEase` 18/s, frame-rate independent), snapped on the first frame on screen: placed raw every
  frame, a head's jitter on screen and a layout flipping between two spots shook the tail (#1140).
- **VR** (`XrSession.Active` when the line arrives): the 2D layers are only a panel in the headset,
  so a 2D overlay would float on the panel, not over the head. Each bubble is drawn in a small
  `SubViewport` of its own (2D only, updated while visible) shown on a `Sprite3D` billboard whose
  bottom (the tail's end) sits on the head, 1.4 m wide at hand and scaled up with distance
  (x1 to x3.2 from 9 m) so it still reads at 36 m. Not checked in a headset yet (no `--xrsim` run).
- **Setting**: `GameSettings.ChatBubbles`, Settings › Gameplay › *Speech bubbles*, on by default;
  turning it off drops the bubbles on screen at the next frame.
- **Cost**: nothing per frame while nobody talks (`_Process` returns on an empty list). With
  bubbles up: one projection and a placement of at most 8 rectangles, no allocation; a bubble
  redraws only when its tail's end moves by half a pixel, its text and outline built once per line.
- **Check**: `<godot> --path . -- --autostart --style cartoon --bubblecheck` stands on foot in
  third person, sends `/time 13` (must make no bubble), a line (a said bubble) and a line in
  capitals (a shout), and writes `test_output/bubbles/{command,say,shout}.png`; `[bubbles] RESULT`.
  Checked at the Gornergrat (`--at 2626790,1092478`), Cartoon: a said bubble and a shout over your own head.
