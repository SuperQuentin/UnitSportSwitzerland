# The Journal (J): quests, birds, fish, and the Adventure plans' tabs (#1223)

`Ui/AdventureLog` (a `Screen` on the shell's stack, like Activities), `Ui/QuestsPage`, `Ui/QuestTracker`,
`Ui/QuestToasts`. The bird journal is no longer a panel of its own: `Birds/BirdJournal` keeps the data
and builds the **Birds** and **Fish** pages.

## Rule

- **A plan adds its page with `AdventureLog.RegisterTab(id, title, order, build, shown)`**, never by
  editing the Journal: Quests 10, Atlas 20, Collection 30, Travel 40, Birds 50, Fish 60. `build` makes
  the page each time the Journal opens; `shown` hides an adventure tab while `AdventureLog.Adventuring`
  is false (the system off, a Battle Royale round). Birds and Fish always show.
- Pages rebuild **on `AdventureService.SectionChanged`**, comparing a shape string (the Activities
  screen's way), plus a 1 s tick for what moves without a sync (distance, pack). Never per frame.
- Open it with `AdventureLog.Open(tab)` (`Toggle()` for J). It remembers the last tab.

## Ways in (keyboard, pad, VR, touch)

| Action | Keyboard | Pad | VR | Touch |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| open / close the Journal (`bird_journal`, id kept so rebinds carry over) | J (also closes) | quick menu (hold Back) › **Journal**; B closes | wrist menu › Journal; phone › Journal | More › Journal; phone › Journal |
| tabs | Q / E, click | LB / RB | laser on the tab | tap |
| list, buttons (Track, Start, Hand over, Abandon) | arrows, Enter, mouse | D-pad, A; B back | laser + trigger | tap |

- **Hand over**: the button in the quest's details, enabled only with the item in the pack and inside
  the target's radius (it says how far otherwise). The `interact` key on a target's marker comes with
  navigation's markers (gap until then, `xr/vr-action-map`).

## The page

- Left: Running / Available / Done, the tracked one marked ◆, the canton beside the title. Right: the
  blurb, the steps (✓ done, ▶ current with its count in amber, later ones hidden as "…" unless
  `Reveal`), the rewards, then **Track** (primary), **Start**, **Hand over**, and **Abandon** at the far
  right in the warning colour, confirmed ("Abandon \"…\"?" / Keep it). A disabled button says why
  under it. Header: the title worn, quests done, and "Progress not saved on this server" (warning
  colour) when the server keeps nothing for us. A status line for the server's refusals and notes.
- Empty: "No quests yet." and where new ones come from.

## HUD

- **Tracker**: right edge at 64 % height (below the invite cards), glass panel: title (amber), the step
  with `count/needed`, the distance (4 Hz, rounded to 10 m, then km). Fades out while a page is open, in
  a Battle Royale round, and over 22 m/s. Texts set only on change.
- **Toasts**: top centre, 3 s, "Quest started", "Step done: … / Next: …", "Quest complete: …" with the
  rewards; a bell (`QuestToasts.Chime`) or a three-bell arpeggio (`Fanfare`), both static, so they
  show in the sound player.
