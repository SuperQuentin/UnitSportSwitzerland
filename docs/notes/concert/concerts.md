# Concerts: random artist events, stages and a DMX light rig (#885)

`src/Concert/`. Plan: `docs/plans/stadium-concert.md` (this is phase 2 and the desk half of phase 3).

## The event (`ConcertEvent`, `ConcertPlanner`)

- Every stadium has a concert every day: `ConcertPlanner.Tonight(stadium, date)`, seeded by the
  stadium's name and the date (FNV-1a, never `string.GetHashCode`, which is randomised per run). Pure:
  every peer builds the same event, nothing is sent.
- A seed picks the genre (rock, pop, electronic, hip-hop, ballad), the layout (end stage south 34 %,
  north 16 %, thrust 16 %, in the round 14 %, side east or west 10 % each), the screens (none, centre,
  sides, both; the round gets the hung cube or nothing), an artist name and tour, a palette by genre,
  a tempo, the stage's size and the rig's counts (an EDM rig is beams and strobes, a ballad's spots
  and washes).
- The stage stands from `ConcertEvent.BuildHour` (08:00) to the show's end (#956); the show plays
  `StartHour`..`EndHour` of the game clock (20:30 to 23:30), a walk-in look before. At the end the
  director strikes the rig (stage, floor, crowd) and the stadium stands empty until the next day's
  build (`ConcertDirector._Process`, every 2 s). `--concert N` plays seed N at any hour and a hosted
  set stands whatever the hour; `--concert off` puts on none.
- A stadium's pitch (not an arena's floor or an open air's field) is boarded for the crowd
  (`ConcertFloor`, #956): 2 m protection panels inside the seating front's rounded corners, grass
  left under the stage (`StagePlan.Grass`, the deck, its wings and its back). The terrain under a
  pitch is not flat, so the director lays the floor 8 cm over its highest point (sampled every 4 m,
  3 m off the stands, 1.2 m at most); one collision quad, and the crowd stands on it.

## The stage and the rig (`StagePlan`)

- Built in the stage's own frame (x across, z towards the audience, z = 0 the deck's front edge) and
  placed in the stadium frame by the layout. End stage: deck, four towers, a roof grid (front, mid,
  back trusses), canopy, black scrim, wings, flown PA and ground subs, centre LED wall, side screens
  (kept inside the run-off: `concertcheck` found them in the stands on wide stages), stairs at the
  back corners (a ramp to walk, steps to see), barrier, FOH riser, two delay towers. Thrust: a 30 m
  catwalk and a 12 m B-stage. In the round: a square deck, a grid on four corner towers, a PA hang per
  corner, the screen cube.
- Fixtures by position as a designer hangs them: spots and blinders on the front truss, beams and
  strobes on the mid, washes on the back, beams on the deck's front edge (and round a B-stage), pixel
  bars on the deck's skirt, two hazers. A round rig faces out on all four sides.

## DMX (`Dmx.cs`)

- Real channel layouts per type: an 18-channel profile spot (CMY, wheel, gobo, prism, zoom), a
  14-channel beam (wheel), a 16-channel RGBW zoom wash, a 4-lite blinder, a 4-channel strobe, an
  8-cell RGB pixel bar (24 ch), a 9-channel media-server layer per screen, a 2-channel hazer. Pan 540°
  and tilt 270°, 16-bit. Patched in order into 512-channel universes, never straddling one: 76 to 126
  fixtures, 3 or 4 universes.
- Pan/tilt geometry: tilt tips the beam from the yoke's axis (`Fixture.Up`: down for a hung head,
  up on the floor) towards the fixture's home side (`Fixture.Home`), pan turns that about the axis.
  Shutter: 0..7 closed, 8..15 open, 16..131 strobe 1..20 Hz.

## The desk (`LightDesk`) and the rig (`ConcertRig`)

- The desk is the only writer of the universes; the rig only reads them, so Art-Net or sACN (phase 5)
  can replace the desk without touching the rig. A pure function of the show time on the shared clock
  (`ClockSync.ServerNow`): every peer shows the same frame.
- Without music, songs are made up: 16-bar sections calm, groove, chorus, groove, peak, chorus (groove
  and chorus swap by the seed), a bar of blackout between songs. Looks: walk-in blue washes; calm warm
  spots on the artist and swaying washes; groove a breathing beam fan with a chase, pixel chase, bars
  on the screens; chorus everything up, blinders on each downbeat; peak ballyhoo with strobing beams,
  strobes, flashing screens. The music (the CD's sections, kick and beat grid) replaces the made-up
  songs in phase 3.
- Drawn: moving-head bodies turned with their beams, additive cones (vertex alpha fading down the
  beam, instance colour × dimmer × shutter × haze; no volumetrics on the mobile renderer), billboard
  lens flares, blinder/strobe/pixel cells, screens as pixel grids playing the layer's clip, six real
  spot lights off the front truss and one omni for the crowd (kept low: at energy 6 / 3 they blew the
  pitch out to neon). Nothing per frame allocates (the desk's effect delegates are made once).
- `ConcertDirector` hangs a `ConcertRig` on the owning tile's node at `TileFurnished`, turned to the
  stadium frame and set on the pitch. Client only: the server has no stage collision (bots would walk
  through the deck; a follow-up if bots attend).

## Checks

- `--concertcheck --systems ui` (quick): determinism, every layout/screen/genre in 300 seeds, patches
  inside their universes, every stage box on the pitch and under the roof, 16-bit round-trip, the
  sections light as they should (blackout dark, chorus over calm). Prints seeds 1..24's events.
- Model viewer `--modelsonly concert`: one stage per layout, live. In the stadium at night:
  `--chunks <main>/terrain_chunks --at 2498751,1114890 --origin 2498751,1114890 --concert 13
  --shot-queue <file>` with `/time set 22:00` first (13: an EDM thrust, 3: in the round, 14: a rock side
  stage, 5: a pop end stage).

## Show control from Chataigne, TouchDesigner or a console (#885)

- **Protocols** (`Protocols/ShowProtocols.cs`, pure, `ShowProtocolsTests`): Art-Net 4 (UDP 6454:
  ArtDmx, ArtPoll answered with ArtPollReply so the controller lists the game as a node), sACN E1.31
  (UDP 5568, multicast 239.255.hi.lo per universe; terminated and preview streams skipped), OSC 1.0
  (UDP 9000: messages and nested bundles, i f s T F N).
- **Input** (`ShowInput`, the operator's game only, off by default): `/concert input artnet|sacn|osc|all
  [on|off]`, `/concert input status`. Client-local commands (`ConcertService.RunLocal`, before the
  line goes to the server). Numbering: the rig's universe i = Art-Net port-address i, sACN universe
  i + 1. `/concert patch` writes `user://concert/patch_<seed>.csv`: id, group, type, footprint, universe,
  Art-Net port-address, sACN universe, 1-based address, the channel layout — map it in the controller.
- **Hosting** (`ConcertService` at `World/Concerts`, server + every client): `/concert host [seed]`
  (admins; offline, always) or OSC `/concert/host <seed>`: the server keeps the seed and the operator,
  tells every peer and every late joiner (`SendTo`), everyone rebuilds that stage and rig
  (`ConcertDirector.Rebuild`); a hosted set plays at any hour. `/concert release`, or the operator
  leaving (`PeerLeft`), gives tonight's back. `/concert status`. `/concert join` (anyone, #1173) teleports
  to a hosted set (`net/chat-commands`).
- **DMX live**: universes the input reads play at once on the operator's rig and go to the server at
  most 30 Hz each, `UnreliableOrdered` on ENet channel 4 (2 is tiles, 3 the web radio); the server
  takes them only from the operator and relays them to players within 1.5 km of the stadium. A rig
  plays outside DMX for 2 s after the last frame (`ConcertRig.TakeUniverse`, `ExternalLive`), then its
  desk again. About 0.5 Mbit/s per listener for a 4-universe rig at 30 Hz.
- **OSC cues** drive the built-in desk when no DMX streams: `/concert/go` (next section), `/concert/section
  <name|n|-1>`, `/concert/bpm <bpm>` (from a shared-clock instant: the beat counts on), `/concert/blackout
  <0|1>`, `/concert/master <0..1>`. Reliable, kept by the server for late joiners.
- Protocol 37. Check: `tools/concertnetcheck.sh` (server + two clients on the fixture world at the
  stadium: A logs in, hosts set 7, turns its input on, plays a controller to its own ports; B gets the
  set, the relayed universes byte for byte, the blackout cue, the release). Seen in the stadium:
  a script reading the patch CSV and sending Art-Net turned every head, wash and screen red.

## The music (`ConcertMusic`)

- A setlist of up to 12 of the server's CDs (`CdLibrary`, positive ids, not the rat beat, over 30 s),
  shuffled by the event's seed, played back to back with a 6 s gap from the PA hangs
  (`StagePlan.PaHangs`): `RadioSpeaker`s with a stadium's `Reach` (650 m) and `Unit` (40 m), fetched and
  kept in sync as a radio's are. The setlist loops on the shared clock (`ServerNow % cycle`): every
  peer on the same song and second, nothing sent.
- The desk reads the song (`MusicMoment`): its analysed section (calm, groove, peak, chorus) cues the
  look, its beat grid clocks the effects, its kick (`RadioGroove`) hits the dimmers, its loudness rides
  the master (0.6 + 0.4 level); the gap is a blackout. A held section or a blackout from the operator
  still wins. No CDs: the desk makes its songs up as before.
- Anyone within 160 m of the stadium dances to it (E): `ConcertRig.MusicNear` in `RadioManager.NearestMusic`.
- **Pogo QTE** (#1150, `PogoQte` HUD, `PogoBeat` judge, tier 0 `PogoBeatTests`): while dancing at a
  concert a ring closes on each beat **as heard** (the loudest hang's `Heard.Delay` plus
  `AudioServer.GetOutputLatency()` off the song clock). `{jump}` within 70 ms is Perfect, 140 ms Good
  (never more than 0.4 beat a side), else Miss; twice on one beat is a Miss. A hit sets `DanceId` to
  the Pogo emote (replicated: every peer pogos on the song clock's beat, as the lights do); two beats
  let go by in a row set it back to 1. `FootPlayer.JumpTaken` keeps the press from also jumping.
  Combo and best combo are this screen's only. Space / pad A / VR pad A; F1 row; bots: `bot-capability-map`.

- The song's finer bands (`MusicMoment.Snare/Hat/Bass/Bright/Build/SinceDrop`, CD analysis v2,
  `docs/notes/audio/cd-beat.md`) ride over the section's look (`LightDesk.Accents`): a snare pops the
  strobes, the hats sparkle the pixel bars, the bass holds the groove washes up, brightness lifts the
  beams; a build sinks the washes, closes the beams and lifts them to the sky, speeds the strobes and
  fills the pixel bars; the drop hits the blinders and fires CO2, flames and gerbs on its first beat.
  Looks move per beat and bar (wash sway 0.5..2 cycles a beat, beam fans per bar, chases over two beats).

- **Phrases** (`LightDesk.Phrase`, every 2 bars from the seed): Sync, Stagger (a wave), Mirror (from
  the centre), Alternate (odd/even), Sparse (one in three), Solo (two heads), Cycle (one at a time round
  the row, a sixteenth each), PingPong. Each head moves on its own lag (`Lag`), lit or not by `Mask`;
  a chorus's first bars, a build and 4 s after a drop sync everything; calm verses stay in sync. Groups
  sit out phrases (spots, floor beams).
- **Rhythm pump** (`Rhythm`): each head's dimmer flashes on its beat (behind it by its lag) and decays,
  or follows the song's kick in sync; depth by section (calm 0.2, chorus 0.4, groove 0.65, peak 0.8),
  washes half as deep.
- **Motors** (`ConcertRig.Motor`): the DMX pan/tilt is a target; each head's steppers chase it at a top
  speed and acceleration (beam 300°/s, 1800°/s²; spot 220, 1100; wash 160, 700; tilt 0.9 of pan) and
  brake to stop on it, so a jump on the desk is a sweep on the rig, as on real fixtures.
- **Beat debug** (`ConcertBeatDebug`, `/concert debug`, the desk's Beat debug button): 8 s of loudness,
  kick, snare, hats, bass, brightness with beats and bars ruled, the song's sections, builds and drops
  with the playhead, and the desk's section, bar, phrase, pump and what fires. Explainer of the
  analysis: CdAnalysis v2 in `docs/notes/audio/cd-beat.md`.

- **Strobe bursts** (`LightDesk.StrobeBursts`, `Strobing`): the last quarter of a build, 0.8 s after a
  drop, one bar in four at a peak. The strobes at 12 Hz (rate 11/24), the lit heads' shutters with them
  (83, 12.07 Hz). The rig (`_strobeHz` from the strobes' channels) sinks the LED walls toward black between
  flashes and burns them white on each, the IMAG feeds too; the artists jump to a new pose on each flash
  (one of a move's eight hits, `ConcertPerformers.Step(strobeHz, t)`), the crowd's beat freezes between
  flashes. A peak's own strobes run slower (rate 0.15) outside a burst.

## The PA (`ConcertPa`, `shaders/concert/pa.gdshader`)

- `StagePlan.Pa`: line arrays flown off the wings (a J of ~0.55 m boxes, about 25° over the hang),
  subs under the deck's edge (a hidden `StageBox` keeps their collision), delay hangs on the FOH towers,
  the round's four corner hangs turned out. One mesh, built once; every vertex carries its box's pivot
  and facing (CUSTOM0/1), and the shader swells the boxes and pushes the woofers on the `kick` uniform
  (subs harder, with `bass`): one draw, two uniforms a frame.
- Not a MultiMesh on purpose: headless, the z-fight scan reads every instance at the origin, and 180
  stacked instances made `--zfightcheck` take over 10 minutes.

## Performance (measured Oct 2026, Geneva, concert 13)

- On the deck, without effects the concert costs little over the bare world (13 against 12.3 ms); what
  spiked it was fill: the effects' particles (CO2, flames, gerbs, confetti: 24..37 fps) and the low fog
  (5..8 m billboards facing a camera standing in them). Now fewer, bigger particles, confetti opaque,
  the fog a low sheet turning about Y only; beams draw their cones' far side only (half the fill), three
  real lamps instead of six. The stadium has an occluder (`Terrain/Stadiums/StadiumOccluder`, hung by
  `ConcertDirector` on every stadium's tile, show or not): the facade ring and the roof's solid part,
  so the city behind the stands is culled. Absolute times vary a lot with what else the machine runs:
  compare within one run (`--shot-queue` with `/concert host 13`, `/concert cue <section|fx>` lines).

### From the floor

- Before: 14 ms a frame against 8.4 ms with no concert. The IMAG SubViewport rendered the whole world
  again out to 3 km (21.7 M primitives against 10.9 M), and the artists' meshes were rebuilt every frame
  (3 ms of CPU for one figure).
- Now ~10.3 ms: the IMAG camera's `Far` is 450 m and it renders every other frame; the artists' poses
  are cached (`ConcertPerformers._poses`, 8 a beat over a move's two bars, at most one new pose a frame).
  A pose is cached as surface arrays (with their format: the figure's custom channels) and copied into
  the artist's own mesh, never swapped in as another mesh: the figure material's face parameters are
  `instance uniform`s, and Godot registers them again for every new mesh on an instance without
  freeing the old, until "Too many instances using shader instance variables" floods the log (13 840
  errors in one play session, worst in busy, fast sections).
  The rest of the rig is under 0.5 ms (desk, effects, rays, heads, screens).

### Streaming the stage in (#1264)

- Walking round Paléo hitched: the stage's tile committed for 1.05-1.6 s on the main thread (under 45 ms with
  `--concert off`). `ConcertRig._Ready` took 1.35 s: the crowd's two 8 s loops synthesised on first use (740 ms,
  now `ConcertSound.Prewarm` on a thread from `ConcertDirector._Ready`, the loops `Lazy`), the crowd's placement
  (`StagePlan.Blocked` x 404 boxes x 18 000 field points, 300 ms, now `StagePlan.BlockedTester` with each box's axes
  worked out once) and two interop calls a person (now one `MultiMesh.Buffer`). Left: ~0.45 s, mostly the performers'
  poses (95 ms), the PA and effects. Find such a stall with `--perflog 90` and `commits.csv` (the slowest commit's tile),
  against the same run with `--concert off`.

## Songs by hand (#994)

- `/concert song` (anyone): what plays at the venue nearest you, n/N, time into it, what comes next; `/concert song list`
  the setlist numbered. `next|skip`, `prev` (back to this song's start after 5 s, else the one before), `restart`,
  `<n|title words>`, `stop|play` (the PA silent, the desk makes its songs up), `auto` (back to the clock): the show's
  operator on a hosted set, an admin on tonight's show; offline the player. Code: `ConcertService.Songs.cs`.
- A cue is `SongCue(Index, At, Off)`: song `Index` of the setlist started at `At` on the shared clock and the set runs on
  from it. `ConcertMusic.Position` (pure) puts it on the set's loop, the same on the server (which builds the setlist
  from its own `CdLibrary`, no rig) and every peer. One per venue, `SongState` RPC (protocol 43), sent to late joiners;
  hosting a set, releasing it or the operator leaving clears it (the index belongs to that set's setlist).
- `/concert host <word>` is refused (it used to read the word as no seed and host a new random set each time:
  `/concert host status`). `--concertcheck` covers the position math, `concertnetcheck` the refusal and the reply.

## Jazz and a season (#1394)

- `Genre.Jazz` is appended to the genre enum and **never drawn** by `ConcertPlanner.Plan`: only a venue with a `StadiumSpec.Season` (Montreux's lake stage, `07-fri#1+15`) forces it, through
  `ConcertPlanner.Tonight` -> `Jazz(stadium, year, night)`. Outside its season such a venue has no stage and no crowd (`ConcertPlanner.Open`; `--concert N` and a hosted set override it); every other
  row has `Season = null` and plays every night.
- A jazz night is small (a 12 x 7 m deck, 4.5 m roof, 33-35 fixtures in one universe: no strobe, blinder or pyro; amber / deep blue / magenta), its songs made up on the set's own clock
  (`JazzSet`: songs, the call for the bis, the bis, a rest; `LightDesk.JazzLook`, never a strobe), played by a quartet of skinned figures at fixed posts with four props that sway as nodes
  (`JazzInstruments`), to a seated lawn (`crowd.gdshader` `seated`). A jazz night's setlist is the `CdLibrary.JazzUrls` playlist only (empty: lights only), every other stage's excludes it.
  Details, the games and the checks: `occasions/festival-montreuxjazz`.
