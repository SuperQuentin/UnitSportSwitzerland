# Concerts: random artist events, stages and a DMX light rig (#885)

`src/Concert/`. Plan: `docs/plans/stadium-concert.md` (this is phase 2 and the desk half of phase 3).

## The event (`ConcertEvent`, `ConcertPlanner`)

- Every stadium has a concert every day: `ConcertPlanner.Tonight(stadium, date)`, seeded by the
  stadium's name and the date (FNV-1a, never `string.GetHashCode`, which is randomised per run). Pure:
  every peer builds the same event, nothing is sent.
- A seed picks the genre (rock, pop, electronic, hip-hop, ballad), the layout (end stage south 34 %,
  north 16 %, thrust 16 %, in the round 14 %, side east or west 10 % each), the screens (none, centre,
  sides, both; the round gets the hung cube or nothing), an artist name and tour, a palette by genre,
  a tempo, the stage's size and the rig's counts (an EDM rig is beams and strobes, a ballad's spots
  and washes).
- The stage stands all day; the show plays `StartHour`..`EndHour` of the game clock (20:30 to 23:30),
  a walk-in look otherwise. `--concert N` plays seed N at any hour, `--concert off` puts on none.

## The stage and the rig (`StagePlan`)

- Built in the stage's own frame (x across, z towards the audience, z = 0 the deck's front edge) and
  placed in the stadium frame by the layout. End stage: deck, four towers, a roof grid (front, mid,
  back trusses), canopy, black scrim, wings, flown PA and ground subs, centre LED wall, side screens
  (kept inside the run-off: `concertcheck` found them in the stands on wide stages), stairs at the
  back corners (a ramp to walk, steps to see), barrier, FOH riser, two delay towers. Thrust: a 30 m
  catwalk and a 12 m B-stage. In the round: a square deck, a grid on four corner towers, a PA hang per
  corner, the screen cube.
- Fixtures by position as a designer hangs them: spots and blinders on the front truss, beams and
  strobes on the mid, washes on the back, beams on the deck's front edge (and round a B-stage), pixel
  bars on the deck's skirt, two hazers. A round rig faces out on all four sides.

## DMX (`Dmx.cs`)

- Real channel layouts per type: an 18-channel profile spot (CMY, wheel, gobo, prism, zoom), a
  14-channel beam (wheel), a 16-channel RGBW zoom wash, a 4-lite blinder, a 4-channel strobe, an
  8-cell RGB pixel bar (24 ch), a 9-channel media-server layer per screen, a 2-channel hazer. Pan 540°
  and tilt 270°, 16-bit. Patched in order into 512-channel universes, never straddling one: 76 to 126
  fixtures, 3 or 4 universes.
- Pan/tilt geometry: tilt tips the beam from the yoke's axis (`Fixture.Up`: down for a hung head,
  up on the floor) towards the fixture's home side (`Fixture.Home`), pan turns that about the axis.
  Shutter: 0..7 closed, 8..15 open, 16..131 strobe 1..20 Hz.

## The desk (`LightDesk`) and the rig (`ConcertRig`)

- The desk is the only writer of the universes; the rig only reads them, so Art-Net or sACN (phase 5)
  can replace the desk without touching the rig. A pure function of the show time on the shared clock
  (`ClockSync.ServerNow`): every peer shows the same frame.
- Without music, songs are made up: 16-bar sections calm, groove, chorus, groove, peak, chorus (groove
  and chorus swap by the seed), a bar of blackout between songs. Looks: walk-in blue washes; calm warm
  spots on the artist and swaying washes; groove a breathing beam fan with a chase, pixel chase, bars
  on the screens; chorus everything up, blinders on each downbeat; peak ballyhoo with strobing beams,
  strobes, flashing screens. The music (the CD's sections, kick and beat grid) replaces the made-up
  songs in phase 3.
- Drawn: moving-head bodies turned with their beams, additive cones (vertex alpha fading down the
  beam, instance colour × dimmer × shutter × haze; no volumetrics on the mobile renderer), billboard
  lens flares, blinder/strobe/pixel cells, screens as pixel grids playing the layer's clip, six real
  spot lights off the front truss and one omni for the crowd (kept low: at energy 6 / 3 they blew the
  pitch out to neon). Nothing per frame allocates (the desk's effect delegates are made once).
- `ConcertDirector` hangs a `ConcertRig` on the owning tile's node at `TileFurnished`, turned to the
  stadium frame and set on the pitch. Client only: the server has no stage collision (bots would walk
  through the deck; a follow-up if bots attend).

## Checks

- `--concertcheck --systems ui` (quick): determinism, every layout/screen/genre in 300 seeds, patches
  inside their universes, every stage box on the pitch and under the roof, 16-bit round-trip, the
  sections light as they should (blackout dark, chorus over calm). Prints seeds 1..24's events.
- Model viewer `--modelsonly concert`: one stage per layout, live. In the stadium at night:
  `--chunks <main>/terrain_chunks --at 2498751,1114890 --origin 2498751,1114890 --concert 13
  --shot-queue <file>` with `/time set 22:00` first (13: an EDM thrust, 3: in the round, 14: a rock side
  stage, 5: a pop end stage).

## Show control from Chataigne, TouchDesigner or a console (#885)

- **Protocols** (`Protocols/ShowProtocols.cs`, pure, `ShowProtocolsTests`): Art-Net 4 (UDP 6454:
  ArtDmx, ArtPoll answered with ArtPollReply so the controller lists the game as a node), sACN E1.31
  (UDP 5568, multicast 239.255.hi.lo per universe; terminated and preview streams skipped), OSC 1.0
  (UDP 9000: messages and nested bundles, i f s T F N).
- **Input** (`ShowInput`, the operator's game only, off by default): `/concert input artnet|sacn|osc|all
  [on|off]`, `/concert input status`. Client-local commands (`ConcertService.RunLocal`, before the
  line goes to the server). Numbering: the rig's universe i = Art-Net port-address i, sACN universe
  i + 1. `/concert patch` writes `user://concert/patch_<seed>.csv`: id, group, type, footprint, universe,
  Art-Net port-address, sACN universe, 1-based address, the channel layout — map it in the controller.
- **Hosting** (`ConcertService` at `World/Concerts`, server + every client): `/concert host [seed]`
  (admins; offline, always) or OSC `/concert/host <seed>`: the server keeps the seed and the operator,
  tells every peer and every late joiner (`SendTo`), everyone rebuilds that stage and rig
  (`ConcertDirector.Rebuild`); a hosted set plays at any hour. `/concert release`, or the operator
  leaving (`PeerLeft`), gives tonight's back. `/concert status`.
- **DMX live**: universes the input reads play at once on the operator's rig and go to the server at
  most 30 Hz each, `UnreliableOrdered` on ENet channel 4 (2 is tiles, 3 the web radio); the server
  takes them only from the operator and relays them to players within 1.5 km of the stadium. A rig
  plays outside DMX for 2 s after the last frame (`ConcertRig.TakeUniverse`, `ExternalLive`), then its
  desk again. About 0.5 Mbit/s per listener for a 4-universe rig at 30 Hz.
- **OSC cues** drive the built-in desk when no DMX streams: `/concert/go` (next section), `/concert/section
  <name|n|-1>`, `/concert/bpm <bpm>` (from a shared-clock instant: the beat counts on), `/concert/blackout
  <0|1>`, `/concert/master <0..1>`. Reliable, kept by the server for late joiners.
- Protocol 37. Check: `tools/concertnetcheck.sh` (server + two clients on the fixture world at the
  stadium: A logs in, hosts set 7, turns its input on, plays a controller to its own ports; B gets the
  set, the relayed universes byte for byte, the blackout cue, the release). Seen in the stadium:
  a script reading the patch CSV and sending Art-Net turned every head, wash and screen red.
