# Stadium sound: the PA as a stadium hears it, the room, the crowd (#984)

`src/Concert/ConcertAcoustics.cs` (pure, unit-tested), `ConcertSpeaker.cs`, `ConcertSound.cs`,
`ConcertSoundProbe.cs`. Cheap: no new voice for the PA, two looping crowd voices, two bus effects.

## The PA (`ConcertSpeaker`, a `RadioSpeaker` per hang)
- **Delay**: each hang is late by its distance / 343 m/s (`StartedAt = SongStart + delay`). The desk,
  the lights and the dances keep the song's own clock (`ConcertMusic.SongStartedAt`): the far stand sees
  the flash ~0.4 s before it hears the kick, as in a real stadium. Off the axis the two hangs flam.
- **Moving listener**: the delay changes as you walk; the speaker pulls onto it with its pitch
  (gain 0.5, at most ±3 %, 15 ms deadband: the Doppler shift) instead of seeking. Past ~17 m/s it seeks as a radio does.
- **Delay towers** (`StagePlan.Pa` cabinets that are not by a hang) are timed to the mains: they add
  level (-6 dB against a main hang) and highs where they cover, never a second arrival.
- **Throw**: a line array, 0 dB at 40 m, -3 dB a doubling to 80 m, -6 beyond, at most +4 under it.
  Godot's inverse-distance falloff stays on; the speaker adds the difference (`PointDb`).
- **Air**: cutoff where ~6 dB of the highs is lost (ISO 9613-1, ~f^1.8): 8 kHz at 50 m, 4.4 at 150 m, 1.5 kHz floor.
  It is the 3D player's attenuation filter, min'd with `Hearing`'s wall cutoff.
- **Coverage**: full within 50° of a hang's axis, down to -12 dB and 500 Hz straight behind (behind the stage).
- **Outside the bowl** (past the facade footprint, 12 m ramp): -9 dB and 650 Hz, scaled by the share of
  `Hearing`'s rays NOT blocked, so a stand that already muffles is not counted twice.
- The hangs of one PA never duck each other (#813 focus); a radio still ducks under them.

## The room (`ConcertSound`, bus `Concert` under `Music`)
- `AudioEffectDelay` two taps from image sources behind the four stands' fronts (`Echoes`, `Taps`: the
  loudest, and one clearly later; twin side stands become one tap from the middle); `ReflectDb` -9 dB
  stadium (seats and crowd soak), -6 arena, none in an open air or outside the bowl; off under -30 dB.
  FOH at Geneva: sides 131 ms -14 dB, far stand 354 ms -20 dB.
- `AudioEffectReverb` by `VenueKind` (size/damp/wet/predelay): stadium 0.5/0.6/0.07/55 ms, arena
  0.82/0.42/0.16/28 ms, open air 0.22/0.85/0.025/8 ms. Wet rises as the direct sound falls
  (`wet x 10^(-directDb/40)`, cap 0.45): the far stand hears more room.
- Effects made once (`EnsureBuses`), written only when a value moves, polled at 5 Hz; enabled only
  while a song plays; the nearest concert owns the bus (10 m hysteresis).

## The crowd (bus `Crowd` under `Sfx`: the cabin filter applies)
- Two stereo 8 s loops, static so in the sound player: `ConcertSound.CrowdRoar` (hubbub, 60 formant
  yells a side, whistles) and `CrowdApplause` (12 close clappers over 1000 far claps a second).
- `ConcertAcoustics.Crowd`, shifted by the ears' delay (they react to what they hear): bed -24..-18 dB
  with the song's level, chorus +7 (singalong swell: level only, nothing sung), peak +3, build up to
  +8, drop -3 dB decaying 3 dB/s, a song's first 5 s a recognition cheer and claps; the gap: cheers
  -5 and applause -4 fading, the finale's longer, a swell in the last 1.5 s before the next song.
- `CrowdAt`: full in the bowl / open-air field; outside -12 dB, 500 Hz, -6 dB a doubling past 40 m; silent past 600 m.

## Checking it: `--concertsoundprobe`

Prints the model at seven spots, then stands the body at each and reads the speakers, the buses' peaks
and the master's level and centroid, jogs 25 m (pitch pull vs seek), a timeline through the next gap,
and the main-thread cost. RESULT line last. Centroids across spots are confounded by the music
changing between samples: read them as a hint, the model lines as the numbers.
