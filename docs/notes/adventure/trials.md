# Trials (#1227)

`Trials.cs` (pure: `TrialDef`, courses, par, medals, `TrialBoard`, `TrialRuns`, `GhostTrack`), `TrialRunner` (client),
the trial part of `RewardService`.

## Rules

- **A trial is made from its id** (`tr:<poiId>:run|glide`), the same on every peer, so a forged one cannot start
  (`Trials.Resolve`). **Run**: from a chapel, hut, tower or castle to the nearest place 0.4-3 km off (not a bus stop or a
  lake), a gate every 200 m on the ground (25 m wide), on foot (max pace 14 m/s). **Glide**: from a peak, a cross or a
  viewpoint to the nearest place 2-8 km off and 300 m lower, a gate every 400 m on the straight glide from 30 m over the
  start, never under the ground + 25 m (40 m wide), wings (70 m/s). The start post (a waymark, `RewardMeshes.StartPost`)
  stands 6 m from its place toward the course.
- **The server times it**: E at the post (`RequestTrial`), the body within 10 m; the course's gates are built on the
  server from its coarse tiles and sent (`TellTrialStarted`). The client reports each gate as its body passes
  (`RequestTrialGate`, again a second later if not counted); the server checks the body within the gate's radius + 20 m
  (+ 40 m gliding), the order, and the pace from the last gate (`TrialRuns`: an impossible pace is out).
- **Par**: gold = a run at 4.5 m/s on the flat + 0.35 m/s up (a glide 13 m/s), + 5 s; silver 25 % and bronze 60 % slower.
  A first finish gives a Fine roll, a first gold a Precious one; `TrialFinished(peer, key, id, seconds, medal)`.
- **Personal best** in the `trials` section (seconds, best medal, runs). **Board**: per server (decided Oct 2026), top 10,
  one row per player, `user://adventure/trials.json` (the check's folder under a check); sent to the runner on a start
  and to every adventuring peer when a run enters it; `RequestBoard` asks. The Collection tab's Trials page shows it.
- **Ghosts**: the client samples its run at 4 Hz (`GhostTrack`, ~4 KB a minute, 64 KB at most); after a personal best it
  uploads it in 8 KB parts, which the server takes only within 2 min of timing that best, checks (`Decode`) and keeps
  while its player is in the board's top 3 (`user://adventure/ghosts/`). A start asks for one's own best, else the
  leader's; it plays as a `Gpx.Runner` (no collision) on the shared clock.
- `/trial leave`: not wired as a chat command yet; `RewardService.AskLeaveTrial` is the way (the quick menu's Activities
  entry is not built: gap).
- **Not built** (follow-ups): the plan's pass drive (`RaceRoute` from the foot of a pass), the ski downhill
  (`BuildPisteAsync`) and the bike ride on road courses; trials ride their own RPCs, not `RaceManager`'s (no `RaceKind`
  exists to extend).
