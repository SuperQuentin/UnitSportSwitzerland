# The travel network (#1226)

`src/Adventure/Navigation/`: `AdventureTravel` (Godot, `World/Adventure/Travel`), the pure rules `TravelNetwork`,
`TravelBook` (section `travel`), `TravelRules` (`fares`), `TravelSigns` + `NavMeshBuilder`, `DepartureBoard` /
`DepartureScreen`, `TravelCurtain`, `NavSounds`. Plan: `docs/plans/adventure-navigation.md` 5.

## Rules

- **Travel points**: every rail station, bus stop (all of them; the map clusters them by screen cell when zoomed out,
  `TravelNetwork.Cluster`, a `VillageStop` before a mere stop), ship landing and cable-car station of the catalogue.
  Unlock radius: station 60 m, stop 30 m, landing 40 m, cable car 60 m.
- **Unlocking (server, 1 Hz)**: on foot (the places' foot class) or slower than 15 m/s; the nearest locked point in its
  radius on **two consecutive ticks**. Then the `travel` section is written and touched, `TravelUnlocked` raised with the
  point's canton and the network's totals (`Points`, `Cantons`), and the owner's `TellUnlocked` (toast "Travel point
  unlocked", through `StampToast.Post`). Unlocks happen in every mode.
- **Modes** (`TravelMode`): `Free` (default; jumps as before, adventure travel from anywhere, free), `Network`
  (`--adventure-travel network`; offline `Settings > Gameplay > Travel`, which a hosted server follows): no free jumps
  for non-admins (`Arrival.FreeTeleport` false, `Permissions.CanTeleport` false, `/city` refused), travel from an
  unlocked **sign** to an unlocked point, or from anywhere with the **GA**; `Off` (`--no-teleport`). Admins always go,
  free. The mode reaches a client by `TellMode` on load, not through `Arrival.Rules` (built differently: see the plan).
- **`TravelNetwork.MayTravel`** decides and prices on the server: off, not adventuring (a BR round: `Active` false,
  admins included), in a match, down, driving (anything but foot, equipment or a bicycle: `MayTravelAs`), racing, not a
  point, locked, not at an unlocked sign (9 m from the published position), same point, within 10 s of the last trip,
  can't afford. The client's `Preview` is display only (the board's greyed rows).
- **A trip**: `Bank.Charge` (no overdraft) then `Bank.Tell`, the section's `LastTravel` / `Trips` / `Spent`,
  `PlacesService.Arrived` (nothing counts at the landing until walked away), then the owner's `Depart(id, fare, e, n)`:
  `TravelCurtain` (the kind's chime, 0.8 s to black, the jump through `Teleporter.TeleportTo` at black, the card, fade
  in; in VR the chime and the jump only). The landing is 5 m east of the point.
- **Cards**: `TravelBook.HasGa` / `HasHalfFare` are the truth; the items `GaCard` 420 and `HalfFare` 421 are their
  picture (worth 0, so no shop buys them back). Sold at a **station's** ticket office (the board at a rail sign,
  `BuyCard`), or given by the `"travel"` reward `card:ga` / `card:halffare` ("Grand tour", "All aboard"). The reward
  with a POI id unlocks that point.
- **Signs**: the nearest 6 points within 400 m, one `MultiMesh` per kind lit (unlocked) / dark, rewritten at 1 Hz only
  when the set changes. E / Y / a VR grip within 4 m opens the board (`PoiMarkers.TryInteract` asks `TravelSigns` first).
- **Board**: unlocked destinations nearest first (40 rows, search), line glyph, canton, distance, the fare or the
  reason; the Journal's Travel tab is the same control, from anywhere.
- **Bots**: `TravelBot(bot, id)`; the 1 Hz tick unlocks for any peer with progress.
- **Portable flag** (rewards plan): `SetPortable` / `ClearPortable` write `TravelBook.Flag`; nothing draws it yet.

## Wire (protocol 52)

Client -> server `RequestTravel(to, fromSign)`, `RequestBuy(ga, station)`, `RequestPinAdd/Edit/Remove` (one trip or
purchase a second, ten pin edits a second). Server -> owner `TellMode`, `TellUnlocked`, `TellRefused`, `Depart`,
`PartyPinsState`. The sections go through `SectionSync`. `Parties.StateSent` (server) re-sends shared pins on any party
change.
