# `AdventureService` at `World/Adventure`: who keeps the progress, the switch, the sync (#1223)

`src/Adventure/AdventureService.cs`, a `Node` on the server and on every client (RPCs route by node
path). `AdventureService.Instance` is null when `Systems.Adventure` is off: every caller uses `?.`.

## Authority and identity

- **The server keeps every player's progress** in an `AdventureBook`. Offline the client plays the
  server's part (`Authority`): the local player is peer `1` with key `local`, the "server" methods are
  called directly, and its `Mine` *is* that progress. Hosting from the menu runs a real server: the
  host is keyed like anyone. Offline and online progress are separate, like the bank's two accounts.
- **Keyed by the proven `PlayerKey`** (`Net/Sleepers`: `Proven(peer, key)`, `KeyOf(peer)`, raised
  after a good `Prove`), never by the requested name. On a `--accounts required` server the key is
  `acct:<Nakama user id>` (`AccountGate.Of(peer)`), loaded at once on join, so it follows the player
  across machines; other servers keep the fingerprint. No merge between the two.
- **Until a peer proves, its progress is the session's** (`peer:<id>`): it works but is never saved.
  If no proof came after 12 s (an old client, a second client on the same `user://`), the owner is told
  once (`TellUnsaved` -> `Unsaved`, the Journal's header) and gets its sections. A bot gets session
  progress through `SessionFor(peer)`.
- Files: `user://adventure/players/<FileName(key)>.json` on a server, `user://adventure/local.json`
  offline, both through `WorldChoice.SavePath` (a test world keeps its own). `--adventure-dir <dir>`
  (and `--adventurecheck` always) moves them to a check's folder.

## The switch

- `Core/Systems.Adventure` (`"adventure"`): on for players (Explore, Multiplayer, dedicated servers).
  **Off** on `--world fixture` and on a test world (#999) unless `--systems` names it, and with any
  `--systems` list that does not name it.
- **`Active(peer)`**: the system on, the peer known, and not in a Battle Royale round
  (`BrManager.InRound`: an entrant of its lobby or match, until the round ends). Every server method of
  every plan checks it. The bus drops an inactive peer's events (`AdventureEvents.Dropped`), requests
  are refused ("No adventuring now."), progress is kept. The client hides the tracker while
  `Permissions.InMatch` and the Journal's adventure tabs (`AdventureLog.Adventuring`).

## Server API (every plan, and bots)

`Progress(peer)`, `Touch<T>(peer, key)`, `Events`, `Grant(peer, reward, why)`,
`RegisterReward(kind, (peer, progress, arg) => error-or-null)`, `Provides(plan)` (quests whose steps
need that plan's events are offered only then: `QuestEngine.Plans`), `Start/Abandon/Track(peer, id)`,
`Deliver(peer, questId, step)`, `PeerJoined/PeerProven/PeerLeft` (from `ServerWorld`).

## Sync to the owner

- Touched sections go to their owner only: `SectionSync(id, version, json)` (reliable, at most 4 a
  second per player, once per server frame at most). The client sets `Mine` and raises
  `SectionChanged(id)`; screens refresh on it, never per frame. On load every section is sent.
- A section that changes in tiny steps over a large body (fog) may send its own delta RPC and `Touch`
  only for the save (that plan says so).
- Saves: `JsonStore.SaveAsync` of `AdventureBook.ToNode`, debounced to once per 5 s per player, at once
  on `PeerLeft`, and on quitting (`_ExitTree` -> `SaveAll`).
- Other peers never receive anyone's progress.

## The tracked target (navigation's contract)

`TrackedTarget` (client): `(Label, E, N, RadiusM)?` of the tracked quest's current step's target, an
area's own or a POI's through `QuestEngine.ResolvePoi`; `TrackedChanged` when it changes (on the quests
section's sync, never polled).

## For the other plans (#1224)

- `SyncsItself(id)`: a section its plan sends by its own RPCs (fog): `Touch` then only saves it.
- `Loaded(peer)`: a peer's progress loaded and its owner hears (an account, a proof, the session's once told,
  offline at once): send a full state then. `Saving(progress)`: write a working copy into its section first.
- `Peers` (loaded peers), `Withdraws(plan)` (the opposite of `Provides`).
- `TrackPlace(label, e, n, radius)` / `UntrackPlace()` / `TrackedPlace` (client only, for the session): a place
  that is no quest's target (the map's and the Atlas's Track button) is the `TrackedTarget` until another place
  or a quest is tracked (`AskTrack` clears it) or its POI is stamped. Navigation may persist it later (its
  `pins` section).

## Wire (protocol 50)

Client -> server: `RequestStart(id)`, `RequestAbandon(id)`, `RequestTrack(id)` ("" untracks),
`RequestDeliver(id, step)`. Server -> owner: `SectionSync`, `QuestNote(id, what, step, count)`,
`Granted(item, count, why)`, `DeliverOk(id, step, item, count)`, `TellRefused(why)`, `TellUnsaved()`.
`World/Bank` gained `Credited(amount, balance)` (`items/cash-account`).
