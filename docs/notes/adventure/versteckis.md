# Versteckis: hide-and-seek as village props (#1268)

`src/Adventure/Versteckis/`, namespace `UnitSport.Adventure.Versteckis`. Plan: `docs/plans/adventure-versteckis.md`
(decisions in `adventure-phase3.md`). One seeker against hiders disguised as hay bales, woodpiles, milk cans, gnomes,
benches, cows and flower boxes round a real village church; each player seeks once; everyone who plays is paid.

## Rules worth knowing

- **Rounds**: registered in `Core/Rounds` after the Battle Royale (`core/rounds`). An entrant is "in a round" from the
  lobby to its release: `AdventureService.Active` is false; `/tp` and admin tools are refused while it plays. A BR
  refuses an entrant of a Versteckis game.
- **Authority**: roles, catches, points, objectives and bounds are the server's. Transforms stay the owners'; the
  server checks each hider's published body 4 times a second: inside the circle, not in a building (an interior, or
  half a metre inside a footprint), on the ground, and its speed **over a one-second window** (positions arrive in
  bursts; a 0.25 s window failed a 2 m/s walk). A failed check reveals after 3 s and catches after 10 s in Seek.
- **Hide**: `InterestService.Withheld` despawns every hider on the seeker (applied at once, not held back by the flip
  limit); the seeker is put back at the church door beyond 6 m; its client is blind.
- **A tag** is judged on server positions only; out of reach (2.5 m + 0.5) it is refused before the cooldown.
- **A disguised body**: `FootPlayer.PropWalk` caps the walk (2.5 m/s, a cow 1.5, a sprint 6.5), no run, jump, slide or
  emote; on a desktop the ordinary third-person camera is lent (VR stays first person); E opens no door.
- **The pay**: `VsRules.Payout` (20 + 6 a round, +10 the top scorer, five paid games a UTC day) as a `Bank.Credit`, the
  same for every human; progress folded into the section (a session record when Adventure is off), titles and
  cosmetics through `AdventureService.Grant`, events raised once the player is out of the round.
- **Chat**: a caught hider's lines go to the game's caught hiders only while a round runs (`Rounds.ChatScope`).
- No offline game: offline `/vs` and the tab say to host one (bots need a server).

## Wire (protocol 57)

Client to server, reliable, 4 a second: `RequestDisguise(kind)`, `RequestTag(e, n, alt)`, `RequestSniff`,
`RequestDecoy`, `RequestSprint`, `RequestMoo`, `RequestObjective(site, step)`. Server to every peer: `SetGames(json)`
(every game: the list, each one's state, the disguises to draw); to entrants: `Enter`, `Kit(role)`, `PutAt(e, n)`,
`Release`, `Caught(hider, by)`; to the asking seeker: `Missed`, `Sniffed(e[], n[])`; to peers within 80 m (the bell to
the whole game): `Sounded(peer, voice, e, n)`. Invite kind `versteckis`, keyed by the game's id.
