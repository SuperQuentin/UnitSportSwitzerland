# Map pins (#1226)

`PinBook` (section `pins`, plain C#): up to 30 pins, ids that only grow, notes of at most 24 characters (trimmed,
control characters out), six icons (`PinIcon`, drawn by `Ui/NavIcons`: flag, star, edelweiss, house, camera, warning).

- **Placed on the adventure map** (`NavMapOverlay`, an `IMapOverlay`): right click, pad X at the centre cross, or the
  side panel's "Pin the centre" (VR, any device); a click on a pin edits it (icon 1-6, note, share, Track, Remove).
  `AdventureMap` forwards right clicks and pad X to the overlays and has `ShowCard(Control)` for an overlay's card.
- **Server-checked**: `AskPinAdd/Edit/Remove` → `PinAdd/PinEdit/PinRemove` (adventuring, ten a second), then `Touch`.
- **Party**: a `Shared` pin goes to the other members by `PartyPinsState` (the whole set, replacing what they had) on
  a change and on every party change (`Parties.StateSent`); never written into their books; leaving empties it.
- **Shown**: on the map (party pins outlined, with the owner's name) and on the compass (icon, distance). Tracking a
  pin is `AdventureService.TrackPlace` (the plan's `NavTracking` was not needed). Not on the VR hand map yet.
