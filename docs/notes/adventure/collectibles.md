# Edelweiss, offerings and cairns (#1227)

`Edelweiss.cs` (pure: `CollectProgress`, `Offering`, `Cairns`, `EdelweissOffered`); the client side is `RewardWorld`
and `Loot/Gathering` (`Resource.Edelweiss`, `Resource.Cairn`).

## Rules

- **Not inventory items**: an edelweiss picked is a progress value (`collect.Taken`, `Held`, `Picked`), never a stack to
  drop, lose on a knock-out or trade. Drawn as one `MultiMesh` (2.5 times life size, so it is seen from the path); a
  picked one leaves the instance list at the next rebuild (on its section's change, never per frame).
- **Pick**: the gather hold (G / pad X, VR grip and pull: the gather row), 0.8 s, offered before water and stone when one
  is within 1.6 m. `RequestPick(id)`: the spot of the server's own placement, the body within 4.5 m, not taken.
  `CollectibleTaken(peer, key, id, "edelweiss", canton)`.
- **Offering** (decided Oct 2026: 5 edelweiss = one stamina step, ten at most): E at the offering box beside a summit cross
  or a chapel (`Offering.IsShrine`: `cross`, a crossed `peak`, `chapel`) asks to offer every whole five held (a
  `Ui/Modal` confirmation: keyboard, pad, the VR ray). The server moves them from held to offered and grants
  `Unlock("stamina", "+1")` per five while the steps are ten or fewer (**the stamina plan registers the handler**, #1225:
  until it is on main the service logs "no system grants 'stamina'"), a Precious roll every fifth step, and raises
  `EdelweissOffered(peer, key, shrine, offered)`. Offerings past the cap still count in `Offered`.
- **Cairns** (Steinmänner, part 4b): twenty per canton (`Cairns.Of`: its passes, peaks, crosses and huts under 2500 m,
  ordered by hash), 12 m from the POI, `cn:<poiId>`. The gather hold adds this player's stone (drawn on top, for it
  alone); the twentieth of a canton gives a Precious roll. The plan's canton-flag cosmetic is not built (no flag
  garments yet): the roll stands in for it.
- **Fauna**: a photo with a cow (`Farming.Herds.CowsNear`), a bird (`BirdLife.Birds`, by species) or the guide marmot near
  the middle of the frame within 40 m adds it to `collect.Fauna` (`RequestFauna`, trusted: it gives nothing but the list,
  known names only). The Journal's **Fauna** tab lists them, ibex and chamois greyed ("not yet in Switzerland's game").
