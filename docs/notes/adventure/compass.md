# The compass, the target marker and the guiding gust (#1226)

## Compass

- `Ui/CompassStrip` draws the strip for any `ICompassSource` (`BrCompass` in a BR match, `AdventureCompass` in free
  roam); `Ui/CompassMath` holds the bearing, placement and declutter maths (tier 0). **Redrawn only when the heading
  moved more than 0.2° or a marker changed** (`Redraws`); tick and bearing texts come from static tables; labels are
  built by the source when a rounded distance changes (`NavFormat.Step`). `CompassStrip.Bearing` is the one bearing
  function (#772's F3 facing line should call it).
- BR: the same look as before (every label, no declutter, no fade). Free roam: the tracked target (amber diamond,
  "1.2 km ▲340 m"), party members, pins, found places within 1.5 km (5 at most, queried at 2 Hz, the name within ±12°);
  declutter (8 drawn, crowded labels dropped); fades to 30 % after 6 s with nothing changing. Not in VR, in a match,
  with a page open, or with `Settings > Gameplay > Compass` off.
- **VR**: `XR/XrWatch` grows a bezel turned so its N points north, an amber notch toward the target, and the target's
  distance on the dial where the speed was.

## Target marker

`TargetMarker` reads `NavTarget.Current` (the service's `TrackedTarget`; altitude from a catalogue POI there, or the
ground). On screen: a diamond 2.5 m over it with "name · 3.4 km ▲1,210 m" (text on change, 28 → 18 px); off screen an
arrow on an inset ellipse (`NavMath.EdgePoint`), and a target behind always points down (`NavMath.BehindDir`, whatever
the camera's pitch). Within max(radius, 15 m) it pulses, and a place or pin tracked from the map is let go. VR: a
`Label3D` billboard (no depth test, at most 400 m away) and a haptic tick within 100 m.

## Guiding gust

`GuidingWind`: the `guide` action (N on foot or mounted, not in the vehicles where N is theirs; quick menu › Guide; VR
the open left hand palm up 0.5 s, or the wrist menu). One `GpuParticles3D` (96, 2.2 s, world coordinates) ahead of the
body, its direction set when the bearing moves more than 2°, tilted by the slope; the shapes from
`guide_wind.gdshader` (`style` 0/1/2, `palette` 1 dark above 2 000 m, `streaks` over 25 m/s), uniforms set on change
only. Sound `NavSounds.Gust` from 8 m toward the target, and `GuideChime`. Settings: on demand / always / arrow only /
off, and "High-contrast guide": the ground chevron (`NavMeshBuilder.GuideArrow`). The dark palette follows altitude only
(not the ground's snow yet).
