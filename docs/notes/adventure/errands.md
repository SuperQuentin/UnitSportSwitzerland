# Errands: givers, the dialogue, the daily Postkarten, canton reputation (#1267)

Plan: `docs/plans/adventure-errands.md`. Code: `src/Adventure/Errands/`. Every server method checks `AdventureService.Active(peer)`, so none of it runs in a Battle Royale lobby or round.

## What is in

- **Givers** (`Giver`, `GiverCatalogue`, `GiverHost`): server-known figures, the same on every peer without a byte sent. Built from the POI catalogue: a `HutWarden` per hut, a `StationMaster` per rail station, a `ShipMaster` per landing (`GiverCatalogue.FromPois`); `--givers fixture` replaces them by eight fixture givers round the start (post office, farm, hut, station, landing, alp dairy, village dairy, Beiz; declared elevations, the ground is flat). `GiverHost` follows `PoiCatalogueHost.Changed`. Ids are `giver:<tag>:<hash of the POI>`. The `--givers` stage (#1339) adds the farms, alp and village dairies, post offices and Beizen from OSM and the built tiles; its `givers.tsv.gz` is embedded when the file exists (see below).
- **Figures** (`ErrandGivers`): a `SkinnedFigure` within 400 m, posed once (a garment by trade: Sennenkäppi, guide hat, hi-vis vest), turning to the player within 8 m at 10 Hz (a transform), no collision on any peer. A card glyph over a giver with an untaken card (amber), a green one where a hand-over waits. In the model viewer (`People`, `Errand givers`).
- **Dialogue** (`ErrandDialog`, `ErrandLines`): E / Y / a VR hand within 3 m (`FootPlayer.TryInteract` after `PoiMarkers`, prompt "Talk to ..."). The card as a postcard (Left / Right, LB / RB between two), or "Hand over" where a running card's current step is at this giver; "What's on around here?" lists the cell's cards with Track; the farewell in the giver's language. Refusals come from the server into the status line. 1-3, Esc, B.
- **Postkarten** (`Postkarte`, `PostkarteSource`, `ErrandRewards`): 8 cards per (Swiss day, 5 km cell), two per giver, rebuilt from the id `pk:<yyyymmdd>:<cellE>_<cellN>:<slot>` (`PostkarteSource.Resolve`). All eight templates (#1339 added two): alp cheese down, PostBus mail run (3 stops, 3 sacks), ferry crate, specialty delivery (10-90 km), hut supplies, farmer's request, the lost hiker (hut wardens: find them by a peak, pass or cross 400-1 500 m off, then bring them to the hut; `Offer.Hiker`, `InteractStep` + `EscortStep`, see `world-events`) and the postcard home (any giver with a curated photo spot within 10 km: `PostcardStep`, which counts a good photo even of a spot already in the album, then the card to a post office within 15 km; `PhotoSeen`). Targets are `AreaTarget`s. Rewards come from `fares.json` (`ErrandRewards`: base + 1.5 x full fare + 0.05 x climb, rounded up to 5, clamped 20-180; at least the fare plus 15).
- **Taking** (`ErrandService.Accept(peer, giverId, cardId)`): the server checks the card (today or yesterday), its giver, the body within 3 m + 5 m, and three a day (`ErrandsProgress.Taken`, reset by the Swiss day). `QuestEngine.CanStart` refuses a `QuestStart.Given` quest unless `given: true`, so the Journal's own `RequestStart` cannot start a card from anywhere. The cargo (`MailSack` 430, `FerryCrate` 431, `SupplyPack` 432, specialties 434-444; Gruyère is `ItemId.Cheese`) is granted on accept and taken back (`TookBack`) when the card is abandoned or expires (`AdventureService.Abandon` raises `QuestAbandoned`). A card lives until the end of the next Swiss day; `Sweep` (1 Hz, on login) abandons the rest and prunes records older than 30 days.
- **Friend bonus, share, join**: the second party member to finish the same card within 10 minutes pays both +50 % cash (`ErrandRewards.Bonus`) and +1 reputation, once per card (`BonusPaid`); `ErrandDone` is raised with `With`. `Share(peer, cardId)` shows a running card to the party ("Shared by Lea"); `Join` takes it from anywhere (it counts as one of the three). Together's +10 % quest cash skips `quest:pk:`.
- **Reputation** (`Reputation`, `ErrandsProgress.Rep`): levels at 10 / 30 / 60 / 100 (Bekannt, Geschätzt, Einheimisch, Ehrenbürger), computed. A card gives +3 in the giver's canton and +1 in a different destination canton. The `"rep"` reward kind (`VS:+5`, in `QuestCatalog.UnlockKinds`) lets any quest grant it. Level 3 records the canton flag as earned; level 4 grants the title "Ehrenbürger von ...". Not built: wearing the flag (`FootPlayer.CantonFlag`), the regional costume, the local recipes (`Recipe.Requires`).
- **Errands tab** (`ErrandsPage`, order 15): today's cards in the player's cell and in each party member's cell (#1339) with Track, running cards with Share, shared cards to Join, the reputation list. The Atlas shows a canton's reputation line.
- **World events** (#1339): the cow, the hiker, the avalanche, the fire: `world-events`.
- **Sounds**: `ErrandSounds.Greeting`, `ErrandSounds.Stamp` (static, in the sound player).

## The givers stage (#1339)

`TerrainPreprocessor --givers src/Adventure/Errands/givers.tsv.gz --osm <switzerland .osm.pbf> --out <chunk dir> [--boundaries <swissboundaries3d .gpkg>] [--jobs n]` reads the OSM points and closed outlines (post offices, cheese makers and dairies, restaurants with cuisine regional or swiss, farmyards) and the region's `.bldg` tiles (a barn of 120 m² or more with a house within 40 m is a farm), keeps only the manifest's tiles, thins farms and Beizen to one per 2 km cell by the hash of their source id, splits dairies at 1 200 m (Senn above), moves a point standing on a solid out to its edge facing outward, takes heights from the tiles and cantons from the boundaries (else the nearest `places.json` town). A rerun gives the same bytes (`GiverFormat`, tier 0 in `GiverPickTests`). The game embeds the file when it exists (`UnitSportSwitzerland.csproj`, `GiverHost`, ODbL like `venues.tsv.gz`); the repo ships none yet, so those givers appear after the owner runs it. **Nationwide** (not run here): the same command with `--out` the full chunk directory (a few minutes for the PBF, the tiles are read once), then commit the file. Not built: GWR categories beyond the barn and house tiles (the cheese makers of a canton's registry), the dairy's door side from the road.

## Not built here (follow-ups)

Wearing the flag, the costume and the recipes, running the givers stage nationwide, the world events' loose extras (`world-events`).

## Section and wire

Section `errands` (`ErrandsProgress`): `day`, `taken`, `rep`, `together`, `done`, `bonusPaid`, `flags`, `flag`, `events` (world events helped per kind, #1339). Node `World/Errands` on the server and every client. Client to server: `RequestAccept`, `RequestShare`, `RequestJoin`; server to owner: `Accepted`, `Shared`, `TookBack`, `Told`. Offers are never sent.

## Commands and checks

- `--givers fixture` (with `--pois fixture`, `--world fixture`), `--errand-day-offset n` (the Swiss day moves n days on).
- `--errandcheck` (quick): `--errandcheck --world fixture --pois fixture --givers fixture --systems ui,terrain,physics,adventure`.
- `tools/errandnetcheck.sh` (net): server plus Lea and Tom (`--errandnet lead|follow`).
- Tier 0: `ErrandTests`.
