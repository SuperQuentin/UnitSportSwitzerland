# World events: a cow loose, a lost hiker, an avalanche, a small forest fire (#1339)

Plan: `docs/plans/adventure-errands.md` part 6. Code: `src/Adventure/Errands/WorldEvent*.cs`. Node `World/WorldEvents` on the server and every client, made with `ErrandService` (needs `Systems.Adventure`).

- **Planning** (the dedicated server, or the game offline; `WorldEventRules` and the four simulations are pure, tier 0): every 120 s slot each 2 km cell an adventuring player stands in rolls one slot in 15 (`--world-events normal`, the default; `rare` is one in 40; `off`). At most one running per cell, six per server, a 15 min cooldown per cell. The kinds come in a hash order of the roll's seed; the first whose anchor passes the place, the month and the hour is the event. `--worldevent cow,hiker,avalanche,fire` (checks) plans one of each in turn near the first player, season and slot aside. `Stage(kind, e, n)` puts one at a point (checks).
- **Off** for a peer in a Battle Royale round (`AdventureService.Active`) and in a Versteckis game (`WorldEvents.Busy`, set by `ServerWorld.Errands`): it is not counted as near, cannot be paid and gets no plan.
- **Anchors** (server data only): the cow is a herd's cow within 600 m (`Herds.CowNear`, month any); the hiker sits 20-80 m off a peak, pass or summit cross 0.6-2 km away and 150 m above, brought to the nearest hut, bus stop or station within 3 km (a lost hiker comes twice as often 17-21 h); the avalanche is a coarse-terrain slope of 28-45 degrees above 1 500 m, 300-900 m off, December-April (or above 2 600 m all year); the fire is a forest edge (`CoverClass.Forest`) 200-800 m off, June-September, below 1 800 m.
- **What helping is**: the **cow** (`CowSim`) walks away from its herd at 1.1 m/s; whoever stands within 7 m pushes it, away from their centre at 1.4 m/s; it is home within 15 m of the herd. The **hiker** (`HikerSim`): E (pad Y, VR grip) talks to them (`EventAct.Talk`), then they follow the nearest helper within 40 m at 1.4 m/s, 3 m behind, to the goal. The **avalanche** (`AvalancheSim`): a debris cone, one or two buried; a transceiver beeps within 60 m (1 Hz at the edge, 4 Hz on top, `WorldEventSounds.Beep`) and the prompt reads "Signal 25 m"; hold Gather (G, pad X, VR grip and pull) within 3 m of the spot to dig: 8 s alone, 8 / n s for up to three. The **fire** (`FireSim`): six burning 4 m cells, one spreads every 15 s to a forest neighbour (a seeded hash, no shared stream) up to 40; hold Gather at a burning cell for 2 s (less with company) to beat it out. There is no health damage anywhere: smoke dims the view up to 55 % within 60 m of a flame (`WorldEvents.Draw`), flames and smoke are two instanced quads with a billboard vertex shader (`WorldEventMeshes`), nothing rebuilds a mesh.
- **Server path** `WorldEvents.Help(actor, at, eventId, act, arg)`: the `RequestHelp` RPC calls it with the sender's published body, a bot calls it with its own. The hold times are the server's (`DoHolds`, 10 Hz): a dig or a beating counts while the body stays within 4 m + 2 m of it. The cow and the hiker credit anyone within 7 m / 10 m while they move (a push is an act).
- **Rewards** (`Pay`): an adventuring human who did an act and stands within 150 m at the end gets CHF 30 (cow) / 40 (hiker, fire) / 60 (avalanche) through `Grant` (the bank account), +5 reputation in the event's canton, a count in `ErrandsProgress.Events[kind]` (five of a kind earn the title: Kuhflüsterer, Bergfreund, Lawinenhund, Feuerwehr), and `WorldEventHelped` for quests (`EventStep`). When the world solves it first (8 / 15 / 10 / 20 min, or 40 cells burning) nobody is paid and the end is told. Bots can act (`Help`) and are credited as helpers but earn nothing.
- **The hiker's Postkarte** (`CardTemplate.LostHiker`, hut wardens): the card's hiker sits where the card says (a peak, pass or cross 400-1 500 m from the hut), exists once someone takes the card (`ErrandService.Take` -> `HostCard`; `Sweep` hosts it again after a restart), and is the same hiker for everyone who holds it. Talking raises `Interacted("hiker:<card id>")` (step 1), the walk to the hut raises `Escorted` (step 2, `EscortStep`). The card pays, not the event.
- **Wire** (protocol 73): server to client `PlanOut` once (reliable, to peers within 3 km, and to a joiner or a walker coming near), `MovedOut` 4 Hz unreliable for the cow and the hiker, `StateOut` on change (hiker found; dug mask and per cent; burning cells), `EndedOut`, `Told`; client to server `RequestHelp(eventId, act, arg)`. Offline the client plays the server's part with the same code.
- **Seen**: compass diamonds (`AdventureCompass`, row 1; a lost hiker not found shows 120 m off as "Last seen near ..."), letters on the adventure map (`EventMapOverlay`), toasts on plan and end. Models and sounds: the model viewer's `World events` category, the sound player's `WorldEventSounds`.
- **Not built**: scorch marks fading over an hour; the water bottle put out a fire (only beating); the cow is an extra stray, not one of the herd's own cows (`Herds` has no loose-cow path); a hiker "sitting" is standing; a Rega helicopter flyby; the `/worldevent` admin command (use `--worldevent` and `--world-events`).

## Checks

- Tier 0: `WorldEventTests` (rolls, seasons, the four simulations, the steps, the hiker and postcard cards), `GiverPickTests`.
- Quick: `--worldeventcheck --world fixture --pois fixture --givers fixture --systems ui,terrain,physics,adventure`: each event staged and driven to its end through `AskHelp`, paid, a busy player gets none, a lonely event ends alone, the hiker's Postkarte end to end.
- Net: `tools/worldeventnetcheck.sh`: server plus two clients; the remote one sees the plan, digs, and is paid.
