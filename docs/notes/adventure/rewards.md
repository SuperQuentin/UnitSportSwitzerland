# Rewards: placement, the service and its wire (#1227)

`src/Adventure/Rewards/`. Plan: `docs/plans/adventure-rewards.md`. The pure rules (`RewardRules`, `RewardPlacer`,
`ChestLoot`, `Seals`, `Edelweiss`, `GuideAnimal`, `Trials`, `PhotoSpots`, `Gadgets`) are plain C#, linked into tier 0
(`RewardsTests.cs`); the Godot side is `RewardService` (World/Adventure/Rewards), `RewardMap`, `RewardWorld` and the UIs.

## Rule: computed, never stored; taken, per player

- **Spots are a pure function of the tile** (`RewardPlacer.Place(tile, coarse grid, POIs, lakes, roads, cairns)`):
  the coarse `.terrc` grid (`LoadCoarseChunkAsync`, else the full grid decimated to stride 10), the catalogue's POIs of
  the tile, the lakes whose box may cross it, the tile's carriageways. Never the fine cover raster: the server must
  recompute any spot to check a claim. One `SpotRng` (splitmix64) per spot, seeded from its own 250 m cell or POI and a
  salt per kind, so adding a kind never reshuffles another.
- **Ids are anchored to the world**: `ch:<E>_<N>` / `ew:<E>_<N>` (the spot's 10 m cell, LV95 metres), `ch:<poiId>:<slot>`
  at a POI (slot 0 Precious, 1 Luxurious), `cn:<poiId>` a cairn. `RewardRules.TryTile` finds an id's tile without
  placing anything. A change that moves spots bumps `RewardPlacer.Version` (stored in the `chests` section).
- **What a player took is kept in its own sections** (`chests`, `collect`, `trials`, `photos`, `gadgets`); the client
  hides only its own. A chest is never used up for the world: two players each open the same chest once, each with its
  own roll (`AdventureHash.Of(openId, key, count)`).
- **Luxurious chests come back every 1 August** (decided Oct 2026): opened as `ch:<id>#<edition>`,
  `RewardRules.Edition(utc)` = the year whose 1 August last passed (UTC on both sides).
- **World kinds**: the real map places everything; a fixture course (`Systems.FixtureCourse`) places nothing unless
  `--rewardspots fixture|<file>` hand-places spots (the checks); the generated world has no `.terrc` tiles, so nothing.
- **Densities** (per 250 m cell before the terrain's say): Common 0.085 (slope < 30°, 6 m off a carriageway, not in a lake
  or on dead-flat water), Fine 0.026 (< 40°, 80 m off), remote Precious 0.0035 (400 m off), edelweiss 0.11 (≥ 1500 m,
  slope > 35°, 150 m off), plus the POIs' (every castle, pass and viewpoint, one peak in four over 2000 m: Precious;
  tier-1 peaks and castles, passes over 2000 m: Luxurious) and 1-2 edelweiss within 30 m of a summit over 1500 m. Budget
  per tile: 8 chests (the highest tiers kept), 10 edelweiss, 2 cairns. Measured with `--rewardstats` on the main
  checkout's tiles (`commands`): Common ~1.1 per km², Fine ~0.35-0.4, edelweiss ~0.77 where all is high (the plan's 1.2,
  0.35, 0.8).

## The server checks every claim

`ServeOpen`, `ServePick`, `ServeSealStep`, `ServeGuide`, `ServeOffer`, `ServePhoto`, `ServeTrialStart`, `ServeTrialGate`
(public: a bot's way in too). Each: `AdventureService.Active(peer)` (off in a Battle Royale round), 4 claims a second, the
id is a spot of the server's own placement of its tile (`RewardMap.FindAsync`: a forged id cannot exist), the body's
**server position** (`PositionOf`, the proxy's `NetGlobal`) within 4.5 m of a chest or edelweiss (8 m of a seal's prop or
burrow, 10 m of a shrine), not already in the player's section, the seal complete. The server places a tile only when a
claim names it and keeps the last 64 (`RewardMap`, LRU); a layout is computed only once its tile's grid is in.

## Wire (protocol 55)

Client to server, `AnyPeer` reliable: `RequestOpen(id)`, `RequestPick(id)`, `RequestSealStep(chestId, step)`,
`RequestOffer(shrineId)`, `RequestGuide(chestId)`, `RequestPhoto(spot, yaw, pitch, fov)`, `RequestFauna(name)`,
`RequestTrial(id)`, `RequestTrialGate(id, gate)`, `RequestTrialLeave()`, `RequestBoard(id)`, `RequestGhost(id)`,
`RequestGhostPart(id, part, parts, bytes)`. Server to the asking peer only (`RpcId`): `TellOpened(id, items, counts,
tier, francs, note)`, `TellPicked(id)`, `TellSeal(chestId, mask)`, `GuideLeg(chestId, leg, serverTime, done)`,
`TellRefused(id, why)`, `TellOffered`, `TellPhoto`, `TellTrialStarted(id, gates, since, par)`, `TellTrialGate`,
`TellTrialEnded`, `TellBoard(id, names, seconds)` (to every adventuring peer when a run enters it), `TellGhost`. Items
arrive by `AdventureService.Granted`, francs by `Bank.Credit` (the account, safe from a knock-out). Sections reach their
owner by `SectionSync`; nothing of anyone's goes to another peer.

## Client

`RewardWorld` (a plain child of the world) draws the tiles round the player within 320 m from its own `RewardMap`:
`ChestNode` (body, lid as its own node turned by a tween, the seal's band shown on a change), edelweiss, cairns and a
player's own top stone as `MultiMesh`es, the seal props, the trial posts and the shrines' offering boxes. A shape string
(ids, taken, sealed, ground known, origin tile) is compared once a second; nothing is rebuilt per frame. E / Y / a VR grip
(`FootPlayer.TryInteract` before `PoiMarkers`) uses the nearest thing within 2.4 m; prompts go through the interiors'
prompt line (`RewardWorld.PromptFor`). The gather hold offers an edelweiss or a cairn within 1.6 m before water and stone
(`Gathering.Resource.Edelweiss / Cairn`, 0.8 s). Rings and plates count as the body reaches them (10 Hz sense).

## Events (raised on the server, after the section is written)

`ChestOpened(peer, key, openId, tier, poiId)`, `CollectibleTaken(peer, key, id, "edelweiss"|"cairn", canton)`,
`EdelweissOffered(peer, key, shrineId, offered)` (new, in `Edelweiss.cs`), `TrialFinished`, `PhotoTaken`, and
`ItemObtained` through `Grant`. The service calls `Provides("rewards")`.

## How to check

- `tools/test.sh unit` (`RewardsTests.cs`): placement determinism, ids in their cell and tile, the budget, no chest on a
  road, edelweiss height, the densities on synthetic lowland, POI chests and the castle's plates, the edition key,
  forged ids, the fixture set; `Chance` against 200k `Roll`s per tier and pity, the Precious guarantee, Fine's pity,
  duplicates; the seal book (windows, order, the wrong bell, plates, guide legs, expiry, co-op radius, layouts on their
  tile); the guide's legs; the offering maths and cairns; `PhotoJudge` (cone, disc, ridge, unknown ground) and the 30
  curated spots; trials (courses, par, medals, the board, pace, ghosts); every section's round trip.
- Quick: `--rewardcheck --world fixture --systems ui,physics,loot,adventure --pois fixture --rewardspots fixture`
  (`commands`).
- Net: `tools/rewardnetcheck.sh` (`commands`).
