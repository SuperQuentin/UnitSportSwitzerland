# Chests, seals and guides (#1227)

## Loot

- **Four tiers**: Common CHF 20-60, 1-2 kinds of material ×1-3, a snack 60 %, Bergkristall 10 %; Fine CHF 60-150,
  2 kinds ×2-4, 1 Bergkristall, a cosmetic 4 % (+2 points per opening without one), a gadget 6 %; Precious CHF 150-400,
  2-3 kinds, 2-4 Bergkristall, a cosmetic 15 % and sure on the 8th without one, a gadget 25 %; Luxurious CHF 400-1000,
  3 kinds ×3-5, 5-8 Bergkristall, always a cosmetic, a gadget 50 %.
- **`Roll` and `Chance` share the table** (`ChestLoot.Of`, the candidates): change one, change the other. `Chance(tier,
  item, pity)` is the published odds at the player's pity now (the Collection tab's Odds page); the reveal card points
  there. Tier 0 compares them with 200k rolls (within 2 points).
- Materials are the build and workbench ones; the cosmetics are the **Alpine set** (`GarmentStyle.Alpine`: Sennenkäppi,
  edelweiss shirt, the mountain guide's hat, breeches, the Swiss-cross bandana, knee socks, gloves; two new shapes,
  `FeltHat` and `Bandana`), never in a wardrobe pool; the gadgets are the detector, the bottled gust and the travel flag.
- **Duplicates**: a cosmetic in `ChestsProgress.Owned` becomes 5 Bergkristall ("already yours"). The pity resets on a
  cosmetic (`PityAfter`).
- The same roll gives an offering's every fifth stamina step (Precious), the twentieth cairn of a canton (Precious), a
  trial's first finish (Fine) and first gold (Precious), a photo spot's first shot (Fine).

## Opening

E at the chest: `RequestOpen(openId)`; on `TellOpened` the lid swings 1.9 rad (0.6 s, a tween of its node), a light
shaft in the tier's colour rises (scale and `Transparency` tweens), the tier's jingle plays (`RewardSounds`), the reveal
card fans the items in over 2.2 s (any key skips, it fades 3 s after), then the chest shrinks away **for this player**.
A sealed chest wears a band of light while its seal is not done (`SetSealed`, on a change only).

## Guides

- `GuideBook` (server): woken, the guide runs leg 1; when its follower's body is within 8 m of the waypoint it has reached,
  it runs on; at the last (beside the chest) the seal is done. `GuideLeg(chestId, leg, serverTime, done)` goes to the
  follower; the client plays it on `ClockSync.ServerNow` (`GuideBook.At`): nothing per frame on the wire.
- A guide's chest is **hidden** until its seal is done for this player.
- The marmot: `MarmotMeshBuilder`, rigid parts as nodes (body, head, four legs, tail), legs turned for the gait, it sits up
  and whistles at each stop (`RewardSounds.Whistle`). The vulture: `BirdMesh` of the Bearded Vulture, wings turned,
  gliding 12 m over the leg. Only the follower sees its guide (others' guides are not drawn).
