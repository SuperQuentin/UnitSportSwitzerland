# Realistic+: Forward+, its effects, the restart

Phase 4 of `docs/plans/visual-styles.md` (#255). Realistic− (`realistic`) plus Forward+'s effects
and the SWISSIMAGE drape (`swissimage`); it borrows every shader and model from Realistic−.

- **Its own look items:** `Effects` and `Photos` (both PS1 false, the base). `--style-report`
  lists them with the rest.
- **Forward+ only.** Godot reads `rendering_method` at startup, so `StyleKit.NeedsForwardPlus`
  styles relaunch (`Styles/RendererRelaunch`): picking Realistic+ in the settings asks and
  restarts with `--rendering-method forward_plus`; a saved Realistic+ launched on Mobile
  relaunches once at boot (`GameShell`, after the VR check). The relaunch carries
  `--forward-plus-relaunch`, and a run with it never relaunches: a machine without Forward+
  draws Realistic+ as Realistic− (`StyleKit.EffectsOn` is false off Forward+). Runs that go
  straight into a world (`--shot-queue`, `--connect`) never relaunch: pass
  `--rendering-method forward_plus` to them yourself (`ENGINE=` for `style-shots`).
- **Gotcha when testing it from a shell:** the relaunched game inherits the parent's stdout,
  so a shell that pipes or captures the output waits for the child, and `timeout` kills both.
  Launch through PowerShell's `Start-Process` (or a desktop shortcut) to see the real behaviour.
- **Effects** (`NewEnvironment`, `EffectsOn`): SSAO (radius 1.5, intensity 1.6), SSR, volumetric
  fog at density 0.0004 over 200 m (0.0015 / 300 m turned the aerials milky and haloed the sun),
  on top of Realistic−'s ACES, haze and glow. SDFGI only with `--sdfgi`: ~15-20 ms on the M1 Pro,
  to be measured on a gaming laptop before it goes into the preset. A floating-origin shift
  re-voxelises every cascade in one frame (25-37 ms of GPU instead of 8-12 on an RTX 4070), with
  no visible pop (`core/floating-origin`). The sun is Cartoon's,
  4 cascades to 400 m.
- **Greyed out in the settings** (`StyleKit.InDevelopment`) while it is unfinished: on some machines
  it renders nothing. `--style real+` still picks it; a saved `RealisticHigh` still loads.
