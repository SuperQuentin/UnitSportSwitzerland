# SWISSIMAGE: the aerial photos under the realistic terrain

The real patchwork of fields, roofs and rivers, draped over the terrain in Realistic+ (#255;
the prototype's finding: free at runtime, better than textures from the air, worse on foot).

- **Fetching:** `TerrainPreprocessor --out <chunk dir> --photos [--tiles-file f] [--io-jobs N]
  [--force]` (`PhotoStage`). One 512x512 JPEG per tile (~1.95 m a pixel, ~100-120 KB),
  `photo_E_N.jpg` next to the tile's files, for every manifest tile (or the tiles file). From
  swisstopo's **WMS** (`ch.swisstopo.swissimage`, EPSG:2056), which renders a box straight to
  JPEG: no GeoTIFF, no GDAL. Out-of-coverage tiles come back blank (< 12 KB) and are not written;
  existing photos are kept without `--force`. Riddes' 49 tiles: 4.9 MB in 2 s.
- **Licence:** swisstopo open government data, free use, attribution **"© swisstopo"**.
- **Runtime** (`Terrain/PhotoLayer`, while the style's `Photos` look is on): the photos of the
  7x7 tiles around the camera in one `Texture2DArray` (512², mipmapped, DXT1: ~0.2 MB a layer),
  decoded and compressed on a worker, one layer uploaded per frame at most per tile. Each tile's
  ground mesh gets its layer through the **instance uniform** `photo_slot` (-1: none), so the
  terrain material stays the one every tile shares; a rebuilt or reloaded tile's new ground node
  is told again. Out of range, the layer is freed.
- **Shader** (`body/terrain`, `STYLE_REAL`): tile UV from the mesh's local x/z (NW corner, x
  east, z south, 1000 m: the photo's own frame). The photo replaces the textured ground with
  distance (120 to 700 m) or with the camera's height over the point (40 to 250 m), up to 90 %,
  and less on cliffs (the triplanar rock is better). Lit on top like any albedo.
- **Client-local:** read from the local terrain folder (`TerrainPaths.FindChunkDir`). A client
  streaming its tiles from a server has no photos and keeps the cover textures; streaming them
  is a protocol change for later. The far horizon has none either.
