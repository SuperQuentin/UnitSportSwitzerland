# Realistic−: how the style is put together

Phase 3 of `docs/plans/visual-styles.md` (#250). Textures and models on the **Mobile** renderer,
no GI or screen-space effects. Realistic+ (phase 4) borrows all of it.

- **Shaders:** `real_*` wrappers (`STYLE_LIT` + `STYLE_REAL`) over the role bodies, lit by
  Godot's own PBR light (no custom `light()`): terrain, road, building, water, tree, tree-far.
  Prop and path borrow Cartoon's; snowfall stays PS1's. `real_interior` is unshaded, with plaster
  and procedural floorboards (`terrain/interior-light`).
- **Textures tint the vertex colours, never replace them** (`style_tinted`): the texture brings
  the grain, the baked cover colour keeps the land readable and every style's palette the same.
  The texture is divided by its own last mip, so it averages to 1.
- **Terrain:** the cover family is read back from the baked colour (grey: rock, scree, paved;
  dark green: forest floor; very bright: snow; else grass); a second scale 7.3x coarser breaks
  the tiling as the ground recedes; top-projected normal maps up close (`style_ground_normal`);
  cliffs triplanar rock. Roughness 0.92.
- **Roads:** asphalt, ballast on rail beds; the network stage's paint (style 6) and the shader's
  markings keep their own colour. **Buildings:** plaster in facade space (metres along, storeys
  times their height), roof tiles in plan; the window grid tracks `glass`, glossy. **Water:**
  dark and glossy, the ripple normal.
- **Trees:** `ModelCatalog` ("tree:conifer", "tree:broadleaf") gives the EZ-Tree meshes,
  unit size, two surfaces (bark with a normal map, alpha-cut leaf cards with backlight), each a
  Tree-role material from `StyleKit.TreeSurface`. Drawn by `NearTrees` to 80 m (the style's own
  `TreeNear`; ~3k triangles a tree). Far: billboards sampling **impostors** baked offline
  (`--bake-impostors`): a side view on the vertical plane through the trunk facing the camera,
  a top view on the horizontal plane at mid crown, picked by the camera's elevation.
  The instance colours vary each tree around its kind's mean green (`tint_ref`,
  `tint_ref_conifer`): one reference for both darkened every conifer by a quarter.
- **Finish** (`Finish.Realistic`): a procedural sky that lights the scene (sky ambient and
  reflections), ACES at 0.8, light aerial haze, glow; the sun from Cartoon's look, brighter.
  At night the palette's own navy sky does the darkening: scaling the sky's energy down too gave
  a black screen. Figures: one PBR material (roughness 0.55) for paint, cloth and skin alike,
  until the builders give each its own surface.
- **Not yet:** car and avatar models (no realistic CC0 source; the avatar `Skeleton3D` adapter
  is its own issue), a species set beyond one conifer and one broadleaf.
