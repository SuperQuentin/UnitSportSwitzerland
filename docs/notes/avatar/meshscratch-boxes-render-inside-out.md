# MeshScratch boxes render inside out

- **`MeshScratch` boxes render inside out** (found on #48). `Box` winds its quads counter-clockwise
  seen from outside, and Godot's front face is clockwise, so with the default back-face culling every
  box shows its FAR inner walls. From outside a lone box still looks like a box, which is why it went
  unnoticed; what gives it away is that **nothing inside a box is hidden**: a part tucked into the
  body shows through it (a car's grille intake 5 mm inside the bumper, a sunk pop-up lamp). Confirmed
  by rendering a car with `CullMode = Front`: solid, lamps hidden, but differently lit — the look of
  every figure and vehicle is tuned on the inverted boxes, so flipping the winding is a project-wide
  visual change of its own, not a drive-by fix (tubes and rings not checked). Until then: never put a
  part away INSIDE another mesh — fold it onto a surface or hide it (`CarRig` squashes the pods into
  lids on the nose, hides their lamps and the roadster cockpit when put away).
