# Your own body seen from your eye (#833)

- First person never removes your figure: on foot the walker is the third-person figure, built and
  posed every frame (`ApplyFootPose`), and in a car's or heavy's cockpit the driver's head (and in
  the bare view the whole driver) stays drawn. `Avatar/OwnBody.See` puts it on `OwnBody.Layer`
  (`XrSession.SpectatorOnlyLayer`, bit 15) while the eye is in its head, back on layer 1 otherwise.
- The eye's cameras leave that layer out: the player's `Camera` (cull mask set at creation), the
  VR headset (`XrRig`), the monitor's first-person view, door portal cameras (`DoorPortals.WorldLayers`).
  Wall mirrors (`WallMirror.Cull`), cab mirrors (`CabMirrors`) and the VR monitor's third-person
  camera keep it, so they show you.
- **Godot 4.7 casts no shadow from an instance on a layer the camera culls** (measured: a box on
  bit 15 cast nothing, on layer 1 a full shadow). So `OwnBody.See` also keeps a `Shadow` child: a
  `ShadowsOnly` twin on layer 1 sharing the figure's mesh (synced each call, so call it after the
  pose sets the mesh). No camera draws a `ShadowsOnly` instance; the twin only casts.
- The twin is never ghosted: `DoorwayGhosts.Collect` skips `ShadowsOnly` meshes. Its ghost (which
  casts nothing) drew it as a plain mesh on layer 1, so by an open door the portal showed your own
  head a second time.
- What hangs on the figure (the radio on the back) follows its layer; the item in the hand
  (`HeldItemVisual._inHand`) goes on the layer too outside VR, the eye having the viewmodel. A shot
  from first person still leaves from the eye (`ItemEvents.MuzzleOf` ignores the hand while
  `FootPlayer.BodyFromEye`), not the now-posed hand.
- The fist fight's side view puts the body back on layer 1 (the camera is out of the head).
- A rig added by `RefreshVisual` while the old walker is still queued is not named `Body`
  (`@Node3D@N`): find it by type, not by name.
- Check: `--ownbodycheck[,dir] --world flat` (`Player/OwnBodyCheck`): headless the layers, twins,
  cull masks and muzzle, on foot and in both cockpit views; in a window also the pictures (the
  screen and a wall mirror, with and without the figure), written to `dir`.
