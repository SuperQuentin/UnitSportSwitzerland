# A bot flies the space shuttle (#1498)

The shuttle itself: `player/shuttle`. The scripted pilot under it: `ShuttlePilot` (#1497, there).

## The command

`/bot shuttle [moon] [orbit] [km] [turns] [pad=<id>]` (admin; offline too, `bots/offline`):

- **Who flies**: the nearest bot within 300 m of you, else a new one beside you. It gets `ShuttleBrain`.
- **What it flies**: by default `turns` whole turns round the Earth in the cruise drive at `km` (150-2,000, default
  400), then the coast, the retro burn, re-entry, the drive home and the landing. With `moon`: the climb, a short coast in
  orbit, the trip to the Moon, down on a colony pad (`pad=` its `MoonPads` id, else the nearest free one), 30 s there, up
  and home to an Earth orbit, then the drive home and the landing.
- **`orbit`** skips the airport: a new shuttle where the bot stands (as the ride menu gives one), put in an orbit at `km`
  (`PutInOrbit`); with `moon` it stops in the Earth orbit when it is back (there is no runway). The checks' way, and a
  quick show.

## The brain (`Bots/ShuttleBrain`)

- **To the shuttle**: the nearest parked one within 5 km (`VehicleBody` of kind Shuttle, `Enterable`), walked to and
  claimed by `BotDriving.GetIn` (its `EntryPoint` by the hatch, `VehicleManager.ServerClaim`, `TakeVehicle(state, 0)`).
- **The runway**: the nearest airport's longest usable runway within 5 km (`AirportStands.Index`), oriented so that more
  of it lies ahead of where the shuttle stands, the ground's height at its threshold. Without an airport: a 3 km runway
  along the shuttle's own heading. Taken off from and landed on.
- **The controls**: the body's `FlyControls` call `ShuttlePilot.Step` every physics step and return its `Input` (the
  pilot's `OwnsControls` is false). The drive through `FootPlayer.ShuttleDrive`, the gear and speedbrake through
  `Airliner.Command`, the target hold through `Airliner.Aim`. These are a player's intents, never a key. The director's own
  `FlyControls` (the parachute) come back when it lets go.
- **The end**: stopped on the runway, it parks (`ExitVehicle`: up into the cabin, `shuttle-aboard`) and gets out by the
  hatch (`OutByShuttleHatch`), then strolls (`WanderBrain`). If the pilot gives up on the ground, the bot gets out. If it
  gives up in the air, the bot lets go and the craft flies on, as a player's would.
- Its thinks are slow (0.5 s): the flying is per physics step in the controls, whatever the director's level of detail.

## The pilot's new stages (`Player/ShuttlePilot`)

- **Between bodies** (`Travel`): `Clear` → `AimFor` → `Cruise`.
  - `Clear`: if the line along the arrival orbit's tangent passes within 200 km of the body it is round, the pilot climbs
    straight up in the drive until it is clear, then switches the drive off: a circular orbit out there. A player
    coasts round until the target rises; this is quicker, and a bot keeps real time.
  - `AimFor`: the hold turns onto `ShuttleFlight.TangentAim`. Within 1° the drive goes on.
  - `Cruise`: full lever, the hold kept. The drive goes off where the craft passes the arrival height in the target's
    sphere: an orbit round it.
  - The target's centre comes from the world frame's body (`Frames.Convert`), as the spectator's cue measures it. So it
    needs an earth world whose frame crosses with the craft: offline, the camera following it.
- **To the pad** (`Approach`, `Jets`):
  - `Approach` is in the drive. The pilot flies level over the Moon toward the pad, the speed by the distance. Once the
    aim point (5 km over the pad) lies 35° under the horizon, it flies straight at that point. The drive drops out at the
    Space exit (8 km) on the way.
  - `Jets` (the belly jets): the stick slides the craft toward the pad (at most 40 m/s, slowing within 500 m). The height
    is held to 0.4 of the distance left, and at least 150 m over the ground under it. Then straight down at a tenth of the
    height (at least 1.2 m/s), the gear down below 1.5 km. `LandedPad`, `PadMiss`.
  - The pad's ground comes from the Moon's heights where this peer has them (`BodyGround.Active`), else from its sphere.
- **Home**: `OnMoon` (a while), `LiftOff` (the jets at 0.8 into Space), `MoonUp` (the nose up, the drive at 0.3 to 100 km),
  `Travel(Earth)`, `HomeOrbit` (the Earth orbit's apsides), then the drive home and the landing (`DriveHome` on) or
  stopped.
- `Mission.Turns`: `Round` drives that many turns. `StartInOrbit()`: from the coast.

## Offline and on a server

- **Offline** (#1974, the game its own server) the bot is simulated here. Round the Earth it needs nothing more. To the
  Moon the world must go with it, which means the camera following it (the check does). No offline passengers yet
  (`PassengerService.Online`), so you watch it from the free camera.
- **On a server** the same brain and pilot run on the bot's server body.
  - Round the Earth: as the shuttle's own server path does today, the body above 60 km stays in the main region's flat
    LV95 frame (`player/orbits`). That frame is wrong far from Switzerland (the stretch): the issue's "after G2".
  - Between bodies: the frame does not cross on a server before C3b (#1975, protocol 97), so `Cruise` fails after
    15 min. No wire change was made here.
- **Players** see it as any pilot's shuttle (the pose).
