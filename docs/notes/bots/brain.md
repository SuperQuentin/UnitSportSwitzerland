# The Battle Royale brain: senses, choices, skills, the drop (#837 phase 2)

- **`BrBrain`** (`src/Bots/BrBrain.cs`), one per BR bot, given at GO by `BrManager.BoardBots`. Each thought:
  1. the drop (`Drop`) while aboard or in the air;
  2. `BotSenses.Look`;
  3. a `BrSituation` from what it knows;
  4. `BrTactics.Choose` gives the goal;
  5. the goal is carried out with the shared skills.

  A thought comes every 0.1-0.4 s, depending on the goal; at most every 0.5 s when no human is within 300 m.
- **Senses** (`BotSenses`, mode-free), what a player standing there could know:
  - **sight**: bodies within 220 m in a 150° cone ahead of where it faces, or anyone within 15 m. A ray
    on the server's collision from the eye to the chest must be clear (other figures do not hide anyone).
    It records `FirstSeen` per streak (the reaction time counts from it) and a 30 s `Memory` of
    last-seen places;
  - **hearing**: `ItemEvents.ServerHeard` (every relayed shot, and bots' own) within earshot (450 m for
    now: the relay passes the kind, not the gun), placed within 25 m of where it was;
  - **hurt**: `FootPlayer.LastAttacker`/`LastAttackedAt`. A bot shot at by someone it cannot see turns
    to where it last saw that body.
- **Choices** (`BrTactics`, pure, `BrTacticsTests`): scores, the highest wins.
  - zone hurting 100, zone about to cut it off 85 (`ZoneRunNeeded`: the distance at 4.5 m/s × 1.5 + 30 s
    against the time left; its run is 5.8 m/s, the margin pays for walls, detours and fights);
  - fight 70-90 in its gun's reach, or 60-85 within 18 m to rush with a blade, or 55 when shot at
    unseen;
  - finish a downed enemy within 40 m: 78;
  - heal (65+) and vest (60), only after 4 s with nobody in sight;
  - loot a crate within 120 m that it sees (a clear ray; any within 25 m): 62 unarmed, 45 short of rounds,
    else 15-40 by greed;
  - investigate a shot heard within 300 m: 30-55 by boldness, only with a gun;
  - roam 10.
- **Skills** (`BotSkills`, mode-free):
  - `BrBrain.GoTo`: further than 80 m, along the streets. `BrManager.Roads` is the match area's
    `Gpx.RoadNetwork`, loaded at GO off the main thread (5101 edges in 7-12 s for a 5 km generated
    town). `RoadNetwork.Path` is an A* over a walking graph with a node at every road vertex; each dead
    end is tied to the nearest vertex of another road within 4 m, because a street ending at another's
    side never joined it in the edge graph, so no route crossed town. The bot walks it waypoint by
    waypoint (skipping ahead to a nearer later one) and plans again when the goal moves 40 m (at most
    every 10 s for the same goal).
  - `MoveTo`: straight there. A knee ray 2.2 m ahead finds a wall: a jump over it when the head ray is
    clear (the body mantles), else a **wall follow** (the "bug" way round): the wall kept on one side
    (the more open side first, `Hug` sweeping from 75° towards it), until the straight way is open and
    it is nearer than where it met the wall. Headway is judged towards the goal over 4 s, so shuttling
    counts as stuck; the follow then switches sides, and a bot still stuck takes the most open way
    whatever the goal for a few seconds. Each new goal (a street waypoint too) is a fresh walk; the stuck
    count stays.
  - Seen before these: a bot stuck in a town's corner for 30 s, another shuttling 15 m to and fro for a
    minute, bots crawling at 30-200 m a minute; each died in the zone.
  - Roaming spots are always inside the circle it must be in; on its way to that circle it keeps going
    until 30 % of the radius inside (no stopping at the edge and wandering back out).
  - Goals are sticky (`BrTactics.Stickiness` 12 on the current goal); a crate it walks to stays its crate
    out of sight.
  - `Wants` (guns it lacks or better than its best, rounds for its guns, meds up to 6, a vest, a
    blade) and `TakeOne` (one stack per thought through `BrCrates.TryTake`).
  - `BotHands.Fire` and `BotHands.Use` (bandage 2.2 s, kit 5.6 s, vest 0.5 s; spent at the end; a shot
    cancels it).
- **Fighting**:
  - it faces the chest and stands inside its gun's full-damage band (`FalloffFrom` × 0.8, 8-60 m), or
    at 1.6 m with a blade;
  - close in, it strafes left and right (0.8-2 s legs);
  - it fires once `ReactionSeconds` have passed since it first saw the target, with
    `BrTactics.AimError`: the profile's error at 50 m × √(distance/50) × up to 2 for a fast target,
    settling from 1.6× to 1× over 2 s on target.
- **The drop** (`BrManager.BoardBots`, `BotJump`, `BotPlane`; `BrBrain.Drop`):
  - **boarding**: at GO, once the plane's line is known, each bot spawns in the hold, carried
    (`Carrier`) from its first frame. A body spawned in the air with nothing holding it would wait for
    ground the server never builds up there. Its entrant is not `Jumped`, so clients hide it, as they
    hide players.
  - **landing spot**: random in the inner half of the first circle (off water by the lattice;
    `--brbotspread m` caps it for checks). Spread over 85 % of the radius, the bots on the far side died
    in the first shrink at every pace: the edge closes faster than anyone runs (26 m/s at pace 0.5).
  - **jump time**: the plane's nearest pass to the spot, ± 1 s, at least 0.7 s from every other bot's.
    Bots leaving as one hit each other at plane speed and crash in the air (seen: a bot dead 1 s
    after the ramp).
  - **the jump**: `BotJump` gives the player's `JumpOut` exit (ramp, a 3 m lateral spread, the plane's
    speed capped at 55 m/s) into the wingsuit. `BotPlane` pushes out any bot still aboard when the
    doors close and sends one state message for all of them.
  - **wingsuit**: faces the spot (`FaceYaw`), keeps trim (pitch -0.35), dives (-0.9) when it would
    overshoot, and asks for the canopy below 300 m over the ground, or 550 m once within 150 m of the
    spot. Its height is the lower of the loaded ground and the horizon lattice
    (`BrManager.GroundAt`), since far tiles are not loaded on the server.
  - **canopy**: `Intent.Steer.x` turns towards the spot (positive turns right, lowering the yaw), it
    brakes the last 15 m, and it drops slowly over the spot. The body lands on foot by itself.
  - **flight controls**: `body.FlyControls` builds the `FlightInput` from `Intent.Steer`, `LookYaw` and
    `LookPitch`. The deploy or cut-away press (`Action`) is true only while the ride is not the one
    wanted (`Intent.Canopy`): a held press toggles canopy and wingsuit every physics step.
- **While looting** (phase 0) it roams in the inner half of the circle: the next circle can be anywhere inside it.
- **Fall damage**: a body with no camera (an NPC, a bot) still takes it on landing
  (`FootPlayer.UpdateCameraFeel`'s early branch).
- **Play-out**: while a player of the match is still connected (even out and spectating), the bots play
  on to a winner. The match ends early only once no player is connected.
