# Bots: server-run players, built to be reused by every mode (#837)

- **What a bot is**: a `FootPlayer` the **dedicated server runs itself** (authority 1). The spawn data is
  `PlayerReplication.BotData(n, at, yaw, name)`: the race-NPC layout plus index 8 = driver kind (1 = bot)
  and index 9 = its name. The node is `npc_1_<n>` and the id is `-(1000 + n)` (`BotId`, `IsBotId`).
  `FootPlayer.Bot` and `BotName` are set on every peer from the spawn data. `Npc` is true too, so the
  body reads no keys and has no camera.
- **Why the server**: a race NPC can run on the nearest client because nobody gains by tampering with
  it. In a fight, the nearest client is the bot's opponent: a modified client could make it freeze or
  miss, and an honest one would make it stutter at that player's frame rate.
- **Layers**, every one shared except the brain:
  - **Body**: `FootPlayer`, driven only through its seams: `WalkControls` (wish, run), `FootActions`
    (jump, crouch), the facing (`LookYaw`/`LookPitch` with `SquareToView`), and in the air
    `FlyControls`, built by the director from the intent.
  - **Hands**: `BotHands`, a player's item rules on the server (`hits`).
  - **Inventory**: `Bot.Inventory`, server-side, never saved (`Persist = false`); a mode turns it into
    a death box.
  - **Brain** (`IBotBrain.Think(bot, now)` returns the seconds until the next thought): writes
    `Bot.Intent` (`BotIntent`: `Wish`, `Run`, `Jump`, `Crouch`, `FaceYaw`, `Pitch`, `FireAt`, and in the
    air `Canopy`, `Steer`). One brain per mode:
    - `WanderBrain`, free roam (`/bot add`): strolls 25 m round home, rests, picks a new goal when it
      makes under 1 m of progress in 3 s;
    - `BrBrain`, Battle Royale (`brain`).
  - **Senses** (`BotSenses`), **skills** (`BotSkills`, `BotNav`) and the **world** a brain may reach
    (`BotWorld`: the physics space and the bodies there): `brain`. Shared by every mode.
  - **Profile**: `BotProfile.For(seed, n, difficulty, taken)`, pure and unit-tested. It gives a name (Swiss
    first names, none repeated in a match) and the knobs `ReactionSeconds`, `AimErrorDeg`, `Boldness`,
    `LootGreed`, from easy / normal / hard.
  - **Director**: `BotDirector` at `World/Bots` (server). It spawns and frees bodies (`Add`, `Remove`,
    `Clear`, `MaxBots` 64) and runs the brains every physics frame. A brain thinks at its own rate when a
    human is within 300 m (`Watched`), at most every 0.5 s otherwise; the body always moves at the
    physics rate. It applies the intent: the facing, and `FireAt` through `BotHands.Fire`. It raises
    `Died(bot, killer, cause)` and `Downed(bot, attacker)` for the mode.
- **A mode** (`BrManager.Bots`, later others) picks a bot's brain, kit and rules, and listens to the
  director's events. The body's per-player rule hooks (`FootPlayer.StayDown`, `Regenerates`,
  `CanBeDowned`) are answered by the mode on the server for its bots, as a client answers them for its
  own player.
- **Clients** draw a bot as a remote player, plus a `BotTag` (name + BOT, 60 m, depth-tested so it never
  shows through walls). The dedicated server draws no held item, even for a bot.
- **Replication**: the server's relay and spawn filters (`RelayNear`/`RelayFar`/`Vis`) are installed on
  every body the dedicated server relays, not only on proxies. Without them a server-run body would
  spawn and stream to every peer.
- **New modes**: write an `IBotBrain`, choose the kit, answer the rule hooks, handle `Died`/`Downed`. Never
  touch the body or the director for a mode. Every new feature a player can use follows
  `general/new-feature-bots` and gets a row in `bot-capability-map`.
- **The bots' clock** (`BotDirector.Clock`, #837 phase 4): the sum of the physics steps, in simulated seconds
  (`/speed` scales each step). Brains, hands and skills time everything with it.
  - Not `GameClock.Now`: it counts physics frames, which `/speed` lengthens. At x4 a 5 s revive hold took 20
    simulated seconds.
  - Not `ClockSync.SimNow`: it runs on the wall clock, ahead of a server dropping frames. A car was "stuck for
    6 s" before it had had 6 s to move.
  - The match's own stamps (zone, flight, crates) stay on `SimNow`, as the match does (#856).
- **Caught in the geometry** (`BotSkills.Unembed`, every mode, from the director): a body on foot, off the
  floor, "falling" over 20 m/s and not 0.2 m moved in 1.5 s is put on open ground nearby, still.
