# Bots in vehicles (#837 phase 3)

- **Server paths** (`Vehicles/VehicleManager`):
  - `Park` on the server spawns the vehicle itself, with itself as the authority (`Owner` 0). It used
    to send an RPC to itself, which never arrives (`CallLocal = false`, and a sender of 0), so a car a
    bot left, wrecked or was thrown from vanished from the world.
  - `ServerClaim(vehicle)` has the claim's checks (still there, not burnt out, nobody else getting in,
    `Enterable`) without its RPC. It frees the vehicle for everyone (the spawner) and returns its state
    for `FootPlayer.TakeVehicle(state, 0)`.
  - **A match car keeps its name**: `Kept` keeps a `br_v…` name when it is parked again (a player's
    `RequestPark` too). Before, a match car a player had driven was renamed `veh_<peer>_<n>`, missed by
    `BrLoot.RemoveVehicles` and kept for good by the object containers. `FootPlayer` remembers the name
    of the vehicle it got into (`_rideName`, cleared on foot) and `CaptureVehicle` hands it back.
- **The skill** (`Bots/BotDriving`, mode-free):
  - `Spot(bot, range)`: the nearest parked vehicle the pilot drives (`AutoPilot.Drives`: cars,
    motorbikes, bicycles), `Enterable`, in sight (a clear ray) or within 25 m.
  - `GetIn`: walk to its door (`EntryPoint`), claim, take.
  - `Towards(goal)`: plan the first time with `RaceRoute.BuildAsync(source, origin, at, smallest: Minor,
    toward: goal)`, off the main thread (minor roads and up, no stairs or tunnels), then drive with
    `AutoPilot.For(route, body)`. It returns Arrived within 45 m of the goal (or half that from the
    route's end) and Failed with no route or stuck under 1 m/s for 6 s.
  - `GetOut`: the pilot's `Finished` (it brakes and pulls over), then `ExitVehicle` once under 1.5 m/s.
    The car is parked again where it stopped, for anyone.
  - The director wires `body.RideControls = BotDriving.Controls(bot)`: the pilot, else the handbrake.
    A null `RideControls` would read the keys.
  - `BotHands.Fire`/`Use` refuse while mounted, as a player's items are on foot only.
- **BR** (`BrBrain`): a trip over 300 m sends it to take a vehicle.
  - **Which**: one in sight within 150 m, else the last one it saw (every 1.5 s it notes the nearest in
    sight within its sight range), if still free, within 300 m and not behind it on the way. At 70 m of
    sight alone, with a car or two a square kilometre, no bot saw one at the right moment.
  - **Getting there**: over 30 m away it walks the streets to it (`Streets`, the same walk as `GoTo`),
    then `GetIn`.
  - **Patience**: after 25 s, or stuck three times, it gives that car up and walks for 60 s. Before this,
    bots stood chasing cars behind fences or across rivers until the zone killed every one of them.
  - **At the wheel** (`Driving`): it drives to where it was going, and gets out on arrival, when stuck
    (then it walks for 30 s), or with an enemy within 80 m (nobody shoots from the wheel).
- **Free roam**: `/bot drive [metres]` (admin, `DriveBrain`) sends every bot to a point that far ahead
  of the sender, by car or bike if one is in sight. `tools/botcheck.sh` checks it: A parks a car beside
  the bots, a bot takes it, drives 120+ m along a 189-point route, gets out, and the car is parked
  again.
- **Not yet**:
  - running a bot over: `KnockedByVehicle` skips an Npc, and players' proxies have no physics body on
    the server;
  - a bot's car and the players' cars do not collide on the server;
  - `AutoPilot.ResetToLine` can teleport a stuck car back onto its line;
  - blasts (a wreck's explosion) do not hurt bots.
