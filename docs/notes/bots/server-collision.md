# The server builds collision only around bots, in a small radius (#837)

- `ServerWorld` makes its `ChunkManager` with `BuildCollision = true, CollisionByRadius = true`.
  - Players' proxies are **plain anchors**: `AddAnchor(player, collision: false)`, and
    `FootPlayer._EnterTree` asks for collision on the dedicated server only for a body it runs itself.
    They keep coarse, collision-less tiles (`net/lean-dedicated-server`).
  - A bot's body registers itself as a collision anchor (`FootPlayer._Ready`, the `Npc` authority branch).
- **Radius, not a ring** (`Terrain/CollisionReach`, pure, unit-tested):
  - A tile gets collision when its square is within `RadiusM` 300 m of the body, or of its look-ahead
    point (velocity × `LookAheadSeconds` 10, clamped to 600 m). That is usually 1 tile, and 2 to 4 at
    an edge or a corner.
  - Nothing is wanted when the body and its look-ahead are both more than `AirGapM` 200 m above the
    coarse ground: the plane, the wingsuit, the parachute.
  - A tile with collision that no anchor wants any more is unloaded once every anchor is `ReleaseM`
    400 m away (`ChunkManager.CollisionHeld`). It comes back as a coarse grid on the next evaluation.
  - The ring key gains a reach key per radius-mode anchor (its 250 m cell, its look-ahead's, airborne),
    so the wanted set is recomputed when a bot crosses a cell, not every 0.1 s.
- **What the collision is**: the client's own collision build, with no meshes.
  - Ground cells, road/bridge/kerb/wall pieces: the road tile is loaded for collision on a server,
    `roadsForBlend` `!BuildMeshes`.
  - Tunnel holes, loaded when the build wants collision.
  - Building cells.
  - `TreeColliders`, now created on the server too; it follows `CollisionAnchors`.
- **Measured** (`--bots 3 --botlog`, Riddes): the bots were placed on the ground within 5 s of spawning
  at sea level, then walked at 1.9 m/s. The bot body is placed only once `HasCollisionAt` is true, never
  below the ground.
- **Not solved yet**:
  - **Float precision**: the server never shifts its origin, so a bot 100 km from it runs physics at
    millimetre steps. A BR match keeps all its bots in one square, and moving the server's origin there
    is the likely fix.
  - **Interiors**: they are not built on the server. A bot cannot go through a door into a building yet
    (phase 2's looting).
  - **Commit budget**: the server keeps the default 4 ms, and one piece can take 8-14 ms. Measure it with
    `--serverstats` once 20 bots spread out.
