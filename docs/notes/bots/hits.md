# Hits both ways: players hit bots, bots hit anyone (#837)

- **A player hits a bot**:
  - `PlayerHits.Targets` takes `p.Bot` bodies. Race NPCs are still not targets.
  - The server's `ItemEvents.RelayHit` finds the victim by `PlayerReplication.NodeName(id)`. For a bot
    (`victim.Bot && IsMultiplayerAuthority()`) it applies the hit itself: `victim.ShotHit(damage,
    shooter)`. That covers armour, kill credit, down or out, and the body's `Died` event.
  - Every check still holds: PvP or the mode's say, the weapon's reach and damage cap, the ground in
    between, the rate of fire.
- **A bot fires** (`BotHands.Fire(bot, target)`, or `BotIntent.FireAt`), by a player's rules:
  - it picks the held weapon, else the best gun with rounds, else a blade;
  - the weapon's `Interval`, one round from `Bot.Inventory` per shot, `WeaponDef` spread and bloom (per
    bot `Heat`);
  - the pellets are traced by `PlayerHits.Shoot`/`Stab` with a **sink**: the bot's hits go to
    `ItemEvents.ServerHit`, which runs `RelayHit` with the bot as the shooter. A player's machine gets
    the hit as from that bot id (the kill credit names it); a bot's body takes it on the server;
  - `ItemEvents.ServerSend` delivers the Shot event to every peer that sees the bot (sound, flash,
    jolt);
  - the arm pose follows from the replicated `HeldItemId` and `ItemAction` (1 while aiming, back to 0
    1.5 s after the last shot).
- **PvP**: `PvpRules.Allows` lets anyone hit a bot, and a bot hit anyone, whatever `/pvp` says. A mode's
  `Override` still decides first: no friendly fire in a BR team.
- **Never `RpcId` a bot's id**: Godot reads a negative id as "every peer but that one". Every send to a
  possible bot id branches first.
- **Aim error** is the brain's business. The hands fire exactly where they are told.
- Check: `tools/botcheck.sh` (net, `commands`).
