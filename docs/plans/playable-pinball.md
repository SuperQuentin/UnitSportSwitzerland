# Playable pinball (#1325, epic #1322)

Status: **built** on `feat/1325-pinball` (PR #1333): the core, four tables, cabinets, net, controls, sounds, checks; open points with the owner (the PR's questions). Details as built: `docs/notes/terrain/pinball-play.md`.

## Decisions (owner, 2026-10-28, on #1325)

- **Physics**: a realistic ball, bumpers, slingshots, targets, spinners, ramps, subways, scoops. The
  **player's client** runs the table: a custom solver in the playfield's plane, small fixed steps,
  continuous collision for the fast ball, ramps and subways as layers. Watchers get the ball, the
  lights and the score streamed. The server keeps the score and the turn and takes over if the
  player leaves.
- **Camera**: the player's eye at the cabinet (zoomed in, backbox in view), a key / button toggle to
  a straight-down playfield view. VR plays from the real head, flipper buttons on the cabinet sides.
- **Rules, modern-lite** per table: 3-4 missions started from a lit scoop, multiball, ball save,
  extra ball, combos, a bonus multiplier, a wizard mode once every mission is done, match.
- **Four original layouts**, each with its own flow, ramps, subway and toys, and a backbox from its
  own era: Clockwork watchmaker (score reels, lit backglass), Space mission (7-segment /
  alphanumeric), Haunted castle (orange dot-matrix), Alpine ski race (modern LCD).
- **Score on the backbox**, seen by everyone around. Tilt on a hard nudge. Pad: shoulders flip.

## Design

1. **Research** → `docs/notes/terrain/pinball.md`: sizes (playfield 51 x 107 cm at 6.5°, ball
   27 mm / 80 g, 3 in flippers), the lower-third conventions (flippers, inlanes, outlanes,
   slingshots), the shots (ramps, orbits, scoops, spinners, drop targets) and the rules vocabulary.
2. **The table is data** (`PinballTable`, pure, Godot maths only): walls, posts, pop bumpers,
   slingshots, flippers, stand-up and drop targets, rollovers, spinners, scoops (with a subway's
   exit), ramps as 3D tracks (a layer of their own), lamps, and named shots. A shared lower third
   (flippers, slings, inlanes, outlanes, shooter lane, arch) and four upper playfields.
3. **The solver** (`PinballSolver`, pure, deterministic): the ball in the playfield's plane under
   5/7 g sin 6.5°, 1 ms fixed steps, swept-circle time of impact against every wall and post
   (no tunnelling), flippers as driven capsules (the surface's speed goes into the bounce), active
   bumpers and slings, ramps and wire forms as 1D tracks with height (a weak shot rolls back),
   scoops capturing and kicking out, nudge and a tilt bob. Emits switch events.
4. **The rules** (`PinballRules`, pure): players and balls, scoring, missions from the lit scoop,
   locks and multiball, jackpots, ball save, extra ball, combos, bonus and its multiplier (top
   lanes), wizard mode, tilt warnings, match from a seed. Fed the solver's events with their table
   time, so the server's mirror of a game computes the same score from the events alone.
5. **Net**: a `World/Pinball` node on the server and clients; state per (plan key, furniture
   index); peers inside only (`InteriorManager.SpaceOf`); a client asks on walking in. The player's
   client streams 20 Hz batches (ball poses, flippers, events); the server applies the events to its
   rules mirror (score, turn) and relays poses, flippers and state to the watchers, who interpolate.
   `Serve*` methods for every action (start, join, events, leave) a bot can call.
6. **The cabinet** (`PinballCabinet`): the static table per layout built once and shared; flippers,
   balls, bumper caps, drop targets, spinners, the plunger as nodes; lamps as a MultiMesh's
   instance colours, set on change; the backbox per era as an image redrawn on change through one
   shader (reels, segments, dots, LCD). In the model viewer (`[Showcase]`), in `--zfightcheck`.
7. **Playing** (`PinballPlay`, a camera takeover like the bowling aim): E / Y at the cabinet; flippers,
   plunger, nudge, start, view toggle, leave on keyboard and pad; VR from the head with the grips /
   triggers as the side buttons. `InputHints`, `ControlsHelp`, `xr/vr-action-map`,
   `bots/bot-capability-map` (bots do not go indoors yet, #1330: a gap).
8. **Sound** (`PinballSounds`): flippers, bumpers, slings, targets, spinner, drain, plunger, knocker,
   chimes and reels for the electro-mechanical table, in the sound player.
9. **Tests**: unit tests for the solver (no tunnelling, flipper shots, bumper kicks, ramps made and
   failed, drains) and the rules; `--pinballcheck` (cabinets built, a scripted game played through
   the nodes); `--zfightcheck`; `--sounds,check`; a two-client net check if memory allows.
