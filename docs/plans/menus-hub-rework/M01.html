M01

Phone open while walking

The phone opens in 0.25 s on the last app; the world stays live around it. Issue #1824.
01One press: open on the last app
Phone key goes straight to the last app (Map here): pocket to open 0.25 s, against about 1.0 s before (out 0.5 + cover 0.55).
02Cursor works the screen
The mouse is a cursor on the glass, hover ring on the button under it; WASD keeps walking the body the whole time.
03Hold right mouse: look
Cursor hidden, a crosshair dot marks the view centre and the phone dims; releasing gives the cursor back where it was.
04Home, 3x3
Nine app tiles in a fixed order; the chevron top-left folds the phone from Home, focus ring starts on Map.
05Gamepad
Sticks walk and look, D-pad picks, A presses, B backs, hold B puts away. No jump while the phone is out.
06Typing and sprint
Left: letters type, the body stops. Right: Sprint puts the phone away into the pocket.
