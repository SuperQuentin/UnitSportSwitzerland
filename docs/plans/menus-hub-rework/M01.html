M01

Phone while walking: folded first

Out folded first, open on a second press; the body walks in both. Issue #1824.
01First press: out, folded
The phone key takes it out folded: narrow cover screen, clock and six quick tiles in a fixed order; the body keeps walking.
02Folded: run a tile
A digit runs its tile and opens straight into that app (Map here); arrows move the focus, the body keeps walking.
03Second press: open on the last app
A second press unfolds into the inner screen (1.4:1) on the last app, Map here: 0.2 s from folded, against 1.05 s before.
04Cursor works the screen
The mouse is a cursor on the glass, hover ring on the button under it; WASD keeps walking the body the whole time.
05Hold right mouse: look
Cursor hidden, a crosshair dot marks the view centre and the phone dims; releasing gives the cursor back where it was.
06Home, 3x3
Nine app tiles in a fixed order; the chevron top-left folds the phone back to its cover, focus starts on Map.
07Gamepad
Sticks walk and look, D-pad picks, A presses, B backs, hold B puts away. No jump while the phone is out.
08Typing and sprint
Top: letters type, the body stops. Bottom: Sprint puts the phone away into the pocket.
