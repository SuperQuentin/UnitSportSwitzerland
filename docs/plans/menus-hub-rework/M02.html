Map app: a large see-through map

A large see-through map over the view; it fades further while you move. Issue #1824.
01Standing: map at rest
Opening Map projects a large panel over the view (about 75% of it) with a thin glass border; the background is about 60% opaque and the open phone stays small in the corner as the source.
02Walking: the world comes forward
Fades over 0.2 s when you start to move and back when you stop: borders, labels and markers stay crisp, the road and hills read through the map.
03Place selected
The map zooms on the pick and a pin marks it; the card carries distance and the two verbs, Travel first.
04Places drawer and search
The drawer covers the left third of the panel: typing filters, then Recent and Near you with distances; one row is focused for the pad.
05Adventure on: fog and travel points
Unexplored regions sit under fog (the scenery is still faintly there); blue squares are travel points, with a note pin and a quest marker glowing through.
06Travel curtain
Only the curtain hides the world: the panel grows to cover the whole view in black, with progress and a Cancel that always works.
