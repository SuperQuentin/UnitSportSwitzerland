# Rideable ski lifts (#912)

Ride up, ski down: the swissTLM3D ropeways (drawn since the aerial-ropeways work, never ridden)
become lifts. Follows the slopes (#887), the ski physics (#899) and slope races (#909).

## What is there

- **Data**: `RoadSegment`s of class Cableway (aerial tramways and gondolas alike), Chairlift,
  SkiLift and RopeTow.
  - The points are tile-local, Y at the surveyed cable height (not draped).
  - Each piece is clipped to its tile, with no feature id and no direction.
  - Every vertex is a pylon or a direction change. `RoadMeshBuilder.AppendCableway` draws the cable
    and towers, baked into the road mesh.
- **Moving from the clock on every peer, nothing sent**: cranes (`CraneMotion.Pose`), the BR cargo
  plane (`BrFlight.At(SimNow)`), the signal lamps (a MultiMesh).
- **Carrying a body**: `FootPlayer.Carrier`, used by the BR plane, also for bots on the server.
  - Skis are not a vehicle, so a carried skier stays on skis.
  - `Release(at, vel)` keeps the ride but not its speed.
- **Interact**: E (`InteractMount`), the chain in `FootPlayer.TryInteract` (a skier returns early at
  `_ride != null`), prompts in `ClientWorld.Prompts()`.

## Design

- **`RopewayLine`** (pure):
  - joined from the pieces of all tiles near the focus (endpoints snapped to 0.5 m, as
    `LaneGraph.Key`);
  - oriented lower end first;
  - arc lengths; kind (Gondola for Cableway, Chair, TBar, RopeTow);
  - a key from the bottom station's LV95 position rounded to 1 m, the same on every peer.
- **`RopewayMotion`** (pure, unit-tested): each kind has a speed and a carrier spacing.

  | Kind | Speed | Spacing | Carrier below the cable |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Gondola | 5 m/s | 45 m | cabin, 3.2 m |
  | Chair | 4.5 m/s | 22 m | chair, 2.3 m |
  | TBar | 3 m/s | 14 m | the bar reaches the ground |
  | RopeTow | 2 m/s | 8 m | handle on the ground |

  - The loop is up the line and back down it, offset 1.5 m to the side (2·L).
  - Carrier i is at s = (SimNow·v + i·spacing) mod 2L. Up while s < L, down after.
- **Drawing** (`RopewayLayer`, client):
  - lines within 3 km of the camera, one MultiMesh per line;
  - transforms set each frame for lines within 1.2 km, every 10th frame beyond;
  - no allocations per frame.
- **Boarding** (`Ropeways.TryBoard(body)`, on whoever simulates the body: the owner, or the server
  for a bot):
  - within 8 m of a bottom station, on foot or on skis;
  - waits for the next carrier to reach s = 0 (at most one spacing; the prompt says "Next chair in 3 s");
  - then `Carrier` follows it up. Chair and gondola: the seat below the cable. T-bar and rope tow:
    the cable's XZ, on the ground, facing up the line;
  - at s ≥ L − 2 m the rider is set down 4 m past the top station, facing on: on skis at 2 m/s (a
    new `ReleaseRiding`), on foot with no speed;
  - leaving early: interact again, only on a drag lift (T-bar, rope tow).
- **Devices**:
  - keyboard E; pad interact; VR grip within reach of the station (`XrHands` allows it on skis
    when a lift is in reach);
  - hints: "Board the chairlift" at a station, "Riding the T-bar · E to let go".
- **Bots**: `Ropeways.TryBoard(body)` called on the server body; a row in `bot-capability-map`.
- **Network**: none new.
  - The carriers are the clock.
  - The rider's position replicates as any player's (30 Hz, interpolated), so a remote rider
    stays within the interpolation error of the chair. A replicated lift key, to draw the remote
    rider exactly on the chair, is a follow-up if that error shows.
- **Model viewer**: `RopewayMeshBuilder`, a `[Showcase("World", "Ropeways")]` set: chair, cabin,
  T-bar, rope-tow handle.

## Steps

1. `RopewayLine` and `RopewayMotion` with unit tests: joining across tiles, orientation, the loop,
   spacing, next carrier.
2. `RopewayIndex` (load and join near a focus, client and server), `RopewayLayer`, the meshes and
   the showcase.
3. Boarding, carrying, setting down (on skis); E, pad, VR; hints; bots.
4. Checks: `--ride` near a real lift (Verbier, Médran), a lift probe that boards and reports the
   arrival, the quick tier. Notes and indexes.
