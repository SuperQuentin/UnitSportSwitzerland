# Bots in squads (#837 phase 4)

Bots play duos, trios and squads: in bot-only teams and as team-mates of humans. Decided with the user
(Oct 2026):

- **Teams:** bots fill human teams too. A human alone in a trio gets two bot team-mates; the rest of the
  field is bot-only teams. `BrState.AssignTeams` already shuffles bots and humans together after
  `BoardBots`, so this is mostly what bots do once they are in a team.
- **Cohesion:** a team lands and roams as a group. One leader per team, the others within ~40 m, spreading
  out to loot and fight.
- **Team actions:** all four of these: revive downed mates, carry dogtags to a Postauto stop, follow a human's
  pings, ping for humans.

## What is there (humans, #231 #469 #475 #480)

- `AssignTeams` at GO (seeded shuffle, parties kept together); `BrState.Hostile` already keeps bots off
  their mates; `TeamsAlive` and the last side standing.
- Downed but not out while a mate stands (`TryGoDown`, `OutIfAllDown`), revive by a mate holding Interact
  for `ReviveSeconds` 5 s within 3.5 m (`RequestRevive`, an RPC by sender; a bot target already works).
- Pings (`RequestPing`, an RPC by sender, one every 1.5 s, within 2.5 km), shown on the HUD, map and compass.
- Dogtags (`DeathBox` adds one, `LeavesTag`), used at a Postauto stop within 6 m (`RequestRecall`, an RPC by
  sender): the mate out last comes back, dropped over the stop by its own client.
- The capability map's gaps: revive a mate, pings, recall (phase 4).

## Steps

### 1. Server paths a bot can call
Per `general/new-feature-bots`: each RPC by sender gets a server method taking the actor.
- `ServerRevive(long reviver, long target)`: the checks `RequestRevive` has; `RequestRevive` calls it.
- `ServerPing(long peer, double e, double n, double alt)`: `RequestPing` calls it. Pinged events also go to a
  server-side `Pinged` event, which the team's bots read.
- `ServerRecall(long user)`, and a bot target: `RequestRecall` only brings back a connected peer, by RPC to its
  client. A recalled bot is placed by the server instead: its body (kept, hidden, since its elimination, or
  made again) drops over the stop by canopy (`BotJump`-like placement, height above the stop).

### 2. The drop as a team
`BoardBots` makes the bots and their entrants, then `AssignTeams`, then plans each team:
- **bot-only team:** one landing spot (the leader's), the members jump one after another (the spacing of
  `SpreadJumps`), each aiming a few metres off the spot;
- **team with a human:** the bots jump within 1-2 s after the human and fly towards the human, landing
  near them. Pushed out at the doors as now if the human never jumps.

### 3. Leader and cohesion (`BrBrain`, `BrTactics`)
- **Leader:** the standing human of the team if any (the first by id), else the standing bot with the
  lowest id. Recomputed on downs, deaths and recalls.
- **Followers:** roam spots are drawn around the leader's goal, not the circle; a new goal `Regroup` when
  further than 60 m from the leader (score 50, above loot greed, below fights and the zone).
- **Fights:** a mate in a fight within 150 m is a fight to join (score like hearing a shot, higher).
- **Shared sight:** what one bot sees, its bot mates know (`BotSenses` memory, marked as told, not seen: no
  reaction-time shortcut on a first sighting).

### 4. Revive
- New goal `Revive`: a downed mate within 150 m and no enemy seen within 40 m for 3 s. Score 75 (above
  loot, below a fight in reach). Go to it, stand within 3 m, `ServerRevive` after holding 5 s (a timed
  action like `BotHands.Use`, cancelled by a shot or a move away).
- **A downed bot:** crawls towards its nearest standing mate (or cover away from the enemy that downed it).

### 5. Dogtags
- `Wants`: a dogtag while recalls are open and a mate is out and not yet recalled.
- New goal `Recall`: carrying a dogtag, walk (or drive) to the nearest Postauto stop (`Stops()`), use it
  there (`ServerRecall`). Score 55, below fights and the zone.

### 6. Pings
- **Follow a human's ping:** a ping from a human mate makes a `Ping` goal for the team's bots, to go there
  (score 55) for 60 s or until they arrive. Not for a bot-only team.
- **Ping for humans:** a bot with a human mate pings an enemy it sees for the first time within 250 m (at
  most one every 10 s per bot), and a crate with a gun or a vest when it is not going for it itself.

### 7. Checks
- `brbotcheck` gets a squad part, or a new `tools/brsquadcheck.sh`: a duos match, A with a bot mate.
  - the bot jumps after A and lands within 150 m of A, and stays within 60 m while A walks;
  - A pings a point 100 m off: the bot goes there;
  - an enemy bot downs A (`--brbotsharmless` off for that bot only, or a scripted down): the mate revives A;
  - A goes out: the mate takes the dogtag from A's box to a stop and A is recalled.
- `brbotwatch` with `/br open ... trios` and `BRBOTS=30`: the match ends; revives and recalls happen
  (`[br] revived:` / `recalled:` lines by bots).

## Risks
- **Leader churn:** a leader that dies or is downed hands the group to another; followers must not run back and forth.
- **Revive under fire:** a bot reviving in the open dies too. The "no enemy for 3 s" rule and holding only
  behind cover if one is near.
- **Recall of a bot:** its body after elimination. Check what `OnBotDied` does to the body and the director
  entry before choosing between keeping it hidden and making it again.
