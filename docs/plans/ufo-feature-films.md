# UFOs and three feature films

Three films of 4-5 minutes that walk through every feature of the game, each with a story and UFOs
in it, plus everything they need built as **real game features**: a UFO that players can fly, UFO
sightings players meet in the world, and comic speech bubbles. The films are made by the trailer
director (`src/Trailer/`, `docs/notes/trailer/director.md`). Every new asset works in the game, not
only on camera.

The deck that will show them: `test_output/presentation/` (five languages; see the session that
made it). The films replace the showcase trailer's cut clips on the feature slides where they show
the feature better.

### A. The UFO (`RideKind` appended, `Player/` + `Avatar/`)

- **Model**: a procedural saucer (`UfoMeshBuilder`): dome, rim lights that chase, underside glow,
  landing legs that fold, an open hatch with a ramp. `[Showcase("Aircraft")]` in the model viewer;
  PS1 and Cartoon looks checked with `--models`. Small (2 seats) and mothership sizes (the
  mothership is scenery and an event prop, not a mount).
- **Flight**: a `Flyer` with arcade hover physics: hold altitude, strafe in any direction, yaw on the
  spot, a boost, a wobble on stop; no stall. Lands on its legs; the ramp opens on the ground.
- **Tractor beam**: a cone of light under it; pulls up loose things in reach (dropped items, a
  dormant vehicle, a cow if B adds cattle, a player who does not resist: walking out of the cone
  frees them) and lets them down when switched off. Server-authoritative like other moving bodies.
- **Three devices** (`docs/notes/general/new-action-three-devices.md`): keyboard, gamepad and VR for
  up/down, beam, legs/ramp; `InputHints`; a row in `xr/vr-action-map`.
- **Bots** (`docs/notes/general/new-feature-bots.md`): intents (`FlyControls` for the UFO, a beam
  intent), a server path for every client RPC, a row in `bots/bot-capability-map` (bots may not fly
  it yet; the PR says what they would need).
- **Sound**: a synthesised hum (rpm-like with speed), beam drone, landing thud; in `SfxBank` or with a
  `[SoundShowcase]` set; `--sounds,check`.
- **Network**: replicated pose (lights, legs, beam on/off) through the existing ride pose; checked on
  the remote peer (tier 2) when the feature is complete.
- **Where to find one**: in the R travel menu (Aircraft), like the other flyers.

### B. UFO sightings (`src/Occasions/`, an occasion)

- An `Occasion` `ufo` (schedule: rare nights, e.g. the first Friday of the month, and on demand with
  `/occasion start ufo`): saucers cross the night sky on scripted paths, hover over fields, beam up
  livestock or hay bales, land in a meadow, fly off. A mothership on special nights.
- Players see the same thing (replicated from the server's clock like the other occasions); a
  landed saucer can be boarded and flown away (it is A's mount).
- Cows: if the farm has no livestock yet, a herd of procedural cows on pastures (model in the viewer,
  a moo in the sound player) that the beam can lift: usable in the game, not only by the event.
- Bots: they look up, point, flee a beam; row in the capability map.
- Cosmetic facets obey the player's occasion preference.

### C. Speech bubbles and subtitles

- **Film**: a `Bubble` caption kind in the director: a comic speech bubble anchored over an actor's
  head (screen-projected, kept on screen), tail to the speaker, text typed in, shout and whisper
  shapes, a thought bubble. Replaces the group chat as the films' dialogue.
- **Game**: the same bubble over a player's head for chat messages said near you (a setting, on by
  default, off in VR or with a VR-friendly world-space panel), so the asset is a real feature.
- **Subtitles**: every film's English lines (captions and bubbles) carry ids; translations live in
  `docs/trailer/<film>.subtitles.json` (`fr`, `de`, `it`, `rm`); `tools/trailer.sh cut` writes
  `<film>.<lang>.srt` beside the MP4 and muxes them as subtitle tracks with language metadata
  (`mov_text`, `language=fra|deu|ita|roh|eng`). The burnt-in bubbles stay English.

### D, E, F. The films

Each film: a script in `docs/trailer/<film>.md` (story, beats, the feature each shot shows), a
`<Film>Script.cs`, `FILM=<film>` in `tools/trailer.sh`, its song (incompetech.com, CC BY 4.0,
credited on the end card), 4-5 minutes, ~45-60 shots.

- **D, "The Stolen Fondue"**: a UFO beams up the friends' caquelon on the Gornergrat; they chase it
  across the country with every way to move, and win it back.
- **E, "The Alien Tourist"**: a small saucer crash-lands in a field; the friends show its pilot
  Switzerland before the mothership returns.
- **F, "Invasion, Swiss Style"**: a fleet invades; the Swiss answer with everything they have, from
  army trucks to alphorns and fondue; it ends with the aliens dancing at the party.

Rules for all three: **Cartoon** style throughout (PS1 only to show the style switch; never the
Realistic styles, which are not ready); **real terrain** only (check stills after any region
download: a shot framed on generated land moves once real tiles are built); every feature of the
deck appears at least once across the three films, and each film covers most of them.

## Shared resources

- **Terrain**: 1,980 real tiles are built on NicoDrive (`terrain_location.json`). More places:
  copy elevation from `/Volumes/Big Key/UnitSportSwitzerland/ressources/data/swiss_chunks/`
  (read only, never write there), buildings from swisstopo, one `MapSetup` run at a time on the
  machine (it refuses a second job and they share the manifest). Watch for #858 (MapSetup reads a
  stale buildings GeoPackage): pass `--buildings-gdb` sheets by hand if a region gets no buildings.
- **Godot runs**: windowed renders steal focus on macOS; renders of the three films at once are
  slow but work. Do not run the whole test suite: the checks each piece touches only.
