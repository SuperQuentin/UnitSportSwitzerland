# Roads carry their lanes, intersections laid out from lane data (#700)

Status: **in progress** (branch `feat/700-intersection-lanes`). Phases 1 and 2 are built on the
synthetic test region (`tools/signal-test-region`), phase 3 waits for an answer on scope (below).

## Why

- **Roads with more than one lane per direction look wrong.** The carriageway width of an
  ordinary road (`Major..Lane`) is TLM's nominal class width (10/8/6/4/3 m) or OSM `width`; it is
  never built from the lanes (`CrossSectionPlanner.Prepare`; only motorways, expressways and ramps
  are, #117). The paint then divides that width into `lanesBwd + lanesFwd` equal strips
  (`PaintEmitter.Emit`: an 8 m road with OSM `lanes=4` gets 2 m lanes), traffic keeps to
  `width / 4` (`Traffic.KeepRight`: on a 2+2 road that is the lane line), `BikePlanner.LaneFor`
  sizes bike lanes from the width alone, and a turn-lane pocket on such a road treats the whole
  half as one lane (`Widening._car = half - bike`; #345's "3-lane road" item).
- **An intersection's setup is inferred even where the data says what it is.** OSM
  `turn:lanes:forward/backward` is parsed per lane (#347, `OsmOverlayReader.Row.TurnLanesFwd/Bwd`)
  and never read; the turn-lane planner decides pockets by geometry (a left pocket where a side
  road leaves to the left; at lights a left and a right pocket on every approach that has room).
  Pedestrian crossings do not exist yet (#292); OSM `highway=crossing` nodes are not even kept by
  `OsmNodes`.

## Phase 1: roads carry their lanes

Everything reads `RoadSegment.Width` and ATTR `lanesFwd/lanesBwd`, so the fix is in the two
places that decide them and in the one shared helper that lays lanes out.

1. **Width from lanes** (`CrossSectionPlanner.Prepare`). For a car road of an ordinary class
   (`IsCarRoad`, not high-speed) whose lane count exceeds the class default (more than one lane in a
   direction on a two-way road, more than one on a one-way road), the carriageway is at least
   `lanes x RoadCrossSection.LaneWidth` (3.0 m) wide: `width = max(width, lanes x 3.0)`. An OSM
   `width` replaces it, as before, when it is within 0.6..1.6 of the inferred width **and** leaves
   every lane at least `BikePlanner.MinCarLane` (2.75 m). A road with the default lanes keeps the
   class width (a 10 m road stays two 5 m lanes; a rebuild without lane data is byte-identical).
   A source that measured the width (France `largeur_de_chaussee`) is floored the same way.
2. **Two-way lanes in `RoadCrossSection`** (shared with the game): `TwoWayLanes(width, leftBike,
   rightBike, back, fwd)` gives the lane boundaries from the drawing centreline (the car lanes share
   `width - bikes` equally, as the paint does today), and `RightLaneOffset(..., forward)` the
   rightmost lane's centre in each direction of a two-way road. `PaintEmitter` draws its lines
   from the same helper, so paint and traffic agree (today they agree by coincidence of formula).
3. **Traffic** (`LaneGraph`, `Traffic`): `LaneEdge` gets a right-lane offset per direction
   (`RightLaneFwd`/`RightLaneBwd`; one-way edges keep `RightLane`); `KeepRight(edge, forward)`
   returns it. For a 1+1 road without bike lanes the value is `width / 4`, today's; with a bike
   lane the car now drives the car lane instead of straddling the bike lane. On a road with more
   than one lane in its direction a car takes the rightmost lane, except a share of them that take
   the next lane in (a hash of the car, so peers agree): `LaneEdge.LanesFwd/Bwd` and `LaneWidth`.
   An approach's `LANE` record still rules near a junction (#353).
4. **Bike lanes** (`BikePlanner.LaneFor`): takes the car lanes of the direction(s); a painted lane
   needs `MinCarLane` per car lane beside it, a Kernfahrbahn only on a 1+1 road.
5. **Turn-lane planner** (`TileRewriter.TurnLanes`): `Widening` and `ApproachLayout` learn the
   approach's lane count and lane width: the "original lane" is the leftmost car lane of the
   direction (`half - bike` split into `lanes`), not the whole half. On a road with 2+ lanes in the
   approaching direction no left pocket is built: the leftmost lane is assigned the left turn
   (phase 2 does the assignment; in phase 1 such approaches are simply skipped, counted).

## Phase 2: approaches from data

1. **The wish** (`OsmOverlayReader.AtEnd(uuid, part, m, towardEnd)`): the row at the junction end
   of the arm's TLM line (OSM maps a widened approach as its own short way, which never covers half
   of the TLM line, so `Best` does not see it). From it the approach's wanted lanes, left to
   right: `turn:lanes` of the direction arriving at that end (`TurnLanesFwd` arrives at the line's
   end, `TurnLanesBwd` at its start) mapped to `SignalMoves` (any left -> Left, through -> Through,
   any right -> Right, `None`/`Unknown` -> every turn the approach has); else `lanes:forward`
   (or `lanes` on a one-way) as N lanes with every movement. No row: no wish, today's rules.
2. **Assign, then build.** With `n` car lanes already in the carriageway (phase 1) and a wish of
   `m` lanes: the rightmost `n` wished lanes are assigned in place (no widening; arrows, stop
   line and `LANE` record over them); the `m - n` extra lanes on the left are built as left
   pockets (the existing widening, `Lane = k x TurnLane` for `k` extra lanes: a double left is
   two left-only lanes, the pocket region two lanes wide), a wished right-only lane on the right
   that the carriageway does not hold as a right pocket (today's `RightPlan`). Geometry still
   decides whether a pocket fits (the room checks, slots and merges of #123/#325 unchanged). A
   turn OSM forbids (#347 restrictions) is never wished. Without a wish: today's inference.
3. **Records and plans.** `ApproachLayout` becomes a list of lanes (From, To, Moves, Kind) with
   the widenings that move them; `PocketLanes` writes them; `SignalArm.LeftPocket/RightPocket`
   stay booleans (a double left is still "a left pocket" for phasing, #349 unchanged); the test
   region's table prints the lanes. Overhead heads for inner lanes (#354) stay open.
4. **Traffic** (`LaneApproach.LaneFor`): among several lanes showing the turn, one by the car's
   hash, so two through lanes both carry cars.

## Phase 3: pedestrian crossings from data (open question)

"Crossings" in the request most likely means pedestrian crossings, which do not exist yet
(#292). The reduced version that fits this issue: yellow zebra bars (SSV 6.17, 0.50 m bars and
gaps, 4 m long) across a junction arm, behind the stop line (the 4.5 m `SignalStopSetback`
already reserves the room), where (a) `osm_nodes.tsv` has a `highway=crossing` node on that arm
within 30 m of the junction (the reader must start keeping them), or (b) no data and the arm is
signalised inside a locality. Kerb ramps, refuge islands and traffic yielding stay with #292.
**Not started until the user confirms the scope**; if "crossings" meant something else (which
roads cross, bike crossings), this phase is dropped.

## Test region

`RoadGen --test-region` gets, east of J5 on the same row: **J6** a 2+2 artery (OSM `lanes=4`)
crossing a 10 m road, every approach `turn:lanes = left|through|through;right` (lanes assigned
in place, no widening) and **J7** a 1+1 road whose west approach row says
`lanes:forward=3, turn:lanes:forward=left|left|through;right` (a double left pocket) and whose
east approach says `through|through;right` only (no left pocket although a road leaves left).
The road between J6 and J7 is the multi-lane stretch for the paint and traffic shots.
`SignalTestRegionTests` check the lanes as built; `--trafficcheck` at J6/J7 (no red run, cars
on their lane).
