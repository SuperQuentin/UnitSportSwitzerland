# Adventure layer, phases 1 and 2: overview

Status: **built** (October 2026): #1229 (core), #1230 (places), #1231 (stamina), #1236 (navigation), #1252 (rewards). Tracking issue: #1228; follow-ups: #1263.

UnitSport already has the expensive parts of a Genshin-like open world: real Switzerland, swimming,
gliding, skiing, ~60 vehicles, farming, fishing, crafting, building, loot, bots, events. What it lacks
is the loop that gives exploring a point: **see a landmark, get there, be rewarded, see the next one**.
There are no quests, no stamina, no compass outside Battle Royale, no unlockable travel, no
collectibles and no discovery reward. The Adventure layer adds that loop, built from the swisstopo
data instead of hand-placed content.

It was designed from a study of Genshin Impact and its peers (BotW/TotK, Wuthering Waves, Ghost of
Tsushima, Death Stranding...). Kept: the reward cadence, visible progress, the reveal. Left out on
purpose: stamina-gated rewards (resin), random gear stats, gacha, power tied to account level, and
co-op where guests miss rewards.

## Build order

1. **Objectives core first**: `AdventureService`, `AdventureBook` with `SectionKey<T>`, `SectionSync`,
   the event bus, `Systems.Adventure`, the empty `AdventureLog` with `RegisterTab`. Quests can follow
   in the same PR or the next one. Every other plan builds on this.
2. **In parallel, once 1 is merged**:
   - places: the preprocessor's `PoiStage` and `PoiCatalogue` can start before 1 lands, since they don't
     touch the service;
   - stamina: it needs only its section and the reward handler.
3. **Navigation** once `PoiCatalogue` and `TrackedTarget` exist.
4. **Rewards** last: it needs the catalogue (`InTile`) and the `"stamina"` handler.

Each plan is its own issue, branch, worktree and PR (root `CLAUDE.md` workflow). Updates to a plan ride
its feature branch (`general/plans-on-main`).

## Shared contract (decided here, used by all five)

- **Code**: `src/Adventure/`, namespace `UnitSport.Adventure`, notes in `docs/notes/adventure/` with an
  index line per note.
- **Gate**: every server method checks `AdventureService.Instance?.Active(peer)`. It is false during a
  Battle Royale round (`BrManager.Playing`) and on test worlds unless a probe passes
  `--systems ...,adventure`.
- **Progress**: one file per player, `user://adventure/players/<PlayerKey fingerprint>.json`, keyed by
  the signed key (`net/sleepers`), never by the requested name. Offline, the client plays the server's
  part as peer 1 with key `local` in `user://adventure/local.json`. A peer with no proven key (a bot)
  gets progress for the session only (`peer:<id>`), never saved.
- **Sections**: each system declares `SectionKey<T> Section = new("id", Version, Migrate)` in its own
  file. Ids: `quests`, `places`, `fog`, `travel`, `pins`, `stamina`, `collect`, `chests`, `trials`,
  `photos`, `gadgets`. Sections reach their owner through the generic `SectionSync` RPC; fog has its
  own delta RPC.
- **Events**: the server raises `AdventureService.Events.Raise(...)` only after checking the act and
  writing its own section. The shared records are in `adventure-objectives` §2. Plans add their own
  records in their own files: `CantonMilestone`, `ChapterCompleted`, `StaminaRaised`, `EdelweissOffered`.
- **Reward handlers**: `"reveal"` (places), `"travel"` (navigation), `"stamina"` (stamina, `"+1"` = one
  step).
- **POI ids**: `<tag>:<slug>.<hash>`, where the hash comes from the TLM uuid, so a rerun or a TLM rename
  keeps progress. Curated aliases like `tower:uetliberg` resolve through `PoiCatalogue.Resolve`.
- **Edelweiss → stamina**: 5 offered at a summit cross or chapel = one step = +10 max stamina, 10 steps
  at most (200). There are ~9 000 edelweiss nationwide, so the cap is reached in a few cantons.
- **Items**: `ItemId` 400-419 is reserved for the rewards plan. Other plans take another block.
- **Protocol**: each PR bumps `Net/Handshake.Protocol` once for its own RPCs (49 before the first).
- **Keys**:
  - J opens one **Journal** with tabs: Quests, Atlas, Collection, Travel, Birds, Fish. The bird journal
    becomes its Birds tab.
  - M opens the adventure map while adventuring (the place search otherwise, `BrMap` in a BR round).
  - N gives the guiding gust (the quick menu's Guide wedge on pad).
  - Every action has its pad and VR form in its plan's Devices section.

## Decisions taken (October 26, 2026)

- **Journal**: one Journal on J with tabs, as above.
- **Map**: M opens the adventure map.
- **Travel**: from an unlocked sign by default. The **GA card** (`ItemId.GaCard` = 420, earned by the
  "All aboard" quest or bought at an SBB ticket office) allows travel from anywhere. `NetworkStrict` is
  dropped (`adventure-navigation` 5.3).
- **Discovery and fog**: only on foot (including climbing, swimming, skiing, the paraglider, wingsuit
  and pigeon) or in a light aircraft (small planes, gliders, helicopters) for landmark stamps. The map's
  fog also clears in any ground vehicle or boat (cars, bikes, trucks, buses, boats). Airliners reveal
  nothing (`adventure-places` 3).
- **Chests**: an opened chest disappears for that player.
- **Trial leaderboards**: per server.
- **Atlas texts**: every POI gets a generated line plus Wikidata facts (CC0). Claude drafts the ~200
  curated cards, and the team reviews them in a PR. Player-suggested facts may come later
  (`adventure-places` 1).
- **Fares** on real SBB prices, paid from the bank account (`adventure-navigation` 5.7). The
  half-fare card (CHF 190) halves fares and is the reward of "All aboard" (5 points). The GA
  (CHF 3,995) makes trips free and allows travel from anywhere; it is earned by "Grand tour" (50 points
  in 5 cantons) or bought.
- **Bus stops**: all ~23 000 are kept, as travel points and stamps.
- **Luxurious chests** come back every 1 August.
- **Accounts**: on `--accounts required` servers, progress is keyed by the Nakama account.
- **Photo spots**: Claude drafts the ~30 with the Atlas cards, for the team to review.
- **Stamina feel**: sprinting and gliding start free; playtests decide.

## Rules each PR checks

- Keyboard, gamepad and VR are decided together, with rows in `xr/vr-action-map`.
- Bots: every action has a server method a bot can call, with rows in `bots/bot-capability-map`.
- Nothing that moves rebuilds its mesh: the chest lid, the marmot and the climbing pose are nodes and
  bones.
- New models go in the model viewer and new sounds in the sound player.
- UI follows `ui/ux-guidelines` and `ui/style-guide`.
- Tests at the cheapest tier that catches the bug: tier 0 for every book and rule; quick-tier probes on
  fixture worlds with `--systems ...,adventure`; a two-client net check for anything replicated
  (`general/testing`).
