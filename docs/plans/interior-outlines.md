# Interiors follow the building's outline (#1290, takes over #1197)

Today blocks of flats follow an L/U/courtyard as rectangular wings (#577), angled outlines as their
largest rectangle; every other planner fills the minimum-area box, so an L-shaped house has rooms in
its yard. Goal: every generated interior close to the real outline, angled facades included.
Close enough, not exact.

## Model: a room keeps its rectangle and may carry a polygon

- `RoomPlan.Shape` (optional, CCW corners in the plan frame): what the room really covers. The
  rectangle stays the room's **core**: furniture, `RoomOf`, the walk map, stairs, doors between rooms
  and the street door keep reading it. Left out of the JSON when null, so a box plan is unchanged.
- An opening on an angled edge: `OpeningPlan.Edge` (the shape's edge index, `Center` along it);
  openings without it stay `Side` + `Center` on the rectangle's wall line, which a shape edge carries.
- Rooms with a floor or ceiling hole (stairs, shafts), lifts, ramps and shelters never get a shape.

## The outline

The base in the plan frame: the roof laid flat on a 0.25 m grid (as `PlanOutline` does: real and
generated solids alike, generated ones have no floor triangles), its boundary traced into rings and
simplified (Douglas-Peucker 0.3 m), so an angled facade is one straight edge again.

## Two fits, by how the plan was made

1. **Extend** (houses and offices `TryCored`, flats `TryWings`): plan inside the outline (flats on
   their wings as now; a cored plan on the largest rectangle touching the front, through a footprint
   shifted to it, then moved back), then each room on a facade grows out to the outline: from each
   point of its facade wall straight out until it leaves the building or meets another room or an
   earlier extension (piecewise linear: exact angled edges), corner fills along the outline. The
   stretch of a street door stays straight (a recessed entrance). An extension deeper than 4 m off
   a connector room is a room of its own (bedroom, office, storage by the building).
2. **Clip** (single rooms, industrial halls, venues, churches, IKEA: planned on the box): each room
   becomes its rectangle cut by the outline. Furniture must stand inside the shape (`Free`). A room
   cut to nothing, an opening left outside, or a failed validation keeps the box plan.

Fallback chain: core + extend, else box + clip, else the box as today.

## Downstream

Mesh (`Room`/`Wall`: floor and ceiling triangulated, a wall per edge, openings on any edge,
collision from the same mesh), `AddWindows` on outline edges, `RoomLights.WindowFrame`, validator
(`OnWall`, shapes inside the outline, no overlaps), `ToSvg`, `Free`.

## Drawn layouts (#1197)

`InteriorGenerator.House` builds a drawn polygon room as one shaped room (core = its biggest
rectangle) instead of 0.5 m steps; doors and windows on angled walls through `Edge`. Rooms holding a
stair keep the strips.

generated shapes, coverage (room area / outline area) and nothing outside. A coverage survey on real
tiles before/after. Screenshots inside an angled and an L-shaped building.
