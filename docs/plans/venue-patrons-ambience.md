# Venue patrons and ambience (#1244, part 6 of #1232)

Status: **built** (phases 1-6) on `feat/1244-patrons-ambience`, PR #1331; what was built is in
`docs/notes/terrain/venue-patrons.md`. Different from the plan: the bowling settees' guests do not wait for #1328 (every
settee in a lanes room has them, cheering on a timer, not on the deco bowlers' rolls); stays run on shared-clock seconds,
not game minutes; a cinema hall's audience is drawn per 15-minute showing and changes only while nobody is in the hall
(the owner's rule, added during planning); `--enter` / `/enter` were added to look inside any building without a door
(the owner's ask); no `[Showcase("Patrons")]` set: the guests show in the viewer's whole-venue interiors. The music half of #1244 is merged (#1247 radio relay, #1248 tracker
player); this is the rest: patrons, the ambience they make, and quiet terraces at night.

## Decisions (owner, 2026-10-28)

- **One issue, one PR**: #1244 is extended from "music and ambience" to "ambience and patrons".
  Chatter, cutlery and coin drops come from where patrons actually are.
- **Patrons are decoration, like `BowlingDeco`** (#1323): client-side figures, a pure function of the
  plan and the shared clock, nothing on the wire, built with the interior (asleep unless someone is
  inside). Real bot bodies indoors are #1330's.
- **They stay in place**: no walking. Small looped actions on their spot; a counter queue steps
  forward when its front customer is served and a new one appears at the back.
- **Busy-ness by schedule**: every spot (a table, a stool, a cabinet, a seat) has seeded **stays**
  (a group there for a while) and **gaps** (empty), on the shared clock. A curve per venue kind and
  hour stretches the gaps: lunch and dinner fill a restaurant, mornings a café, nights a bar or club.
  Terraces empty in rain or snow and after 23:00. A stay starts or ends only **while its spot is out
  of view** (outside the camera's frustum or in another room); on screen it waits until you look away.
  Peers may differ by those few seconds, which decoration allows.
- **A cinema hall's audience changes only while no one is in the hall** (owner): nobody ever sees a
  seat fill or empty, even off-screen behind them.
- **Which patrons**: diners inside and on terraces, the audience of a showing cinema hall, arcade
  players, counter queues, plus bar drinkers, friends on the bowling settees, figures at pool, darts and
  table football (decoration until #1324), and dancers on a club's floor.

## Design

### Spots (`Interiors/Patrons/PatronSpots`, pure)

From a built `InteriorLayout` (and a tile's `TerraceItem`s for terraces), a list of spots: position,
facing, floor, pose kind, group (a table's chairs are one group, they come and go together), activity.

| Furniture | Spots | Pose, activity |
|---|---|---|
| `TableSet`, `BanquetteSet`, `Table` + `Chair` in a dining room | each chair / bench place | seated: eat, drink, talk |
| `HighTable`, `BarStool`, `BarCounter` front | stools, standing places | standing or stool: drink, talk |
| `TerraceKind.BistroSet`, `BeerBench` | each place | seated: eat, drink, talk |
| `SeatingBank` of a hall showing a film | seats (`InteriorGenerator.RowsOf`) | seated: watch, popcorn, a laugh |
| `ArcadeCabinet`, `Racer`, `Pinball`, `DanceMachine`, `DrumGame`, `BasketballGame`, `AirHockey`, `ClawMachine`, `SimPod` | the player's place | standing or seated: play loop per machine |
| `OrderCounter`, `KebabCounter`, `ConcessionStand`, `BoxOffice`, `ShoeCounter`, `PastryCase`, `PrizeCounter` | a queue of 1-5 in front | standing: wait, step forward |
| `Settee` behind a lane pair with deco bowlers | settee places | seated: watch, cheer after a roll |
| `PoolTable`, `DartBoard`, `TableFootball` | 2 players | standing: shot loop |
| `DanceFloor` | a scatter, 1 per 1.5 m² at most | dance |

A spot a player stands or sits on is skipped (as `BowlingDeco` hides a bowler on a lane players took),
so `CinemaSeats` and bar stools stay free for players.

### Schedule (`Interiors/Patrons/PatronSchedule`, pure, xunit-tested)

- `Busy(kind, hour)` in 0..1, a curve per venue kind (`VenueMood` + room kind): Beiz, pizzeria, Asian,
  fast food peak at 12:00 and 19:30; café 8:00-11:00 and 15:00; bar and pub from 17:00 to 01:00; club
  from 23:00 to 04:00; arcade and bowling afternoons and evenings; closed hours give 0.
- Stays and gaps in game minutes, drawn from the spot's hash and an index: a café table 20-50, a
  dinner 60-120, a drink 30-90, an arcade game 3-10, a queue customer 1-3. A gap's length is scaled by
  `1 / Busy`, so a slot is full about `Busy` of the time. With the default 24-minute day that is a
  dinner every couple of real minutes, which the out-of-view rule keeps from showing.
- `Occupied(spot, envNow)` and `Phase(spot, envNow)` (where in its loop) are arithmetic on
  `WorldClock.EnvNow`: every peer agrees, a joiner sees the same tables taken.
- Terraces: `Busy` is 0 after 23:00, in rain or snow (`SnowWeather.ForDay` and the rain state), and under
  about 8 °C.
- Cinema: a showing hall's audience (30-80 % of its seats) is drawn per loop of its film
  (`CinemaVideo.LoopStart`), but a new draw takes effect only once the hall is empty of players: the
  hall keeps the audience it had until the first loop boundary with nobody inside. Every peer sees the
  replicated players in the hall, so they agree on when that was; a joiner walking into an empty hall
  gets the current draw.

### Figures and drawing (`Interiors/Patrons/VenuePatrons`, a node per built interior)

- **Near patrons are `SkinnedFigure`s** posed by bones (`PosePosed`, `SeatedFigure.Pose`, the dance
  rigs), a look from `HumanPalette.ForRider(hash)`. At most about 32 per interior, the nearest to the
  camera; a pose is set at the loop's rate (10-15 Hz), not every frame, past 15 m slower still.
- **The rest of a big room are baked**: a cinema's far rows (and a large terrace's far tables) are
  `MultiMesh`es of a few baked seated looks (`SkinnedFigure.Baked()` once per look), still. A spot
  switches tier as the camera moves.
- **Loops** (new `ItemArmPose`-style arm layers where none fit): fork to mouth (`Mouth`), glass up,
  talking hands, head turns toward table mates, popcorn, button mashing / joystick, cue stroke, dart
  throw (`ThrowWindup` / `ThrowRelease`), table football rods, a cheer (both arms up). Props by hand
  where cheap: a glass, a cup, a popcorn tub.
- **Queues**: N figures 0.7 m apart in front of the counter; when the front one's stay ends, it
  vanishes once out of view and the rest step forward over half a second.
- `MeshRebuildDisciplineTests` keeps it honest; no mesh is built per frame.

### Ambience (`Audio/VenueAmbience`, in the sound player with `[SoundShowcase]`)

- Synthesised loops: dining chatter (formant-filtered noise bursts, density by the number of patrons
  near), cutlery and plates, coffee grinder and milk steamer, beer taps; arcade attract jingles and coin
  drops; cinema lobby popcorn machine; pool balls, darts thuds, table football rattle as one-shots on the
  figures' loops; a cheer in the bowling settees. Bowling's own roll and pins are #1323's.
- A source per room at the patrons' centre, its level from how many are there (an empty restaurant is
  silent), heard through walls by `Hearing`, quiet outside, like the music's sources (`VenueMusicSource`
  sleeps out of earshot, #1281). Terraces outside on the street.
- Night: terraces silent and empty from 23:00 (the schedule's 0).

### Bots, devices, viewer

- Bots: decoration only, nothing a bot meets or uses. A row in `bots/bot-capability-map` saying so,
  with #1330 as the way to real ones.
- No new key or interaction: nothing for `xr/vr-action-map`.
- Model viewer: a `[Showcase("Patrons")]` set with each loop on a figure at its furniture piece.

## Risks

  answer; measure early in phase 3.
- **Pop-in** if the out-of-view test misses a mirror or a door portal: test against every active
  camera, as `FootPlayer.HoldRemoteFigure` does.
