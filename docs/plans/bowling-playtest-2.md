# Bowling, playtest 2 (#1336)

Status: **built** on `feat/1336-bowling-playtest-2`, PR #1337. Different from the plan: house balls are 2 per pair at the start (`HouseStart`), not 4; a player who leaves keeps their waiting ball on the return as a house ball; the third-person body is dithered see-through (`see_through`) while aiming so the line stays visible; protocol 75.
Follows #1323 (playable bowling) and #1334 (decoration bowlers, the pinsetter).

## Decisions (owner, 2026-10-28)

- **Ball return**: a short ramp with a curled end. A hood (the lift) on the pin side; a sloped double-rail track,
  about 1.6 m, that goes down one side, round the end and back up the other, holding about 12 balls.
- **House balls**: each return starts with a few house balls (3-5) anyone signed in on that pair takes with E; your
  ball comes back to the return after every roll; the racks still offer the full choice.
- **No separate replay**: when not in VR, the camera looks at the lane's own score screen up close while it plays its
  replay; the result word (STRIKE, SPARE, SPLIT...) is on that screen near the replay's end.

## Design

1. **Pins into the pit** (`LaneRig.Step`): on a reset the table's rack is hidden in the machine (pins at rest behind the
   masking), so the ten pin instances stay deadwood on the deck until the sweep has pushed them over the end and into
   the pit (`PitDrop`); they take their place in the rack only once the table starts down (`PinsetterCycle`: `ResetForth`).
   A pin still on the deck then is dropped into the pit first.
2. **The return** (`LaneGeometry.ReturnSlot(i)`, the LanePair mesh): the hood at the pin end of the return, the U
   track (two rails, a slope of about 4 %), slot 0 at the lowest point (the bowlers' end, on the U's bend), slots
   numbered back up toward the hood. `LaneRig.SetHoodBalls` lays balls on the slots; a ball newly back rises at the
   hood (`HoodLift`) and rolls down the track to its slot over a second, with the thunk.
3. **House balls on the return** (server state per pair: `ReturnBalls`, colours and weights, seeded per pair, 4 at the
   start): `Use.Return` for a signed-in bowler with no ball near the return (E, pad Y, VR reach and grip as the rack),
   `ServeTakeReturn`. A player's ball back from a roll joins the queue; a sign-out puts the ball back on the rack.
4. **Third person while bowling** (`FootPlayer.BorrowThirdPerson`, as an item throw borrows it): the bowling camera
   then sees the body as everyone sees it, the ball in its hand (`BowlingLanes.Client`, `HandLocal`).
5. **Aim** (`BowlingAim`): one aim step, A/D (left stick) moves the spot, the arrow keys (D-pad) turn the line, Jump
   starts the swing; sideways during the swing (A/D, stick, mouse) is the hook as before. The preview is the straight
   line only. Hints by `InputHints`; VR unchanged; `xr/vr-action-map` row updated.
6. **Bot partners** (`BowlingDeco.Partners`): the server sets a bot's `BallBack` to now + `DecoThrow.Release` when it
   takes its ball; each client runs `DecoThrow.At` from that moment (figure from the return to the stance, the
   approach, the swing, released as the server rolls), the ball in its hand; between turns it waits by the return.
7. **The word** (`LaneRig.Screens`): from the roll's result (pins down, standing, the frame, the bowler's last
   rolls): STRIKE, SPARE, SPLIT (head pin down, two standing groups apart), GUTTER, DOUBLE, TURKEY, 4-BAGGER... and
   300 on a perfect game; a Label3D in the replay's own world, swept in over its last second.
8. **The camera on the screen** (`BowlingWatch`, not VR): after your release the bowling camera follows the ball,
   then glides to the lane's screen as its replay starts, holds until it ends, and gives the camera back.
