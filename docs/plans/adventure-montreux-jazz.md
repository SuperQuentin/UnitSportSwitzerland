# Montreux Jazz Festival: a quay stage, a jazz set, the encore

Status: **planned**. Issue: [#1394](https://github.com/SuperQuentin/UnitSportSwitzerland/issues/1394). Part of the festivals plan
([`adventure-festivals`](adventure-festivals.md), #1269, its open question 6; phase 3 question 14 in
[`adventure-phase3`](adventure-phase3.md)). Scoped there as "the concert system plus a new open-air `StadiumSpec` on the
Montreux quay (small, its own issue)". This plan keeps it small: one venue row, one genre, one festival def, two games, one item.

## Goal

In the first half of July a party walks along the Montreux quay at dusk, finds a small stage on the lawn by the water, flags and
string lights, and a jazz quartet playing to a seated crowd with the lake behind them. Standing in the crowd through a song earns
Batzen; at the end of the set the crowd calls the band back by clapping on the beat, and the more friends clap together, the easier
the encore. The last night is the festival's grand concert.

Out of scope: the real programme (artists are made up from the seed, as every concert's), the Auditorium Stravinski and the
indoor stages, paid tickets, a playable instrument (the jazz-jam follow-up is in the table of open questions), a second stage.

## 2. The jazz set

What the concert system already does: seeded `ConcertEvent`, `StagePlan` deck + roof grid + PA, the DMX rig and the `LightDesk`
looks, the crowd multimesh, music from the server's CDs (`ConcertMusic`), the sound of the PA and the crowd. What it does not
have: a jazz genre, a small ensemble with instruments, a seated crowd.

- **`Genre.Jazz`** appended to the enum (never reordered: existing seeds keep their events byte for byte; Jazz is *forced by the
  spec/festival*, never drawn by `ConcertPlanner`'s random genre, so the 300-seed check's determinism holds). `ConcertPlanner.Jazz(stadium, instance, night)`
  builds the event from a festival seed (night n of the instance): artist name from a jazz-flavoured name list, end stage
  (`StageAt` North, deck about 12 x 7 m, 4 m roof, no screens or one small side screen), tempo 80-150 BPM, palette amber / deep blue / magenta.
- **Rig**: about 30 fixtures instead of 76-126: 6 spots, 6 washes, 4 floor beams, 2 hazers; no strobes, no blinders, no pyro
  (`Flames = Co2 = Gerbs = Confetti = 0`), one DMX universe. `Wristbands` stay (cheap): amber twinkle.
- **Looks** in `LightDesk`: a `Jazz` look set replacing the genre's section looks (calm = warm spot on the soloist, slow washes
  swaying; groove = beams fanning slowly; chorus = washes up in the palette, never a strobe; peak = a gentle build, no blackout flashes).
  The song's analysed sections still cue them, the made-up songs are slower and longer (32-bar sections).
- **The band**: a quartet of the existing performer figures (`ConcertCameras`' performers) at fixed posts, a **sway pose per beat**
  instead of the pogo moves, each holding a new procedural prop: **upright bass, saxophone, piano (with a stool), drum kit**. Props are
  built once and posed as nodes (rules: no mesh rebuilt per frame, `general/no-mesh-rebuild-per-frame`). `[Showcase("Concert")]` on the
  four builders and a `jazz` stage in the model viewer (`--models,test_output/models`).
- **Crowd**: the same multimesh, `Seated` = a shader flag (the figure lowered 0.45 m and still, swaying with the beat, hands up only in a
  chorus), laid out on the lawn by `StagePlan.Blocked`; about 600-900 people, 80 % taken; nobody on the quay's walkway (the road
  stays free for walking through).
- **Sound**: the PA hangs, the `RadioSpeaker`s and the crowd loops as now, with a quieter, longer-tailed crowd bed (applause between songs,
  murmurs) as `[SoundShowcase]` entries so `--sounds,check` lists them; no new music is committed. **Music**: the setlist reads the
  server's CDs as today; a jazz playlist is a content question for the owner (copyright-free tracks only, never committed: memory
  `royalty-free-music`). Without CDs the desk's made-up songs play (silent PA, lights only), as everywhere.
- **Night schedule**: the show plays 20:30 to 23:30 of the game clock as every concert, one `ConcertEvent` per game evening, a new
  one on each festival night (`night` = the festival day, so the 16 days show 16 sets).

## 3. The festival (`montreuxjazz`)

`FestivalDef` in `FestivalCatalog.MontreuxJazz.cs`, `src/Occasions/Festivals/MontreuxJazz/` (`MontreuxJazzRules.cs` pure), same shape as the
Christmas market and the Zibelemärit parts.

- **Id** `montreuxjazz`, window `07-fri#1 .. +15` (the first Friday of July and 15 days on: 3-18 July 2026, 2 July 2027; the real festival is
  announced late, the rule is a stable stand-in), Atmosphere off (the sky stays the season's), Decorations on (flags, string lights, the
  stage), Audio on. Token cap **400** (two games, 16 days; under the 600 framework cap, an economy-check row).
- **Site**: `FestivalSite("Quai de Montreux", E, N, 120)` = the venue's lawn, first in `Sites`; the relocation rule moves it to the region's
  largest town as for the others, **without a stage** there: a relocated Montreux Jazz is a decorated square with a stage prop and a
  recorded-style show from the seed (a `StadiumSpec`-less `StagePlan` is out of scope), so the games only run at the real venue; open question 3.
- **Day table**: the **Listening** game from day 1; the **Encore** from day 1 too (it needs a set's end); day 8 the **Jazz Night**
  (a double-length set, the Batzen of both games x1.5 that evening); day 16 the **closing concert** (below).
- **Games** (both on existing keys, `interact` / `jump`):
  1. **Listening** (`listen`, day cap 60): while a set plays and the player stands within 35 m of the stage, a Batzen drip is *not* used (no
     idle farming): a song pays **when it ends** if the player was in the audience zone for at least 80 % of it, **6 Batzen and 15 points**,
     12 songs a night at most. E (pad Y) at the **stage-front rail** marks "I'm listening" (so a bot or a lurker at 36 m is not counted
     by accident); a short toast per song. **Together**: every member of a party in the zone gets `TogetherRules.Yield(6, n, u)`
     (+25 % a friend, up to x1.75) and the **same** points; a member who left the zone earns his or her own song only.
  2. **Encore** (`bis`, day cap 40): after the last song of a night's set the band leaves and a clap ring closes on each beat like
     `PogoQte` (reuse `PogoBeat`, bottom-centre HUD, `jump` on the beat). **A crowd meter** sums the Perfect claps of every audience member
     on the *server* within the 8 beats; it needs `12` Perfects for the band to return. Alone you need all 12 yourself; two friends need 6
     each (the meter is the party's, sums with anyone near, strangers add to it without being paid); the band returns for a **bis**
     (one more made-up or the CD's next song) and pays **10 Batzen and 25 points to every member whose own claps counted at least 2**.
     So it is never worse together, and strangers help each other without a party (one-sided: only the party is paid the group yield).
  3. Both feed the **party board** (`FestivalBoard`), milestones 300 / 800 / 1500 points pay 15 / 30 / 60 Batzen; a def's milestones stay
     under a quarter of the cap (`Problems()`).
- **The item** `ItemId` **511** `JazzPass`: a wristband keepsake given on the first Listening of a festival, worn on the wrist like the crowd's
  LED band (a texture on the avatar's right wrist, cosmetic; a Journal card "Montreux Jazz 2026"). 512-514 stay reserved for this plan;
  `Headwear` none. Free in the block today: 511-514, 516-519, 522-524, 529-539 (used: 500-510, 515, 520-521, 525-528; re-check
  `ItemId.cs` at build time).
- **The closing concert** (day 16, `FestivalDef.Show = ClosingShow.Concert` or `Both`): `ConcertPlanner` takes the festival seed and plays a
  **long finale set** (a big-band look: all 30 fixtures up, the confetti cannon x2 as the only effect) with everyone in the Listening zone
  paid a **finale bonus 20 Batzen** (x`Yield` with friends). With `Both`, the **lake fireworks** (`FireworkShow`, the lake rule from
  `August1Rules`: launch point 200 m out on the bearing to open water) go off in the blackout after the last song, which `ConcertMusic` already
  marks `Finale`. This is `ClosingShow.Concert/Both`'s first user (festival-fireworks note, "not built yet"). No fireworks on the other nights.

## Network

the server judges from the shared clock (`ClockSync.ServerNow`) and the pure `PogoBeat`, needs an adventuring human within the zone
(`AdventureService.PositionOf`), 10 requests a second at most, a BR round refuses. The set itself is pure, nothing is sent (every peer builds
the night's event from the instance and the day). **Protocol 85** (one bump for the PR; main is at 84 as of writing, re-read
`Net/Handshake.Protocol` and take the next number at merge time). Persistence through the `festivals` section and
