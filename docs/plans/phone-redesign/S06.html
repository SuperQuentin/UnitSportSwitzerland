KEYBOARD / MOUSEEnter opens sheet or Confirm, Esc / right click closes one level. Confirm opens on Cancel so a double Enter is safe.
PADA opens the sheet / action, B back, LB/RB page long lists. Result line is read-only: no focus stop.
VRTap row, action, Confirm with fingertip (6 mm) or ray. Holding-hand face button closes one level; on-glass chevron too.
TOUCHRows and sheet actions 9 mm, Confirm and Cancel 9 mm apart. Drag list with inertia, tap outside the sheet to close it.
