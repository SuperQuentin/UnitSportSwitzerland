S02 Take-out and put-away / one toggle, four devices

The key that takes the phone out puts it away (except in text entry). Esc is only ever Back while the phone is out.

1 Keyboard first-spawn hint

Phone: Up or P
Press again to put it away
Shown once on first spawn

Up (kept) or new P. Same key puts it away from any page, but not while typing.

2 Pad hold ring, 0.4 s

D↑
Hold D-pad Up: phone
tap D-pad Up = emote wheel (unchanged)

Ring fills while D-pad Up is held; releasing early cancels and falls through to the emote wheel. Toast shown once.

3 Touch Phone button

stick
Phone
More
Jump
More › Phoneopen

9 mm Phone button in the on-foot layout, plus a More-sheet row. Quick-menu entry kept. Tap it again to put away.

4 VR pocket glow

hand within 15 cm: grip to take out
Free-hand grip at the pocket, or wrist menu Phone

Pocket glows amber as the hand approaches. Releasing the grip away from the pocket hangs 0.5 s, then flies home.

5 VR loose-edge ghost

open
half
Grab the edge to open
Edge ghost appears within 15 cm; hint shown once

Swing the loose edge to unfold; Holding-hand X (A if left-handed) also opens.

6 Refusal toasts, with reason

Phone unavailable: you are in a cab
Finish typing first (Enter or Esc)
Busy: travel in progress, B to cancel
toast always names the reason

Blocked in a cab, while typing, or busy. The reason is always stated; nothing fails silently.

7 Put-away hold ring

Hold B 0.4 s

Hold B (or Back on the holding hand in VR) from any page. Tap B stays Back. Click or tap outside the glass also puts away.
