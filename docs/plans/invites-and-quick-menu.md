# Quick menu, Activities screen and one invite system

Status: **planned**. Tracking issue: #840. One PR.

| Part | State | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Invite service | planned | One server-owned book of invites; each activity registers a kind with its accept handler |
| 2 Client invites | planned | Toasts with a timer, `F2` / `Shift+F2`, `/accept` `/decline` `/invites`, the Start menu's first entry |
| 3 Kinds | planned | Battle Royale, teleport requests, parties, fist fights and race duels (moved on), open races |
| 4 Quick menu | planned | A radial on hold `F2` / hold pad `Back`: Activities, Invites, Inventory, Vehicles, Map, Emotes, Bird journal, Controls |
| 5 Activities screen | planned | A shell page with tabs Invites, Battle Royale, Race: configure, launch, join |
| 6 Checks and notes | planned | Unit tests for the book and parties, a two-client invite check, screenshots, notes |

## Goal

Everything the console does for an activity can be done from a menu, on keyboard, pad and VR. And
every "someone asks you to do something together" goes through one system, so a new activity gets
invites by registering a kind, not by writing its own challenge code again (the fight and the race
duel each have one today).

Out of scope: friends lists that outlive a session, invites across servers, non-admins opening a
Battle Royale (still admin only, as `/br open`).

## 1. The invite service (server)

`src/Net/Invites/`, at `World/Invites` on the server and every client (RPCs route by path, as
`World/Chat`). Created in `ServerWorld` beside the chat and in `ClientWorld.StartNetworking`.

- **`InviteBook`** (plain C#, unit-tested): the data and its rules, with no Godot in it.
  - `Invite { Id, Kind, From, To (peer, or 0 = everyone online), Title, Detail, Expires, Key, Answered }`.
    `Key` is the kind's own handle (a race id, a lobby seed, a party id), so a kind withdraws its
    invites when the thing they point at is gone (`WithdrawKey(kind, key)`).
  - One live invite per (kind, from, to): a second one replaces the first and restarts its timer.
  - Cross invites answer each other: A invites B to fight while B's invite to A is open = B accepts
    (what `FightManager.Challenge` does today, now for every kind that says so).
  - A broadcast (`To = 0`) is answered per peer: each peer accepts or declines it for themselves.
  - Limits: 8 open invites per sender, expiry clamped to 10..300 s.
- **`InviteService`** (the node): `Register(InviteKind)`, `Send(...)`, `Withdraw`, `Accept(peer, id)`,
  `Decline(peer, id)`, the expiry tick, `PeerLeft` (drops their invites, both ways).
  - `InviteKind { Id, Label ("Battle Royale"), Seconds, Mutual, Accept(peer, invite) -> reply,
    Declined?(peer, invite), Expired?(invite), StillValid?(invite) }`. `Accept` runs on the server
    and returns the private reply (the activity's own one, as its chat command would).
  - Bots: never offered an invite (a negative id is "every peer but that one" to `RpcId`); a bot
    joins activities through the activity's own server method (`new-feature-bots` rule 2).
- **Commands** (`ChatManager`): `/invites` (what is open to you and from you), `/accept [player|id]`,
  `/decline [player|id]` (no argument: the newest one).

## 2. Client invites

- `InviteService` client side keeps `Pending` (id, kind, from name, title, detail, deadline) from
  `Offer` / `Gone` RPCs, and raises `Changed`.
- **`Ui/InviteToasts`** (CanvasLayer, top right under the BR kill feed's band): one card per invite,
  newest on top, max 3 shown, a timer bar, the keys (`{invite_accept}` / `{invite_decline}` through
  `InputHints`). It fades with the style guide's rules. A soft two-note chime (`SfxBank`, shows in the
  sound player by itself).
- Keys: **tap `F2`** accepts the newest, **`Shift+F2`** declines it (only while a toast is up; with
  none, F2 is the quick menu). Pad and VR answer through the quick menu's Invites wedge and the
  Start menu, whose first entry becomes `Accept: Nico · Battle Royale` while one is pending
  (Start, A).

## 3. The kinds

| Kind | Sent by | To | Accept does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `br` | a lobby opening (`/br open`, the screen) | everyone online not in it | `BrManager.Join` |
| `br` | `/br invite <player>` (an entrant), the screen | one player | `BrManager.Join` |
| `tp` | `/tpa <player>` (anyone) | that player | the sender goes to the accepter |
| `tphere` | `/tphere <player>` (anyone) | that player | the accepter goes to the sender |
| `party` | `/party invite <player>` | that player | joins the sender's party |
| `fight` | E at a player, `/fight <player>` | that player | `FightManager.Begin` (range checked again) |
| `race` | `/race duel <player>`, once its course is built | that player | `RaceManager.Join(race)` |
| `race` | `/race start`, once open | everyone online not in a race | `RaceManager.Join(race)` |

- **Teleport requests**: refused in a running Battle Royale or a race, as `/tp` is
  (`ChatManager.RefusedInMatch`), checked again on accept. 20 s cooldown per sender. Admin `/tp`
  and `/bring` stay as they are.
- **Parties** (`Net/Parties`, the rules in plain C#, unit-tested): a party is a leader and up to 8
  members, for the session. `/party` (who is in yours), `/party invite <player>`, `/party leave`,
  `/party kick <player>` (leader), the leader leaving passes it on, a party of one disbands. Each
  member gets the party's names (`PartyState` RPC) for the HUD and the screens. A Battle Royale
  entrant without a `/br team` name takes their party as their team; `/br team` still overrides.
- **Fights**: `FightManager` keeps the match and its rules; `_challenges`, `Challenged` and
  `ChallengeOver` go, and the client's `Pending` and `ChallengedBy` read the invite service. E at
  the challenger still accepts. Range and on-foot are checked again on accept, as now.
- **Race duels**: the "/race join N within 30 s" line becomes the invite. Declined = the duel ends
  ("X declined"); expired = as when the entry window closes today.

## 4. The quick menu

`Ui/QuickMenu` (a CanvasLayer, made by `ClientWorld` beside the emote wheel), drawn as the emote
wheel is; the ring drawing moves to a shared `Ui/Radial` helper that both use.

- Wedges, top first, clockwise: **Activities**, **Invites (n)**, **Inventory**, **Vehicles**,
  **Map**, **Emotes**, **Bird journal**, **Controls**. One that cannot be used now is drawn faint and
  says why in the middle (Vehicles while riding or in a match, Activities offline, Emotes mounted).
- Each wedge presses the action it stands for (`XrPad.Tap`, as the wrist menu does), so it opens
  the screen the key opens. Emotes opens the emote wheel latched: it stays open until a pick (click,
  A) instead of until a release.
- **Keyboard**: hold `F2`, aim with the mouse, let go (a click picks too). A tap with a toast up
  answers the invite (above).
- **Pad**: hold `Back` 0.3 s opens it, the right stick aims, let go picks. A tap of `Back` is the
  inventory, as today, opened on the release instead of the press (the only change in feel).
  `InventoryUi` hands a pad `Back` press to the quick menu, which taps `inventory` itself on a short
  release.
- **VR**: the wrist menu gets **Activities** and **Invites (n)** entries; the hand aims the ring if
  it is opened by `Back` (the controller's menu hold), as it aims the emote wheel.
- New actions `quick_menu` (F2, Back), `invite_accept` (F2), `invite_decline` (Shift+F2); rows in
  `Core/ControlsHelp` and `xr/vr-action-map`.

## 5. The Activities screen

`Ui/ActivitiesScreen` on the shell's stack (it gets the pointer and the movement keys like the pause
menu), glass panel sized to the window, tabs (Tab / LB RB): **Invites**, **Battle Royale**, **Race**.
Opened from the quick menu, the Start menu (`Activities`) and the wrist menu. Online only.

- Every button sends the same command the console takes (`/br open zug 6 normal duos`), so the
  server keeps one path and re-checks everything; the screen shows the reply in its status line
  (`ChatManager.Replied`), and also floats in the chat as it does now.
- **Invites**: rows of the open invites (who, what, time left) with Accept / Decline, and the ones
  you sent with Withdraw.
- **Battle Royale** (state from `BrManager.State`, live):
  - Header: phase, area, size, team mode, joined / bots, countdown.
  - Players: the entrants and their team names; Join / Leave; a team name field; Invite a player
    (a picker of who is online, from `RequestPlayerNames`).
  - Admin only: area (random, here, a town with the place search), size (auto, 5, 6, 7 km), length
    (short, normal, long), teams (solo, duos, trios, squads), bots (count, difficulty), and
    Open lobby / Start now / Force the zone / Cancel, by phase. Non-admins see the settings read-only.
- **Race**: the open races (`RequestRaceList` RPC: id, host, what, km, entrants, seconds left) with
  Join; Start a race (distance, mount, class, or air to a place), Duel a player, Add NPCs, Leave.
