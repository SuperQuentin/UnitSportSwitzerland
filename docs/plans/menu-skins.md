# Menu skins

> **Status (2026-10-23)**: the owner turned down Alpine and Night Arcade after seeing them in the game, then chose
> **Low-poly Sport** from four new directions (Topo, Swiss Grid, Low-poly Sport, PS1; the "Menu Theme Directions"
> canvas). Phase 1 ships it as the default skin, Classic as the other; skins are JSON files, so the colours stay
> themable. What follows is the first plan, kept for the record. Built: `docs/notes/ui/menu-skins.md`.

## The first plan: Alpine by day, Night Arcade by night

Decided with the owner on 2026-10-22, from three mockups (Candy Pop, Alpine Sticker, Night Arcade; the design
canvas "Welcome Menu Directions"):
- **One layout, two skins.** **Alpine Sticker** by day and **Night Arcade** by night.
- **Every menu, not only the welcome.** The title, solo, multiplayer, settings, pause, Character, welcome, map and
  activities pages, and the HUD panels that use the menu look (`ui/style-guide`).

## The two skins

| | Alpine (day) | Night Arcade (night) |
|---|---|---|
| Ground | cream `#FFF7EC`, green mountain silhouettes, drifting clouds | indigo `#1B1640`, twinkling stars, a moon |
| Cards | white, a thick ink outline `#3A2A22`, a hard offset shadow (sticker), sometimes tilted 1-3° | `#241D55`, a thin `#5B4FB0` outline, a soft accent glow |
| Text | ink `#3A2A22`, dim `#8A6A55` | `#F4F0FF`, dim `#B9A3FF` |
| Accent | Swiss red `#E8434B`; second accent sunflower `#FFD75E` | mint `#7FF5D1`; second accent pink `#FF8FD8` |
| Buttons | pressable blocks: lift on hover, sink on press | glowing pills; the main one pulses |
| Zoom window (Character) | a magnifying glass that swings when the part changes | a photo-booth polaroid with camera corners |
| Step progress | a little train between stations | hearts |

- **One rounded font for both**: **Baloo 2** (SIL OFL 1.1). It is bundled in `assets/fonts/` with its licence and
  listed in Settings › About.
- **Juice everywhere**:
  - hover lifts and tilts a control a little, and pressing squashes it (`Tween`, `TRANS_BACK`, ~0.25 s);
  - the chosen option pops;
  - page content eases in.
  - It is all through `UiKit`, so every page gets it by itself.
- **Accessibility**:
  - text contrast is ≥ 4.5:1 in both skins;
  - focus stays visible to a pad (a thick accent ring);
  - **Settings › Video › Reduce motion** stops the ambient animation and the tilts, keeping only plain fades.

## Which skin

- Settings › Video › **Menu look**: Auto (the default), Day or Night.
- **Auto** uses the player's own clock on the title screen (day 07:00-19:00). In a world it follows the world clock
  (`WorldClock.Hour`), so the pause menu at a world sunset turns to Night Arcade.
- A page takes the skin that is active when it is built. Pages are built fresh on every push (`ui/screens`), so the
  next page shows a change.

## Phases (each its own PR)

1. **Foundation**:
   - `Ui/MenuSkin` holds the two palettes and the Auto rule;
   - `UiTheme` colours become properties of the active skin, with the same names, so the ~70 files that use them
     need no edit;
   - sticker and glow styleboxes;
   - Baloo 2;
   - juicy `UiKit` buttons, rows and option buttons;
   - the Menu look and Reduce motion settings.
   - Then a screenshot pass of every page and HUD panel in both skins (`--uishot`), fixing the hard-coded colours
     the pass shows.
2. **Backdrops and ambience**:
   - `Ui/SkinBackdrop` behind framed pages: mountains, clouds and edelweiss by day, stars and a moon by night;
   - the title diorama's light and sky follow the skin;
   - the welcome's step progress becomes the train or the hearts.
3. **Character juice**:
   - the zoom window, a second camera in `FigurePreview`'s own `SubViewport`, glides to the head, eyes, hair or
     whole body for the part being edited (spring overshoot);
   - the figure's idle bob and blink;
   - parts as chips, colours as swatches;
   - the magnifier (day) or polaroid (night) frame;
   - a pop on each choice.
