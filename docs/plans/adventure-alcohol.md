# Alcohol: a blood alcohol level, its effects, and two breathalyzers

Status: **planned**. Tracking issue: #1271 (phase 3: #1270). Asked by the owner while deciding
phase 3 (October 27, 2026): a real alcohol level in the body, an electronic breathalyzer, and a
single-use one. Builds on `adventure-together` (wine and kirsch, the shared fondue pot) and on
`adventure-festivals` (Glühwein, the festival tent).

| Part | State | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Body model | planned | `Bac` (pure): grams of ethanol per drink, absorption, Widmark distribution, elimination; the level in ‰ at any time |
| 2 Drinks | planned | The alcohol in beer (exists), wine, kirsch, Glühwein and the fondue; alcohol-free beer |
| 3 Effects | planned | Graded by ‰: view sway, reaction delay on vehicle and walk input, drift, stumbling, no climbing, passing out |
| 4 Electronic breathalyzer | planned | Reusable, reads ‰ and mg/L to two decimals, waits out mouth alcohol, tests a friend who agrees |

## Goal

A drink has a real, measurable effect that builds up and wears off the way it does in life, and the
player can measure it. The point is realism and choice: drink at the fondue and take the train home,
or drive and feel the difference. Nothing in the game rewards drinking.

Out of scope:
- police checks and fines (a possible world event later, open question 2);
- addiction or long-term health;
- drunk talk in chat.

## 1. The body model (`src/Player/Bac.cs`, plain C#, tier 0)

**Grams of ethanol** in a drink: `ml × ABV × 0.789`.

**Absorption**: each drink enters the blood over time, not at once. The unabsorbed grams decay with a
half-life of **15 min** on an empty stomach and **30 min** while `StaminaBuff.Fed` (food slows it), so
the peak comes 30 to 60 min after the drink.

**Distribution (Widmark)**: ‰ = absorbed grams / (body kg × r).
- Body mass comes from the figure's build: about 60 to 90 kg, 75 by default.
- r = 0.6 for every figure. The game has no sex in its body model, and this sits between the textbook
  0.68 and 0.55.

**Elimination**: **0.15 ‰ per hour**, linear, never below 0.

**Clock**: real time (decided: "real"). A beer is gone in about 1.5 to 2 hours, so a player who logs off
drunk comes back sober.
- `Bac` is a drink log of (time, grams), the last 24 h, plus the closed-form level at a time `t`.
- No per-frame integration: the level is computed when asked, at most 4 times a second for effects, and
  on change for the HUD.

**Authority**: the server keeps each player's drink log in a new `alcohol` progress section (keyed like
all progress), so a reading by someone else is the server's number.
- The client reports a drink when it consumes one (`AlcoholService.Drank(itemId)`). A client that does
  not report only spares itself the effects, so nothing is trusted that matters to anyone else.
- Offline, the client plays the server's part, as everywhere in the Adventure layer.
- A peer with no proven key keeps a session-only log.

## 3. Effects

The thresholds are the Swiss road limits, so the numbers mean something. The 0.1 ‰ limit applies to new
and professional drivers, 0.5 ‰ is the general limit, and 0.8 ‰ is a qualified offence.

| Level | What the player feels | Where it is applied |
|---|---|---|
| < 0.3 ‰ | nothing | |
| 0.3-0.5 ‰ | faint view sway (0.3° at 0.2 Hz) | `PlayerFeel` camera offset |
| 0.5-0.8 ‰ | sway ×2; vehicle input lags **100 ms** | `RideInput` filter in `FootPlayer`'s ride path |
| 0.8-1.5 ‰ | input lags **200 ms** with a little steering noise; on foot, a slow drift in the walk direction; edge blur | the `RideInput` filter, the `WalkControls` filter, a post shader |
| 1.5-2.5 ‰ | stumbles (a short loss of control every 20-40 s); free climbing refused ("Not in this state"); input lag 300 ms | `FootPlayer` stumble state, `Climbable` gate |
| ≥ 2.5 ‰ | passes out: the existing knocked-out state for 60 s, wakes at 2.4 ‰ | `FootPlayer.KnockedOut` |

- **One filter for everyone**: the effects act on the intents (`RideInput`, `WalkControls`), not on raw
  keys. Keyboard, pad, wheel and VR are affected the same way, and a bot would be too, though bots never
  drink.
- **Comfort**: `Settings > Accessibility > Alcohol view effects` turns off the sway and blur and keeps the
  input lag. VR never sways the head: it gets a soft vignette and the lag only (motion sickness).
- **Remote players** see the stumbles and passing out through the existing pose states. Nothing else
  about a player's level is sent to others.
- **No mesh rebuilds**: every effect is a camera offset, a shader uniform set on change, or an input
  filter.

## 4. Electronic breathalyzer (`ItemId.Breathalyzer` = 486, reusable)

- **Price**: about CHF 120 in shops and pharmacies (`ItemCategory.Gear`).
- **Use on yourself** (the use key / pad RT / VR: bring it to the mouth):
  - a 5 s blow with a rising tone;
  - the device's own screen then shows `0.62 ‰ · 0.31 mg/L`, the two Swiss units (breath mg/L = ‰ / 2);
  - it also shows the limit line: `> 0.5 ‰: do not drive`.
- **Mouth alcohol**: within **15 min** of the last drink it reads `WAIT 12 min`, as a real device does.
- **Testing a friend**: hold the device out to a player and press E / pad Y / VR offer.
  - This sends an invite of the new kind `breath` (the existing invite system).
  - The friend accepts or declines, and the result shows on both screens.
  - Bots accept and read `0.00 ‰`.
- **The reading is the server's**: `AlcoholService.Test(peer, target)` computes it from the drink log, so
  nobody can show a false number.

## 5. Single-use test (`ItemId.BreathTestSingle` = 487, consumed)

- **Price**: about CHF 4, sold in packs of 2 at pharmacies and petrol stations.
- **How it works**: blow for 5 s, and the crystal tube turns **green** (under 0.5 ‰) or **amber** (over).
  There is no number. The tube is then used up.
- The same mouth-alcohol rule applies; an early reading is unreliable and says so.
- The colour is drawn from a shader uniform set once.

## 6. Switches, persistence, network, devices, bots

- **Server switch** `--alcohol off|mild|real` (default `real`, as decided), carried in `Arrival.Rules`:
  - `off`: drinks carry no alcohol;
  - `mild`: half the effects, and no passing out.

  Offline uses the setting `Settings > Gameplay > Alcohol`.
- **Persistence**: the `alcohol` section holds the drink log of the last 24 h. Readings are never saved.
- **Network** (one protocol bump):
  - `Drank(itemId)` (client → server);
  - `Level(permille ×100, short)` (server → owner, on change and at most once every 2 s);
  - `TestResult(target, value, mode)`;
  - the `breath` invite kind.
- **Devices**:

  | Action | Keyboard | Pad | VR |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | drink | use | RT | to the mouth |
  | blow | use | RT | to the mouth |
  | test a friend | E | Y | offer |

  Rows go in `xr/vr-action-map`, and `InputHints` shows the prompts.
- **Bots**: never drink. They accept a test and read 0.00 ‰ (a server method). Rows in
  `bots/bot-capability-map`.
- **Models and sounds**:
  - the device and the tube in the model viewer;
  - the blow tone, the beep and the crystal hiss in the sound player.
- **UI**:
  - no permanent ‰ on the HUD; you measure it;
  - a faint "you have been drinking" icon by the stamina ring while above 0.3 ‰;
  - `ui/ux-guidelines`.

## 7. Checks

- **Tier 0** (`BacTests`):
  - grams per drink;
  - the absorption curve and its peak time, fed and fasting;
  - Widmark at 60, 75 and 90 kg;
  - linear elimination to 0;
  - the threshold table;
  - mouth alcohol;
  - the single-use result at 0.49 and 0.51 ‰;
  - `off` and `mild`.
- **Quick probe** `--alcoholcheck --world flat --systems ui,physics,adventure`:
  - drink three beers on a scaled clock (`--alcohol-clock 60`, a check-only speed-up);
  - check the curve, the input lag on a car's steering, the climb refusal at 1.6 ‰, and passing out at
    2.5 ‰;
  - check the electronic reading and the used-up single test.
- **Net check** `tools/alcoholnetcheck.sh`:
  - A drinks;
  - B tests A with the electronic device after A accepts, and the value matches the server's;
  - A's level never reaches B otherwise;
  - `--alcohol off` reads 0.
