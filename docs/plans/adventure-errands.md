# Adventure errands: errand givers, daily Postkarten, world events, canton reputation

Status: **planned**. Tracking issue: #1267 (phase 3: #1270).

This is one of the four phase 3 plans ("things friends do together"), written alongside `adventure-together`,
`adventure-versteckis` and `adventure-festivals`. It builds on phases 1-2 (`adventure-overview`, whose
"Decisions taken" hold here). This plan gives the world **people who ask for things**. They are a few
server-known figures at real places. Each day they hand out a set of errands, the same for everyone in a
place. Small emergencies come up near players, and anyone nearby may help. What a player does for a canton
earns reputation there.

**Shared contract used here**: namespace `UnitSport.Adventure`, code in `src/Adventure/Errands/`, notes in
`docs/notes/adventure/`. Every server method checks `AdventureService.Instance?.Active(peer)`. Progress
goes in a `SectionKey<T>` section, read with `progress.Get` and marked with `Touch`. Events go through
`AdventureService.Events.Raise`, raised after the section is written. Rewards go through `Grant`, so cash
lands in the bank account. Quests come from an `IQuestSource` added with `QuestEngine.AddSource`. Pages are
added with `AdventureLog.RegisterTab`, and map layers with `IMapOverlay`. Offline, the client plays the
server's part (peer 1, key `local`).

## Goal

A player who walks into a village sees a postmistress with a card icon over her head. She greets them in
the village's language and offers one or two errands of the day. The errands fit the place: cheese down from
the alp, mail along the PostBus line, Läckerli to a Beiz in Bern. A friend who joins finds the same cards
and earns more for doing them together. Sometimes a cow breaks out of its pasture, or smoke rises from a
forest edge, and whoever is near can help. Weeks later the player is "Einheimisch" in Valais and cooks its
Cholera.

Out of scope:
- walking NPC schedules and the server-tracked pedestrians (#217): givers stand at their place;
- branching dialogue trees, voice acting and translated texts;
- shops at givers (the co-op stays farming's, and the specialty buyers stay `FarmBuyers`);
- real PostBus or ferry rides (`traffic-trains` and traffic buses stay cosmetic; trips are the travel
  network's);
- health damage from events (see Open questions);
- the group yield, rope-up, fondue party buff and mentor rewards (`adventure-together`);
- Schwingen and the Alpabzug (`adventure-festivals`).

## 1. Giver places: server-known data only

A dedicated server builds no building meshes (`ChunkManager.BuildMeshes` off, an empty `DoorIndex`:
`farming/produce-economy`). So a giver may only stand where the server and every client agree without a
message. That means an embedded list or the POI catalogue, never a client's building.

**`PoiCatalogue` has no farm, dairy or post office kind** (`poi-pipeline`: peak, pass, cross, waterfall, lake,
tower, church, chapel, castle, bridge, dam, station, busstop, ship, cablecar, hut). So:

| Giver kind | Source | Stand point | Count (to measure, `--giverstats`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `HutWarden` | `pois.bin` kind `hut` (264 nationwide) | the stage's outside point (below), else POI + 6 m | 264 |
| `StationMaster` | per `places.json` town, the `station` POI nearest its centre within 2 km (`TownStation`, like `VillageStop`) | 5 m east of the POI (the travel arrival rule) | ~1 per town |
| `ShipMaster` | `ship` POIs (363) | the landing | 363 |
| `Farmer` | GWR: a cluster of `BuildingKind.Agricultural` ≥ 120 m² with a dwelling within 40 m (a Hof), at most one per 2 km cell (by hash) | the yard side of the barn | [E] ~8 000 |
| `Senn` (alp dairy) | OSM `craft=cheese_maker`, `shop=cheese\|dairy`, or a name ~ `Käserei\|Sennerei\|Chäsi\|Fromagerie\|Caseificio` at ≥ 1 200 m; plus a `Farmer` cluster at ≥ 1 400 m with a herd within 300 m | as farms | [E] ~400 |
| `Dairy` (village Käserei) | the same OSM tags under 1 200 m | the door side | [E] ~600 |
| `Postmaster` | OSM `amenity=post_office` | the door side | [E] ~2 000 (branches and agencies) |
| `Host` (Beiz) | `venues.tsv.gz`: per town, the `VenueType.Restaurant` with cuisine `regional\|swiss` nearest the centre, else the nearest restaurant | the terrace side | ~1 per town |

- **The stage**: `TerrainPreprocessor --givers src/Adventure/Errands/givers.tsv.gz --osm <pbf> --gwr <dir> --out <chunk dir>`.
  It reuses `VenueFix` and `CastleStage` code and writes `GiverFormat`, a gzipped TSV that reruns
  byte for byte: `id kind e n heading canton elevation name source`. It is **embedded in the assembly**,
  like `venues.tsv.gz`, so it is on the server and every client from boot and is never sent. It follows
  the ODbL rules (`tools/osm-odbl-licence`).
- **Outside point**: the stage moves the point to 1.5 m outside the solid's footprint, on the side facing
  the nearest road or path (`FarmBuyerSite.Candidates`, the same code). The heading faces that way, so the
  figure stands at its door and the server's reach check uses the same point the client draws.
- **Ids**: `giver:<kind>:<hash>`. The hash comes from the source's stable id (OSM id, GWR EGID, the POI's
  TLM-uuid hash), so a rerun keeps reputation chains and saved cards.
- **Canton**: `CantonRings` (the PoI stage's), else the nearest `places.json` town.
- **Runtime**: `GiverCatalogue` (plain C#, tier 0), modelled on `PoiCatalogue`: `Near(e, n, r, into)` into a
  reused list with no allocation, `InCell(cellE, cellN)`, `Resolve(id)`, `Fixture(startE, startN)`, `Empty`
  (for the generated world). The fixture has 7 givers round the fixture start: a farmer at the herd's
  pasture south-west of the spawn (`0xF1E1D004`), a Senn at the cable-car top, a hut warden, a station
  master at the station, a ship master at the lake course's landing, a post office by the village stop,
  and a Beiz.
- **Names**: `GiverNames.For(id, canton)` makes a surname and a role by language region (de, fr, it, rm), for
  example "Frau Brügger, hut warden" or "M. Rossier, fromager". The same id always gives the same person.

## 4. The daily Postkarten (`PostkarteSource`)

### 4.1 Offers: the same for everyone in a place

- **Day**: the Swiss calendar date, `ErrandClock.Day`. It is the `Europe/Zurich` date of
  `ClockSync.ServerUnixNow` (offline, the local clock), as an int `yyyymmdd`, listed in
  `ClockDisciplineTests`. Cards turn over at midnight in Switzerland, wherever the server runs.
- **Cell**: 5 km LV95 cells (`cellE = floor(E / 5000)`). For each (day, cell), `PostkarteGen.Offers(day, cell)`
  (pure) picks **8 cards**. It walks the cell's givers in a hash order of `(day, cellE, cellN)`. For each one
  it tries the templates its kind can give (4.2) until one finds a valid route, giving at most 2 cards per
  giver and 8 per cell. A cell with no givers (high glaciers, lakes) offers none. It needs only
  `GiverCatalogue`, `PoiCatalogue` and `places.json`, so the server and every client get the same 8.
- **Id = everything**: `pk:<day>:<cellE>_<cellN>:<slot>`, for example `pk:20261027:517_102:3`.
  `PostkarteSource.Resolve(id)` parses it and rebuilds the `QuestDef` (cached per (day, cell), at most 64
  cells). The quests note says a generator "rebuilds from its id and `QuestState.Seed`", but
  `IQuestSource.Resolve(questId)` gets no seed. So the id carries it, and `Seed` stores the template's
  hash only as a check: a build whose generator changed refuses the stale card instead of swapping it for
  another errand.
- **"Near you or near your friends"**: the Errands tab and choice 2 list the player's own cell and the cells
  of every party member (`Parties.Members` and their replicated `GlobalPos`). That makes "go to where Lea
  is" a plan. **A card is taken at its giver only** (talking is the point), except through sharing (5).
- `For(ctx)` returns the cards of the context's cell with `Start = QuestStart.Given`. The log's
  `Available` lists only `FromLog`, so Postkarten never show as "Available" quests.
- **Gate fix needed in the engine**: today `AdventureService.Start` (the `RequestStart` RPC) starts any
  quest `CanStart` allows, so a client could start a `pk:` id from anywhere. `QuestEngine.CanStart` gains
  `bool given = false` and refuses a `QuestStart.Given` quest unless `given` is set. Only
  `ErrandService.Accept` passes it.

### 4.2 The eight templates

Every target is an **`AreaTarget`** round a giver's stand point or a POI (radius 6 m for a giver, 30 m for a
stop), never a `PoiTarget`. With places running, a POI reach advances only on its *first* discovery
(`QuestEngine.PoisByDiscoveryOnly`), and an errand must work in places already stamped.

| # | Template | Given by | Steps | Cargo granted on accept |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Alp cheese down** | Senn ≥ 1 200 m | `Deliver(cheese, 2, Dairy/Host 300-1 400 m lower, ≤ 12 km)` | 2 of the canton's cheese (4.3) |
| 2 | **PostBus mail run** | Postmaster | 3 × `Deliver(MailSack, 1, the village stop of town k)`, 3 towns within 15 km, in order | 3 `MailSack` |
| 3 | **Lost hiker** | HutWarden | `InteractStep(hiker:<id>)` (find them, 400-1 500 m off, by a peak, pass or cross), then `EscortStep(hiker, hut)` | none |
| 4 | **Ferry run** | ShipMaster | `Deliver(FerryCrate, 1, another landing of the same lake)` (`PoiCatalogue.InLake`, ≥ 4 km) | 1 `FerryCrate` |
| 5 | **Specialty delivery** | Host, Dairy, StationMaster | `Deliver(specialty of A's canton, 3, Host of town B)`, B 10-90 km away, another canton preferred | 3 of the specialty |
| 6 | **Hut supplies** | StationMaster, Postmaster ≤ 1 200 m | `Deliver(SupplyPack, 1, a hut ≥ 500 m higher, ≤ 10 km)` | 1 `SupplyPack` |
| 7 | **Farmer's request** | Farmer | `Deliver(a farm item, n, the farmer)`: potatoes, flour, carrots, a hay bale, seed (`FarmTables`), bought or grown | none |
| 8 | **Postcard home** | any, when a curated photo spot is within 10 km | `PhotoStep(view:...)`, then `Deliver(Postcard, 1, Postmaster)` | 1 `Postcard` (blank) |

- **`EscortStep(entityId, AreaTarget At)`** is a new step kind in `Errands/ErrandSteps.cs`. It advances on
  `Escorted(Peer, Key, EntityId, TargetId)`, which `ErrandService` raises when the hiker it moves reaches
  the area. Its `Plan` is `"errands"`, and `QuestCatalog.KnownPlans` gains `errands` (the catalog test).
- The hiker uses the world-event hiker code (6.3). A card's hiker exists on the server only while someone
  holds that card, and the same hiker serves everyone who holds it.
- Template 7 is farming's sink: it turns the co-op's 35 % counter into a better price for a little walking.
- Templates are checked for `Playable`: template 8 waits for `rewards` (photos), and 3 needs `places` for the
  POIs.

### 4.3 Items (block `ItemId` 430-459, the errands plan's)

- **Cargo** (`ItemCategory.Quest`, new, value 0, `NeverCrafted`; a shop never buys it): `MailSack` 430,
  `FerryCrate` 431, `SupplyPack` 432, `Postcard` 433 (written: the photo's spot name in its blurb is not
  stored; the item is generic). These are code-drawn icons in `ItemIcons`. When a card ends (done,
  abandoned or expired), the server's `Granted(item, -n)` takes the leftover cargo back.
- **Specialties** (Food, `Eat`, 2-letter icons, real values, sellable). The table is `Errands/Specialties.cs`:
  canton → item. It first ships the 8 from the brief plus 4: Gruyère (FR, the existing `ItemId.Cheese`),
  Raclette cheese (VS, 434), Basler Läckerli (BS, 435), Engadiner Nusstorte (GR, 436), Marroni (TI, 437),
  Zuger Kirschtorte (ZG, 438), Appenzeller (AI/AR, 439), Tête de Moine (JU, 440), St. Galler Bratwurst
  (SG, 441), Saucisson vaudois (VD, 442), Zürcher Tirggel (ZH, 443), Sbrinz (OW/NW/LU, 444). A canton
  without its own uses its region's (`Specialties.For(canton)`, tested to cover all 26). Values are 8-18
  CHF. A taken specialty can be eaten or sold: at most 3 cards a day × 18 × 35 % ≈ CHF 19, which is
  negligible.

### 4.4 Rewards, balanced against `fares.json`

`ErrandRewards.For(template, route)` (pure, reads `TravelRules` from `assets/adventure/fares.json`, so a
December fare change moves rewards with it):

```
chf = Ceil5(Base[t] + 1.5 × Fare(route, full) + 0.05 × climb_m)      clamped to [20, 180]
Base: alp 20, mail 25, hiker 30, ferry 15, specialty 15, supplies 20, farm 10 + 1.5 × item value × n, postcard 20
Fare: rail/bus = TravelRules.Rail(straight km), ship = TravelRules.Ship(km), template 1/6 add a cable car's line price
      when the route's two ends straddle a known line (else nothing)
```

| Route (straight) | Full fare (tariff, ×1.25 detour) | Reward | Net by train at full fare / half-fare |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 km, mail run | 5.20 | 35 | +30 / +32 |
| 30 km, Fribourg → Bern specialty | ~19 | 45 | +26 / +36 |
| 75 km, Basel → Zürich specialty | ~44 | 85 | +41 / +63 |
| 12 km ferry, Lake Geneva | ~18 (ship) | 45 | +27 / +36 |
| alp → village, 900 m down, 6 km | — (on foot) | 65 | +65 |

- **Rule (tier 0)**: every generated card pays at least its full fare + CHF 15. Taking the train is always
  worth it, and walking or driving pays best.
- **Scale**: a day's 3 cards make ~CHF 120-200, and ~300 with the friend bonus. Compare the catalog's
  CHF 60-150 quests and farming's ~1 000 for a combine load. The half-fare card (190) is about a day of
  errands; the GA (3 995) is about three weeks. Errands are a daily pocket, not a farm.
- **Reputation**: +3 in the giver's canton, and +1 in the destination's canton when it is a different one.

### 4.5 Taking, expiry, limits

- `ErrandService.Accept(peer, giverId, cardId)` is the server path (the `AskAccept` RPC calls it, and so can a
  bot). The checks are: `Active(peer)`; the card is one of today's (or yesterday's) offers in the giver's
  cell; this giver gives it; the body's published position is within 3 m + 5 m of the stand point; the day's
  `Taken.Count < 3`; and then `QuestEngine.Start(q, id, now, seed, given: true)`. On success it grants the
  cargo, touches `errands` and `quests`, and plays the stamp toast.
- **Expiry**: a card lives until the end of the next Swiss day (it is not lost at midnight mid-run). On
  `Loaded(peer)` and at each day change the service abandons expired `pk:` quests, takes the cargo back and
  notes "The card from Grimentz expired". It keeps `Done` small by pruning `pk:` entries older than 30 days
  in `Saving(progress)`.
- Abandoning does not give the day's slot back (no reroll by abandoning).

## 5. The friend bonus and sharing

- Friends standing in the same cell see **the same card ids**, so "doing it together" needs no new data:
  the same quest is active in both books.
- **Bonus**: when a `pk:` quest completes (`QuestCompleted`), `ErrandService` looks for a party member
  (`Parties.Server.Of(peer)`) who completed the same id within the last 10 minutes, or who has it active
  and is within 60 m (`SealBook`'s co-op rule: 60 m, forgotten after 10 min). If it finds one, both get
  `Cash(+50 %, rounded to 5)` and +1 reputation, once per card per player. The first to finish gets it
  later: the bonus is a `Grant` when the second finishes. The toast says "Together with Lea: +CHF 25".
- **Share**: the Errands tab's **Share with party** on a running card calls
  `ErrandService.Share(peer, questId)`. A party member then sees it under "Shared by Lea" and may
  **Join** from anywhere: `ErrandService.Join(peer, questId, fromPeer)` checks the same party, the card
  running for the sharer, and the joiner's daily cap (a joined card counts as one of the 3). The joiner gets
  their own cargo.
- After 10 cards done together, the title "Gemeinsam unterwegs" is earned. A hook for `adventure-together`:
  it owns group bonuses in general and may read `ErrandsProgress.Together` and the `ErrandDone` event's
  `With` list instead of re-deriving them.

## 6. World events (`WorldEvents`, in the UFO sightings' manner)

`src/Adventure/Errands/WorldEvents.cs` at `World/Adventure/WorldEvents`, on the server and every client.
Planning is the authority's (the dedicated server, or the game offline), and playback is every peer's from
the shared clock (`SimClock.SimAt(ClockSync.ServerNow)`), as in `ufo-sightings`.

### 6.1 Planning

- Every **120 s slot**, each 2 km cell holding an `Active` player rolls `WorldEventPlanner.Roll(cell, slot)`
  (pure, tier 0). It hashes the cell and slot, so there is no shared stream. An event comes 1 time in 15
  (about one per 30 min while someone stays). Limits: at most 1 running per 2 km cell, 6 per server, and a
  15 min cooldown per cell. The kind is drawn among those whose **anchor** passes; with none, there is
  nothing.
- A plan is `WorldEvent { Id, Kind, Start, LengthMax, E, N, Ground, Canton, Seed, AnchorRef }`. It is sent
  once (`Plan`, reliable) to peers within 3 km, and through `SendTo` to a joiner. Events are drawn within
  2.5 km.
- Server switch: `--world-events off|rare|normal` (normal by default; rare = 1 in 40). The admin command
  `/worldevent start <kind>`. For checks, `--worldevent <kind>` plans one at once near the player, as
  `--sighting` does.

### 6.2 The four kinds

| Kind | Anchor (server data only) | What helping is | Ends |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Runaway cow** | a herd's cow within 600 m (`Herds.CowNear`); any month | The cow breaks out at 1.1 m/s toward the nearest gap (away from the herd's centre). A helper within 7 m behind it **pushes** it: it walks away from the helpers' centroid at 1.4 m/s. It is back when within 15 m of its herd's centre. | back home: success; 8 min: it walks home itself, no reward |
| **Lost hiker** | a peak, pass or cross POI 0.6-2 km away and ≥ 150 m above; twice as likely 17-21 h (`WorldClock.CurrentHour`) | Find them (a figure sitting 20-80 m off the POI; "last seen near Pointe de ..." on the compass as a 300 m circle); E talks; they follow the nearest helper at 1.4 m/s to the nearest hut, bus stop or station POI within 3 km | reached: success; 15 min: a Rega helicopter flyby takes them (cosmetic), no reward |
| **Avalanche rescue** | a coarse-terrain slope of 28-45° above 1 500 m, 300-900 m away; December-April, or above 2 600 m all year | A debris cone (one mesh, built once) with 1-2 buried. Within 60 m of the cone every helper hears a transceiver beep that quickens with distance (the treasure detector's 4 Hz rule, `RewardHud`). Dig with the gather hold (G) at the spot: 8 s for one digger, 8 / n s for up to 3 | all dug out: success; 10 min: "the rescue team arrived", no reward |
| **Small forest fire** | a `Forest` cover cell at a forest edge 200-800 m away; June-September; not above 1 800 m | 6 burning 4 m cells; every 15 s one spreads to a forest neighbour (by the event's hash and tick, the same everywhere), up to 40. Out by beating (gather hold 2 s in the cell) or a `WaterBottle` use aimed at it (instant, the bottle is emptied) | none burning: success; 40 cells: "the fire brigade took over", no reward; scorch marks fade over an hour |

- **Server state**: the planner owns each event's small state: cow or hiker 2D position, dug progress, the
  burning-cell bitset, the helpers. **Acts** are `WorldEvents.Help(peer, eventId, act, arg)`: dig, beat a
  cell, talk to the hiker. Each is checked against the peer's published body position (≤ 4 m from the
  cell, spot or hiker, + 2 m slack), and the hold times are timed on the server (the gather hold's start
  and stop come as two acts).
- **Moving things**: the cow uses `Herds`' authority path. A loose cow is an override like a beamed one's,
  a new `CowLoose(herd, cow, e, n, heading)` at 4 Hz unreliable, and a reliable `CowDown` when home. The
  hiker is a `SkinnedFigure` posed walking (`PoseStride`) by bones, server-moved in 2D
  (`HikerAt` 4 Hz, unreliable, Hermite-smoothed on clients), set on the ground by each client.
  Avalanche and fire send `State(eventId, phase, byte[] bits)` reliably, on change only.
- **Rewards**: everyone who did at least one act and was within 150 m at the end gets CHF 40 (the cow 30,
  the avalanche 60), +5 reputation in the event's canton, and a count in `ErrandsProgress.Events[kind]`.
  After 5 of a kind they earn a title: "Kuhflüsterer", "Bergretter", "Lawinenhund", "Feuerwehr".
  `WorldEventHelped(Peer, Key, EventId, Kind, Canton, Success)` is raised for quests. `EventStep(Kind?)` is a
  step kind, so a later catalog quest can say "help at 3 events".
- **Seen by everyone, opt-in for no one**: events are gameplay, not an occasion's cosmetic facet. They are
  shown on the compass (an `ICompassSource` registration added to `AdventureCompass`, priority after the
  party), on the adventure map (`ErrandMapOverlay`), and in choice 2 of the dialogue.

## 7. Canton reputation

- `ErrandsProgress.Rep[canton]` holds points; the levels are computed, never stored (`Reputation.Level`, pure):

- **Pace**: one canton's daily cards give ~10 points (3 × 3, +1 with friends), plus 5 per event. That is

- **Flag**: `ErrandsProgress.Flags` (earned) and `Flag` (worn, ""). It is replicated as one byte,
  `FootPlayer.CantonFlag` (0 = Swiss, 1-26 by canton order). The held `SwissFlag` (item 4) and a planted
  `TravelFlag` (402) draw it with a shader parameter on a texture atlas of 27 code-drawn flags (no mesh
  rebuild). It is chosen in the Errands tab.
- **Costume**: 5 regional garments (Bernese Mutz, Appenzell Sennenhemd, Valais Hut, Ticino Fazzoletto,
  Romandie Dzaquillon apron), through `Avatar.Garments`: one per region, earned by the first level 4 in it.

- **Shown**: the Errands tab's reputation strip (26 crests, filled by level), the Atlas canton page's line
  "Reputation: Geschätzt (34/60)" (a hook in `AtlasPage`), and the giver's greeting.

## Network

- **Authority**: the server owns offers (computed), takes, the `errands` section, the bonus, every world
  event's state and its rewards. Clients compute the same offers and giver places themselves, from the
  embedded list and the catalogue. Neither is ever sent.
- **`ErrandService`** at `World/Adventure/Errands`:
  - client → server: `AskAccept(giverId, cardId)`, `AskShare(questId)`, `AskJoin(questId, fromPeer)`,
    `AskFlag(canton)`;
  - server → owner: `Accepted(cardId)`, `Bonus(questId, chf, withName)`, `Shared(questId, fromPeer, name)`
    (to party members, as `PartyPinsState` is), and `TellRefused` is reused;
  - the `errands` section goes through `SectionSync`. **Talking needs no RPC**: the client already knows the
    giver's cards and its own section, and only accepting is asked.
- **`WorldEvents`** at `World/Adventure/WorldEvents`:
  - server → peers within 3 km: `Plan(...)` reliable, `State(id, phase, bits)` reliable on change,
    `CowLoose` / `HikerAt` 4 Hz unreliable (LV95 doubles, `positions-on-the-wire`), `Ended(id, success)`
    reliable;
  - client → server: `AskHelp(id, act, arg)`.
- **Never `RpcId` a bot's id** (negative): the senders loop over `Connected` peers (`AdventureService`'s
  idiom).
- **Protocol**: one bump for this PR (56 today, `Net/Handshake.Protocol`). The phase 3 PRs each bump once,
  whoever merges first takes 57. `FootPlayer.CantonFlag` rides the player's existing replicated state
  (`RelayNear`, `OnChange`).

## Persistence

- **`errands`**, a new section declared in `Errands/ErrandsProgress.cs`:
  `public static readonly SectionKey<ErrandsProgress> Section = new("errands", Version: 1)`. Add it to the
  reserved list in `progress-store`.
  ```json
  { "day": 20261027, "taken": ["pk:20261027:517_102:3"], "rep": { "VS": 34, "FR": 6 },
    "events": { "cow": 2, "fire": 1 }, "together": 4, "bonusPaid": ["pk:20261026:517_102:1"],
    "flags": ["VS"], "flag": "VS", "recipes": ["VS"], "garments": [] }
  ```
  `taken` resets when `day` changes; `bonusPaid` is pruned with the 30-day `Done` pruning.
- The running cards are ordinary `quests` entries (`Active["pk:..."]` with `Seed` = the template hash).
- World events are not saved: a server restart forgets them (they last minutes). Givers are data.
- Offline, the same code runs through peer 1 and `local.json`.

## Performance

- **Givers**: at most ~12 within 400 m in a town, each a cached `SkinnedFigure` look (shared mesh per look),
  posed once. A turn is a transform at 10 Hz within 8 m. The card sprite changes on sync only. `Near` runs
  at 1 Hz into a reused list. No LINQ, strings or lists per frame. Names and lines are built when the dialog
  opens (`perf-no-per-frame-allocations`).
- **Offers**: computed per (day, cell) once and cached (8 records, at most 64 cells). Target: under 0.2 ms
  on the main thread at a cell change, measured in tier 0.
- **World events**: the planner is one hash per occupied 2 km cell per 120 s, under `ServerStats.Ran`. Fire
  is one `GpuParticles3D` per event, its emission points updated on `State` only, plus one scorch decal
  mesh built when the event's cells change (an event's shape, not a pose). The cow is the herd's existing
  MultiMesh slot. The hiker is one skinned figure. Beeps are a 4 Hz pure distance check.
- Budget: < 0.1 ms client frame with 12 givers and one event (fixture, `--perf`). Server: no measurable
  change at 32 swarm bots (`--serverstats`), since bots do not plan events.

## Open questions

1. **Daily cap: 3 cards a day, or 3 at a time?** *Recommendation*: 3 a day (the brief's "pick 3 of 8"). The
   other 5 stay visible as a reason to come back tomorrow, or to visit a friend's cell.
2. **Swiss date or the game's day for "daily"?** A farm day is 24 real minutes. *Recommendation*: the
   Europe/Zurich calendar date, as Luxurious chests use 1 August. A 24-minute daily would flood the economy.
3. **OSM post offices and dairies in a new `givers.tsv.gz`, or new `VenueType` values in `venues.tsv.gz`?**
   *Recommendation*: a separate list. A `VenueType` implies an interior plan and a sign (#1232), while a
   giver only needs a door point; the stage shares `VenueFix`.
4. **Farms from GWR clusters, or only OSM `place=farm` / named farms?** *Recommendation*: GWR clusters, at
   most one per 2 km cell. OSM farm names are patchy outside the German-speaking cantons. The count is
   measured with `--giverstats` before choosing the density.
5. **Health damage in a fire or an avalanche?** *Recommendation*: none in this plan. Health is client-side,
   and a rescue that hurts the rescuers reads badly with friends. Smoke dims the view instead.
6. **Should events run on BR-capable public servers by default?** *Recommendation*: `normal` by default,
   with the `--world-events` switch. They are off for BR entrants anyway (`Active`).
7. **Specialty items: 12 now, all 26 cantons later?** *Recommendation*: 12 items covering all 26 through
   regions (`Specialties.For`). Item ids are append-only, so adding canton-own ones later is cheap.
8. **Recipe unlocks are client-trusted** (crafting is the client's today). Is that acceptable?
   *Recommendation*: yes, the same trust as the pack. The server-side gate comes with any future
   server-side crafting.
9. **Joining a shared card from anywhere skips the talk.** *Recommendation*: allow it only inside a party
   and count it in the 3. It is the "near your friends" path, and it is what makes the bonus reachable
   for a friend who logs in late.
10. **Hiker and cow when the helper boards a vehicle or travels.** *Recommendation*: the hiker sits down
    where it is when no helper is within 80 m for 20 s, and any helper can pick it up again. The cow just
    keeps its run.
