# Server regions: physics in several local frames (G2a of #1443)

Phase G2a of `docs/plans/global-tiling.md`. The dedicated server runs its physics and its measuring in
several local frames, one per region with players or bots, still on today's LV95 world. G2 C4 then
gives each region's frame #1430's ECEF anchor and basis instead of an LV95 offset (below).

## Why

The server keeps one world frame at the manifest origin that never shifts, because its players can
be anywhere (`core/floating-origin`), and builds collision round its bots within 300 m
(`bots/server-collision`). A bot 300 km from that origin runs physics at 3 cm float steps today.
With global tiling one flat frame cannot serve players 500 km apart at all: far tiles would sit
tilted in it. So the server needs a frame, and a physics space, per region.

## Decisions (with the user)

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| Mechanism | **Hybrid**: a region's own content (its collision terrain, its trunks) lives in a `SubViewport` with its own `World3D`, so its own Jolt space (the bowling `PinDeck` already runs a second space). Replicated bodies keep their node paths (RPCs and the spawner find nodes by path, the clients' paths must match) and only their physics RID moves into the region's space; their coordinates are local to the region's frame. Rejected: raw `PhysicsServer3D` spaces with every RID moved by hand (hooks in the terrain and trunk builders, which the clients run too), and region-local scenes (each body split into a replicated node and a physics body: a rework of `FootPlayer`). |
| Keying | **Clusters**: bodies within a link distance always share a region; a region is created for a body far from every other, merged when two link, split past a larger distance. Keyed by an id and its anchor (whole-km LV95 now, the Earth position at G2). Rejected: fixed coarse cells (two bodies either side of a border could not touch). |
| Scope | **Bodies, and measuring in `GlobalPos`**: bots, players' proxies (the bots' targets) and the bots' collision live in regions; every cross-body measure moves to `GlobalPos` or a same-region filter. Parked vehicles, dropped items, radios, crates, herds and birds stay in the main frame (asleep, no physics on the server): a far bot does not bump a parked car until a follow-up. |
| The check | **Both**: a portable `--regioncheck` on the generated world in the net tier, and a one-off run on real terrain (Geneva and Val Müstair, ~330 km) recorded in the PR. |

## Design

- **`ServerRegion`**: a frame (`WorldOrigin`), a `SubViewport { OwnWorld3D }` holding its
  `ChunkManager` (collision by radius round its bots, coarse grids round its proxies) and its
  `TreeColliders`, all sharing the server's one tile cache. Its `Space` is the viewport world's.
- **The main region** is today's: the manifest origin, the root world, `World/Terrain`. It is
  permanent and never re-anchors; a body within `HomeM` of its origin, or linked to one of its
  members, belongs to it. Every check played near the default spawn therefore runs exactly as
  today, and `tools/test.sh net` is unchanged by construction.
- **`RegionPlanner`** (pure, unit-tested, like `Terrain/CollisionReach`): given each body's Earth
  position and its current region, returns each body's region and the regions to create, merge,
  re-anchor and drop. Single linkage with hysteresis: `LinkM` (2 km) joins, `SplitM` (6 km) parts,
  `ReanchorM` (8 km from the anchor) re-anchors. Decided at most every 0.5 s and when a body
  crosses a 250 m cell (as the collision reach key).
- **A body in a region** (`FootPlayer.Region`, dedicated server only): its node stays at
  `World/Players/<name>`; `PhysicsServer3D.BodySetSpace` moves its RID into the region's space and
  again on every `ENTER_WORLD` (Godot resets it to the viewport's); `Origin`, `Terrain` and a new
  `FootPlayer.Space` resolve through it. `Space` replaces the 19 `GetWorld3D().DirectSpaceState`
  calls, `Bot.Space` and `AutoPilot`'s.
- **A handover** moves a body from one region to another: the target adds it as an anchor first
  and builds collision under it; once `HasCollisionAt` (at once for a proxy), its position is
  re-expressed in the target frame (`target.ToWorld(source.ToGlobal(p))`, velocity unchanged
  without rotation), its RID switched and its anchor dropped from the source. A merge is every
  member handed over; a re-anchor is a handover into a new region at the members' centre.
- **Measuring**: the A sites move to `GlobalPos` (`Global`, `NetGlobal`, `VehicleBody.Global`) or
  to bodies of the same region (`BotWorld.Bodies()` lists only the bot's region). Two bodies in
  different regions never compare floats. The B sites in the bot stack take the body's own
  `Origin`, not the service's.
- **Ground at an Earth position** (`ServerRegions.GroundAt(GlobalPos)`): the region holding that
  tile answers in its own frame. It replaces the ground lambdas of `ServerWorld` (sleepers,
  places, structures, Versteckis, errands).

## Status (PR #1457)

All six steps built. What came out differently from the design above:

- **Shadows**: the main `ChunkManager` keeps a plain anchor for every body in another region, so the
  data the server serves and reads in its main frame (heights, the steamer, the airport stands, the
  dormant fleets, the generated fill) loads as before. `GroundAt(GlobalPos)` was not needed: the ground
  lambdas keep reading the main terrain.
- **The parked things moved into scope** (decided with the user once the real-terrain run showed Riddes
  and Geneva, ~45 km from the real map's manifest origin, play in regions of their own): not as region
  members but as **mirrors** (`RegionMirrors`). Every solid thing the server holds in its main frame
  (parked vehicles, Versteckis decor, BR structures and crates) gets a static copy in each far region
  within 300 m of its bots; bots spot, reach and take cars and react to saucers through Earth
  positions in their own frame. The replicated nodes stay where the network and the services find them,
  as bodies do.
- **Teleports across regions**: `ServerRegions.Put(body, at)` (a bot called to a Schwingen ring).
- **Statics bound to the main terrain**: `WaterField.In(terrain)` while a far body steps and thinks.
- **The real-terrain run**: the D: tile set spans 93 km (Geneva to Valais), so it ran at Geneva and
  Riddes (each in a region of its own); the 300 km case is the generated world's.
- Results: the tracks of a run with both sites match each site alone to 0.000 cm; `--noregions` puts the
  far site 3.7 cm off; `--nomirrors` lets the far walker through the parked car (22.7 m).

## G2 C4: regions on Earth positions (#1513)

The position half, built in PR #1584:

- **Anchors**: a region's anchor is its members' Earth mean, the mean of their ECEF positions put on the ellipsoid
  (`RegionPlanner.Anchoring.Earth`), and its frame the ENU there (`WorldOrigin(FrameAxes)`): true north, true metres,
  its own up and gravity. The planner already measured in Earth distances (`GlobalPos` since #1502).
- **When**: whenever the server's main world is an anchor frame (`--frame earth`). Under the flat frame regions keep
  their whole-km LV95 frames, so nothing changes there; ENU regions over the flat tiles do not work (the flat
  `ChunkManager`'s heights are LV95 altitudes in world Y).
- **Handovers turn**: `OriginFrame.Carry` keeps a body's bearings against the local east-up-south (exact at its
  place; true heading kept across a 300 km teleport), mirrors likewise.
- **Entity interest** buckets by level-13 `CubeKey` (~1.2 km), neighbours through `CubeKey.Neighbour` across face
  edges (seven at a cube corner).

Left for the follow-up, now that C1's earth streamer (#1576) is on main: the server under `--frame earth`, the main
region and each ENU region on `EarthWorld`'s leaves (their `ChunkManager` is still the flat one), and `InterestService`'s line of sight over the pyramid with
refraction and Earth occlusion (still the 100 m horizon lattice).

## Out of scope

- Dropped items, radios and herds have no collision on the server to mirror; birds fly in the main frame.
