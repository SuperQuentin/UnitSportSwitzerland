# Adventure together: group yield, the shared pot, rope-up, mentors, the punch card

Status: **built** (PR #1274). Tracking issue: #1266 (phase 3: #1270). Parts 1-7 and the checks are in; see "Built differently" at the end for what changed.

One of four Adventure phase 3 plans, written in parallel: `adventure-errands` (errand givers,
Postkarte errands, world events, canton reputation), `adventure-versteckis` (prop hunt),
`adventure-festivals` (Älplerfest, Alpabzug, seasonal festivals) and this one. This plan makes playing
with friends the best way to play. It adds no new progression of its own. It multiplies what the
other systems already give, and it adds a few things that only two or more players can do.

**Shared contract used here** (phases 1-2, `adventure-overview`): namespace `UnitSport.Adventure`, code
in `src/Adventure/Together/`. Every server method checks `AdventureService.Instance?.Active(peer)`
(off in a Battle Royale round and on test worlds unless `--systems ...,adventure`). Progress goes in a
`SectionKey<T>` declared in the owner's file, written then `Touch`ed and synced by `SectionSync`.
Events are raised after the section is written. Rewards go through `AdventureService.Grant`.
Offline the client plays the server (peer 1, key `local`). Parties are `Net/Invites/Parties`
(`Of(peer)` on the server, `Members` on the client, a leader and up to 8 for the session).

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| 1 Together | One rule for "playing together": a party member, adventuring, within 60 m; counted on the server and, for local loot, on the client |
| 2 Group yield | +25 % harvest, gathering and fishing per friend counted (3 at most), and better odds for rare fish and finds; every player gets their full share |
| 3 Links | Holding hands (Sky) and the rope (a Seilschaft): a soft follow constraint on the walk intent, a hand pose made of bones, a rope node, shared regen, belayed falls |
| 4 Two-player routes | Leg-up (Räuberleiter), two-handle gates, roped chests, hidden crevasses on glaciers |
| 5 The shared pot | Fondue or raclette at a restaurant, a hut, a chalet or a campfire: everyone adds an ingredient, everyone stirs figure-eights, a buff for the whole table, a forfeit for lost bread |
| 6 Mentors | A veteran who guides a newcomer through a first summit, glacier, ride or build: both get a unique cosmetic |
| 7 This week and the punch card | A weekly "played together" chest, and a Stempelkarte that never resets |
| 8 Checks and notes | Unit tests, `--togethercheck` and `--fonduecheck`, `tools/togethernetcheck.sh`, notes |

## Goal

Two friends who meet in the Alps should earn more than two players alone, without competing for the
same loot. They can tie in and cross a glacier no one would cross alone. They can sit down at a
Beiz and stir a fondue that keeps the whole party going for the afternoon. A veteran who shows a
newcomer the first summit gets something to wear for it. Showing up together on any day counts,
and a week off loses nothing.

Out of scope:
- Voice chat, and friend lists that last beyond the session. Parties stay per session; friends are
  remembered only as short keys in the player's own file (part 7).
- Splitting loot, a group inventory, trading.
- Producing cheese, wine or kirsch on the farm. Cows give no milk today. Cheese still comes from
  groceries, chests and the festivals plan's Chästeilet (hook).
- The errand givers and their co-op errands (`adventure-errands`).
- The festival long table (`adventure-festivals`, which uses part 5's session).
- Restaurant staff serving the pot (venues epic #1232, later).

## 1. Together

`src/Adventure/Together/TogetherRules.cs` (pure, linked into tier 0):

- `public const float Radius = 60f` (the same as `Seals.Coop` in rewards, so one distance means
  "together" everywhere), `VerticalRadius = 40f`, `MaxCounted = 3`.
- `int Count(GlobalPos self, ReadOnlySpan<Mate> mates)`, where `struct Mate { GlobalPos Pos; bool Active; bool Down; }`.
  It counts party members, other than the player, who are `Active`, not knocked out (the replicated
  `.:Down`), and within 60 m horizontally and 40 m vertically. The result is capped at 3. The mode
  does not matter: a friend in a car beside your field counts.
- **Who is a friend: party members only.** A party is joined by consent through an invite
  (`net/invites`), so a stranger cannot follow you around to farm the bonus. Bots are never in
  parties (they get no invites), so they never count.
- **Server**: `TogetherService.Near(peer)` reads `Parties.Server.Of(peer)` and
  `AdventureService.PositionOf`. It is used for everything the server grants.
- **Client**: `TogetherService.Client.Near` is cached and refreshed at 1 Hz from `Parties.Client.Members`
  and the members' replicated bodies (the same read the compass uses for party markers). It is used
  for local loot (part 2). `NearChanged` fires on a change; the HUD shows a small "Together ×2" chip
  beside the stamina ring (amber when 1 or more, hidden at 0).
- **Gate**: off (0) while `Permissions.InMatch`, and in a Versteckis round (that plan reports its
  round through `AdventureService.InMatch`, so `Active` is false).

## 2. Group yield bonus

`TogetherRules.Yield(int baseCount, int n, double u) = floor(baseCount * (1 + 0.25 n) + u)` (u
uniform in [0,1): stochastic rounding, so the expected yield is exact). Every harvester rolls for
themselves. Nothing is split, and a friend's harvest takes nothing from yours, because gathering
spots are already counted per player (`loot/gathering`: local spot counters).

| Source | Where it changes | With n friends (cap 3) |
|---|---|---|
| Gathering: firewood, stone, dead wood, water bottles | `Loot/Gathering` before `ItemController.Give` | count × (1 + 0.25 n) |
| Hand harvest | `HandFarming.HarvestAt` (`FarmRules.HandYield`) | × (1 + 0.25 n) |
| Fishing: bite wait | `FishRules.MeanBiteSeconds(..., together: n)` | ÷ (1 + 0.15 n) |
| Fishing: rare species | `FishRules.Weight`: Rare, VeryRare, Legendary | × (1 + 0.5 n) (×2.5 at 3) |
| **Rare find** (new) | `Gathering`: stone above 1 800 m can give a `Bergkristall` (ItemId 400) | base 0.4 % per stroke × (1 + 0.5 n) |
| Quest cash (catalog quests; errands pay their own bonus) | `AdventureService.Grant` of `Cash` while `Near(peer) > 0` | + 10 % per friend (server) |

- Chests, edelweiss, cairns and trials are unchanged. They are already per player and their odds are
  published (`ChestLoot.Chance`). The together rule only reaches them through seals (`Seals.Coop`,
  as built).
- The pure rules take `n` as an argument and stay Godot-free. Fishing and gathering are the owner's
  (the inventory is local, `items/inventory`), so the client applies its own `Near`. This is the same
  honest-client trust the inventory already has. The server-granted cash uses the server's `Near`.
- **Feedback**: the gather toast reads "+3 firewood (+1 together)". The bonus part is a separate
  string, built only when n > 0, from a static table. A bonus catch says "Together: a rarer bite".

## 3. Links: holding hands and the rope

### 3.1 The link

`LinkBook` (pure) keeps follower → leader pairs and a `LinkMode { Hand = 1, Rope = 2 }`:
- **Hand**: pairs only, 1.6 m at most.
- **Rope**: a chain of up to 4 (a Seilschaft), 8 m between neighbours.

`TogetherService` (node `World/Together`, made beside `World/Adventure` when `Systems.Adventure` is
on, on the server and every client) owns the book on the server. It sends
`Links(int[] follower, int[] leader, byte[] mode)` to **every** peer on any change (reliable,
a few bytes). Everyone needs it to draw the hands and the rope.

- **Asking**: **hold** `interact_mount` 0.5 s at a party member: "Hold E: hold hands". A tap is
  still the fist-fight challenge (`combat/fist-fight`). This sends the invite kind `rope`
  (`InviteKind`, 30 s, `TellAccepted = false`, `Mutual`: two players asking each other link at once).
  The asker leads. Also from the Activities screen's party row ("Hold hands", "Rope up") and
  `/rope <player>`, `/rope off`.
- **Mode**: Hand by default. It pays out to Rope by itself when either body is on `CoverClass.Glacier`
  or `Snowfield` cover, climbing, swimming, or more than 1.6 m apart for 1 s, and back to Hand when
  both are on foot within 1.2 m on other ground. "Rope up" from the Activities screen forces Rope (for
  a chain of 3-4).
- **Breaks**: either side lets go (tap E with no target, `/rope off`, VR: open the grip). Also: a
  vehicle that is not a shared ride (riding along, `RidingWith`, keeps the link), more than 25 m apart
  for 3 s (lag, a teleport), leaving the party, leaving the server, `Active` false.
  `LinkBook.Unlink` returns the pairs that broke, and the server tells both sides.
- `ServeLink(leader, follower, mode)` and `ServeUnlink(peer)` hold the rules. The invite's
  `Accept` and a bot both call them.

### 3.2 The follow constraint (on the intent, so bots get it too)

`Tether` (pure): `(Vector3 Wish, bool Run) Apply((Vector3 Wish, bool Run) own, in TetherState s)`.
It is applied where `FootPlayer`'s walk path resolves its wish (`WalkControls?.Invoke()` else the
stick), so a bot's `WalkControls` goes through the same constraint.
- **Inside the length**: your own wish, untouched.
- **No input while following in Hand mode** (Sky's hand-hold): the wish points at the leader's trail.
  The trail is a ring of 32 points, one every 0.4 m of the leader's replicated position. The target is
  the point 1.1 m behind the leader. `Run` follows the leader's speed (> 4 m/s). A newcomer can let go
  of the stick and be walked up a path.
- **Beyond the length**: the part of the wish pointing away from the partner is removed, and a pull
  toward them is added (0.8 per metre over, capped at 1). Nobody is dragged off a cliff: the pull never
  points over a drop the walk code would refuse anyway.
- The owner applies it to its own body from the partner's replicated position (about 100 ms old).
  That is fine for a soft constraint. Nothing new is sent per frame.

### 3.3 Shared stamina and belays

`Stamina.Step(dt, rest, company)` gains `StaminaCompany { None, Linked, Belayed }`, set by the owner
from the link and the partner's replicated pose:
- **Linked** on the floor or seated: regen × 1.2 (`CompanyFactor`).
- **Belayed**: you are climbing and holding still (no climb spend for 1 s), and your rope partner
  stands on the floor or holds their own climb still. You regen 6 /s (`RegenBelayed`) instead of 0.
  The rope takes your weight. This is what makes a long route possible for two that one cannot climb.
- **Catching a fall**: a roped body that falls while its partner stands (or holds a climb) is held at
  the rope length. Its velocity is projected onto the sphere round the partner, it swings, then hangs
  (`Hanging`, the climbing pose on a vertical rope). From there it climbs any steep face, or the
  partner **hauls** it (hold E 2 s at the rope, pad hold Y, VR hand over hand on the rope): the
  hanging owner rises 0.6 m/s toward the partner on `Haul` (`TellHaul`, server-relayed). If both fall,
  nothing holds.
- Owner-side like all stamina (`player/stamina`). `StaminaWatch` learns that a hanging, roped body is
  not an implausible climb (it reads the link book on the server).

### 3.4 What people see (nothing rebuilds a mesh)

- **Hands**: a new arm override `ArmHold` beside `ItemArmPose` in `HumanMeshBuilder.ApplyArms`.
  Every peer computes it from the link book and both bodies' replicated positions: the leader's left
  wrist and the follower's right wrist meet at the midpoint between their shoulders' side points, at
  0.95 m. The point is clamped to the arm's reach (0.62 m), and the elbow is solved with `Limb.Solve`.
  It is keyed to 2 cm in `FootPoseKey`. A `SkinnedFigure` only moves bones. A held item keeps the
  other hand. VR hands (`VrHands`) override it as they override every arm pose.
- **Rope**: `RopeNode` (a `MeshInstance3D`) is one shared unit-length tube mesh (8 sides, 12 rings),
  stretched between the two harness points (hips + 0.1 m) by its transform each frame. The sag is in
  its shader (`rope_sag` uniform: 0 taut, up to 0.15 × length slack, set on a change of more than
  0.05). There is one node per rope link, made and freed on `Links`. `[Showcase("Adventure", "Rope")]`
  with a harness and a carabiner on the figure (the `BackItem` slot's mount, not a garment).
- **Sounds** (`SfxBank`, static, in the sound player by themselves): a carabiner clink on tying in, a
  rope zip on a caught fall, a "Zieh!" haul grunt from the existing voice synth.

### 3.5 VR

Holding hands: grip with your hand within 0.15 m of a party member's hand (their replicated `VrHands`
hand, or a desktop player's posed wrist bone). That sends the same invite. If both players grip, the
link is mutual and immediate. Your controller then stays near their hand. `XrHandHold` (new, beside
`XrClimb`) pulls the tracked hand's target, never the body. Walking still goes through the tether.
Opening the grip lets go. Hauling: grip the rope (within 0.12 m of the rope's line) and pull hand over
hand. Each pull of more than 0.2 m sends `AskHaul` for 0.5 s.

## 4. Two-player routes

- **Leg-up (Räuberleiter)**: a linked player who crouches facing a wall (`FootActions` crouch, held)
  offers a step. A partner within 1 m who jumps toward the wall gets a free climb-jump of 2.6 m: no
  stamina, and allowed while winded. Both owners check it from replicated poses. It makes the ledges
  of the climbing plan's "too high alone" boulders reachable. No new action.
- **Two-handle gates** (`Zweimanntor`): a rewards layout like the seals (`RewardPlacer`, from the
  spot's hash). A heavy iron gate in front of a castle cellar, a hut's storeroom or a mine adit, with
  two wheel handles 3.5 m apart. Holding E (pad hold Y, VR grip and turn) at a handle sends
  `AskHandle(gateId, handle, held)`. `ServeHandle` opens the gate for 60 s (for everyone present)
  when both handles are held by different bodies within the same 1.5 s. It is server state like
  `SealBook`. Behind it: a Fine or Precious chest from the rewards tables. 1 in 6 castle and hut spots
  get one (rewards' `SealFor` table gains the row).
- **Roped chests**: a new `SealKind.Roped`, for chests on glaciers or past a crevasse field (all
  Luxurious chests above 2 800 m, 30 % of Precious ones on glacier cover). The seal is done when a
  roped pair (Rope mode, both `Active`) stands within 8 m of the chest. `SealBook` checks the link book.
  A solo player sees "Tie in with a friend to reach this".
- **Hidden crevasses** (glacier tours): `Crevasses.At(e, n)` (pure). It hashes 12 m cells of glacier
  cover: 4 % hold a hidden crevasse, a strip 1.5 m wide across the cell, pointing down the slope.
  - **Unroped**: stepping on one breaks the snow bridge on the owner. A `CrevasseNode` (an ice slot
    10 m deep, with its own collision walls and floor, `[Showcase("Adventure", "Crevasse")]`) is made
    under the body. The terrain collision layer is dropped from the body's mask for as long as the
    body is inside the slot's box. The player climbs the ice walls out (climbing costs; the walls are
    `Climbable`), or a friend hauls them out with the rope.
  - **Roped**: the partner catches you at the lip. You get a stumble and the zip sound, and no fall.
  - Glacier tours are why the rope exists. The cover comes from `ChunkManager.TryGetCover`, which is
    only kept near a player: enough for the owner.
  - The server never needs the crevasse: it only sees a body 10 m under the ground for a moment.
    `StaminaWatch` and the speed checks skip a body flagged by a new `PoseKind` value `Crevasse` (the replicated int,
    on change) for at most 60 s.

## 5. The shared pot: fondue and raclette

### 5.1 Where

A pot needs a **pot spot**, checked on the server from data it has:

| Spot | Server check | Pot |
|---|---|---|
| A dining venue (`Venues.IsDining`, Restaurant or Pub/Beiz rooms) | body within 18 m of `Venues.At(i)` E/N and inside its building's footprint (the tile's solids, as `Venues.Contained`) | provided (a caquelon on the table) |
| A mountain hut (`PoiKind.Hut`) or a chalet restaurant (#1232 maps `alpine_hut`/`chalet` to one) | body within 25 m of the POI | provided |
| A burning campfire (`PlacedKind.Campfire`, `CampfireClock.Burning(payload, now)`) | body within `CraftStations.FireReach` + 1 m | **bring one**: a `FonduePot` item (#478, `ItemUse.Share`), used up when the pot ends |
| A festival long table (`adventure-festivals`) | that plan's own check | provided, up to 16 seats |

- **Starting**: E at a dining table (`TableSet`, `BanquetteSet`, `HighTable`), at a hut or chalet
  sign, or Use of a `FonduePot` at a fire. Using the pot item **at a fire** opens the session. Used
  anywhere else, it keeps today's BR behaviour (40 health to everyone within 4 m).
- **Who sits**: the starter's party members within 6 m join with one press ("Sit down at the pot",
  E). The starter can also `/fondue invite <player>` (invite kind `fondue`) a non-member for that
  table. 2 to 8 seats (16 at a festival). One player alone may cook a fondue at a fire as today
  (`Recipes` Fire row), but the session needs 2.
- **Venue hook (#1232)**: the venues epic's Fondue Stübli / Carnotzet room (`venue-dining`
  archetypes), when it lands, gets a `Caquelon` piece on its tables. Until then any Gaststube or pub
  table works. Raclette needs a `RacletteHeater` piece, or a campfire (a half cheese by the fire, the
  real way).

### 5.2 What goes in

`FondueRules` (pure) maps the table's ingredients to the result:

| Ingredient | Item | Needed | Effect on the result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cheese | `Cheese` ("Gruyère", ItemId 10) | 1 per seat, at least 2 | each one is 6 dips (the pot's length) |
| Bread | `Bread` (7) | 1 per player, **your own** | you cannot dip without it; it is also what you can lose |
| White wine | `WhiteWine` (**new**, 480: "Fendant", groceries CHF 9) | optional, 1 | `Warm` for everyone (climbing × 0.75) |
| Kirsch | `Kirsch` (**new**, 481: groceries CHF 14) | optional, 1 | `Hearty`: regen × 1.25 |
| Potatoes (raclette) | `Potato` | 2 per seat | the raclette variant: cheese + potatoes, scraping instead of stirring |

- Adding: `AskAdd(potId, ItemId)`. The owner takes the item first (`TakePlain`, the build's pattern)
  and the server answers with `Added`, or a refusal that gives it back.
- **Result**: everyone seated when the pot is empty gets the existing fondue effect (`Fed`: +25 max
  and full at once, `ItemDef.Heal` 90). The `Fed` time is 300 s × quality: 600 s at the best, 150 s if
  it split. Wine and kirsch add their buffs for the same time.
- **Buff delivery**: `TellFeast(byte buffMask, float seconds, float heal)` per seated peer. The owner
  calls `FootPlayer.BuffStamina` (owner-only, as food is). `StaminaBuff` gains `Hearty = 3`.
- A pot is a party-wide buff for **the people at the table**, not absent members. Sitting down is the
  point.

### 5.3 Stirring the figure-eight (la huit)

`StirRules` (pure):
- **Input**: 2D samples at 30 Hz from the mouse (held LMB, the captured mouse's motion), the right
  stick, or in VR the right hand's horizontal path over the pot (a fork in hand: a real stir).
- **One eight**: the tangent angle turns +360° then −360° (two lobes), within 1.4-3.6 s (the target
  is 2.4 s).
- **Quality** q in 0..1, from three parts:
  - the turn balance between the lobes (|Δ+| vs |Δ−|);
  - smoothness (the variance of the angular speed);
  - the tempo (the distance to 2.4 s).
- **Split meter** S in 0..1, kept by the server:
  - it rises 0.02 /s with nobody stirring, +0.08 for an eight with q < 0.3, +0.04 if the heat is high
    (wine missing);
  - it falls 0.05 per eight with q ≥ 0.7;
  - at 1 the cheese **splits** (tranchée): a hiss, the pot greys, quality is capped at 0.25, and S
    stops.
- The client sends `StirEight(potId, byte q)` once per eight it finished (at most one per 1.2 s, the
  server drops faster ones). The server integrates S and sends `PotState(potId, byte s, byte dipsLeft,
  int[] seats, byte[] ingredients)` to the seated at 2 Hz, on change only.
- **Turns**: anyone may stir at any time. Two stirring at once both count, but the third eight in 3 s
  counts nothing (a crowd of spoons does not help). Quality = the mean of q over the pot's eights,
  minus S at the end.
- **Raclette**: no stirring. The cheese face bubbles at a rhythm (3-5 s, server time, a shader on the
  half cheese). Scrape (Use) in the window of 0.6 s for a full portion. Too early it is raw, too late
  burnt. The same q and the same result table.

### 5.4 Dipping and losing your bread

- Dip: Use (LMB, pad RB, VR: dip the fork into the pot), at most once per 2 s. Each dip takes one of
  the pot's dips and heals 6.
- **Lost bread**: a dip loses your bread with chance `0.03 + 0.12 × (1 − q_recent) + 0.05 if you dipped
  within 3 s`. The server rolls it (`ServeDip`) and tells everyone at the table ("Lea lost her bread!",
  a plop sound, the bread cube sinks on the pot's surface: one small node with a sink tween). You need
  another `Bread` to dip again.
- **The forfeit** (the table's tradition). A popup offers the one who lost it three choices, 20 s,
  and the first is the default:
  1. **A line**: a system chat line to the table, picked from a static list ("…buys the next round",
     "…sings the next song", "…does the dishes").
  2. **Buy a round**: CHF 5 × the others at the table, `Bank.Charge` (no overdraft; greyed if short).
     Each other seated player gets one `BeerBottle` or `WhiteWine` (`Grant`, source "round").
  3. **A yodel**: the yodel emote (`EmoteWheel` id) plays for 4 s; others see it.
- **Server setting**: `--forfeits off|mild|all` (default `all`). `mild` drops the money one.

### 5.5 The pot on screen

- `FondueTable` (a `Node3D` per session on every seated peer and anyone within 30 m):
  - `CaquelonMeshBuilder` (`[Showcase("Adventure", "Caquelon")]`): a red caquelon on a réchaud with a
    blue flame (particles);
  - the cheese surface: a shader with `split` and `bubble` uniforms, set on change;
  - forks as rigid nodes held by the seated figures through a `Hold` arm pose (`ItemArmPose`).
- **Seats**: at a venue, the figures sit (`SeatedFigure.Pose`, `player/sitting`) on the table's
  benches. At a fire, they crouch round it.
- `Ui/FonduePanel` (bottom centre, `GlassPanel`, `ui/style-guide`):
  - the seats with their ingredient chips;
  - the split meter as a ring round the pot glyph (amber, `Bad` past 0.7);
  - the figure-eight guide: a faint lemniscate with your last trace (a fixed 64-point ring buffer,
    drawn with `_Draw` on change);
  - "Dips left 14";
  - one big action: the next useful thing (Add cheese / Stir / Dip).
- VR: the same panel in world space over the table, plus the real fork.
- `Esc` (pad B) leaves the table. Whoever leaves before the end gets no feast.
- **Sounds** (`[SoundShowcase("Adventure")]`, built per call): bubbling cheese (rate from S and
  heat), the split hiss, the spoon scrape, the plop of lost bread, "Prost" glass clinks at the end.

## 6. Mentor rewards

- **Milestones** (`MentorRules`, a pure table): `summit` (a first `PoiDiscovered` of kind Peak or
  SummitCross), `glacier` (a first roped crossing: 200 m on glacier cover in Rope mode; `TogetherService`
  raises `GlacierCrossed`), `ride` (a first 2 km on a mount or a vehicle of one's own,
  `RideKindId != OnFoot`, read from the replicated state at 1 Hz; `RideStarted` is raised at 2 km),
  `build` (a first structure of 10 pieces; `Structures.AddPiece` raises `StructureBuilt(peer, key,
  structureId, pieces)` at 10, a new record in `Build/`).
- **Newcomer** for a milestone: has never done it (`firsts` in the section). Age does not matter: a
  returning player who never stood on a summit is a newcomer there.
- **Veteran** for it: has done it at least 3 times (`done` counts in the section; for `summit`, 3
  peaks).
- **It counts** when the newcomer does it while linked to the veteran, or in their party within 60 m
  (`Near`). On the server: the event arrives, `TogetherService` checks both, writes both sections,
  then `Grant`s:
  - the **newcomer** gets the milestone's first-time cosmetic: `summit` Erstbesteigung cap
    (`Beanie`), `glacier` Gletscher scarf (`Bandana`), `ride` Mitfahrer gloves (`Gloves`), `build`
    Zimmermann cap (`FeltHat`). ItemId 482-485.
  - the **veteran** gets the guide's version: Bergführer cap, Seilschaft scarf, Fahrlehrer gloves,
    Polier hat (486-489), the first time they mentor that milestone. At 5 and 20 mentorships (all
    milestones together) they get the titles "Bergführer" and "Bergführer mit eidg. Fachausweis"
    (`Title` rewards).
- **New `GarmentStyle.Together`** for these eight: never in a wardrobe pool, a chest or a workbench,
  only earned. Model viewer: the Garments catalogue shows them by itself (a new enum value).
- **Anti-farm**: one pair counts once per milestone (`mentees: "summit:<short key>"`). A newcomer is
  mentored once per milestone ever. Session keys (unproven, bots) never count on either side. These
  are cosmetics only, so a player with two accounts gains nothing that matters.
- Raises `Mentored(peer, key, milestone, role)` for quests and the errands plan.

## 7. Played together this week, and the punch card

- **Seconds together**: the server's 1 Hz tick adds 1 s to each `Active` peer whose `Near` is at least
  1 (a 200 m radius here, `PlayRadius`: playing together, not standing together). Writes are batched
  (`Touch` once per minute).
- **This week** (ISO week, Europe/Zurich, Monday 00:00 by the server's clock):
  - 30 min together in a week grant once a **Fine chest roll** (`ChestLoot.Roll(ChestTier.Fine, rng, pity)` through the
    rewards plan's `GrantRoll`) and the toast "Zäme unterwegs: your weekly chest".
  - Nothing is lost by missing a week, and nothing stacks: one chest a week.
- **The punch card** (Stempelkarte, from the study: progress that never resets):
  - 20 min together on a calendar day gives 1 stamp, at most 1 a day. 10 stamps fill a card.
  - Stamps never expire, and a gap of weeks keeps them.
  - A full card is a **Precious chest roll**.
  - Cards 1, 3, 5 and 10 are titles ("Gspänli", "Seilschaft", "Stammtisch", "Unzertrennlich").

## Network

- **Authority**: the server owns the link book, the pots (`PotBook`: seats, ingredients, S, dips),
  the gates, the together seconds, the mentor checks and all grants. The owners own their bodies,
  stamina, the tether, belays and crevasses (from replicated positions), and local loot with the
  yield bonus.
- **RPCs** on `World/Together`:
  - Client → server: `AskUnlink()`, `AskHaul(bool)`, `AskHandle(string gate, byte handle, bool held)`,
    `AskSit(int spotKind, string spotId)`, `AskAdd(int pot, ushort item)`, `StirEight(int pot, byte q)`,
    `AskDip(int pot)`, `AskForfeit(int pot, byte choice)`, `AskLeave(int pot)`.
  - Server → all: `Links(int[], int[], byte[])`, `GateOpen(string gate, double until)`.
  - Server → seated: `PotState(...)` (2 Hz on change), `BreadLost(int pot, int peer)`,
    `TellFeast(byte, float, float)`.
  - Server → one: `TellHaul(bool)`, `TellRefused(string)` (reused).
- Linking itself rides the invite system (kinds `rope` and `fondue`), so no RPC is needed to ask.
- Every handler calls a `Serve*` method that does the checks: body positions from `PositionOf`, the
  spot, `Active`, party membership.
- **Protocol**: one bump of `Net/Handshake.Protocol` per PR of this plan (56 today; each phase 3 PR
  takes the next free number when it merges).
- Replication: no new per-frame state. The crevasse state is a new value of the existing replicated `PoseKind`
  (on change).

## Performance

- `Near`: 1 Hz on the client (8 members at most, no allocation: a reused `Mate[8]`). On the server,
  1 Hz per active peer, under `ServerStats.Ran("together")`.
- Tether: a few vector operations per physics step for a linked body. The trail is a fixed ring of 32.
- Hand pose: inside the existing arm solve, so bones only (`general/no-mesh-rebuild-per-frame`,
  `MeshRebuildDisciplineTests` covers `ArmHold`).
- Rope: one transform per link per frame. The sag uniform is set on change. The mesh is shared.
- Pot: `PotState` at most 2 Hz to 8 peers. The panel redraws on a state change or a new stir sample
  (while stirring only). The 64-point trace buffer is reused. The panel builds no strings per frame:
  "Dips left n" is cached per value (`general/perf-no-per-frame-allocations`).
- Crevasses: one hash per 12 m cell entered on glacier cover. A slot node exists only while you are in
  it.
- Target: < 0.1 ms client frame for all of it, measured on the fixture with `--perf`.

## Open questions

1. **Hold E vs tap E at a party member.** Holding E asks to hold hands, and a tap still challenges to a
   fight. Alternatively, E at a party member could mean "hold hands" outright, with fights through
   `/fight`. *Recommendation*: hold E. It changes no existing habit, and the prompt shows both.
2. **Who counts as together: party only, or anyone near?** *Recommendation*: party only. It needs
   consent, and it makes `/party invite` the thing to do. Revisit if playtests show strangers
   cooperating without forming parties.
3. **Bonus size: +25 % a friend, 3 at most (×1.75).** *Recommendation*: ship it, then check prices in
   the econ check (`produce-economy`). The farm stand and the buyers take the extra produce. If prices
   sag, lower to +20 %.
4. **Hidden crevasses drop the terrain collision for one body.** This is the riskiest part.
   *Recommendation*: build it last, behind `Settings › Gameplay › Crevasses` (on), and fall back to a
   "stumble and winded" without a slot if the collision trick misbehaves in a playtest.
5. **Money forfeit by default.** *Recommendation*: `all` by default, capped at CHF 5 a head. Hosts who
   dislike it set `--forfeits mild`.
6. **Wine and kirsch as items.** Beer already exists (`BeerBottle`). *Recommendation*: add both
   (480, 481), sold at groceries and in Fine chests' snack pool, their alcohol counted by `adventure-alcohol` (#1271: blood level, effects, breathalyzers). Cheese stays a
   bought item until a dairy follow-up or the festivals' Chästeilet.
7. **Weekly chest timezone.** *Recommendation*: Europe/Zurich, Monday 00:00 by the server's clock, the
   same for every player on a server.
8. **Mentor veteran threshold** (3 times). *Recommendation*: 3. A player who has done something three
   times can show it, and it keeps the pool of mentors large early on.
9. **Hand-holding outside the Adventure layer** (in plain free roam with `adventure` off).
   *Recommendation*: not now. It rides `Active` like everything here. Move `LinkBook` to `Net/Invites`
   later if players ask for it.

## Built differently

- **Crevasses**: the plan's fallback only. A step onto a hidden strip breaks the bridge: unroped, the body is pushed back and left winded (stamina 0) with a blue slot marking the spot for 30 s; roped to a standing partner, a stumble. No slot with its own collision, no terrain-layer drop, no `PoseKind.Crevasse`: follow-up. Behind `Settings > Gameplay > Crevasses`.
- **VR**: the grip gestures (`XrHandHold`, gripping the rope, the wheel, a real fork) are not built; VR reaches every action through the pad route (`XrPad`). Rows in `xr/vr-action-map` say so.
- **Server and campfires**: `PlacedObjects.Instance` is client-only, so the server's `TogetherService` takes its placed objects from `PlacedSource` (set by `ServerWorld`).
- **Test seams**: `--together-glacier E,N,R` (a pretend glacier), `--together-test` (server: `/rope test veteran|<milestone>`), `--together-day-seconds`, `--fondueseed`, `--forfeits`.
- **Pending save**: the batched once-a-minute `Touch` of the seconds together is flushed in `AdventureService.Saving`, so a save never finds an untouched section.
- **Net check**: `tools/togethernetcheck.sh` runs on `--systems network,adventure,sky`. The Battle Royale part checks the link, not the client's `NearHere` (a lobby is not `Permissions.InMatch` on the client, the server's `Active` is what ends it).
- **Protocol**: 60 (58 is the cinema halls #1279, 59 the festival framework #1295).
- **Pings fixed**: the synthesised ping's envelope broke at t = 0, which silenced the clink and the Prost sound; fixed after `--sounds,check` found it.
- Not verified: a real glacier, hut or castle gate, a headset and a pad (see `tests/TO-TEST-BY-HUMAN.md`).
