# Ski slopes from OSM, a daily snow state and a virtual weather (#887)

Real ski slopes, painted as snow, whose snow changes through the in-game day (wear, sun, temperature)
and is reset every morning at 06:00 as if the groomers had been out, from a virtual weather. Decided
with the owner (Oct 19 2026):

- **Slopes first**, before the ski physics rework, rideable lifts and the snowboard (their own issues).
- **Always winter on slopes**: slopes are snow all year, whatever the real month. The weather only
  changes the kind of snow. No grooming gameplay: the 06:00 reset stands for it.
- **Wear = a virtual crowd + real skiers**: each slope wears down by a deterministic crowd (no network
  traffic), plus extra wear where players and bots really ski.

## What is there

- swissTLM3D has no slopes (`tools/swisstlm3d-maps-no-ski-pistes`); ropeways come from TLM, drawn, not rideable.
- OSM, counted on `switzerland-latest.osm.pbf` (Oct 2026): 9,418 `piste:type=downhill` ways (6,187 km),
  4,854 nordic (2,715 km), 597 sled, 496 skitour, 114 connection, 99 snow_park. Downhill difficulty:
  easy 4,358, intermediate 3,495, advanced 865, freeride 321, novice 232, expert 19, none 127.
  Grooming: classic 3,358, backcountry 438, mogul 30, untagged 5,592. 727 are areas (closed, `area=yes`),
  the rest lines; width is almost never tagged (36). 5,300 named, 3,533 with a ref.
- `PbfReader` (7 s for CH), `OsmFields` (ring assembly), the ODbL decision (`tools/osm-odbl-licence`):
  OSM layers are opt-in and their tiles carry an `Osm` flag.
- Terrain: 1 m grid, mesh = vertex + colour (alpha holds the surface pattern); UV2 and CUSTOM0-3 free.
- Clock: `WorldClock.EnvNow` (server-owned, a day = 24 real minutes); day number
  `floor((EnvNow + HourShift) / 86400)`, already used by the construction sites.
- `Surfaces.At` has `Snow` and `Ice`; skis ignore the surface (constant `SnowFriction`).
- Small replicated world state: the sea-state / occasions pattern (RPC on change, `SendTo` on join).

## Design

### Data: the `.piste` layer (build time)

- `PisteStage` in the preprocessor, `--pistes --osm-pbf <pbf>`, one `PbfReader` pass:
  ways and relations with `piste:type` in downhill, connection, snow_park, nordic, sled
  (skitour and hike are routes, not prepared snow: not painted).
- Lines buffered to a default width (tag `piste:width`/`width` wins): novice/easy 40 m,
  intermediate 30, advanced 25, expert/freeride 20, snow_park 40, connection 8, nordic 6, sled 4.
  Areas taken as drawn. Overlaps: area over line, then the nearest centreline.
- Rasterised per 1 km tile on the terrain grid into `pistes_E_N.pst` (magic `USPS`, v1, `TileHeader`,
  flags `Osm | Deflate`), written only for tiles a slope touches:
  - a record table: stable id (OSM id, top bit for relations), type, difficulty, grooming, name, ref,
    the centreline (tile-local) for markers and the groom direction;
  - per grid vertex: slope index (u16, 0 = none), edge mask (u8, ramp over the outer 3 m),
    groom direction (u8 angle, along the nearest centreline).
- MapSetup layer `pistes` (needs `osm`, never in `all`), `--pistes-check` self-check, `pistes_report.txt`.
- Game: `IChunkSource.LoadPistesAsync` in every source (local, caching, fallback = null, network with
  a new `AssetKind`, HTTP mirror), `Handshake.Protocol` bump. Absent file = no slopes, nothing breaks.
- Licences: the OSM entry covers it; README data table line.

### Drawn

- `TerrainMeshBuilder` fills CUSTOM0 (slope slot, edge mask, groom dir x/z) where the tile has a `.pst`;
  `flat` varying for the slot.
- `PisteLayer` (client) gives each loaded slope a global slot (keyed by OSM id, shared across tiles,
  refcounted) in a global `piste_state` texture (two texels per slot), updated only when a slope's
  state changes, never per frame (`general/perf-no-per-frame-allocations`).
- `terrain.gdshaderinc`: a shared `snow_shade()` (high albedo held under the tonemap, blue-tinted shade,
  wrap light, a sparse sparkle; PS1 banded and dithered, Cartoon in flat bands, Realistic with the snow
  texture), used for slopes and also for glacier/snowfield cover. On a slope, by its state:
  corduroy stripes along the groom direction (fade with wear), ice (grey-blue, sheen), slush
  (darker, grey-brown), loose snow (brighter, lumpy), moguls (periodic bump shading, no geometry).
- Edge markers: poles along both edges every 40 m, blue / red / black / yellow (freeride itinerary),
  a MultiMesh per chunk, the pole builder `[Showcase("Ski")]`.
- Map screen: slopes in their colour.

### Virtual weather

- `SnowWeather.ForDay(day, worldSeed)`: deterministic, so every peer computes it alone. Overnight new
  snow (most days 0, some 5-40 cm), snowfall during the day by hour, temperature at 2000 m by hour
  (a min/max curve), cloud cover.
- Server override with `/weather` (show, `snow <cm>`, `temp <°C>`, `clear`, `reroll`), replicated as a
  small JSON on change and on join (the occasions pattern).

### Snow state per slope

`SnowModel`, pure and unit-tested. Inputs: the day's weather, hour, altitude (temperature lapse
−6.5 °C/km), sun exposure (slope aspect and cloud), the slope's grooming tag, steepness and wear W.
Outputs, each 0-1:

| Output | Grows with | Shrinks with |
|---|---|---|
| freshness (corduroy) | 06:00 reset on groomed slopes | wear |
| loose (powder) | new snow, ungroomed slopes | wear (it turns to chopped, then packed) |
| hardness (ice) | yesterday's melt refrozen, cold mornings, wear on cold days | sun, warmth |
| wetness (slush) | melt degree-hours since 06:00 (low, sunny, afternoon) | cold, altitude |
| bumps (moguls) | `grooming=mogul`, wear on steep ungroomed slopes | 06:00 reset (groomed) |

- **06:00 reset**: wear back to 0, groomed slopes fresh corduroy; snow fallen before 04:00 is packed
  into the corduroy, later snow lies loose on top.
- **Wear**: the crowd's share is a function of the hour (from 08:30, peak around noon) and the slope's
  popularity (novice/easy most, freeride least), computed on every peer; the real share is metres
  skied on the slope by players and bots, counted by the server.
- A slope is one state, evaluated per point only for altitude and sun, so a long run can be icy at the
  top and slushy at the bottom.

### Server, network, bots

- `SnowService` in `ServerWorld`: the weather override and the real wear per slope. Every 0.5 s it maps
  each skiing player and bot to a slope through a server-side `PisteIndex` (loads `.pst` tiles around
  skiers, no meshes; a headless server does not load `.cover`). Every 5 s it sends the changed real
  wear (OSM id + byte); the full table on join; both reset at the 06:00 day change.
- Bots: slopes are terrain; a server-run body reads its slope condition through `PisteIndex`.
  Skiing bots stay out of scope: a row in `bots/bot-capability-map`.
- No new key: `/weather` is an admin chat command. The HUD shows the slope (name or ref, colour,
  condition) while on skis, updated on change.

### Felt

- `Surfaces.At`: on a slope, `Snow` or `Ice` by its state; `Surfaces.SnowAt` gives the state itself.
- `Skis.Step`: friction from the state (corduroy and ice fast, loose and slush slow), a minimal hook;
  the force model of the research brief is the ski physics issue.

## Steps

1. **Data**: `PisteFormat`, `PisteStage`, MapSetup `pistes`, `--pistes-check`, chunk sources and
   `AssetKind`; build western Valais (Verbier, Nendaz, Veysonnaz, Crans-Montana) and report counts.
2. **Drawn, static**: the mesh channel, `snow_shade()`, slopes as fresh corduroy, markers, map.
   Screenshots in the three styles.
3. **Weather and snow state**: `SnowWeather`, `SnowModel` with unit tests, `PisteLayer` texture,
   `SnowService` and replication, `/weather`, HUD, `Surfaces` and the skis friction hook.
4. **Checks**: `--pistecheck` (one slope at 07:00, 12:00, 16:00, with snow and without, three styles),
   `tools/test.sh quick`, `tools/test.sh net` (weather and wear on the remote peer). Notes and indexes.

## After this

Rideable lifts (TLM lines + OSM aerialway details: type, duration, capacity, stations), the ski force
model (heading vs velocity, carve/skid, snow-dependent friction off slopes), the snowboard, off-slope
snow coverage and ski tracks.

## Status (on the branch, Oct 19 2026)

- **Built**:
  - step 1, data: `.pst`, MapSetup `pistes`, streamed;
  - step 3, weather and state: `/weather`, `--weather`, wear replicated, HUD, `Surfaces`, skis friction;
  - from step 2: slopes drawn by state in every style, trees cleared off runs.
- **Built differently**:
  - The terrain mesh is untouched. Slopes come from a per-tile texture, as `PhotoLayer` does,
    instead of a CUSTOM0 channel, so every LOD has per-pixel edges and a state change never
    rebuilds a mesh.
  - The groom direction became a stored groom phase (`notes/world/ski-snow`).
  - The records carry their altitude range, sun exposure and steepness.
- **Not built yet**:
  - slopes on the map screen (step 2). Edge markers and snow over roads under a slope came later in the PR;
  - a `--pistecheck` probe: the model is covered by unit tests, the look and the ride by
    screenshots and `--ride` runs.
