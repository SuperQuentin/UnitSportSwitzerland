## Decisions (owner, 2026-10-28)

  stands (`MoveEntries`), makes facade rooms follow the outline, and edits
  `InteriorGenerator.Dining.cs`. #1292 builds on that and does not start before it merges.
- **Scope: dining venues and shop units.** Every dining venue that does not take its whole
  building (#1242's `DiningUnits`), and every shop unit on a mixed block's ground floor (shops under

- **A building with one door still gives its venue a door of its own** (Q3, replacing "that door is the

- **Washrooms shrink where space is short** (Q4): a room per gender (a WC and a basin each), else a

## Design

1. **A venue door slot** (`BuildingFootprint`, #498's extras). The venue's point is tile data
   (`VenueRecord`), so its door is placed at footprint time: on the facet nearest the venue's point
   (the terrace's side), `DoorBudget`'s `MinGap` from the other doors. Where the building has a
   single door and the venue is its ranked winner, that door is the venue's (no lobby in front of a
   café). `DoorSpot` carries which venue (or shop unit) it serves and its front's span (`Run`, bay
   count), as `WholeHall` / `Top` / `Run` do for halls.
2. **Shop unit doors** cannot come from the plan (plans are not computed at footprint time), so
   the footprint lays them: on a mixed block, along its street front, one per unit width (a rule
   tried on real Geneva blocks first; `map-downloader-on-demand`). The plan then fits a shop unit
   behind each door, the plan following the doors.
3. **The room behind the door** (`InteriorGenerator.Dining`'s `DiningUnits`, `Apartments`'
   `Shop` / `AptEntrances`): the guest room is the ground-floor room on the facade nearest the
   venue's point, with the venue door's `Entry` cut into it (`FitEntry` / `MoveEntries` into that
   room only). The building's other doors stay the building's (lobby, stairwells, school).
4. **The front mesh** (a sibling of `HallFront`, built per tile in the door's frame): mullions and
   glass bays across the span, proud of the wall so the shader's window grid under it is hidden;
   the panes in a glass material using the same `room_behind` include. Per-span blanking of the
   grid (a span flag in UV2, as `BlindGroundFlag` is per building) if hiding under a proud mesh
   shows z-fighting or peeking (`zfight` note).
5. **Interior windows at the bays**: the guest room's facade wall takes its windows from the
   door's front (bay centres, sill, top) mapped into the plan frame, replacing `AddWindows`' own
   spacing on that wall.
6. **Which door the dressing uses**: `VenueSigns`, `VenueTerraces` (terrace and awning in front of
   the venue door), the venue's music `AtDoor`, all from the venue's own door, not slot 0.
