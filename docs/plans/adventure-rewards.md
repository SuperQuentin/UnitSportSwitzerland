## Goal

Exploring real Switzerland pays, often and in small amounts, the way Genshin Impact's chests and Oculi
do: every ridge, castle and pass has something on it, a few things are worth a detour, and the rare
ones are behind a little challenge. Everything is the same for every player (the world is shared),
and everything is taken once **per player**: friends exploring together each open the same chest,
nobody snipes anybody's.

Out of scope: PvE guardians (no combat against creatures in phase 2), stat gear (there are no stats:
rewards are francs, materials, cosmetics, gadgets and max stamina), land animals beyond cows and the
new marmot (ibex and chamois are fauna-page placeholders until a wildlife system exists), a trading
market for rewards, rewards in a Battle Royale round (`Systems.Adventure`; every server method is gated by
`AdventureService.Instance?.Active(peer)`, false during `BrManager.Playing`).

## 1. Placement (`src/Adventure/Rewards/RewardPlacer.cs`, pure)

**Deterministic, never stored.** Like `LootTables.Roll` (contents are computed, only *taken* is
remembered) and `OccasionHash` (hashed from world anchors, never a shared random stream), a tile's
rewards are a pure function `RewardPlacer.Place(TileId, ChunkGrid coarse, IReadOnlyList<Poi> pois,
RoadMask roads) -> RewardSpot[]`. Client and server run the same code on the same inputs.

- **Inputs are what the server has for any tile**: the coarse `ChunkGrid` (`source.LoadCoarseChunkAsync`,
  the same path `RaceCourse` uses for air courses), the POI catalogue rows of that tile (places plan),
  and the tile's `.road` segments (chests stay 6 m off a carriageway). Never the fine cover raster
  (`ChunkManager.TryGetCover` only holds it near a player): the server must be able to recompute any
  spot to validate a claim. The client only snaps the drawn height to its fine grid.
- **Ids are anchored to the world, not to an index**: `ch:<E>_<N>` (chest), `ew:<E>_<N>` (edelweiss),
  `cn:<E>_<N>` (cairn), E/N being the spot's LV95 metres rounded down to a 10 m cell. A POI-anchored
  spot is `ch:<poiId>:<slot>`, so it survives a re-run of the preprocessor that moves the POI a few
  metres. A generator change that moves a spot to another cell makes a new id: `RewardPlacer.Version`
  is bumped and the change note says how many ids moved (`--rewardstats` prints it).
- **Hash**: `AdventureHash.Of(tileE, tileN, salt)` (FNV-1a 64, like `OccasionHash`), salts per kind.
  One `Random` per spot, seeded from its own cell, so adding a kind never reshuffles another.
- **World kinds**: the real map uses POIs + terrain. `--generated-world` has no POIs: terrain spots
  only (peaks found as local maxima of the coarse grid). A fixture course places nothing unless
  `--rewardspots <file>` lists spots (checks only).

**Where things go** (densities per km² of land, measured and tuned with `--rewardstats`):

| Spot | Where | Density | Nationwide (41 285 km²) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common chest | a 250 m cell lottery on walkable ground (slope < 30°), off roads and roofs, preferring forest edges, ruins, the foot of a boulder | 1.2 | ~50 000 |
| Fine chest | same, slope < 40°, at least 80 m from a road | 0.35 | ~14 000 |
| Precious chest | at POIs: every castle and pass, one in four peaks over 2000 m, viewpoints; plus 0.04/km² of remote terrain (> 400 m from a road) | ~0.1 | ~4 000 |
| Luxurious chest | major POIs only: the catalogue's rank-1 peaks, the large castles, pass summits over 2000 m; always sealed | ~0.007 | ~300 |
| Edelweiss | above 1500 m, on slope > 35° ledges (a flat 3 m step on a steep face) or within 30 m of a summit, never reachable from a road in a straight walk | 0.8 above 1500 m | ~9 000 |
| Cairn (Steinmann) | trail crests and saddles below 2500 m, one set of 20 per canton | — | 520 |

- **Seals**: 0 % of Common, 20 % of Fine, 60 % of Precious, 100 % of Luxurious chests.
- **Budget per tile**: at most 8 chests, 10 edelweiss and 2 cairns per 1 km tile, after the lottery;
  a tile over budget keeps the highest tiers first. A tile is a few dozen hashes: placing is
  microseconds, done when `ChunkManager.TileFurnished` fires, dropped on `TileUnloaded`, exactly as
  `OccasionDecor` does (`docs/notes/occasions/occasions.md`). The server places a tile only when a
  claim names it, and caches the last 64 tiles.

## 2. Tiered chests

### Loot (`src/Adventure/Rewards/ChestLoot.cs`, pure)

Its own tables, in the shape of `LootTables`: `ChestLoot.Roll(tier, Random, Pity) -> List<ItemStack>`
and its closed form `ChestLoot.Chance(tier, item, pity)`, shared candidates so they cannot drift
(the `loot` note's rule: change `Roll`, change `Chance`). The odds are **published**: the chest's
reveal card has an "Odds" link that shows `Chance` per item for that tier.

| Tier | Francs | Materials (`ItemUse.Material`, 7-36 and farming 310+) | Bergkristall | Cosmetic | Gadget |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 20-60 CHF | 1-3 of 1-2 kinds, plus a consumable 60 % | 10 % one | — | — |
| Fine | 60-150 CHF | 2-4 of 2 kinds | 1 | 4 % | 6 % |
| Precious | 150-400 CHF | 2-4 | 2-4 | 15 %, sure by the 8th without one | 25 % |
| Luxurious | 400-1000 CHF | 3-5 | 5-8 | 100 % | 50 % |

- **No RNG stats**: items are the game's items; a cosmetic is a garment or hat (`ItemUse.Wear`,
  `Avatar.Garments`) from a new "Alpine" set (Sennenkäppi, edelweiss shirt, mountain guide's felt hat,
  Bergführer rope belt, a Swiss-cross bandana).
- **Bergkristall** (rock crystal, `ItemId.Bergkristall`): the Adventure crafting material.
  Workbench recipes in `Crafting.Recipes.All` turn it into the gadgets (part 8) and the Alpine set.
- **Duplicates**: a cosmetic the player already owns (`ChestsProgress.Owned`, the `chests` section, since the
  inventory is local and could be dropped) becomes 5 Bergkristall, said on the card ("already yours:
  5 Bergkristall").
- **Bad-luck protection**: `ChestsProgress.Pity[tier]` counts openings without a cosmetic; Precious
  guarantees one at 8, Fine raises its chance by 2 points per miss. `Chance` takes the pity, so the
  published odds are the player's real odds right now.
- **Per player, seeded per player**: the server rolls with `Random(AdventureHash(chestId, PlayerKey fingerprint,
  openedCount))`. Friends opening one chest get different rolls; reopening is impossible anyway.
- Everything goes through `AdventureService.Grant(peer, Reward, "chest:<id>")` (objectives 4.5):
  francs as `Cash` (to the bank account, safe from a knock-out), stacks as `Item` (its `Granted` RPC).

### Opening and the reveal

- `ChestNode` (client, one per drawn chest, parented to the tile node): a body mesh and a **lid as its
  own node** turned by a tween (`no-mesh-rebuild-per-frame`). A sealed chest wears a band of light
  (a shader parameter, set on change only, `perf-no-per-frame-allocations`).
- Open: the client sends `RequestOpen(chestId)`; on `Opened` the lid swings (0.6 s), a light shaft
  rises in the tier's colour (`styles/style-kit` palette, Cartoon first), the tier's jingle plays,
  and `ChestRevealUi` fans the item cards (2.2 s, any key skips). Then the chest fades and is no
  longer drawn **for this player**.
- An opened chest is never drawn again for that player (the client skips every id in its
  `chests` section's `Opened`). Another player standing beside it still sees it closed.

## 3. Seals (`src/Adventure/Rewards/Seals.cs`)

A sealed chest's seal kind comes from the spot hash and the terrain. Seal progress is **per player**
and held by the server in memory (`SealBook`, pure, tested), forgotten 10 minutes after the last
step or when the player leaves. Every step is a server method; the client only shows it.

| Seal | Where | What | Server checks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Höhenfeuer | ridges and peaks | Light 3 wood stacks within 40 m in 90 s (E at a stack, or a torch / flare aimed at it). 1 August bonfires, the Swiss way | body within 3 m of each stack, the 90 s window |
| Ring run | open slopes, cliff tops | 4-6 rings, on foot, jumping or gliding (wingsuit, paraglider), in a time | each ring in order, `RaceManager.Plausible`'s pace test (`MaxPace` by mount) |
| Pressure plates | ruins, castles | Two stone plates held at once: two players, or one player and a dropped item (`DroppedItems` server positions) on the other | both plates occupied in the same server tick |
| Cowbell tune | alpine pastures | 4 cowbells hung on a rail; ring them in the order a fifth plays (Simon in the world; `SimonUi`'s idea, no panel) | the order, nothing else |
| Guide | anywhere hidden | Follow the guide animal (part 5); its arrival unseals | the guide's last leg reached |

- **Co-op**: a seal step done by anyone in the same party (`net/invites` parties) within 60 m counts
  for every party member within 60 m. Two strangers each light their own fires.
- Seal props (stacks, plates, bells, rings) are drawn from the same placement; their *lit/pressed*
  look is per player (from `SealState` the server sends that player), not shared.

## 4. Edelweiss and cairns

- **Not inventory items**: picking one is a progress value (the `collect` section's `Taken`), never a
  stack that can be dropped, lost on a knock-out or traded. Drawn as one `MultiMesh` per tile (the
  occasions' pattern); a picked one has its instance set to zero scale once (not per frame).
- Pick: the gather hold (G / pad X, 0.8 s, `Loot.Gathering` offers it before stone and water when one
  is within 1.6 m). The client asks `RequestPick(id)`; the server checks the spot exists in its
  placement of that tile and the body is within 3 m (`ServerPos`), then answers `Picked`.
- **Offering**: at a summit cross or a chapel POI (places plan kind tags `cross`, `chapel`), E opens
  `OfferingUi`: "Offer 5 edelweiss". The server moves 5 from *held* to *offered*:
  `CollectProgress.Held` (picked, not yet offered) and `Offered` (total), then calls
  `AdventureService.Grant(peer, new Unlock("stamina", "+1"), "offering:<shrineId>")` per 5. **The
  stamina plan registers the `"stamina"` reward handler, stores the bonus in its own `stamina` section
  and owns the curve** (+10 max per step, 10 steps at most, so 50 of the ~9 000 edelweiss reach the
  cap; offerings past the cap still count in `Offered` and the Collection tab). Each offering also gives
  a Precious-tier roll every 5th step.
- **Cairns** (Steinmann): the gather hold adds a stone to it (a stone is drawn on top, a node, for that
  player). 20 per canton; the 20th gives the canton's flag cosmetic and counts in the places plan's
  canton %. Optional in phase 2 (part 4b).

## 5. Guide animals (`src/Adventure/Rewards/GuideAnimal.cs`)

The Genshin Seelie, Swiss: a **marmot** pops out of a burrow mound (E / Y / grip the mound) and runs
ahead along a path of 2-4 legs to a hidden chest; on a cliff path a **bearded vulture** takes off from
a rock instead and glides from perch to perch.

- **Path**: the placement gives the waypoints (walkable for the marmot, along the cliff line for the
  vulture), so it is the same for everyone and the server knows it.
- **Server-led**: `GuideBook` (server) advances a guide to its next leg when its follower's body is
  within 8 m of the guide's current waypoint, and sends `GuideLeg(guideId, leg, serverTime)` to the
  follower and to peers whose interest covers it (`net/entity-interest`). Every peer plays the leg from
  `ClockSync.ServerNow` like `UfoSightings` plays `SightingPath`: no per-frame traffic. A bot body
  follows a guide the same way (the server measures bodies, not inputs).
- **Bodies that do not rebuild**: the marmot is rigid parts as nodes (body, head, four legs, tail)
  from a new `MarmotMeshBuilder` (`[Showcase("Animals", "Marmot")]`, like `CowMeshBuilder`), a gait of
  turned legs; the vulture is `BirdMesh` for the `Bearded Vulture` species already in `BirdSpecies`
  (wings are separate meshes that flap by rotation), flown by a path follower, not by `BirdLife`'s
  flocking. The marmot whistles at each stop (a synthesised sound, part 9).
- A guide is per player: a second player summoning the same burrow gets their own marmot.

## 6. Trials (`src/Adventure/Rewards/Trials.cs`)

One-off timed runs at landmarks, on the race machinery (`docs/notes/world/races.md`): a trial is a
single-entrant race of a new `RaceKind.Trial` with a **fixed course** made from the trial's id.

| Trial | Where | Course |
|---|---|---|
| Pass drive | every pass POI with a road | `RaceRoute` from the foot of the pass to its summit (car or moto, the player's own or an AE86) |
| Downhill | every ski area with a run | `RaceRoute.BuildPisteAsync` from the top station |
| Glide rings | peaks and viewpoints with a 300 m drop | the air course (paraglider or wingsuit gates) toward the valley POI |
| Ride | lakeshore and valley roads | a 5-10 km road course, bike |

- **Start**: E / Y / grip at the trial's start stone (a waymarked post). The server times it and
  verifies every checkpoint (`RaceManager.Plausible`), as for races. Leave with `/trial leave` or the
  quick menu's Activities.
- **Par times**: bronze / silver / gold from the course length and the class's pace. First finish =
  a Fine roll, first gold = a Precious roll (`TrialFinished` event either way).
- **Personal best** in the `trials` section (`TrialsProgress.Best`: id → seconds, medal). **Leaderboard**: server `TrialBoard`, top 10 per
  trial, `user://adventure/trials.json`, shown on the start stone and in the Activities screen.
- **Ghosts**: the client samples its run at 4 Hz (LV95 + altitude + yaw, ~3 KB/min) as a `GpxTrack`;
  a new PB is uploaded in chunks like `PhotoTransfer` (cap 64 KB); the server keeps the top 3. On a
  retry the player races their own PB, or the leader's, as a `Gpx.Runner` ghost (no collision,
  translucent).

  a line of sight from the eye to the target clears the coarse terrain (sampled every 50 m, the air

## Bots

Rewards are a player's own progression; bots fill matches and gain nothing from them. Each act still
has a **server method** a bot could call (`RewardService.ServeOpen(body, id)`, `ServePick`,
`ServeSealStep`, `GuideBook.Follow`), checked against the body, never the input. Rows in
`bots/bot-capability-map`: open chest, pick edelweiss, seals, guide, trials, photo quest, detector,
gust — all **n/a** (a player's progression) except the **bottled gust** (ok: a thrown item through
`BotHands.Use` once a brain wants a lift) and the **pressure plate**, where a bot's body counts as a
weight (ok, nothing to build). Sense for a future co-op bot: the same `RewardPlacer` on the server.

## Network

- **One node**: `World/Adventure/Rewards` (`RewardService`) on the server and every client, RPCs routed
  by path like `World/Loot`. Offline the client plays the server's part (as `LootService`), peer 1, key `local`.
- Client to server (`AnyPeer`, reliable): `RequestOpen(string id)`, `RequestPick(string id)`,
  `RequestSealStep(string chestId, int step)`, `RequestOffer(string shrineId)`, `RequestGuide(string
  burrowId)`, `ReportPhoto(string spot, float yaw, float pitch, float fov)`.
- Server to the asking peer only (`RpcId`, never a negative id): `Opened(string id, int[] items,
  int[] counts, byte tier)`, `Picked(string id)`, `SealState(string chestId, int mask)`,
  `Refused(string id, byte reason)`; to the peers that see it: `GuideLeg`.
- **Validation** on every request: the id parses and the server's own placement of that tile has
  it (a forged id cannot exist), the body's server position (`ServerPos`, LV95) within 3 m (chests,
  edelweiss) or 8 m (stones, plates), not already in the player's section, the seal complete, and a
  rate limit of 4 claims a second per peer.
- **Grants** follow `LootService.Granted`: the server decides, the client adds to its local inventory,
  what does not fit is dropped at its feet (`ItemController.Give`).
- **Visibility**: nothing about a player's opened chests is sent to others. Each client hides only
  its own. A chest is never "used up" for the world.
- Trials ride `RaceManager`'s RPCs with the new kind; ghosts are a new chunked upload beside
  `PhotoTransfer`.
- **Protocol bump** (`Net/Handshake.Protocol`): this plan bumps it for its own RPCs, on top of the
  objectives plan's bump from 49.
- **Owner sync**: the five sections reach their owner through the objectives plan's generic
  `SectionSync(id, version, json)` (≤ 4/s); the client redraws on `SectionChanged(id)` (opened chests
  hidden, picked edelweiss zeroed). No rewards-specific sync RPC.

## Persistence

- **Per player**, in the objectives plan's `AdventureBook` (`user://adventure/players/<fingerprint>.json`,
  offline `user://adventure/local.json`). Each section is declared in its own file as
  `public static readonly SectionKey<T> Section = new("id", Version, Migrate)`, read with
  `progress.Get(key)`, marked with `Touch(key)`:

| Section id | Type (file) | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| `collect` | `CollectProgress` (`Edelweiss.cs`) | `Taken` (edelweiss and cairn ids), `Held`, `Offered`, `Fauna` (name → count) |
| `chests` | `ChestsProgress` (`ChestLoot.cs`) | `Opened` (chest ids), `Pity[4]`, `Owned` (cosmetics ever granted), `PlacementVersion` |
| `trials` | `TrialsProgress` (`Trials.cs`) | `Best` (trial id → seconds, medal) |
| `photos` | `PhotosProgress` (`PhotoSpots.cs`) | `Taken` (photo target ids) |
| `gadgets` | `GadgetsProgress` (`Gadgets.cs`) | adventure gadgets ever crafted or granted (a lost detector can be re-crafted cheaply) |

  Ids are strings so a section stays valid across generator versions; ~50 000 opened ids would be
  ~1 MB, so `chests` may move to per-tile bitsets (open question 4). The max stamina bonus is **not**
  here: it is the stamina plan's `stamina` section, written by its `"stamina"` handler.
- **Server only**: `user://adventure/trials.json` (boards), `user://adventure/ghosts/<trial>_<rank>.bin`.
  Seal and guide progress is memory only.
- **Events** (`AdventureService.Events.Raise`, server only, after the section is written and touched),
  the shared records of the objectives plan:
  - `ChestOpened(Peer, Key, ChestId, Tier, PoiId?)`;
  - `CollectibleTaken(Peer, Key, CollectibleId, Kind, Canton)`, Kind `edelweiss` / `cairn`, Canton the
    2-letter code of the spot;
  - `TrialFinished(Peer, Key, TrialId, Seconds, Medal)`;
  - `PhotoTaken(Peer, Key, TargetId, PoiId?)`;
  - the one new record, in `Edelweiss.cs`: `EdelweissOffered(Peer, Key, ShrineId, Offered)`.
- **UI**: a **Collection** tab in `Ui/AdventureLog` (`AdventureLog.RegisterTab("collection",
  "Collection", order, build)`): chests by tier and canton, edelweiss held / offered, cairns per canton,
  trial medals and bests, photo targets, the fauna list. The bird journal's Fauna tab (part 7) stays
  beside the birds and fish it belongs with and links to the same `collect.Fauna` data.
