# Ski physics: carve and skid, the fall line, stride to move off snow (#899)

Follows the slopes (#887, PR #888). Decided with the owner (Oct 19 2026):

- **Off snow, skis barely move.** You move by **pressing forward again and again**, a stride per
  press. Holding the key does nothing. On snow the same presses are a skate on the flat.

## What is there

- `Skis.Step` moves one scalar speed along the heading.
  - `SteerByLean` turns the heading by the bank.
  - The edges scrub speed by the square of the edge angle.
  - W is a continuous pole push (`PoleWatts`, capped at 6 m/s).
  - `ground.Grade` is the slope along the heading only, so a traverse is flat.
- `RideMotion.Slip` (cars' drift) already lets the travel differ from the heading
  (`FootPlayer.RidePhysics`).
- #887 gives the snow's `FrictionScale`/`GripScale` on slopes; off a slope friction is the snow's.

## Research (brief of #887's discussion)

- **Kinetic friction μ**:

  | Snow | μ |
  |---|---|
  | Ice | 0.03 |
  | Groomed | 0.04–0.06 |
  | Very cold | 0.08–0.12 |
  | Slush | 0.08–0.15 |

- **Drag**: CdA is 0.2 m² tucked against 0.65 m² upright.
- **Carving**: the radius is about the sidecut radius × cos(edge). The edge holds while
  v²/R < μ_lat·g·cosθ (μ_lat 0.6–0.9 groomed, 0.3–0.4 ice); past that it skids, with sideways
  friction μ 0.3–0.6, which is the brake.
- **Hockey stop**: about 0.6 g. **Skating** on the flat: up to ~6 m/s.
- **Landing**: EFH = v⊥²/2g. Legs absorb ~1.5 m; 3 m and more is a crash.
- **From other games**: assist the carve (steer the velocity toward the skis), or realism reads as
  random loss of control.

## Design

- **The skis' frame**: the velocity is split into `u` along the skis and `w` across them, from
  `Speed` and `Slip`. `RideGround` gains the slope's pull along and across the skis
  (g·n.y·(n.x, n.z) from the floor normal), filled for skis only.
- **Each step on snow**:
  - `u` takes the slope along, friction (μ × the snow's `FrictionScale`) and drag.
  - `w` takes the slope across, then the edges:
    - **carve**: while the slip angle is under the carve window (Sim 0.12 rad, Game 0.2), the
      edges turn `w` into `u` at no cost, up to the grip (μ_lat·g·`GripScale`·edge);
    - **skid**: beyond the window, or past the grip, `w` is scrubbed by sideways friction
      (μ_skid·g, more with the edge set): speed lost, which is the brake.
  - Turning stays `SteerByLean`: the heading swings, the velocity lags, and the window decides
    carve or skid.
- **Hockey stop**: brake + steer yaws the skis at up to 3 rad/s whatever the speed. The slip grows,
  the skid scrubs it.
- **Snowplough**: brake alone, as now.
- **Off snow** (not `Snow`/`Ice` underfoot): μ 0.45. A 25° slope still creeps; anything gentler stops.
- **Stride**: a new press of forward (`Throttle` rising past 0.5) adds a push:
  - on snow +1.1 m/s up to 4.5 m/s;
  - off snow +0.5 m/s up to 1.4 m/s;
  - at most one push every 0.15 s;
  - holding gives nothing.
  - **VR**: `XrRig.UpdateSki`'s pole push is already a decaying pulse, so each push is one stride.
  - **Pad**: the throttle trigger or the stick flicked forward.
  - **Bots** (`AutoPilot` on skis): it pulses the throttle when slow on the flat.
- **Landing**: on touchdown on skis, EFH from the velocity into the ground's normal.
  - Under 1.5 m: clean.
  - 1.5–3 m: a stumble, speed × 0.75, with a camera shake if there is one.
  - Over 3 m: the existing bail.
  - The trick grades stay as they are.
- **Profiles**: Game keeps more grip, a wider carve window and a slower skid scrub; Sim follows
  the numbers above.

## Steps

1. Pure core: a `SkiDynamics` step on (u, w), inputs and constants, with no Godot. Unit tests:
   - a straight run's top speed by snow;
   - a carve keeps its speed and a skid loses it;
   - a traverse holds with edges and slips without;
   - a hockey stop distance;
   - off snow, a slope that stops a skier;
   - stride caps.
2. `Skis.Step` on it; `RideGround` slope along and across; landing EFH in `FootPlayer`.
3. Stride on three devices: the hint ("Tap W to skate"), `vr-action-map`, `AutoPilot` pulses,
   `bot-capability-map`.
4. Checks: `--ride skis` on La Pasay (top speed, a skid stop), off snow on grass, `racecheck skis`
   (the AutoPilot still finishes), quick tier. Notes.

## Status (on the branch, Oct 19 2026)

- **Built**: steps 1-4.
- **Built differently**:
  - Off-snow friction is 0.30, not 0.45, and the off-snow push is 0.7 m/s. At 0.45 friction ate
    each push before the next one, and six presses a second went nowhere.
  - Carving needs the skis on edge: flat skis do not carve.
  - `--ride skis` taps six times a second.
- **Found**: ski race courses follow roads, so off snow they are now a walk. Follow-up: build
  courses from the slopes' centrelines.
