## Context
The user researched real-life aura battles (the doc "Aura Battles: How Real-Life Aura Fights Work"). They want the same thing in the game as a two-player duel, separate from the existing Tekken-style fist fight ([#495](https://github.com/SuperQuentin/UnitSportSwitzerland/issues/495)).

What the user decided:
- **Separate mode.** It has its own challenge and reuses the fight's invite, arena, camera and HUD patterns.
- **Plan, then perform.** Each player chooses a combo of moves, then performs it with **one key that advances to the next move**. The better the timing of each press, the more aura that move earns.
- **Judging.** A deterministic score decides, plus a capped bonus from nearby players cheering. The end screen shows who had the best combo.

## How a battle plays
- **Challenge.** `/aura <player>` or Activities → Invites sends invite kind `aura`. It is Mutual, like the fight. Both players stand on marks 4 m apart, facing each other, using the fight's arena code.
- **Rounds.** There are 3 rounds, each one turn per player. A goes first in round 1 and B in round 2. In round 3, whoever is behind goes second, so they get to answer.
- **Plan (15 s).** The performer slots 3–6 moves into a combo strip. A live estimate shows the expected score. The rival only sees "X is planning…", so the plan stays secret until the performance starts.
- **Perform, with one key.**
  - The beat runs at 120 BPM on the shared server clock. The first move starts on the next bar.
  - Each move lasts its beat count (2 or 4 beats). The player presses **Next** to cut to the following move. The ideal press lands on the move's last beat, the downbeat where the next move should begin.
  - Timing windows around that downbeat: Perfect ±0.05 s → ×1.0, Good ±0.10 s → ×0.8, Ok ±0.16 s → ×0.5.
  - **Early** (rushed, "try-hard") or **late** (holding too long, "frozen") beyond Ok is a Miss → ×0. The move is drawn as a stumble and costs −2.
  - With no press by 1 beat after the downbeat, the move auto-advances as a Miss.
  - The last press "drops the pose". A Finisher move graded Good or better earns +5.
- **Verdict (4 s).** The score breakdown counts up. After round 3, the winner and each player's **best combo** card (their highest-scoring turn) are shown.

The score adds up as follows:
- **Base:** Σ base × timing factor.
- **Repeats and stale moves:**
  - A repeat inside the combo scores ×0.5.
  - A move used in your previous turn scores ×0.5, and one used in an earlier turn ×0.7.
  - Copying a move from the rival's last combo ("biting") scores ×0.5.
- **Variety:** +3 per extra family, up to +12.
- **Chains:** a bonus for a good pair, only when neither move was missed. Examples: Walk→Pose +4, Stare→Meme +3, Dance→Pose +3, Lasso→LeadRival +5.
- **Opener / Finisher:** +2 / +5.
- **Answer:** +8 for each rival move you counter, ×1.25 when it is your first move (a "flip"), capped at +18.
- **Streak:** +1 per Perfect after the third in a row, up to +8.
- **Try-hard:** −3 per point of total difficulty over 10, and −5 for each Meme beyond the second ("cringe").
- **Crowd:** +2 per spectator within 15 m who plays Cheer, Clap or Fist pump during the turn. It is capped at +8 and at 25 % of the score, so skill always matters more than the crowd.
- **Total:** `max(0, round(sum))`, shown ×100 as "+4100 AURA" (cosmetic).

## Network and authority
- **Client → server** (`AnyPeer`, reliable):
  - `RequestChallenge`.
  - `SubmitPlan(id, turn, byte[] moves)`, only from the performer during Plan, checked with `ValidatePlan`. On timeout, the server pads the plan with StandTall.
  - `Next(id, turn, index, double ts)`, where `ts` is the client's `ClockSync.ServerNow` at the press.
  - `RequestLeave`.
- **Grading a Next press:**
  - The server grades it against its own downbeat times.
  - It rejects a press from the wrong peer, for the wrong index, a duplicate, or with a `ts` in the future.
  - When `|ts − (arrival − rtt/2)| > 0.12 s`, the server uses arrival − rtt/2 instead of `ts`.
  - Limitation (stated in the note): a modified client can still fake perfect timestamps inside that slack.
- **Server → the two players** (`Authority`):
  - `Begin` and `State` (phase, end time, totals).
  - `Perform(t0, bpm, moves)`, which reveals the plan.
  - `Graded(index, grade)`.
  - `Verdict(parts[])`.
  - `Ended(winner, totals)`.
- **Spectators:** they see the moves through the replicated `AuraPose`. The crowd bonus reads their existing replicated `DanceId`, so cheering needs no new RPC.
