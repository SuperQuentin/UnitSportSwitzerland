`adventure-errands` (NPC givers, daily errands, world events), `adventure-together` (group yield, the

## Goal

A party logs in during the Älplerfest and finds Interlaken dressed in flags and bunting, a sawdust ring
on the meadow and a beer tent. Today the stone and the alphorns are open; tomorrow the flag swingers;
Saturday the wrestling and Sunday night's Schlussgang ends on fireworks over the Höhematte. Everything
they did added to one party score, the tokens they won bought a flower crown, and what was left became
francs. In autumn they bring a decorated herd down to Charmey together; and every year 1 August lights a
bonfire on every hill. Bots stand in when friends are missing.

Out of scope: horses (there is no horse `RideKind`; mounted herding means bikes, quads and tractors),
real-money or cross-server leaderboards, festival-exclusive power (cosmetics and francs only),
Montreux Jazz and the Zibelemärit (Open question 6), a playable Guggenmusik orchestra with more than
three parts, and a festival in a region the server does not hold (it relocates, section 1.2).

## 1. Festival framework

### 1.1 A festival is an occasion with a site and a day table

- **Code**: `src/Occasions/Festivals/` (namespace `UnitSport.Occasions.Festivals`), notes in
  `docs/notes/occasions/festival-*.md` (no new notes area). Pure parts linked into tier 0.
- **`FestivalDef`** (pure, a C# catalog like `QuestCatalog`): `Id` (= the occasion id), `Title`,
  `Sites` (`FestivalSite(Name, E, N, RadiusM, PoiId?)`, real first, then fallbacks), `Days`
  (`FestivalDay(int From, GameId[] Opens, string Blurb)`), `Show` (`ClosingShow.Fireworks | Concert | Both
  | Bonfires`), `TokenCap`, `Milestones` (party score thresholds and their rewards).
- **`FestivalOccasion : Occasion`**: one per festival, registered in `OccasionRegistry`; its
  `OccasionEntry` default goes into `OccasionConfig.Defaults` (priority 8: under Halloween/Christmas for the
  sky, over `ufo`). `Facets`: Decorations, Atmosphere, Audio, Hats as cosmetic; a new **`Games`** facet
  (`1 << 6`, gameplay, not in `Cosmetic`: a player's "Off" hides the bunting, never the score).
- **Day index** = days since the instance's window opened (server's local date, as the occasion's own),
  pure `FestivalCalendar.Day(entry, date)`. A game is open from its `From` day to the end: a late player
  meets every game at once (Lantern Rite's pattern without the FOMO). The closing show plays **every
  game evening of the last real day** (21:30 game time; a game day is ~22 real minutes), so anyone
  logging in that day sees one.
- **Two new schedule tokens** in `OccasionWeekdays` (pure, tested): a month-qualified weekday
  `04-mon#3` (Sechseläuten's third Monday of April) and an Easter-relative day `easter-46` (Ash
  Wednesday; Gauss/Anonymous Gregorian algorithm), both with the `+N` tail. Existing tokens unchanged.

### 1.2 Sites in the real world

- A site is placed only when its tile is in the server's region (`ChunkManager` source covers it). If no
  site of a festival is, it **relocates** to the largest `OccasionTowns` town of the region with at least
  `MinBuildings` x 10, and says so ("Älplerfest, held this year in Bulle"). The western dev region
  therefore runs the Älplerfest at Val-de-Charmey or Bulle and the Désalpe at Charmey (its real place).
- `FestivalDecor` (through `Occasion.Decorate`, hashed per tile as `OccasionDecor` requires): within
  `RadiusM` of the site, bunting along eaves facing the site square, cantonal and Swiss flags on poles,
  a beer tent (`Festzelt`: open-sided, benches, a bar counter reusing the restaurant pieces of #1242/#1245
  from the venues epic #1232 as props, not an interior), the game grounds (sawdust ring, stone run-up,
  flag lawn, alphorn row). `ps1_prop` with `PropColors.Matte`; lamps on strings use vertex alpha
  (`Lamp`). Every new prop in the model viewer: `[Showcase("Festivals")]`.
- **Game grounds are anchored once per instance**: the site's square first, else the flattest open
  cell within 150 m (level within 0.8 m over 20 m, the landing test of `UfoSightings`); a pure function of
  the tile, so the server and clients agree with nothing sent.

### 1.3 The `festivals` section, the party board, Batzen

- **`FestivalBook`** (`SectionKey<FestivalBook> Section = new("festivals", Version: 1)`), per instance:
  `Batzen` (tokens), `Points`, `Best` (per game), `Played` (per game per day), `Milestones` taken,
  `Converted` (bool). Kept for past instances as a record (like the hunt's `claims.json`).
- **Batzen** (the old Swiss coin) are festival tokens held in the section, not as items: the inventory
  is the client's, the section is the server's. Earned per game (Perfect play pays more; a daily cap
  per game, `TokenCap` 600 per festival). Spent at the festival stall (`FestivalShop`: flower crown,
  Edelweiss shirt variants, Sennenkäppi, a Treichel for the wall, fireworks for one's own 1 August).
- **Nothing is lost**: when an instance has ended, the next `Loaded(peer)` converts what is left at
  **1 Batzen = CHF 1** (`Bank.Credit(peer, n, "festival")`, account, once: `Converted`), with a card in
  the Journal. A player who was away for a year is paid the next time they come.
- **Party score**: `FestivalBoard` on the server (`user://festivals/<instance>.json`, `WorldChoice.SavePath`,
  saved every 30 s and at the end). Points a player earns while in a `Parties` party are added to that
  party's crew entry (`Party.Id` is per session, so the entry is keyed by the instance and the **sorted
  proven keys** of the members who earned in it; a party that re-forms the next day with the same people
  finds its score). A milestone reached pays **every member who contributed**, guests included
  (overview: no co-op where guests miss rewards). Solo players have a crew of one.
- **Journal**: a `Festival` tab (`AdventureLog.RegisterTab`) while a festival runs or has unconverted
  Batzen: the day table (open, tomorrow, the show), the party score bar with milestones, the best per
  game, the shop. Refreshes on `SectionChanged("festivals")` and `FestivalService.BoardChanged`.
- **Quests**: festival steps are catalog entries with new step kinds in this plan's file:
  `FestivalGameStep(game, minScore?)`, `FestivalShowStep(festival)`. Events: `FestivalGamePlayed(peer,
  key, festival, game, score)`, `FestivalMilestone(peer, key, festival, n)`.

## 2. Fireworks and the closing show

- **`FireworkShow`** (pure, tier 0): from a seed and a launch line (LV95 points: a lake's shore or a
  barge, a hilltop), a 6-12 minute script of shells `(t, point, kind, colour, height)`. Kinds: peony,
  chrysanthemum, willow, palm, ring, crossette and a **Swiss cross** (white stars on a red peony). Three
  acts, a finale of 40 shells in 20 s. Played from `ClockSync.ServerNow` like a UFO sighting: every peer
  sees the same shell at the same time; one `Plan` RPC per show.
- **`Fireworks`** (client node): one `GpuParticles3D` pool per kind with a trail sub-emitter, shells
  started by `EmitParticle`; 4 pooled `OmniLight3D` flashes; bangs and crackles synthesised
  (`FireworkSounds`, a `[SoundShowcase("Fireworks")]` set) and **delayed by distance / 343 m/s** (a
  shell 2 km off is heard 6 s late). Drawn within 15 km. The concert finale's airbursts may call it later.
- **The closing show**: `Fireworks` over the site, or a concert where the festival names an open-air
  `StadiumSpec` (`ConcertPlanner` gets a festival seed instead of the date's), or both (concert, then
  fireworks in the blackout after the last song, where `ConcertMusic` already marks `Finale`). Watching
  it within 1.5 km counts `FestivalShowStep`.

## 3. Steinstossen (the Unspunnen stone)

- The stone weighs **83.5 kg**; real throws land around 3.5-4 m. Classes: Unspunnen 83.5 kg, 40 kg,
  18 kg (for a first go). Rules in **`SteinRules`** (pure).
- **Flow**: E at the stone (`interact`) steps onto the run-up (walk locked, like `AuraPhysics`). **Lift**:
  hold `use_item`: a meter swings with a period that grows with the weight; **release** on the peak to
  hoist it to the shoulder. **Run-up**: three steps on the beat of a 90 BPM drum (`PogoBeat` grades each).
  **Push**: the last press; the angle comes from the camera pitch (best 38-42°).
- **Authority**: the meter's phase is `SteinRules.Meter(seed, t)`, seeded by the server per attempt; the
  client sends only the press stamps (`ServerNow`, checked as `AuraManager` does); the server computes
  power, the step grades and the distance (`SteinRules.Distance`: release 1.9 m high, a ballistic
  flight, a sawdust landing) and sends `Landed(peer, attempt, metres)` to everyone within 300 m. Every
  peer draws the arc from that result. Three attempts a day; the best counts.
- **Bots** compete: a bot's attempt is `SteinRules.BotAttempt(skill, rng)`, through the same server path
  (`ServeAttempt(peer, ...)`), with a stone-lifting pose; `/stein <bot>` or the ground's 2-4 ambient bots.

## 4. Alphorn and yodel

- **Instrument**: the held `ItemId.Alphorn` (Use is today's call; on a festival ground, E at the alphorn
  row starts a **song** instead). A real alphorn plays only natural tones: partials 2-12 of F♯1
  (46.2 Hz; `AlphornVoice.Note(partial, length)`, synthesised from `SwissItems.Horn`'s overtone model,
  `[SoundShowcase("Alphorn")]`), including the characteristic out-of-tune 11th, the "Alphorn-Fa".
- **The song**: a seeded chart of 16-32 held notes at 60-76 BPM (`AlphornChart`, pure): each note a
  partial, a start beat and a length. The player picks the partial (wheel / stick up-down), starts it on
  the beat (`PogoBeat.Press`) and lets go on its end beat (the same judge). Score: timing x the right
  partial, a breath bar that refills in rests.
- **Yodel** is the second part of the same chart: no instrument, `yodel` toggles chest and head voice;
  the break (the "Juuz") must land on the chart's flips. Synthesised two-formant voice, in the sound player.
- **Harmony**: up to 4 players within 30 m who start the same chart (the first one's start bar, joining at
  the next) each get a voice part (I: melody, II: a third or sixth below, III: the drone on partials
  2-4, IV: yodel). The **server** scores each beat where two voices sound: unison, octave, fifth (3:2),
  fourth (4:3), major third (5:4) add; a clash subtracts; the Alphorn-Fa against the drone adds. A
  harmony multiplier up to x1.6 on every player's score, and to the party board.
- **Echo off the real valley** (client, cosmetic): `EchoFinder` (pure, a height delegate) marches 5 rays
  in the bell's direction ±20°, 25 m steps out to 4 km on the coarse terrain, and finds the first face
  that rises over the line of sight. The note is replayed from that wall point with delay
  `(|blower-wall| + |wall-listener|) / 343` s, -6 dB per doubling of the path, low-passed: a valley 1 km
  across answers ~6 s later. No wall within 4 km (a plateau, a lake): no echo. Computed once per note
  start, never per frame.
- **Bots** play a second voice: `BotSide` like `AuraManager.Bots`: the chart's presses plus a timing
  error by skill, through `ServeNote(peer, ...)`.

## 5. Fahnenschwingen

- A 1.2 m square flag on a short staff (a skinned prop: staff as a bone, cloth in a shader
  (`flag_cloth.gdshader`, wave from the swing speed uniform), never rebuilt).
- **Figures** (`FahnenRules`, pure): Ufzug (raise), Mühlirad (mill wheel), Achter (figure eight),
  Lufthieb, Unterschwung, Hochwurf (the high throw) and the catch. A routine is 8-12 figures on a 96 BPM
  march; each figure is a stick or mouse gesture (a circle, an eight, a sweep, recognised by `GestureRing`,
  pure: 8 sectors sampled at 30 Hz) that must complete by its last beat (`AuraRules`-style windows). The
  Hochwurf: hold `use_item`, release on the beat; the flag flies for 2-4 beats (higher = more points);
  the catch is a `PogoBeat` press as it comes down. A dropped flag ends the routine.
- Two or three players in a line are scored for being in sync (each figure's grade on the same beat) x1.3.
- Authority as Steinstossen: press stamps to the server, grades there, `Routine` result to the ground.
  Pose replicated through one new `FootPlayer.FestivalPose` (`[Export] int`, OnChange), drawn by the
  dance layer (`HumanMeshBuilder.FestivalMoves = 7000` + pose), shared by parts 3-5.

## 6. Schwingen

**Does the fist fight fit?** The *match shell* fits; the *strike rules* do not. Reused: the invite
(kind `schwing`), the marks on a line and forfeits (18 m, getting into anything, disconnect), a pose
field drawn by the dance layer, the side-on `FightView`, the HUD frame, `AuraManager.Watch`'s spectator
feed. Not reused: `FightRules.Resolve` (high/mid/low strikes and HP have nothing to do with a grapple).
So `Combat/SchwingManager` (at `World/Schwing`) copies the shell's structure, with the pure
**`SchwingRules`**; a shared base is extracted only if the two drift into duplicating more than the
challenge and forfeit code (open question 4).

- **The ring**: the festival's sawdust ring (14 m across); off a festival, anywhere flat with
  `/schwing <player>`. Both in Schwingerhosen (a festival item, worn: `ItemUse.Wear`, Bottom slot).
- **Gang**: one bout, 3 minutes (the Schlussgang 6), wrestlers start gripped (marks 0.9 m apart, right
  hand on the belt, left on the rolled trouser leg).
- **Grapple loop** (`SchwingRules`, 10 Hz on the server): each has **balance** (0-100) and **grip**
  (0-100). Holding the move stick leans; the rival's lean against yours costs both grip. A **throw** is
  a named attack with a direction and a 0.35 s telegraph: Kurz (pull in, lift), Übersprung (step over,
  sideways), Hüfter (hip), Brienzer (arm over the neck, back), Gammen (leg hook), Wyberhaken (inside
  leg). The defender answers in the telegraph: brace against its direction (block + stick), or the throw's
  own counter (a Hüfter is countered by stepping round, the stick 90°). Answered: the attacker's balance
  drops; not answered: the defender's balance drops by the throw's weight; under 20 the defender goes
  down. **Bodenarbeit** on the ground: a short tug (alternate presses) to roll the rival onto both
  shoulder blades.
- **Win**: both shoulder blades in the sawdust, the winner's hand still on the trousers (the server checks
  grip > 0): 10 points; a time-out is "gestellt", 9 each; a loss 8.5. Tradition: the winner brushes the
  sawdust off the loser's back (an automatic 2 s pose).
- **Festival**: Saturday's Anschwingen pairs whoever is in the ring's queue (bots fill an odd count),
  three Gänge; Sunday the two best meet in the **Schlussgang**, watched by everyone on the ground. The
  winner gets the bull's bell (a cosmetic Treichel and a title, "Schwingerkönig 2027").
- **Poses**: pair poses authored together (`HumanMeshBuilder.Schwing.cs`, `SchwingMoves = 6000` +
  pose, on `FootPlayer.FestivalPose`); a throw is a timed pair animation both peers start from the
  server's `Thrown(id, throw, winner, serverTime)`; each owner places its own body on the server's marks.
  `[Showcase("Schwingen")]` for each pair pose.
- **Bots**: a `SchwingManager.BotSide` and `SchwingHoldBrain` like the aura's (the server drives the bot's
  stick and presses: throw choice by situation, an answer chance and reaction by `BotDifficulty`).
  This also proves the server path the fist fight lacks; its own row stays a gap.

## 7. Désalpe (Alpabzug)

- **When and where**: `desalpe`, `09-20 .. 10-05` (Charmey's Désalpe is real, the last weekend of
  September); sites Val-de-Charmey (2578750 / 1163750, the fair in the village), Urnäsch (AR), then the
  relocation rule. Day table: decorating opens day 1, the drive day 3, the vote day 4, the fair all along.
- **Decorating**: the party picks one herd on an alp pasture above the site (a `Herds` herd: the nearest
  `HerdPlan` pasture over 1 200 m within 6 km, else a staged herd, `Herds.Stage`, at the alp's POI).
  Players craft or buy flower headdresses (3 shapes, 6 colours) and Treicheln (3 sizes) and put them on
  with Use at a cow (E / Y / VR: hold it to the cow's head and let go). The **Leitkuh** wears the biggest bell.
- **No mesh rebuild**: decorations are their own meshes, attached, never baked. A decorated herd draws
  two more `MultiMesh`es beside its bodies and heads: headdresses on each head transform, bells at the
  neck (`CowMeshBuilder.Neck`), written in the same posing loop of `Herds.Draw.cs` (same LOD steps:
  every frame to 300 m, every 8th to 650 m). The bell's swing and the flowers' sway are in the shader
  from `INSTANCE_CUSTOM` (phase, walk speed). Undecorated slots: zero scale. The decoration set is a
  per-herd byte array (shape, colour, bell per cow) the server holds, sent on change.
- **The drive**: the server takes the herd over (`DrivenHerd`, overrides like the beam's) and plans the
  road with `RoadNetwork.Path` from the alp to the fair (paths and minor roads first, 4-12 km, 300-900 m
  down). Steering (`HerdFlock`, pure, tier 0, server 10 Hz): separation 1.6 m, alignment and cohesion,
  the Leitkuh follows a carrot 15 m ahead on the route at 0.9 m/s, the rest follow her. **Drivers push**:
  each player or bot body has a flight zone (5 m on foot, 9 m on a bike, quad or tractor); a cow in it
  moves away from the driver, and **the point of balance** at its shoulder decides forward or back
  (stockmanship's real rule). Flanking keeps the herd on the road.
- **Strays**: at each junction and open meadow, a cow with low herd drive (seeded) and grass in reach
  may leave; it grazes, then drifts back only when a driver flanks it. A cow 150 m from the herd is lost
  for the score (it walks home to its pasture). **Gates**: 3-6 fence gates where the route crosses a
  pasture boundary or a junction branch (hashed on the route): E / Y / VR grip opens and shuts; an open
  gate behind the herd lets cows into the field.
- **Replication**: `HerdNet` snapshots like `BirdNet`'s (8 Hz, unreliable, ≤ 40 cows, ~24 B each: key,
  LV95 delta, yaw, gait), to peers within 400 m; the decorations and gates reliable on change. At the
  fair the herd is set down (`CowDown`) and grazes the fair's pasture until the end of the instance.
- **Score**: cows arrived x 50, time against par, decorations x 10; the party board.
- **The vote**: day 4, every herd that arrived stands in the fair's ring; each player (not bots) gives
  one vote to another party's herd (`VoteHerd(herd)`, one per instance, not one's own); top three get
  milestones' worth of Batzen and the "Schönste Herde" title. Ties by arrival time.
- **Bots** drive too: `HerdDriverBrain` holds a flank slot (left, right, rear) at the flight zone's edge,
  goes after the nearest stray, shuts gates behind the herd. A solo player with two bots can do a drive.

## Network

- **Authority**: the server decides the day, the open games, every grade and score, Batzen, the board,
  the herd and the bet. Clients send press stamps (checked like `AuraManager`'s: within 0.12 s of
  arrival − rtt/2) and requests; every server method checks `AdventureService.Active(peer)` and that the
  body (`PositionOf`) is at the ground.
- **Nodes and RPCs** (all reliable unless said):
  - `World/Festivals` (`FestivalService`): client → `RequestJoin(game)`, `RequestBuy(item)`,
    `RequestBet(minute)`, `RequestVote(herd)`; server → all `TellDay(festival, day, openMask)` (and on
    join), → party `TellBoard(crew, points, milestones)`, → all within 15 km `ShowPlan(seed, kind, start,
    line)`, → owner `TellRefused(why)`. Sections by `SectionSync`.
  - `World/Festivals/Stein`, `.../Alphorn`, `.../Fahnen`: client → `Press(attempt, index, ts)` /
    `Note(chart, index, partial, on, ts)`; server → ground (300 m) `Landed`, `Graded`, `Harmony(beat, mask)`.
  - `World/Schwing` (`SchwingManager`): `RequestChallenge`, `Act(id, lean, brace, throw, ts)` (20 Hz cap),
    `RequestLeave`; server → two + watchers `Begin`, `State` (10 Hz while live, unreliable), `Thrown`, `Ended`.
  - `World/Herds/Drive` (`HerdService`): server → near `HerdSnap` (8 Hz, unreliable), `HerdDecor`,
    `GateState`; client → `RequestDecorate(herd, cow, kind)`, `RequestGate(gate, open)`.
  - Replicated property: `FootPlayer.FestivalPose` (OnChange; in `ActorIo` for movie clips).
- **Protocol**: each PR bumps `Net/Handshake.Protocol` once (56 today; whatever `main` holds + 1 at merge).
- Positions as LV95 doubles. Rate limits: 20 presses a second, 2 requests a second per peer.

## Persistence

- Per player: the `festivals` section in the `AdventureBook` (online by proven key, offline `local`);
  `Migrate` per version bump. Bots: session only.
- Server: `user://festivals/<instance>.json` (the board, the herd's decoration and arrival, the bet pool,
  votes), `JsonStore.SaveAsync`, every 30 s and on quit; read back after a restart mid-festival.
- Config: festivals' `OccasionEntry`s in `user://occasions.json` like any occasion (an operator moves
  the dates or turns one off); the defaults stay in code.

## Performance

Rules: `perf-no-per-frame-allocations`, `no-mesh-rebuild-per-frame`.
- Decorations: `MultiMesh` per tile through `OccasionDecor` (unloaded with the tile); nothing per frame.
- Cows: two extra `MultiMesh`es per decorated herd, written in the existing posing loop; sway and bells in
  the shader. `HerdFlock` on the server: ≤ 40 cows, O(n²) at 10 Hz = 16 000 pair tests a second, under
  `ServerStats.Ran`; ~8 KB/s per watching peer.
- Fireworks: ≤ 6 000 live particles, GPU only, 4 lights; CPU per frame is the script cursor.
- Höhenfeuer: one `MultiMesh` of billboards (≤ 400 in view), flicker in the shader, rebuilt on a 1 Hz
  POI query when the set changes.
- Games: HUD text on change only (static `StringName`s), echo found once per note.
- Budget: < 0.3 ms client frame at a full festival ground with 12 players (`--perf` on the fixture).
