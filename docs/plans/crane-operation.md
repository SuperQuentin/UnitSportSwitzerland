# Operating a tower crane (#618, part of #605)

#610 made every building site's crane scenery whose pose is a pure function of the clocks. This
plan lets a player take one over, lift a pallet with it, and hand it back to the clocks without a
snap.

## Decisions (agreed with the user, 2026-10-14)

- **Two ways to the controls.** On a tower crane, **climb the mast ladder into the cab**. On any
  crane, self-erecting ones included (they have no cab), **take the radio remote** from its box at
  the mast's foot and work it from the ground.
- **The hook lifts pallets only**: the site's bricks and cement, the yards' stacks, any loose one,
  through `PalletService` as the forks and the bucket do. Big bags and skips as loose objects are a
  follow-up.
- **A hook / release button**, not the forks' no-button rule.
- **The operator's own view, plus a hook camera** on the view toggle: a camera on the trolley
  looking straight down, as real cranes have.

## Controls (three devices together)

ISO crane pattern, as the excavator's dig mode already reads its sticks:

| What | Keyboard | Pad | VR |
|---|---|---|---|
| climb the mast / take the remote / leave | E | Y | Y, or grip the ladder or the remote box |
| slew left / right (`arm_slew_*`) | A / D | left stick X | cab: left joystick (two `Kind.Hold` levers); remote: left stick, not turned by the head |
| trolley out / in (`arm_stick_*`) | W / S | left stick Y | as above |
| hoist up / down (`arm_boom_*`) | ↑ / ↓ | right stick Y (back raises) | cab: right joystick lever; remote: right stick Y |
| hook on / release (`couple`) | H | D-pad ← | cab: a poke button on the console; remote: right stick ← (already `couple` when mounted) |
| hook camera (`camera_toggle`) | V | R3 | gap: a headset cannot be handed another camera |

On the ladder: forward / back climb, jump lets go (the steamer's and the gadget's rule).

## Network: server-owned state, the operator's pose streamed

- **`Terrain/Construction/CraneService` at `World/Cranes`**, made on both sides like
  `PalletService`. A crane is named by its job key, `<building>|<k>`, which every peer and the
  server work out from the tile's files.
- **Claim** (`AskOperate(key)`): granted to one peer at a time, the asker within reach of the
  mast's foot (the server places it from the tile files, off the main thread, as it places a site
  pallet). Refused to the asker otherwise ("Someone is working this crane").
- **While operated**, the operator sends its pose (yaw, trolley, hook height) unreliably at 10 Hz;
  the server clamps it to the crane's reach and relays it to every other peer, who ease towards it.
  Nothing goes through the player's own synchronizer: vision interest can drop a small figure in a
  cab 40 m up while its jib is still in view.
- **Released** (leaving, or the operator's peer going away): the server stamps the pose and the
  simulation time and sends both to everyone, and every peer works out the same resume from them:
  the crane **holds that pose until its next cycle starts**, then eases from it into the schedule
  over the first quarter of that cycle (`CraneMotion.Resume`, tier 0). A crane released **with a
  pallet on its hook holds it** and keeps off the schedule until someone sets the pallet down.
- **The hook's pallet** is server state on the crane's record (`Pallets.Carried` code), set when
  `PalletService` grants a take to an operator and cleared on a drop. `PalletService`'s
  `Forks`, `Holds` and `InReach` get a crane branch: an operator's carrier is its crane's hook, and
  reach is measured from the hook, not the body.
- Protocol bump.

## The hook

- The hook takes a pallet when its block is at **sling height** over the pallet's top (the slings
  are ~1 m) and within 0.9 m of its centre, on the button.
- With a pallet on, the button **sets it down** when the pallet's underside is within 0.3 m of what
  is under it (a ray down from its centre: the ground, a slab, scaffolding, the top of the
  materials), at that point, square to the jib.
- The pallet hangs from four slings, drawn on the hook; no collider while carried, as on forks.

## The cab and the ladder

- A ladder up the face of the mast towards the yard (rungs in the site mesh); the remote's box on
  the next face round, on both kinds of crane.
- `FootPlayer.Carrier` carries the climber (the steamer's ladder is the template: frame, clamp,
  release at the top), then holds the operator in the cab, slewing with it. Leaving the cab puts
  the player back at the ladder's top.
- On the remote the player stands where the box is, held still (the left stick is the slew).

## Checks

- Tier 0: `CraneMotionTests` for `Resume` (hold before the next cycle, no jump through the blend,
  the schedule after it, a loaded crane holding), the hook's take and set-down geometry.
- Tier 1: `--cranecheck --world flat`: a hand-made site, climb the ladder on the real bindings,
  operate, hook a pallet, lift it onto the top slab, leave, the crane holding then resuming.
- Tier 2: `tools/cranenetcheck.sh` (the issue's check): B sees A slew the crane and lift a pallet
  onto the top slab, and the crane resume its schedule after A leaves.
