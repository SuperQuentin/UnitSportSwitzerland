# Adventure layer, phase 3: things friends do together

Status: **planned**. Tracking issue: #1270. Builds on phases 1-2 (`adventure-overview`, all
merged).

Phases 1-2 gave each player a reason to explore. Phase 3 gives friends reasons to play **together**, and
every one of its rewards is at least as good played with friends as alone. Genshin's co-op gives friends
nothing in its card game and hide-and-seek, and that is the mistake not to repeat.

## The four plans

| Plan | What it adds | Depends on |
|---|---|---|
| [`adventure-together`](adventure-together.md) #1266 | **Together** rule (`TogetherService.Near`: party, 60 m), group yield (+25 % a friend), hand-holding and rope-up (`LinkBook`, `Tether`), shared fondue / raclette pot with a stirring game and a party buff, mentor rewards, the weekly bonus and the punch card | phases 1-2 |
| [`adventure-errands`](adventure-errands.md) #1267 | NPC errand givers at real places (`--givers` stage), dialogue (`ErrandDialog`), the daily **Postkarte** (8 cards per 5 km cell per day, 3 a day), world events (runaway cow, lost hiker, avalanche, forest fire), canton reputation | together's `TogetherService` |
| [`adventure-versteckis`](adventure-versteckis.md) #1268 | **Versteckis** prop hunt: 1 seeker against 3-7 hiders disguised as village props, objectives (church bell, Höhenfeuer, milk can), bots in both roles, its own `VersteckisManager`, a shared `Core/Rounds` registry extracted from BR | phases 1-2 |
| [`adventure-festivals`](adventure-festivals.md) #1269 | A festival framework on occasions (`FestivalOccasion`, Batzen tokens, a party score board, fireworks), the Älplerfest (Schwingen, Steinstossen, Fahnenschwingen, alphorn and yodel with valley echo), the Désalpe cow drive, six real-calendar festivals | together (shared pot), errands (festival givers) |

Plus [`adventure-alcohol`](adventure-alcohol.md) #1271 (asked by the owner while deciding): a real blood
alcohol level, its effects on walking and driving, an electronic and a single-use breathalyzer. It
builds after `together` (wine, kirsch, the fondue pot), and its ItemIds 486-488 are in together's block. Built in [#1309](https://github.com/SuperQuentin/UnitSportSwitzerland/pull/1309).

## Decisions taken (October 27, 2026)

Every recommendation in the table below is accepted, except question 8, which became the alcohol plan.

## Build order

1. **In parallel:**
   - `together` part 1 (`TogetherService`, group yield) followed by the rest of together;
   - `versteckis`, which touches BR and `Core/Rounds`, not the others' files.
2. **`errands`**, once `TogetherService` is merged.
3. **`festivals`**, one PR at a time:
   1. the framework;
   2. fireworks and the closing show;
   3. the four Älplerfest games, which can run in parallel;
   4. the Désalpe;
   5. one PR per calendar festival, the next real date first.

## Shared decisions

- **Item id blocks:**
  - `ItemId` 430-459 errands;
  - 460-479 versteckis;
  - 480-499 together;
  - 500-539 festivals.

  `Headwear` 5-20 goes to festivals.
- **Progress sections:** `together`, `errands`, `versteckis`, `festivals`.
- **The "together" rule** is `TogetherService.Near` (party, adventuring, not knocked out, within 60 m, at most 3 counted), in one place:
  - errands wraps it with its 10-minute window;
  - seals keep `Seals.Coop`.
- **Rounds:**
  - Versteckis rounds go through the new `Core/Rounds` registry, which feeds `AdventureService.InMatch`.
  - In a round, errands, world events, links, pots and the group yield are all off.
  - The round's own payout is a `Bank.Credit`, equal for every player who played.
- **No double pay:** together's +10 % cash per friend skips Postkarte cards, which already pay +50 % together.
- **Protocol:** each PR bumps `Net/Handshake.Protocol` once, in merge order (56 at the time of writing).
- Every PR keeps the project rules: keyboard, pad and VR; a bot path and capability-map rows; no mesh rebuilt per frame; models in the model viewer, sounds in the sound player; `ui/ux-guidelines`.
