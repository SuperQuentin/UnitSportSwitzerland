# G2 "The switch": the game on cube tiles in true Earth positions

Phase G2 of `docs/plans/global-tiling.md` (#1443), the row "G2 The switch" in its Phases table. The game
leaves the flat LV95 world: every tile kind lives in cube leaves in true Earth positions, the game's frame
is the ENU anchor frame of #1430 H3 and turns as it re-anchors, positions are Earth positions everywhere
(the wire, saves, the server), headings are true north, and the curvature is in the geometry.

Status: **agreed with the user** (2026-11-04); nothing built yet. Issue: #1500, one sub-issue per step (0 #1501; A1-A3 #1502-#1504; B1-B5 #1505-#1509; C1-C5 #1510-#1514; D #1515).

## Decisions (with the user, 2026-11-04)

### Track A: positions (flat world, no switch)

**A1 `GlobalPos` in ECEF** (several PRs, area by area)
- `GlobalPos` holds three ECEF doubles. New: `FromLv95(e, n, h)` and `Lv95` (the notation view, the
  rigorous projection, explicit and costly), `Geodetic`, `Altitude` (above the geoid: LHN95 through
  `SwissGeodesy` inside its grid), `Up`/`East`/`North` (ENU at the point), `+ Vector3` and `−` in the
  local ENU at the point, `DistanceTo` the chord, `HorizontalDistanceTo` the distance along the
  ellipsoid (the same as today to the mm within a few km).
- `E`/`N`/`Alt` first become `[Obsolete]` computed views; `FlatLv95RatchetTests` counts their uses
  (and `Lv95` uses) and may only shrink; each area's PR moves its sites to Earth maths, the frame, the
  cube key (`CubeKey.At(ecef, 14)`) or `Lv95` where it is notation; the last PR deletes them.
  Saves and the wire read `Lv95` until A2.
- In the flat world a `ToWorld` now costs one rigorous inverse projection: measured first, with a
  cache per frame if a hot path shows it (the flat world dies at D).
- Check: `tools/test.sh coords` against step 0's baseline after each area (no change), the quick
  tier, a microbenchmark of the conversions.

## Risks

- **Size**: A1 alone touches ~600 files and B3 ~52k lines of tools. Each is split by area, and the
  ratchet holds what is done.
- **Two worlds until D**: flat and earth share builders and gameplay; a check that passes in one may
  not in the other. Both run in the coords set from C1 on.
- **Parallel work**: new code written against LV95 during G2 adds to the migration; the ratchet
  catches `GlobalPos.E/N` and tile corners, the note `general/flat-lv95-ratchet` says so.
- **The flat world's cost**: with ECEF inside, flat-mode `ToWorld` runs the rigorous projection;
  measured in A1.
- **Every `OriginShift` handler meets a rotation on the ground** (#1430's net covers Space).
