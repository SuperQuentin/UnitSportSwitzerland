# Adventure Versteckis: hide-and-seek as village props

Status: **planned**. Tracking issue: #1268 (phase 3: #1270).

One of four Adventure plans for phase 3, "things friends do together", written in parallel:
`adventure-errands` (NPC givers, daily errands, world events), `adventure-together` (group yield,
fondue pot, rope-up, mentor), `adventure-festivals` (Älplerfest, Alpabzug, seasonal festivals) and
this one. "Versteckis" is Swiss German for hide-and-seek. The game is modelled on Genshin's Windtrace
and on prop hunt. One seeker plays against 3-7 hiders in a real village, chalet hamlet or farm. The
hiders become hay bales, milk cans, garden gnomes and cows. The game runs over several rounds, the
seeker's role rotates each round, and everyone who plays earns something.

**Phase 1-2 contract used here** (`adventure/service`, `adventure/rewards`): code in
`src/Adventure/Versteckis/`, namespace `UnitSport.Adventure.Versteckis`. Progress is a
`SectionKey<VersteckisProgress>` in the `AdventureBook`, synced by `SectionSync`. Rewards go through
`AdventureService.Grant` (cash, item, `Title`). Events go through `AdventureService.Events.Raise`. The
round gate is `AdventureService.InMatch`, which is wired to `BrManager.InRound` in
`ServerWorld` today.

## Goal

Two to eight friends who meet in a village can start a game from the Activities screen in under a
minute. Everyone, the seeker included, has something to do every second. A round lasts 4-6 minutes.
The game is readable on keyboard, pad and VR. Every player who took part earns Adventure progress
and francs, whoever won. Missing players are filled by bots, so a pair of friends can still play a
4-player round.

Out of scope:
- a jail-and-rescue variant (Windtrace's), and an "infection" variant where caught hiders seek
  (open question 3);
- disguises beyond the seven props (seasonal ones are a festivals hook, section 9);
- voice chat;
- the offline game: bots need a server, so offline play means hosting one (open question 1);
- ranked or matchmade play across servers.

## 1. Round host: next to BR, not inside it

**Decision**: Versteckis has its own `VersteckisManager`. It reuses the BR pieces that are not BR,
and leaves `BrManager` alone. Reasons, checked in the code:
- `BrManager` holds **one** `BrState` per server (`_state`).
- Its phases (`Lobby -> Countdown -> Playing -> Ended`) are welded to the plane (`Board`, `BrFlight`),
  the zone, `MatchLoot`, and a 5-7 km `BrRegion`.
- A village game is 200 m across and 8 players at most, so several can run on one server. A BR match
  blocks the whole server for 20 minutes.

What is shared (extracted, or reused as it is):
- **`Core/Rounds`** (new, static): `Register(name, inRound, playing)`. It answers `InRound(peer)` and
  `Playing(peer)` over every registered mode. These callers stop calling BR directly:
  - `ServerWorld`'s `adventure.InMatch`;
  - `ChatManager.RefusedInMatch` and `TeleportRefusal`;
  - `_structures.InMatch`;
  - `VehicleManager.MayPark`.

  BR registers first, so its behaviour stays the same; `tools/brcheck.sh` must still pass.
- **Client lending**, as BR's `EnterMatch`/`Release` do:
  - the client stores where it stood (LV95);
  - `Inventory.BeginMatch()` lends the pack, and the round kit fills the hotbar (section 4);
  - `Permissions.SetInMatch(true)`: no ride menu, no fly camera, no admin tools;
  - at the end: `EndMatch()` and a teleport back.

  Cash is never at stake.
- **`Ui/FollowCam`** (new): `BrManager.Client.Spectate` moves here (the Camera3D, the chunk anchor,
  the ui_left / ui_right cycling). It drops the per-frame LINQ and list that it has today
  (`perf-no-per-frame-allocations`): the list of who can be followed is rebuilt only when the state
  changes. BR and Versteckis both use it.
- **Invites**: a new kind `versteckis` (`InviteService`, key = game id). The opener's party gets it,
  any player can be invited by name, and an "everyone" invite goes to the peers within 1.5 km of the
  area. Accept calls `VersteckisManager.Join`.
- **Bots**: `BotDirector.Add` and `BotProfile.For` (names, difficulty), as `BrManager.Bots` uses them.

**Games** (`VsState`, pure, sent whole as JSON to the game's entrants on change, like `BrState`):
- a server holds at most 4 games (`MaxGames`), and their areas must lie 600 m apart;
- each game has an id, its area, its rounds and a `VsEntrant` per player (peer, name, bot, `Score`,
  `Seeks`, `Role`, `Prop`, `Caught`, `CaughtAt`, the tool charges).

**Phases**: `Lobby -> Gather -> Hide -> Seek -> RoundEnd -> (Hide | Results) -> Closed`. Times are on
`ClockSync.SimNow`, so `/speed` and `--checkspeed` pace them, as for BR (#855).
- **Lobby**:
  - opens with up to 90 s for the others to join; the opener can start at any time from 2 entrants
    with bots on, or from 4 without;
  - anyone may open a game (it is local and small), one game per opener;
  - an admin can close any game (`/vs close <id>`).
- **Gather** (5 s): everyone is teleported to the village square, at the area centre, 3-6 m apart.
- **Hide** (45 s, 35 s at 4 players):
  - the seeker stands blind at the church door (section 4.1);
  - the hiders choose a prop and a spot.
- **Seek**: 150 s + 25 s per hider, at most 300 s (3 hiders: 225 s). It ends when every hider is
  caught or the time runs out.
- **RoundEnd** (12 s): the round's points and who was where. Every hider's spot is shown as a pin on
  the area map (the funniest moment of prop hunt).
- **Rotation**:
  - the number of rounds is the number of humans, clamped to 3..6; the opener can set 2..8;
  - each human seeks once, in join order, before anyone seeks twice;
  - the rounds left over go to a bot seeker;
  - with one human, the human seeks in round 1 and a bot seeks in the others.
- **Results**: 20 s, then `Release`. Leaving mid-game (`/vs leave`, a disconnect) keeps that player's
  rounds for the reward. A seeker who leaves ends the round at once, and nobody loses points for it.

**Console** (`ChatManager` -> `VersteckisManager.Command`, the same server path the Activities buttons
use):
- `/vs open [here|nearest|random|<village>] [rounds] [bots off|n] [easy|normal|hard]`;
- `/vs join [id]`, `/vs leave`, `/vs start`, `/vs invite <player>`, `/vs close <id>`;
- `/vs list`, `/vs stats [name]`.

**Activities**: a new tab, **Versteckis** (`Ui/ActivitiesScreen.Versteckis.cs`), with:
- the games on the server (area, players, phase, Join);
- an open form: where, rounds, bots and their skill, Open;
- in a game: the roster with roles and scores, Start (the opener), Leave.

## 2. The play area

`VsArea.Pick` (pure, tier 0) is modelled on `BrRegion.Pick`.

**Candidates**: each `PoiKind.Church` or `PoiKind.Chapel` (`PoiCatalogue.OfKind`) within 600 m of a
`places.json` `Town` with 15..600 `Buildings`, so villages and hamlets but not cities.
- The centre is the POI.
- A town with no church or chapel uses its own point, but only from `/vs open here` (a farm, a chalet
  hamlet), where the opener's position snaps to the nearest candidate within 1 km and otherwise is
  used as it is.
- `nearest` (the default) takes the candidate closest to the opener within 5 km.
- `random` draws a candidate anywhere, skipping the last 5 centres (`user://versteckis/history.json`,
  as BR keeps its own history).

**Radius**: `R = clamp(100 + 15 × players, 130, 220)` m (4 players: 160 m, 8: 220 m).

**Validation**: once the server has the area's tiles (buildings, roads, the coarse grid and cover, as
`RewardMap` loads them), the area is rejected when it has:
- fewer than 8 buildings inside;
- fewer than 60 decor spots (below);
- more than 20 % water or more than 30 % of its cells steeper than 30°.

A rejected `nearest` tries the next candidate (5 at most), then refuses: "No village to hide in near
here."

**Boundary**:
- red-and-white marker posts every 12 m on the circle (one `MultiMesh`, built once per game);
- a translucent band in a shader, faded in within 15 m of the line (a fragment term, no geometry);
- a HUD warning at 10 m from the line.

**Out of bounds**:
- a hider outside the line is **revealed** to the seeker after 3 s (a puff and a marker) and caught
  after 10 s;
- a seeker outside cannot tag.

**`HidePlan.For(area, doors, roads, cover, fields)`** (pure, tier 0, hashed per spot with
`OccasionHash`, as `TileDecor` and `HerdPlan` are) places the **decor** that the disguises blend into.
Every peer and the server compute the same list, and none of it goes on the wire.

| Prop | Where the plan puts copies |
|---|---|
| Hay bale | meadow and pasture cells (`CoverClass`), in rows of 2-5, 10 m off roads |
| Woodpile | against the long wall of a `BuildingKind.House` or `Agricultural`, one in two |
| Milk can | in pairs at `Agricultural` doors, and on the delivery bench (4.3) |
| Garden gnome | 1-3 within 6 m of a `House` door, one door in three |
| Bench | road verges every 40-70 m, and facing the church door |
| Cow | the real herds (`Farming.Herds`, `HerdPlan`) inside the circle; a pasture with no herd gets none |
| Flower box | under a front window of one house in two (the venue `Planter`) |

- 120-300 decor props per area.
- The plan is solid on the server too: it builds a `BoxShape3D` per prop round bots
  (`bots/server-collision`), so a bot and a player walk round the same bales.

## 3. Props and the disguise

`VsProps` (pure table): kind, name, footprint box, mesh factory, voice, walk cap.

| Kind | Mesh | Footprint (m) | Voice (Moo) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `HayBale` | new `VsPropMeshes.RoundBale` (the 250 kg round bale of `ItemId.HayBale`) | 1.2 × 1.2 × 1.25 | rustle |
| `Woodpile` | new `Woodpile`, its logs from `RewardMeshes.WoodStack`'s builder | 1.6 × 0.6 × 1.1 | knock |
| `MilkCan` | new `MilkCan` (a 40 l churn) | 0.4 × 0.4 × 0.7 | clank |
| `Gnome` | new `GardenGnome` (red Zipfelmütze) | 0.35 × 0.35 × 0.6 | squeak |
| `Bench` | new `ParkBench` (1.6 m; the venue `BeerBench` is a whole table set) | 1.6 × 0.6 × 0.85 | creak |
| `Cow` | `CowMeshBuilder.Body` + `Head` (Braunvieh) | 2.3 × 0.8 × 1.4 | moo (`Audio/CowSounds`) |
| `FlowerBox` | `VenueTerraces.Planter` (`PropKind`) | 1.0 × 0.4 × 0.84 | bee buzz |

- The meshes are built once, lazily (`??=`), with the `StyleKit` Prop role material, in Cartoon
  first. They show in the model viewer as `[Showcase("Versteckis")]`, and are checked with
  `--models,test_output/models`.
- **`VsDisguise`** (client): a `Node3D` child of every `FootPlayer`, made on the first disguise.
  - It holds one `MeshInstance3D` for the prop, plus a head node for the cow, turned as the herd's
    heads are.
  - Disguising:
    - sets `.Mesh` to the shared mesh;
    - hides the figure (`SkinnedFigure` `Visible = false`, no rebuild), the held item, the
      `NameTag` / `BotTag` and the shadow;
    - puts the prop's base on the body's feet.
  - Moving tilts the node up to 6° by speed (a transform, `IsEqualApprox` first). Crouch "settles" the
    prop to the ground normal and stops the tilt.
  - Undisguising flips the visibility back. `MeshRebuildDisciplineTests` covers the path.
- **Choosing**:
  - in Hide, the Disguise tool opens a radial of the kinds the area's `HidePlan` holds (no cow without
    a herd);
  - Aim + Use on a decor prop within 4 m copies that prop;
  - Hide allows free changes; Seek allows one change every 20 s, with a visible, audible poof.
  - The server checks the kind against the area (`ServeDisguise`).
- **A disguised body**:
  - walks at most 2.5 m/s (a cow 1.5 m/s);
  - cannot jump, run or emote (`FootPlayer.CanEmote`);
  - is in third person on desktop (`CameraToggle` refused).

  In VR it stays first person, with a prop silhouette on the wrist (open question 5).
- **Replication**: the prop is `VsEntrant.Prop`. Every peer that holds the hider's body must draw the
  prop, bystanders included (a human crouched in a garden would give the hider away). So the server
  sends `Disguises(long[] peers, byte[] props)` to every peer on a change, and to a joining peer
  (`SendTo`). Nothing is added to `FootPlayer`'s synchronizer.

## 4. Tools and objectives

The round kit is given as hotbar items (`ItemUse.Round`, new). It works through
`ItemController.UseSlot` on the client and through `BotHands.Use` for a bot, so a bot uses the same
entry points (`general/new-feature-bots`).

### 4.1 Seeker

- **Blind**:
  - in Hide the seeker is put at the church door (the `DoorSpot` of the `BuildingType.Church` nearest
    the centre, else the centre);
  - the screen goes black with a counting voice ("…achtundvierzig, nüünevierzg…") and the timer;
  - in VR the comfort vignette closes fully.

  The server enforces it (below), so blindness does not rely on the client.
- **Tag** ("Gfunde!"):
  - Use at a point within 2.5 m;
  - the server catches a hider whose footprint box (prop, `NetYaw`) is within 0.3 m of the point;
  - cooldown 1.5 s, and **4 s after a miss** (a decor prop or a decoy);
  - in VR, a hand that touches a prop and squeezes the trigger tags it.
- **Sniff** ("Schnüffle"): 3 charges, plus one back every 60 s.
  - For 3 s, each hider within 30 m gives off a puff of pollen. Its position is offset up to 4 m, so it
    narrows the search without pinning the hider.
  - A sound: one sniff tells nobody else anything.
  - The positions are sent to the seeker only.

### 4.2 Hiders

- **Decoy** ("Lockvogel"), 1 per round:
  - leaves a still copy of your current prop at your feet for 60 s;
  - tagging it costs the seeker a miss, and it poofs with your voice;
  - it is a server-side `VsState` decoy, drawn like a disguised body with no body.
- **Sprint** ("Gump"): the `Sprint` key while disguised gives a 2 s burst at 6.5 m/s, with a wobble and
  thumps the seeker can hear within 25 m. 1 charge, recharging in 30 s.
- **Moo** ("Muuh"):
  - plays the prop's voice at your position, audible within 60 m;
  - **+1 point**, 20 s cooldown, 5 points a round at most, never in the last 20 s;
  - the seeker hears it as spatial sound only, with no marker.

### 4.3 Objectives

Objectives break the disguise. They are one-shot per round, worth points for the whole hider team,
and placed from data at Gather. The disguise drops when the hold starts and comes back 6 s after the
hold ends.

| Objective | Site | Act | Points |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ring the church bell | the church or chapel door | hold E / Y 3 s (VR: grip the bell rope) | +3 the ringer, +1 every hider; the church toll (the `Ambience` church bank) heard over the whole area |
| Light the Höhenfeuer | the highest open-cover cell in the circle; a `RewardMeshes.WoodStack` | hold 4 s | +3, +1 each; burns to the round's end (`RewardMeshes.Flame`), seen from everywhere |
| Deliver a milk can | pick one at an `Agricultural` door; the bench ("Milchbänkli", new mesh) on the road nearest the centre | carry it (walk only, undisguised the whole way, 120 m at most) | +4, +1 each |

All three done: every hider still hidden gets +3, and the seeker's HUD shows "The village is
awake". The server checks each act in `ServeObjective(peer, site, step)`: the body within 2.5 m, the
hold's time on `SimNow`, the carry's path under the walk cap.

### 4.4 Score

| Who | Points |
|---|---|
| Seeker | +3 per catch; +1 per 20 s left when all are caught; +2 if all are caught |
| Hider | +1 per 30 s hidden; +3 for surviving the round; objectives and Moo as above |

- The game's winner is the highest total. Ties are shared.
- A caught hider **spectates** through `FollowCam` (the hiders first). Every hider is outlined for
  spectators.
- Spectators' chat goes to the game's spectators only while a round runs (`ChatManager`: a
  `Rounds.ChatScope(peer)` hook). This stops anyone calling out spots in-game.

## 5. Rewards: everyone who plays earns

Genshin gives co-op guests nothing, and players resented that. Here:
- **The server grants every human entrant the same way**, at `Release` and on leaving.
  - Being the host, winning or being in a party changes nothing, except a small top-scorer bonus.
  - No group yield in a round: the payout is a `Bank.Credit`, not a harvest, and `adventure-together`'s links and bonus are off while `AdventureService.InMatch` (phase 3 overview).
- **Payout per game** (`Bank.Credit`, `why = "versteckis"`): 20 CHF + 6 per round played, +10 for the
  top scorer.
  - The francs count for the first 5 games a day per player (`Daily` in the section).
  - Progress always counts.
  - A game alone with bots pays the same: one human with bots is still a game.
- **The `versteckis` section** (`VersteckisProgress`, version 1): `Games`, `Rounds`, `SeekRounds`,
  `Catches`, `Survived`, `ByProp[7]` (rounds survived as each prop), `Bells`, `Fires`, `Cans`,
  `Moos`, `Cantons` (the cantons played in, from the POI), `BestHideSeconds`, `Daily(day, games)`.
- **Titles** (`Reward.Title`, worn as quest titles are):
  - **Dorfchind** (the first game);
  - **Spürnase** (25 catches);
  - **Gartenzwerg** (15 rounds survived as a gnome);
  - **Sigrist** (10 bells);
  - **Füürwächter** (10 Höhenfeuer);
  - **Dorftour** (games in 5 cantons).
- **Cosmetics** (`ItemUse.Wear`, ids from this plan's block, `ItemId` 460-479 (phase 3 overview)):

- **A quest**, "Versteckis im Dorf": play one game in any village. It starts by itself when the player

- **Events**, raised at `Release`, once `Rounds.InRound` is false, because the bus drops an inactive

## Bots

Bots top up to 4 players by default, and to 8 at most (`/vs open … bots n`). They play both roles
through `VersteckisBrain : IBotBrain` (`src/Bots/VersteckisBrain.cs`). Choices are in `VsTactics`
(pure, `VsTacticsTests`), as `BrTactics` is. Thoughts come every 0.25-0.5 s. The mode
(`VersteckisManager.Bots.cs`) answers the director's events, as `BrManager.Bots` does.

- **Hider bot**:
  - **Spot**: from the `HidePlan`, a spot whose nearest decor within 8 m is of the bot's prop. It must
    be at least 25 m from the seeker's door and 15 m from other hiders, scored by cover from the
    door's line of sight (a ray on the server's collision). Easy bots pick from the first 10 spots,
    hard bots from all of them.
  - **Hold** still, settled.
  - **Flee** (Sprint, then walk away from the seeker) when the seeker is within 7 m and facing it.
  - **Moo** when the seeker is more than 40 m away and not facing it, by boldness.
  - **Objective** when the seeker is more than 80 m from the site and the site is not done. One bot at
    a time.
- **Seeker bot**, using honest senses only (`BotSenses` and what the HUD gives a human):
  - **Sight**: the props in its cone within 40 m. A brain never reads `VsState` roles or the hiders'
    positions.
  - **Memory**: each prop it sees, with its position. A prop that **moved** more than 1 m since last
    seen, or a prop **out of place** (no rule of the `HidePlan` table above puts that kind there),
    gains suspicion; a human notices the same things.
  - **Hearing**: `BotSenses.Hear` for Moo (60 m), Sprint thumps (25 m), the bell and the poof, placed
    within 10 m.
  - **Sniff** puffs when it sniffs.
  - **Acting**: it walks a sweep of the circle (`MoveTo`, wall follow), tags the most suspicious prop
    within reach, and sniffs when its suspicion list is empty. Decor carries a base suspicion of
    0.05-0.15 by difficulty, so bots make real misses.
- **Server paths**, which the RPCs call and bots call directly: `ServeTag`, `ServeSniff`,
  `ServeDisguise`, `ServeDecoy`, `ServeSprint`, `ServeMoo` and `ServeObjective`. Never `RpcId` a bot
  id: `Sniffed` goes to a human seeker only.
- **Server collision**: the decor boxes are built round bots (section 2). Gap: a client-only solid
  (a garden fence) is not seen by bots.
- **`bots/bot-capability-map`** rows: disguise, tag, sniff, decoy, sprint burst, moo, the three
  objectives, follow-cam (n/a). Each one is `ok` once the brain step lands, `gap` until then.

## Network

**Authority**: the server decides roles, catches, points, objectives and bounds. Transforms stay
client-authoritative (`net/multiplayer`), so the server checks what a hider's client publishes (the
proxy's `NetGlobal`, as rewards' `PositionOf` does), 4 times a second per entrant:
- **Speed**: under the walk cap (2.5 or 6.5 m/s), +30 % slack;
- **Bounds**: inside the circle;
- **Ground**: within 2 m of the server's coarse height (`BrManager.GroundAt`-style lattice);
- **Not inside a building**: tested against the area's footprints.

A failed check counts as out of bounds (revealed, then caught). Catches are judged only on these
server positions, never on where the seeker's client drew a prop.

**Hide phase: hiders are withheld from the seeker.** `InterestService` gains
`Func<long viewer, long target, bool>? Withheld`, which wins over `Together`. In Hide, every hider's
body is despawned on the seeker's client, so a hacked client that skips the black screen sees an empty
village. The seeker's own position is held within 3 m of the door: beyond that, the server puts it
back (`Teleporter.TeleportTo`, as travel does). A bot seeker just stands. In Seek,
`InterestService.Together` makes all entrants see each other (the area is under 300 m).

**Residual risk**: a modified seeker client can tell a networked body from local decor. Decoys are
server entities too, which blurs that; full hardening is open question 4.

**RPCs** on `World/Versteckis`, all `Reliable`, rate-limited to 4 a second per peer:
- client -> server:
  - `RequestDisguise(byte kind)`;
  - `RequestTag(double e, double n, float alt)`;
  - `RequestSniff()`, `RequestDecoy()`, `RequestSprint()`, `RequestMoo()`;
  - `RequestObjective(byte site, byte step)`.

  Opening, joining and leaving go through chat commands (`/vs …`), as BR's do.
- server -> the game's entrants: `SetState(string json)` (the `VsState`) and `Caught(long hider, long
  by)`. Server -> the asking seeker: `Sniffed(double[] lv95, float[] alts)` and `Missed()`. Server ->
  everyone: `Disguises(long[] peers, byte[] kinds)`. Server -> peers within 80 m:
  `Sounded(long peer, byte voice)`, for Moo, Sprint and the poof.
- Positions travel as LV95 doubles (`net/positions-on-the-wire`).

**Protocol**: one bump for the new node, the `versteckis` invite kind and the `Withheld` filter: 56
on `main` today, so it becomes whatever `main` holds + 1 at merge (the other phase-3 plans bump too).

## Persistence

- The `versteckis` section in the player's `AdventureBook` file (`user://adventure/players/<key>.json`),
  saved with `JsonStore.SaveAsync`, debounced as every section is. Offline and online progress stay
  separate, as for everything Adventure. A bot gets session progress (`SessionFor`), never saved.
- Server: `user://versteckis/history.json` (the last 5 centres). Games themselves are not saved: a
  restart ends them, and each player is granted for the rounds already played (`_ExitTree`).
- Client settings (`GameSettings`): the counting voice on or off, outline colour for spectators.

## Performance

- **Decor**: one `MultiMesh` per prop kind per area. It is built once at Gather from `HidePlan` (on a
  worker; the upload on the main thread) and freed at `Closed`. A disguise is a mesh reference swap.
  The cow's head is a node turn.
- **No per-frame allocations**:
  - static `StringName`s for the actions;
  - the HUD's text on change;
  - `FollowCam` without LINQ;
  - the boundary band a shader uniform.
- **Server**:
  - the 4 Hz validation (8 bodies a game, 4 games) runs under `ServerStats.Ran`;
  - the `Withheld` hook is O(entrants²) only in Hide;
  - brains as BR's, at most 7 a game;
  - measured with `--serverstats` on a full bot game.
- **Budget**: under 0.2 ms client frame for decor, disguises and HUD at 8 players (`--perf` on the
  fixture), and under 1 ms server busy per game.

## Open questions

1. **Offline**: no offline game, or a bot game on the client? Bots are server-run (`BotDirector` is
   server-only). **Recommend**: no offline mode; offline, the tab offers "Host a game", which starts
   a hosted server (`net/hosting`) with bots.
2. **Bystanders**: free-roam players who walk into a running area. **Recommend**: they see the props
   (the `Disguises` broadcast), cannot tag, and get a toast offering "Join the next game"; nothing is
   withheld from them.
3. **Variants**: Windtrace's jail and rescue, or caught hiders joining the seekers. **Recommend**:
   ship the plain rules first; add "infection" as a lobby option once the bots seek well (it reuses
   Tag).
4. **Hardening against a modified seeker client** that can tell bodies from decor. **Recommend**:
   accept it for v1 (the hide phase is already withheld, and the server judges every catch). If it is
   abused, replace the hider's replicated body for the seeker with a server-run prop entity in Seek.
5. **VR hider view**: first person with a wrist silhouette, or a forced third-person tether?
   **Recommend**: first person (comfort); decide after a headset playtest (`vr-headset-sessions`).
6. **Who may open a game**: anyone, or an admin as in BR? **Recommend**: anyone, one game per opener,
   4 per server, an admin can close any.
7. **Cows**: may a hider join a real herd's graze? A real cow moves on the shared clock
   (`CowMotion`), so a still "cow" stands out. **Recommend**: yes, it is the skill ceiling. Bots get no
   herd-mimic step in v1.
