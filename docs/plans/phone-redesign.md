# Phone interactions v2

Status: design only (mockups in `docs/plans/phone-redesign/`), nothing implemented. Tracking issue: [#1253](https://github.com/SuperQuentin/UnitSportSwitzerland/issues/1253)

Source: a five-lens review (keyboard/mouse, pad, VR, touch, apps and flows) of `src/Phone/`, then three redesigns judged into one.

## Phone Interactions v2: Five Verbs on the Existing Foldable

### Principles

- Five verbs, one meaning each on every device: Pick (move highlight), Open (activate), Back (exactly one step up, never folds except from Home), Put away (leave from anywhere), and Take out. Esc is only ever Back while the phone is out.
- Keep the structure; fix what breaks it. Foldable, cover, 3x2 Home, apps and the Esc/B/swipe-right chain stay. No recents stack, dock, widget board, second Home page or settings app: scope is a tune-up plus targeted rework of Map, Contacts, Activities and Camera.
- Phone key is a toggle: the key that takes the phone out puts it away from any page except text entry. Pad gets a hold D-pad Up chord, touch gets a real Phone button, VR keeps the pocket grip plus wrist menu.
- Back is generic: any page except Home and Travel goes up one level, so Training or any future app can never fold the phone by accident. Every app shows a visible Back chevron. Travel gets an explicit Cancel.
- Decide four devices together: every action has keyboard/mouse, pad, VR and touch cells, an InputHints row, and a row in xr/vr-action-map and touch-controls. The phone.md notes get a touch column and one stated rule (folded walks, open stands, camera walks).
- Selection never shifts under the hands: calls are a pinned banner, not a re-indexed quick list. Quick tiles keep a fixed order (Open, Map, Camera, Contacts, Photos, Activities) and are individually pickable.
- Sizes follow the body: touch slop 2 mm, back swipe 10 mm, targets 7 mm minimum and 9 mm primary via pxPerMm; mouse thresholds scale with window height; VR travel look at 25-45 cm instead of 14 cm. Touch uses the enlarged square-on inner screen.
- Nothing essential lives only in a tooltip, key hint or toast: labels and badges under tiles, a persistent hint bar generated from the focused control, error banners that stay, and an action button on every empty state.
- Social actions are safe and visible: Fight and Bring here confirm (Cancel default), 'Call' becomes 'Invite', and a result line reports the outcome. Server slash commands (/tpa, /tphere, /party, /fight) stay unchanged for bots.
- Pad-first Map: Recents, Near you and categories as focusable rows; search is optional. Single amber accent (0.98, 0.72, 0.10) for selection, focus, hover and the VR fingertip ring; dark glass otherwise.

### Risks and open points

- Hold D-pad Up 0.4 s may feel laggy next to the emote tap; keep the hold-Back quick menu as the fallback and playtest the timing. Hold-B 0.4 s put-away must not collide with hold-Back 0.3 s (quick menu) and tap-Back (inventory); verify on a real pad.
- New P key and right-click-as-Back need a conflict check against the key map and against selfie right-click (selfie keeps it only inside Camera, where Back is Esc); update controls table, InputHints and vr-action-map in the same change.
- Merging Menu and Training into Activities changes Home tiles and the cover list; --phonecheck flows that relied on Training folding the phone, the 3x2 VR layout assertions and the notes must be updated together; Journal and Host overlays need a Back-to-phone path.
- VR comfort changes (25-45 cm travel look, edge ghost, handedness-swapped face buttons, ear-answer, selfie hold) cannot be verified headless; --phonevrcheck must cover false positives and accidental ear-answer, and on-headset checks are the user's.
- Millimetre gestures need pxPerMm available to PhoneController, which has no touch path today; enlarging the inner screen on touch changes camera framing and must not alter the desktop look. Re-run TouchLayoutTests at 2220x1080 and small phones; keyboard lift over a 3D quad is unproven and Android on-phone checks are the user's.
- Contacts result lines (Sent, Accepted, Declined) need server feedback that the raw slash commands do not give today: a small net change (tier 2 check on the remote peer) and a bots/bot-capability-map row (#837); the phone must keep acting on intents, not raw Input reads.
- Pad A as shutter needs a clear definition of 'standing still', or it either jumps instead of shooting or shoots when the player wanted to jump.
- On-screen keyboard for pad and VR is new UI; if it slips, Map still works from rows alone.
- Staging: one oversized PR would be hard to test. Suggested split: (1) input verbs, Back rule, toggle, hint bar and notes; (2) Home/cover and Activities merge; (3) Map and Travel; (4) Contacts and confirm; (5) Camera, touch and VR polish.
