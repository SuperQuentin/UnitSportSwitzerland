# Traffic light timing from conflict times (#979)

Today a phase change waits for the longest amber of the groups that stop plus a fixed all-red (2 s, 1 s after an arrow or
a crossing), and 3 s more whenever a bike group starts (its lead). That is too much all-red, the same everywhere. The user's
rule: the time between two conflicting movements is the theoretical shortest one that lets the last vehicle entering before
red (previous phase) avoid the first vehicle entering at green (next phase), from the geometry. It gives a table of conflict
times between movements, negative in some cases, from which finer phase plans can be built later.

## Decisions (the user, Oct 20 2026)

- **Time base**: from the end of the ending group's green to the start of the next group's green. The last vehicle of the
  ending group crosses its line as its amber ends (it runs the amber); the next group's red-amber is not counted.
  `t(i, j) = amber(i) + (clear(i) + length(i)) / v_clear(i) − enter(j) / v_enter(j)`.
- **Speeds and lengths** (SN 640 837 / RiLSA-like): clearing car 10 m/s straight, bike 4, walker 1.2; entering car 11.1 m/s
  (40 km/h), bike 5, walker 1.5; length car 6 m, bike 2 m, walker 0. Capped by the road's speed limit (the arm's); a turning
  car at the speed its path's radius allows, `sqrt(a · r)` with the lateral acceleration traffic uses (2.5 m/s²).
- **Granularity**: today's phases kept; each group starts its green as soon as its own conflicts with the groups ending
  allow (per group), not at one shared phase change. Finer phase plans from the table come later.
- **Geometry**: RoadGen's, as laid. The old layout (no paths) keeps a simple fallback.
- **Bike lead**: only where a car group with a permissive conflict with the riders goes green with them; where every car
  that would cross them is red, none.

## Steps

1. **Movements as lines** (`tools/RoadGen/Layout/ConflictTimes.cs`, new): per signal group of a lit junction its movements'
   lines from its own stop line: the car paths (`Connector`s of its arm and moves, kind Car), the riders' (kind Bike and
   Path ways), the crossing (kerb to kerb on the zebra, the walkers both ways). Each line carries its kind, its stop line's
   distance before the line's start (the stop bar's station against the mouth's), its width, its speeds (capped by the arm's
   limit) and, for a turn, the radius along it.
2. **Conflict points**: for each pair of lines of two groups with a conflict in the plan's matrix (hard or permissive: a
   permissive pair may share a green, but the table holds it too), where they first come within half their widths of each
   other: the clearing distance along the ending line to past that area, the entering distance along the starting line to
   its start. Lines that only merge (same exit) meet where they first touch. The pair's time by the formula; a group pair's,
   the most of its lines' pairs. A table `float[groups, groups]`, NaN where no conflict.
3. **Timing** (`SignalPlan.Build(arms, seed, amber, times)`): when given, a phase change no longer adds the all-red; each
   group starting at a phase change starts at the latest of (end of green of each conflicting group ending there + its
   time), never before the phase change itself, its red-amber before that. The phase's own length (the cycle) uses the
   latest start. A negative time is kept in the table; the timing clamps at 0 for now (no conflicting greens overlapping:
   `Validate` stays as it is). Without a table (the old layout, older callers) today's timing.
4. **Bike lead**: `Time` gives the lead only where a car group with a permissive conflict with the bike group starts green
   in the same phase; else the riders start with their phase.
5. **Checks**: tier 0 tests: the conflict time of two crossing straight lines and of a turn against a crossing (by hand);
   the table at J1 and J2 (every hard pair present, times in a plausible range); the plans stay valid. Test region and the
   168 real tiles: plans valid, cycle lengths before and after. A J2 clip of the green-paths view over a whole cycle.
6. **Notes**: `tools/traffic-signals` (the table, its parameters, the timing).
