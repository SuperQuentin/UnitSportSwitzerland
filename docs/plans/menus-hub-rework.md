# Menus rework: the phone as the hub, a lighter inventory, one motion language

Status: design only, nothing implemented. Tracking issue: [#1824](https://github.com/SuperQuentin/UnitSportSwitzerland/issues/1824).

Source: a read of the inventory, menu and phone code and notes (November 2026), then three rounds of
questions to the owner about how the game feels to play. Builds on `docs/plans/phone-redesign.md`
(#1253, done) and changes one of its rules (see 1A).

## What is wrong today

The owner plays on keyboard and mouse and in VR, and finds the panels "not fluid enough". Asked what
that means, three things came out, in this order:

- **Too many keys and places.** On foot there are about eleven entry points, each with its own key
  and look: Esc (pause), I / Tab (inventory), F2 (the 12-wedge ring), hold X (item wheel), R (travel
  menu), J (Journal), P (character), M (map or place search), Up (phone), F1 (controls), Enter (chat).
  Several lead to the same thing: Activities is in the ring, in the phone's Activities app and behind
  its Host button.
- **Too many steps per task.** In the inventory, getting a pack item into use is: select the slot,
  read the side card, press Use / Wear / In hand. Crafting is a third column of the same panel with
  no search and no way to remember what a recipe still needs.
- **A stiff look.** `PauseScreen`, `QuickMenu`, `AdventureLog`, `ActivitiesScreen` and the phone's
  screens have no transition; only the inventory and the travel menu tween anything. Shell pages are
  built fresh on every push and pop in.

Two more, specific:

- **The phone's Map app is a list of places, not a map.** A real map already exists
  (`Adventure/Places/AdventureMap`: relief, fog, borders, places, pins, travel points), but only on M
  while the adventure is on, and not in the phone.
- **The phone is slow for something used often.** Out and open is about 1 s of camera moves
  (`LookSeconds` 0.5, `CoverSeconds` 0.55), the map adds a 0.4 s fade to black, and the body stands
  still on every app but the camera.

Reference feel, the owner's pick: **Minecraft** and **Fortnite / Rust / ARC Raiders**: one key, one
panel, nothing slow, direct gestures.

## Decisions

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| Overall direction | The phone is the hub |
| Inventory | Stays its own panel (the bag is not an app) |
| Map | A flat map on the phone screen and a hologram mode; the hologram is the default in VR |
| Phone feel | Opens in one press on the last app; the body walks while it is open |
| Mouse with the phone open | The cursor works the screen; hold right mouse to look |
| Equip gesture | Double-click, and a drop on a figure of the character |
| Crafting | Search, a pinned recipe on the HUD, crafting from a bench or a fire |
| F2 quick menu ring | Stays as it is |
| Pause menu | Used only for Settings and Leave: it shrinks to those |
| VR wrist menu | Remembered as "not very functional", not re-tested: test on a headset before planning |
| First slice | The phone |

Not chosen, so not planned: the inventory as a phone app, a physical backpack, retiring the ring,
a single phone key replacing M / J / P / R.

## Phase 1: the phone is the hub

### 1A. Open in one press, walk while it is open ([#1825](https://github.com/SuperQuentin/UnitSportSwitzerland/issues/1825))

- The `phone` action goes from the pocket straight to **open on the last app**, in about 0.25 s:
  `Stage.Drawing` and `Stage.Opening` run together, the view move is one short ease. The folded cover
  stays reachable (fold from Home, as today) and still shows when a call rings, but it is no longer
  a stop on the way in.
- **The body walks on every app**, as it already does on the camera app (#1149). This replaces the
  rule from #1253, "folded walks, open stands, camera walks", with "the phone never stops the body".
  The phone keeps the events it needs and leaves the rest to the body.
- **Views**: no app dollies square onto the screen any more, since the world must stay visible to
  walk. The open phone is framed large in the lower part of the view. The black backdrop stays only
  for the Travel curtain, which still has to hide the jump.
- **Sprint** puts the phone away, by the same rule that holsters an item on a sprint start.

| | Keyboard / mouse | Gamepad | VR | Touch |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| take out and open | Up (as now), one press | quick menu (hold Back) › Phone, lands open | pocket grip or wrist entry (unchanged) | PHONE pill, lands open |
| walk | WASD | left stick | as today | floating stick |
| look | hold right mouse (cursor hidden while held) | right stick | head | drag off the phone |
| work the screen | cursor, click, wheel, arrows, Enter | D-pad, A | fingertip or ray (unchanged) | tap, drag |
| back one step | Esc, Backspace, the chevron, mouse button 4 | B | unchanged | chevron |
| put away | Up again, hold Esc 0.4 s, or sprint | hold B 0.4 s | unchanged | PHONE pill |

- **What this takes away**: right click is no longer Back (it is look), and no longer the selfie in
  the camera app (F and the Selfie button remain). WASD no longer move the highlight (the arrows do).
  On a pad, A is the phone's while it is open, so there is no jump with the phone out. While a text
  box has the keys, letters type and the body stops.
- **Net**: `PhoneState` stays one byte with the same values. A remote peer now sees a walking body
  with the open-phone arms, a pairing that only the camera app produced before: tier 2 check on the
  remote peer.

### 1B. The Map app is a real map ([#1826](https://github.com/SuperQuentin/UnitSportSwitzerland/issues/1826))

- `Page.Map` shows a **pan-and-zoom map** filling the screen: relief, canton borders, places, the
  player's arrow, online friends, pins, travel points. Drag pans, the wheel zooms, a click selects a
  place and its card offers Travel.
- The list of #1253 stage 3 (Recent, Near you, the chips, the optional search) becomes a **drawer**
  over the map, so a pad still travels without touching the map.
- **One map, not two**: the drawing is `AdventureMap`'s, made embeddable (its canvas and its
  `IMapOverlay` layers), on the shared `MapView` and `MapRelief`. With the adventure off: no fog, and
  the places of the place search. M opens the same map in the phone.
- Travel keeps `Teleporter.Curtain` and `Permissions.CanTeleport`.

### 1C. The hologram ([#1827](https://github.com/SuperQuentin/UnitSportSwitzerland/issues/1827))

- A button on the Map app (and the default in VR) projects a **3D relief above the open phone**:
  the country from the `MapRelief` lattice, and the ground round the player from heights the client
  already has, as `XR/XrMap` does today for 3 km. Markers (you, friends, pins, quests, travel points)
  are small nodes on it.
- **Desktop**: the phone lies flat in the hand and the view tips onto the relief; drag turns it, the
  wheel zooms, a click picks a place. **VR**: it floats over the phone, the free hand grabs it to
  turn, two hands zoom, a fingertip picks.
- **Later, with G2**: zooming all the way out reaches the globe (`Globe`). Not in the first version.
- Rules it must keep: the relief mesh is built when what it shows changes, never per frame (turning
  and zooming move the node); the look (scan lines, edge glow) is a shader, built in Cartoon first;
  its builder is in the model viewer (`[Showcase]`).
- **Overlap**: [#535](https://github.com/SuperQuentin/UnitSportSwitzerland/issues/535) (the VR
  table-top map for downloads) should use the same relief model; `XrMap` (the hand-held map) is
  replaced by this.

### 1D. More apps, a smaller pause menu ([#1828](https://github.com/SuperQuentin/UnitSportSwitzerland/issues/1828))

- Home gains **Character** and **Vehicles** beside Journal (3x2 becomes 3x3, the VR layout with it).
  Each opens its existing screen with a Back-to-phone path, as Journal does now. A native app is
  written only where the hand-off feels wrong in play.
- M, J, P and R stay and open the same things, so no key anyone knows is lost.
- The pause menu keeps Resume, Settings, the tutorial skip and Leave.

## Phase 2: the inventory, still its own panel

### 2A. Equip in one gesture, a figure to dress ([#1829](https://github.com/SuperQuentin/UnitSportSwitzerland/issues/1829))

- **Double-click does what the item is for**: clothes and bags are worn, food and drink are used,
  anything else goes to the hand. One new `Inventory.Equip` operation, so the pad and VR ways call
  the same thing. Collecting matching stacks, today's double-click, moves to **Ctrl + double-click**.
- **The figure**: the character stands in the panel with the ten body slots and the bag slot around
  it. A carried item lights the slot it fits; dropping it there wears it, dropping it on the hand
  holds it. It replaces the 5x2 "Wearing" card. The figure is rebuilt only when the outfit changes.
- Devices: pad, A held 0.3 s equips (a tap still picks up); VR, a second trigger pull on the same
  slot, and the figure's slots are laser targets; touch, a double tap.
- The panel gets wider with a figure in it. Layout to settle in the slice: figure left, grid in the
  middle, and the right column as tabs (item card / crafting) instead of two columns side by side.

### 2B. Crafting ([#1830](https://github.com/SuperQuentin/UnitSportSwitzerland/issues/1830))

- **Search**: a box over the recipe rows filters them as you type.
- **Pin**: one recipe can be pinned; a small HUD card (in the manner of `QuestTracker`) lists what is
  still missing, updated only on `Inventory.Changed`.
- **From the world**: at a workbench, a burning campfire or a stove, the prompt offers Craft and
  opens the panel on that station's recipes. E on a workbench already searches it as loot, so the
  prompt becomes `[E] Search  [R] Craft`; the pad button and the VR way are chosen in the same change.

## Phase 3: the rest

### 3A. One motion language ([#1831](https://github.com/SuperQuentin/UnitSportSwitzerland/issues/1831))

- One helper in `UiKit`: open (a fade and a short rise, about 0.12 s), close (about 0.08 s), and
  hover / selection feedback. Used by the shell pages, the pause menu, the ring, the Journal,
  Activities, the inventory and the travel menu.
- Motion never delays input: a panel takes keys from its first frame.
- A "Reduce motion" setting makes it a plain fade; VR panels only fade.
- Goes into `ui/ux-guidelines` as the rule for new panels.

### 3B. The VR wrist menu

- No design yet. First a headset session with the watch ring and the wrist list
  (`XrWristRing`, `XrWristMenu`), recorded in `tests/TO-TEST-BY-HUMAN.md`. With the phone as the hub,
  the likely outcome is that the wrist list shrinks to what the phone does not hold.

## Order

1A, 1B, 1C, 1D, then 2A and 2B, then 3A. 1A comes first because every other phone slice is judged
through it. 2A, 2B and 3A touch other files than the phone and can run beside it; 3A should land
after 1D and 2A so it does not conflict with their layout changes.

## Rules every slice follows

- **Four devices together** (`general/new-action-three-devices`): keyboard, pad, VR and touch cells
  for every new gesture, an `InputHints` row, and its row in `xr/vr-action-map` and
  `core/touch-controls`.
- **Bots** (`general/new-feature-bots`): all of this is UI over actions that already have server
  paths, or local only (inventory, crafting). Each PR still says so.
- **G2** (`general/working-during-g2`): `MapView` is LV95 maths today. No slice adds flat LV95
  arithmetic (`FlatLv95RatchetTests`); markers and picked places are `GlobalPos`.
- **`ui/ux-guidelines`** self-review on every reworked panel; the look from `ui/style-guide`.
- Checks: `--phonecheck`, `--phonevrcheck`, `--mapcheck`, `--invcheck`, `--invuicheck`, `--menucheck`
  are updated in the slice that changes what they assert; headset and real-pad checks go in
  `tests/TO-TEST-BY-HUMAN.md`.

## Risks and open points

- **Walking with a cursor.** Mouse-on-screen with hold-to-look is the owner's choice, but it is
  unproven here: playtest 1A before building 1B on it. The fallback is "the mouse looks, keys work
  the screen" for list apps.
- **Losing right click as Back** undoes part of #1253. Mouse button 4 is not on every mouse; Esc and
  the chevron must be enough alone.
- **No jump on a pad with the phone out.** Acceptable if the phone goes away fast; to confirm on a
  real pad.
- **The hologram on desktop** needs a view that is quick to reach, or it brings back the slow dolly
  this plan removes.
- **Vehicles.** The phone is on foot only. A hub that vanishes in a car is odd; not solved here.
- **Double-click moving** breaks a Minecraft habit. If it grates in play, the alternative is to keep
  collect on double-click and equip on middle click (which uses an item today).
- **`[E] Search  [R] Craft`** puts two verbs on one object, against the one-interact-key habit of
  `core/input-conventions`; it is the smallest change, not the only one.
- **Panel width** with a figure, a grid and crafting must still fit 1152 px.
- **No mockups yet.** `phone-redesign` had ten; the map, the hologram and the inventory figure would
  each be cheaper to judge as a picture before code.
