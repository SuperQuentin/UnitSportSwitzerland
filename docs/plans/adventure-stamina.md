## Goal

Exploring the Alps becomes a loop of reading the mountain: how far can I climb before my arms give
out, where can I rest, which sunny face will lift my paraglider to the next ridge. One budget,
stamina, limits the body's effort. It is spent by the muscles (climbing, a climb-jump, a sprint
stroke, a pigeon's wings, sprinting in a fight) and never by equipment that flies on its own. Lift is
the counterweight: the sun and the wind, computed the same way on every peer, carry a glider up.

Out of scope: ledge hanging and shimmying along a lip, overhangs and roofs (a face leaning out past
vertical is let go), ice tools and crampons, rain making rock wet (no weather system yet), Föhn/Bise
valley streams (kept as an open question), stamina for vehicles and bikes (the home trainer has its
own power model), charged melee (a hook only, see 2).

## 1. The stamina rules (`src/Player/Stamina.cs`, tier 0)

A `struct Stamina` beside `AirReserve`, no Godot in it, linked into the unit tests. Units are points;
the HUD shows a share.

- **Max** = `Base` 100 + 10 x `steps` (0..10, so 200 at most) + a food bonus (25, see 3).
- **Spend** (`Spend(kind, dt)` for rates, `Pay(kind)` for one-off costs, both returning false when
  there is not enough):

| What | Cost | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Sprint on foot, out of combat | **free** | the Wuthering Waves rule: Switzerland is big and walking is 1.6 m/s |
| Sprint on foot in combat | 12 /s | in combat = `InDuel`, or hurt or hurting a player in the last 6 s (`FootPlayer.CombatUntil`, new) |
| Climbing, moving | 9 /s | x1.3 on `LooseRock`, `Scree`, `LooseScree` (it crumbles) |
| Climbing, holding still | 1 /s | you can rest a little on a face, not for ever |
| Climb-jump | 18 once | 1.9 m along the face in 0.4 s: cheaper per metre than climbing (Genshin's trade) |
| Kick off a face (back-jump) | 0 | the way down and into a wingsuit |
| Swim, sprint stroke | 8 /s | the easy stroke is free; empty = easy stroke only |
| Swim, dive held with sprint | 8 /s | the plain dive is free |
| Pigeon, flapping | 5 /s, Shift 10 /s | gliding, soaring and walking free; empty = glide only |
| Wingsuit, parachute, paraglider | **free** | sink already limits a flight; a drain would make thermals pointless |
| Mantle, slide, wall jump | free | already bounded (height, speed, twice per airtime) |

- **Regen**: nothing for `RegenDelay` 1.0 s after the last spend, then 25 /s on the floor (standing,
  walking, running out of combat), 35 /s seated (`Sitting`), 15 /s riding a ground mount or a
  vehicle, 25 /s for a pigeon walking or perched. Nothing in the air, climbing or swimming: a ledge
  you can stand on is the rest stop.
- **Winded**: hitting 0 sets `Winded` until 25 % is back. Winded: no climb entry, no climb-jump, no
  sprint stroke, no flap, no sprint in combat; already climbing = let go (5).
- **Food**: `Restore(points)`; `Buff(kind, seconds)` with `StaminaBuff.Warm` (climbing costs x0.75)
  and `StaminaBuff.Fed` (+25 to the max). One of each at a time, a new one restarts its timer.
- `Infinite` (a flag): spends succeed and nothing drains. Used when the rules say off (below).

**When it applies** (`Stamina.RulesFor(player)` on the owner, one place):

- On only where the Adventure layer is: `AdventureService.Instance?.Active(peer)` (the shared gate:
  false with `Systems.Adventure` off, on test and fixture worlds unless a probe names `adventure`,
  and per player during a BR round, `BrManager.Playing`). The server sends the answer to the owner
  with the section sync (below); offline the client is peer 1 and asks the same service.
- Off (infinite) as well in creative mode (`FootPlayer.Creative`) and when the server runs
  `--stamina off` (sent in `Arrival.Rules`: a free-roam server can keep Adventure and drop the meter).
- **Battle Royale**: stamina off through `Active(peer)` **and free climbing off** in phase 1
  (client side `Permissions.InMatch`, server side `BrManager.Playing`). A match keeps today's
  movement (mantle, wall jump, `XrClimb` with its own 15 s arm fatigue), so no balance changes land
  with this plan. Argued: climbing changes sniping and the zone, and a BR wants its own numbers;
  open question 1.
- Free climbing itself is on wherever the Adventure gate is on, or in creative mode. With
  Adventure off (a race server, a fixture world without `adventure`) the movement is today's.
- Admins are not exempt by rank: an admin who wants it off uses creative mode or
  `/stamina infinite` (admin online, free offline; also a test hook for the checks).

## 5. Free climbing (`src/Player/FootPlayer.Climb.cs`)

**What can be climbed** (`Player/Climbable.cs`, pure, tier 0, given what one ray found):

- A face whose normal's Y is between **-0.15 and 0.6** (53° to a slight overhang). 0.6 is
  `XrClimb.Steep` today: both climbs share the constant. Under 52° (`StandMaxDeg`) you walk; 52-53°
  you slide (`steep-ground`). Leaning out past -0.15: let go.
- **Terrain**: any land cover except `Glacier`, `Snowfield` and `Water` (ice and firn need tools;
  out of scope). The cover is read with `ChunkManager.TryGetCover` only on the owner. Where the
  cover is unknown (a server-run bot, a far tile) it counts as rock.
- **Buildings and structures**: any `StaticBody3D` face (walls, bridge piers, retaining walls,
  dam faces) unless its node is in the group `no_climb` (glass towers later, the airliner's
  stairs, fences). Never a vehicle, a moving body (`AnimatableBody3D`, the steamer), a player, a
  tree trunk (`trees-solid`: a thin capsule reads as a pole) or a placed object.
- Wet rock: open question.

**Entering** (on foot, `_ride == null`, not sliding, not carried, not holding a `Heavy` load,
climbing allowed by 1):

- Desktop and pad: pushing the stick into a climbable face (`direction.Dot(-wallNormal) > 0.6`)
  - in the air: at once, on contact, if `TryBeginMantle` did not take it first (a low ledge still
    vaults);
  - on the ground: after 0.25 s of pushing, and only if the face rises past `MantleMax` (2.1 m)
    above the feet; a garden wall still needs Jump to mantle, as today.
- **Wall jump** (`foot.md`) keeps working where climbing does not apply: Space in the air against
  a face you are not pushing into, any face in a match, and non-climbable faces. Pushing into a
  climbable face grabs it instead; Space then climb-jumps.
- An NPC or a bot never enters by pushing (a `MoveTo` into a cliff must not start a climb): it
  needs the new `FootActions.Climb` intent (`(bool Jump, bool Crouch, bool Climb)`).

**The state** (`_climbing`, dispatched in `PhysicsStep` right after the mantle, before the swim:
`if (ClimbPhysics(dt, onFloor)) return;`):

- Each physics step one ray from the chest (1.25 m) toward the face, 0.9 m long, gives the face
  point and normal (`Core.RayQuery`, no allocation). The body is held `BodyRadius + 0.05` off it and
  moved with `MoveAndSlide` (so a ceiling or a roof edge stops it) along the face plane:
  `up = (Vector3.Up - n * n.Y).Normalized()`, `right = n.Cross(up)`.
- Speeds: **1.2 m/s up, 1.4 sideways, 1.6 down**, scaled by the stick (a half stick climbs at half
  speed for half the cost: the cost is per second x stick share, at least the 1 /s of holding).
- **Jump** = climb-jump: 1.9 m along the stick's direction on the face (up with no stick), 0.4 s,
  costs 18. **Back + Jump** = kick off: 4 m/s out, 3 m/s up, free, then an ordinary fall; with
  12 m under the feet the base-jump rule (`flying.md`) opens the wingsuit on the next Jump.
  **Crouch** = let go (falls; landing damage as any fall).
- **Top-out**: when the chest ray finds no face (a lip), `TryBeginMantle(-n, 1.5)` runs and the
  existing `StepMantle` pulls the body over (the mantle's ceiling sweeps included). If it fails the
  body stays at the lip (a cornice too high): let go or traverse.
- **Leaving at the bottom**: moving down onto a floor you can stand on (`!TooSteep`) ends the climb.
- **Empty** (0 points): let go, a grunt, the hands slide 0.3 s (the figure's arms drop), then a fall.
  Remote peers see the same from the pose.
- **Rides**: no climbing on any ride. Skis, a bike, a worn paraglider (it is a `Flyer` ride even on
  the ground): push into the cliff and you stop, as today. Mounting from a climb is impossible
  (`ApplyRide` ends the climb first, like `LeaveWater`).
- Items holstered (`UsablePlayer` null while `IsClimbing`), the held item hidden (`HeldItemVisual`).
- Camera: the third-person chase keeps its arm (`camera-arm-reach`) but swings 30° off the face
  so the figure is not hidden by the rock; first person looks freely, the body follows the face.

**The pose everyone sees** (no new field on the wire, a new value):

- `PoseKind = PoseWallClimb` (8; 4 is swim and hang, 5 the ladder, 7 the fall, so 8 is next).
  `Anim = (speed along the face, limb phase 0..1, climb-jump 0..1, 0)`.
  `BodyPose` leans the figure into the face (rotation about the body's right axis from `n`, plus the
  0.05 m stand-off), so a 60° slab and a vertical wall read differently. Both already travel in
  `NetPose` (`perf-player-snapshot-size`).
- The figure: `HumanMeshBuilder.PoseWallClimb(figure, phase, jump)` sets the `SkinnedFigure`'s bones
  from a new `Rig WallClimb(phase, jump)` beside the ladder's `Climb(side)`: diagonal limbs move
  together (left hand with right foot), hands and feet on a plane 0.35 m in front of the chest, the
  climb-jump a full stretch. No mesh is built (`no-mesh-rebuild-per-frame`); a remote advances the
  phase from `Anim.X` between updates, as `AdvanceSwim` does. Hands are on the face plane by
  construction: no IK ray on remote peers.
- `--skincheck` gets the pose (phases 0, 0.25, 0.5, and a jump) in its pose list.
- **XR**: `XrClimb` keeps its grip-and-pull and the `Carrier` it drives (`CarriedPose = 2`), but
  spends from `player.Stamina`: 1 /s holding, 9 points per metre gained (the desktop cost per metre
  at 1.2 m/s ≈ 7.5, the arms work harder), and lets go when empty. Its private `Stamina` 15 s
  remains only where the rules say off (a match), so VR in BR does not change. On the stick, a VR
  player climbs exactly like a pad player (seated play, comfort).

## 6. Lift (`src/World/AirLift.cs`, pure, tier 0)

One query, `AirLift.At(double e, double n, float alt, double serverNow, IAirLiftTerrain t)` -> the
air's velocity there (m/s, almost always vertical), the sum of three parts. `IAirLiftTerrain` is a
thin face over `ChunkManager.TryGetHeight`, `TryGetCover` and the placed campfires, so the tests feed
a function. Everything is a function of LV95 position, the height grid and the shared clock: every
peer and the server compute the same (`clock-sync`), nothing is summed per frame.

- **The sun**: the azimuth/elevation formula in `DayNight` (sunrise 6, sunset 18, 62° at noon) moves
  into a pure `World/SunPath.Direction(hour)`, which `DayNight` and `AirLift` both call; the hour is
  `WorldClock.HourAt(serverNow)` (the server has no sky, `day-night.md`).
- **Thermals**: one candidate per 400 m LV95 cell, jittered by a hash of the cell. Its strength =
  `sun` (max(0, sun.Y), 0 at night) x `aspect` (0.4 + 0.6 max(0, n·sun) with `n` the terrain normal
  from heights 20 m around the source) x `cover` (rock, scree, open, vineyard, paved 1.0; forest
  0.6; water, glacier, snowfield 0; unknown 0.8) x `life` (each thermal breathes on a 6-10 min cycle
  of its own: `max(0, sin)`, phase from the hash). Under 0.25 it does not exist.
  - Column: radius 35 + 45 x strength m, lift in the core **up to 3.5 m/s** with a Gaussian falloff,
    a -0.8 m/s sink ring at 1.5 radius; top 300 + 900 x strength m above the source ground, faded
    over the last 15 %. A paraglider (trim sink 1.15) climbs ~2.3 m/s in a strong core.
  - Cells cached per LV95 cell (`ThermalSource`: position, normal-derived aspect, cover), bounded
    LRU of 256; filled when a 3x3 neighbourhood is asked and the tile's grid is loaded.
- **Ridge lift**: a wind of the day, from a hash of the day index on the world clock: west/south-west
  60 %, Bise (north-east) 25 %, Föhn (south) 15 %, 3-8 m/s. Lift = wind x clamp(rise, 0, 1) x
  fade(AGL, 0..200 m), where `rise` = the ground's climb over the 40 m upwind of the point. A
  windward face holds a glider; the lee (negative) gives a -0.5 x sink.
- **Updraft volumes**: a registry `AirLift.Volumes` of vertical cones (centre, radius, top, speed).
  - A **burning campfire** (`PlacedKind.Campfire` while `CampfireClock` says it burns): radius 4 m,
    6 m/s, 25 m tall. The player-lit Genshin updraft: open the paraglider over it.
  - Authored ones: the places and rewards plans may put fixed updrafts at trials or summit crosses
    (data in their catalogue, registered on load). The registry is the contract; this plan ships
    only campfires.
- **Who uses it**: `FlightEnv` gets `Vector3 Air` (default zero). `FootPlayer.FlyPhysics` fills it
  (`AirLift.At` once a physics step for the own flyer only). `Canopy.Fly` adds `Air` to its target
  velocity; `Wingsuit` adds it to the air-relative velocity (its 13 m/s sink barely notices a
  thermal, a campfire still kicks it); `Pigeon` adds it to its glide (a pigeon can soar for free).
  Plane, helicopter and `VehicleBody`'s NPC flyers pass zero (phase 2 if wanted). Not on foot.
- **Off in a match** (`Permissions.InMatch`): a parachute hovering in a thermal would change the BR
  drop. Off with `--lift off` on the server (in `Arrival.Rules` too); on by default, independent of
  the stamina switch (lift is world physics, not progression).

## 7. Lift cues

- **Birds circle in thermals**: `BirdLife` asks `AirLift.ThermalsNear(at, 1500)` once every 5 s for
  the local player and gives up to two strong thermals a soaring raptor (`FlightStyle.Soar`, already
  `Mode.Soaring`) whose circle centre is the thermal and whose height rises with it. The shared
  birds (`shared-birds`) are the server's, so the server picks them for every peer.
- **Rising specks**: one `GPUParticles3D` per visible thermal within 1.5 km (at most 4), pale
  pollen-dust specks rising in the column, motion in the particle shader; a cumulus puff (one shared
  mesh) at the top while strength > 0.5. Placed on change, never rebuilt (`no-mesh-rebuild-per-frame`).
  Cartoon first (`styles/style-kit`); PS1 and Realistic get the style's own speck colour.
- **Campfire**: its smoke particles rise taller and a heat-shimmer quad shows while it lifts.
- **Variometer**: on the paraglider, the pigeon and the wingsuit, a beep whose pitch and rate rise
  with the climb (`SfxSynth.VarioBank`, static, shown in the sound player); a low tone in sink
  over 2 m/s. Settings > Audio > "Variometer" (on).

## Bots

- Rule 1: `FootActions` gains `Climb`; a bot climbs only when its brain asks, and spends the same
  `Stamina` (server-run, infinite where the rules say off). Rule 2: no client RPC is added, so
  nothing to mirror; `StaminaUpgrades.Offer` is server-side already. Rule 3: the climb pose is
  replicated, so a bot sees a climber like a player does. Rule 4: the server's collision round a bot
  has the ground and buildings (`bots/server-collision`), so a bot's climb rays hit what a
  player's do; the cover is unknown there (counts as rock, so a bot may climb a glacier wall: named
  as a gap).
- BR bots: n/a while climbing is off in a match. Free-roam and co-op bots: a `Climb` skill
  (`MoveTo` that, blocked by a cliff, climbs it when the stamina estimate reaches a ledge) is a
  follow-up, row `gap` in `bots/bot-capability-map`. Lift: n/a (bots do not fly gliders).

## Network

- **Authority**: foot movement is client-authoritative (`net/multiplayer`), and the air reserve,
  health and the swim are decided by the owner. Stamina follows: the meter lives on the owner, is
  **not replicated**, and nothing waits for the server.
- **Wire changes**, one protocol bump of this plan's own (`Handshake.Protocol` +1 after the
  objectives plan's bump from 49, `protocol-handshake`): `Arrival.Rules` gains `stamina` and `lift`
  bools; the section rides the objectives plan's `SectionSync`; `PoseKind` 8 is a new value on an existing
  field (old clients would draw nothing sensible). `FootActions.Climb` is local.
- **Server checks, proportional** (`Net/StaminaWatch.cs`, server, 10 Hz over replicated state only):
  the server tracks each non-creative player's climbs: vertical metres gained in `PoseWallClimb`
  since the last floor contact. Over `1.5 x (max + 75) / 9 x 1.2 + 4` m (the most the rules allow
  with a full ring, the best food and climb-jumps, plus slack) it logs `[stamina] implausible climb
  <name> <m>` once per climb and counts it in `--serverstats`. Same for a glider rising more than
  `AirLift.At(...) + 2 m/s` sustained over 10 s outside any updraft (`[lift] implausible climb`).
  No kick, no correction: there are no movement checks today, and this is the first data.
- The max bonus comes from the server's progress book; a client faking it only cheats itself, and
  the check above catches the visible part.

## Persistence

- The `"stamina"` section of the player's `AdventureBook` file
  (`user://adventure/players/<key>.json` on a server, `user://adventure/local.json` offline):
  `StaminaProgress(int Steps)` only. Current points and food buffs are not saved: a join starts full.
- `/stamina infinite` is per session.

## Performance

- Stamina: a struct step on the owner, no allocation. The ring: uniforms only on change, hidden at
  full (no draw).
- Climbing: one chest ray a step (two at a lip, plus the mantle's sweeps once); remote climbers cast
  nothing (`perf-camera-rays` spirit). The pose is a `SkinnedFigure` bone update.
- Lift: one `AirLift.At` a step for the own flyer only (9 cached cells, a few Gaussians, the
  campfire list), none for remote flyers, ~0 on a server (only `StaminaWatch` at 10 Hz for gliders).
  The specks: at most 4 emitters, placed on change.

## Open questions

1. **Climbing in a Battle Royale**: off in phase 1. On, it changes sniping and the zone; it would
   want stamina on in matches too (and BR bots climbing).
2. **Sprint cost outside combat**: free here. If the Adventure playtests find sprinting trivialises
   distances, a 3 /s sprint cost above 1,500 m altitude (thin air) is a Swiss-flavoured middle.
3. **Glider drain**: free here. If thermals make paragliding too strong for exploration, a drain
   only while the brake is held (flaring) is the softest lever.
4. **Wet rock and ice**: needs weather and an ice-axe item (crafting) first.
5. **Föhn/Bise streams**: a valley-following wind that carries a glider along (Genshin's wind
   currents); needs valley axes from the terrain preprocessor.
6. **Thermals on the map**: should the atlas (places plan) or binoculars show likely thermals, or
   must the player read birds and specks?
7. **XR climbing pose**: `XrClimb` still shows the ladder's `PoseClimb`; switching it to
   `PoseWallClimb` needs the face normal, which its grip point already has.
